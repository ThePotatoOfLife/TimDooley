COMPARATIVE LAYER

Hawkins Scale

David R. Hawkins' Map of Consciousness is preserved here as an interpretive model, alongside contemporary psychology and a strict separation between symbolic “frequency” language and physical Hertz measurements.

Hawkins level ≠ Hz. Emotion ≠ electromagnetic radiation.

The scale

The original model uses a 1–1000 level system. The atlas does not treat the levels as scientifically established measurements of consciousness, nor does it assign them electromagnetic frequencies.

Frequency bridge

Hertz means cycles per second. Electromagnetic radiation has physical frequency and wavelength. Emotions can be studied through psychological, physiological and neural measurements, but there is no validated one-to-one conversion from an emotion or a Hawkins level to an electromagnetic frequency.

How the atlas uses it

  • Psychology: study appraisal, action tendencies, arousal, cognition and social context.
  • Trajectory: model transitions between states rather than treating people as permanently occupying one level.
  • Phase: identify threat, approach, conflict, agency, integration and stabilization patterns.
  • Symbolism: preserve Hawkins' spiritual vocabulary without presenting it as experimental physics.
