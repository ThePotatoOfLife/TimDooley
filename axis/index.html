Enter one relationship, not three floors

The base topology is now simpler: Source and Manifestation remain complete fields while overlapping. Their shared region is Door. Plane and Cross are internal coordinates of that relation; Axis supplies the vertical orientation through it. The familiar Plane → Cross → Door sequence survives only as a reader prompt.

The fork

Knowledge is upstream of both Life and Strife

Knowledge creates distinction. Distinction creates memory, responsibility, judgment and consequence. What happens next determines whether the same buried material becomes nourishment or repetition.
Living RootMemory and history that remain connected enough to nourish new growth.
Root of StrifeUnresolved material that keeps reproducing conflict, dependency, capture or division.
Root of AshResidue after destruction, severance, exhaustion or burning. Ash may remain residue—or become soil.

Four routes through one structure

The Axis is a grammar of movement

D1–D11 are a navigation projection over the House. Life, Strife, Repair and Tim's ascent are routes through the same overlap/Axis structure. They are not four cosmologies and not four competing ladders.

Developmental overlay

The lifecycle starts before the Plane and continues after Fruit

The canonical practice path gives the Axis a longer developmental cycle without turning it into another D-level scheme.

Mud→Burial→Eye→Root→Sprout→Plane→Door→Ladder / Mountain→Tree→Fruit→Seed→Return

Lifecycle stages and D-levels answer different questions. A stage can intersect the same D-level more than once.

Seed and Spiral routing

One Seed Junction, several possible spirals

The strongest geometric seed pivot is CS: Son-center and exact lower tip of the shared Door. This is where Seed of Death and Seed of Life are best understood as states of one generative potential. D5 is the coarser navigation projection of Door as threshold; it does not replace the geometric CS pivot.

Seed of DeathCompressed, surrendered or buried potential at CS. It can decay, sleep, root or become viable for sprouting.
Seed of LifeThe same or recovered seed once burial has become viable rooting/sprouting potential. It begins at the root pivot, not in Heaven.
Fruit SeedUpper growth produces seed again. Mature Life descends with transferable capacity and may return to CS for a new cycle.
Rooting Spiraldownward · generativeCS → Below → D3 Soil/Living Root. Descent anchors memory/context and prepares future growth.
Drain / Strife Spiraldownward · degenerativeUnresolved event → Ash → Roots of Strife → Wide Road → Swamp/Drain. Recurrence loses agency, correction and exit.
Sprouting / Life Spiralupward · generativeLiving Root / Seed of Life → CS → Door → Plane → D6 Spiral Ladder → Tree of Life → Garden → Fruit.
Repair Spiralreversal · regenerativeAsh/Swamp → evidence → root cause → acknowledgment → Door → Living Root → Life. Memory remains; compulsory repetition does not.
Fruit / Return Spiraldownward · generative returnGarden/Crown/Fruit → Seed/transmission → Heaven → Door/Axis → World → service/planting → possibly CS again.
Ringzero axial displacementReturn without meaningful state change. A Ring becomes a Spiral only when recurrence changes level, capability, rooting, integration or disintegration.

So there is no single “downward spiral.” Down may mean incarnation/rooting, Strife/Drain, or mature return. Likewise, “up” is not automatically Life: the Life test is branching, fruit and accountable return.

Where things actually go

Planes are floors; Trees are routes through them

The three planes answer where the reader stands. The Trees answer how knowledge, memory, conflict, repair and growth move through those floors. This keeps “Below” from becoming a synonym for Strife and keeps “Heaven” from becoming a synonym for Life.

Heaven Plane · Crown JunctionCanopy, Garden, integration, transmission, angels/messengers and divine/celestial traditions where their own sources support those roles, Fruit and accountable return.

The exact center is the Axis. Research Lab is the inner Forge ring around that center: unresolved material is tested there before promotion into stable Room ownership or a stronger route.

Three standing surfaces

Heaven, World and Below sit on the same Axis

The ordinary Plane remains the middle/world-facing standing surface. The new Heaven Plane and Below Plane are circular navigation projections above and beneath it: Heaven gathers integration, Garden, North, Tree of Life and return; Below gathers roots, archive, shadow, subculture, residue, Strife, Wide Road and Swamp. The Axis connects all three, while the Door remains the shared Source↔Manifestation relation rather than one of these planes.

Heaven PlaneCrown and upper branches: integration, orientation, transmission, Life and return.
World PlaneTrunk and visible cross-section: body, evidence, history, geography, institutions and consequence.
Below PlaneRoots and underside: provenance, buried cause, shadow, subculture, residue and unresolved Strife.
Open the three circular platformsLife routeStrife route

These are navigation standing-points, not literal cosmic floors or moral rankings. Below is not Earth itself; roots may nourish as well as entangle.

One organism

Roots are not the opposite of Heaven

The cleanest model is temporal and biological at once. Root, trunk and branch are different functions of the same life. Descent can reveal what sustains or traps the present; ascent can transform it; the future branches from what the organism successfully carries through.

Roots · pastMemory, ancestry, causes, stored consequence, buried potential and unresolved residue.
Trunk · presentContinuity through the Cross and Door: the living relation capable of carrying traffic both ways.
Branches · futureDifferentiation, possibility, fruit, seed and the consequences of what the present successfully integrates.
