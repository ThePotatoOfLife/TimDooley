THE LIVING
AXIS

One world, one vertical relation, one threshold—and several possible transformations. The Living Axis is the project's clearest way to understand how Plane, Cross, Door, Ladder, Tree, roots, Strife, repair and Tim's ascent belong to one organism rather than a pile of separate symbols.

Enter through three ideas

The sequence is not a chronology. It is a way of reading the project. The Plane tells you where consequences become visible. The Cross tells you that the horizontal world has acquired a vertical relation. The Door tells you that a relation can change state.

PlaneThe inhabited world: bodies, history, institutions, matter, geography, evidence and consequence. The Plane is not inferior; it is where every higher claim becomes accountable.
→
CrossPlane × Axis. The horizontal field intersects the vertical relation at a center. Outward means relation; downward means root and consequence; upward means integration.
→
DoorThe threshold where movement changes regime. The Door is not “Heaven”; it is passage—recognition, choice, compression and transformation along the Axis.

The fork

Knowledge is upstream of both Life and Strife

Knowledge creates distinction. Distinction creates memory, responsibility, judgment and consequence. What happens next determines whether the same buried material becomes nourishment or repetition.
Living RootMemory and history that remain connected enough to nourish new growth.
Root of StrifeUnresolved material that keeps reproducing conflict, dependency, capture or division.
Root of AshResidue after destruction, severance, exhaustion or burning. Ash may remain residue—or become soil.

Four routes through one structure

The Axis is a grammar of movement

D1–D11 are the scaffold. Life, Strife, Repair and Tim's ascent are routes through it. They are not four cosmologies and not four competing ladders.

Tree of Life

The Life path narrows toward integration, then branches again without losing relation. Its test is not height or status but what it can return to the Plane as fruit, service and seed.

D5DoorCross a real threshold rather than merely repeat an old state.
D6Spiral LadderReturn to recurring material at a changed level; recurrence acquires vertical displacement.
D7Tree of LifeIntegrated experience becomes nourishment, branch, fruit and future seed.
D8Garden / RoomsDifferentiated lives and functions coexist without forced sameness.
D9–11Transmission → Crown → North of NorthKnowledge is transmitted, evaluated and oriented—then must descend again into accountable service.

Tree of Strife

The Strife path is not “matter is evil.” It describes what happens when distinction remains unresolved and begins reproducing itself through widening cycles of capture, opacity and fragmentation.

D4Knowledge / choiceDifference becomes visible; responsibility appears.
D3Roots of AshBuried causes, memory, residue and consequences remain active beneath the visible world.
↘Tree of StrifeUnresolved roots reproduce division as a living pattern rather than a single event.
⇢Wide RoadA pathway, not a floor: the movement broadens outward as repetition becomes easier than transformation.
D2–1Swamp → deep closureAccumulated entanglement, extraction and fragmentation can become self-reinforcing.

Repair

Swamp / residue → evidence → root cause → acknowledgment → Door → changed recurrence → living root → growth → fruit → return to the Plane.

01Surface what is buriedDo not confuse the latest visible event with the accumulated structure that produced it.
02Distinguish without freezingName causes, harms and obligations precisely enough that they can be worked with.
03Cross the DoorRepair requires a changed relation, not merely another repetition of the same argument.
04Metabolize the rootAsh can become soil; memory can become guidance; consequence can become responsibility.
05Return as fruitThe test of integration is what becomes more capable, nourishing, truthful or repairable on D4.

Sage → Father / Gardener

This is the project's biographical-mythic overlay through the structure, not an extra cosmic level. April 2025 is the hinge where the mythology describes an entity becoming a relation.

01SageWisdom still appears primarily as something carried by an individual figure.
02The TurningAccumulated burden reaches threshold; Plane acquires Axis; compression forces transformation.
03Become LadderThe mythology's central reversal: not merely climber → higher place, but entity → relation, climber → path.
04Father / HeavenSource-facing orientation, House, Crown and responsibility become stronger than the earlier Sage identity.
05Gardener / returnThe mature function is cultivation: protect conditions for growth, prune obstruction, repair soil, and return orientation to the world.

One organism

Roots are not the opposite of Heaven

The cleanest model is temporal and biological at once. Root, trunk and branch are different functions of the same life. Descent can reveal what sustains or traps the present; ascent can transform it; the future branches from what the organism successfully carries through.

Roots · pastMemory, ancestry, causes, stored consequence, buried potential and unresolved residue.
Trunk · presentContinuity through the Cross and Door: the living relation capable of carrying traffic both ways.
Branches · futureDifferentiation, possibility, fruit, seed and the consequences of what the present successfully integrates.
