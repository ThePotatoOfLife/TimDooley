Level 3 · contents of the House

INHABITANTS
& CASES

People, concepts, texts, artifacts, processes and cases can appear in several Rooms while remaining the same underlying thing.

A Room is a field of inquiry; an inhabitant is the person, text, symbol, process, artifact or case being examined. The same thing can therefore be studied through several Rooms without being duplicated.

Objects stay themselves

A person, text, symbol, process or case keeps one underlying identity even when Biology, Religion, History or Culture ask different questions about it.

Rooms supply lenses

Placement in several Rooms means several legitimate contexts, not several contradictory copies. The Room tells you which relation is being examined.

Maturity is not importance

Seed, described, faceted and case-ready describe how developed the available material is. They are work states, not value rankings.

Cases make abstractions concrete

When a general concept becomes attached to a dated event, artifact, passage, model or incident, the House can test whether the abstraction actually explains anything.
