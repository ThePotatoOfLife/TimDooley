INHABITANTS
& CASES

People, concepts, texts, artifacts, processes and cases can live in several Rooms without becoming duplicate owners or new Rooms.

This surface answers a different question from the Rooms directory. A Room is a field of inquiry; an inhabitant is a thing examined inside one or more fields. The same object can therefore be viewed through several lenses while remaining one object. That distinction keeps the House from multiplying a new page architecture every time a concept crosses domains.

Objects stay themselves

A person, text, symbol, process or case keeps one underlying identity even when Biology, Religion, History or Culture ask different questions about it.

Rooms supply lenses

Placement in several Rooms means several legitimate contexts, not several contradictory copies. The Room tells you which relation is being examined.

Maturity is not importance

Seed, described, faceted and case-ready describe how much structured material the repository currently has. They are work states, not value rankings.

Cases make abstractions concrete

When a general concept becomes attached to a dated event, artifact, passage, model or incident, the House can test whether the abstraction actually explains anything.
