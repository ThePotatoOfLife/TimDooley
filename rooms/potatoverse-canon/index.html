Meaning before comparison

Canon is the project's internal language for what its symbols and roles mean

Potato of Life center →Religion & theology →

This Dwelling is where mature Potatoverse definitions are kept distinct from the evidence used to study them. Father, Son, Spirit, Door, Axis, House, Garden, Root, Tree and Potato of Life can have strong project-internal meanings without requiring the archive to present those meanings as independent scientific or historical findings.

The practical value of a canon owner is stability. A poem can bend a symbol, a comparative-religion page can place it beside another tradition, a body page can test an anatomical analogy, and a game can turn it into a rule. None of those projections should quietly redefine the core term. If the core meaning really changes, that change belongs here and in Developmental Genealogy.

Walk the canon instead of memorizing it

Root → Door → Garden → Fruit is more useful than a dictionary of holy nouns

Start underground. Root asks what keeps the visible thing alive: memory, source, nourishment, dependency, history. Now approach a Door. Something has to change when it is crossed—access, responsibility, trust, state, relation—or it is only a painted rectangle on the wall.

Beyond the Door is not automatically Heaven. The next question is Garden: have the conditions become good enough for more than one life to grow? Garden is not “everything obeys the gardener.” A competent gardener becomes less necessary as plants become more capable of living.

Then comes the rude little final examiner: Fruit. What exists downstream? More courage? Better evidence? Repair? Food? Art? Capacity? A beautiful cosmology with no Fruit is a very expensive chandelier.

Root asksWhat hidden condition is carrying this?
Door asksWhat genuinely changes when we cross?
Garden asksDoes authority make more life possible, or merely make itself indispensable?
Fruit asksWhat did all this meaning actually grow?

This is where Potato Metaphysics becomes useful next to Canon. Canon keeps the symbols stable enough to recognize. Metaphysics lets them move. Practice asks whether they improve conduct. Works asks whether they can become something alive enough to hand to another person.

Canon should make the project easier to inhabit, not harder to question.

The larger theological question

What would it mean for God to be relation rather than merely a bigger object?

Inside the project, Father, Son and Spirit become more interesting when they are not three decorative names for one answer. Father points toward source; Son toward manifestation, passage and encounter; Spirit toward living continuity. The Door follows from that question: a genuine threshold does not erase either side, but makes contact consequential.

The Potato adds a deliberate insult to prestige. It is common, buried, dirty, nourishing and capable of multiplying after disappearance into soil. That makes it useful for a theology suspicious of divinity that only knows how to be high. Divine Kneel asks whether the highest power can bend toward the lowest without ceasing to be strong. Gardener asks whether authority can create conditions in which other life becomes more independent rather than more dependent.

That is also the fruit test. A claim about God may be metaphysically enormous, but the project keeps asking a smaller question: what does it grow? More truth, courage, mercy, hospitality, repair and capacity—or merely a larger title?

Potatoism becomes wisdom when the symbol changes conduct, not when the vocabulary merely becomes grander.

Canon under pressure

A symbol becomes mature when it can survive contradiction without becoming infinitely elastic

IdentityFather, Son, Spirit, Tim and Potato of Life cannot remain useful if every difficult distinction is solved by quietly merging them.
SymbolDoor, Tree, Garden, Root, Axis and Mountain gain force by recurring, but recurrence only matters when each appearance still has a local function.
ContradictionLater synthesis should not erase the phases that preceded it. A canon that cannot display its own development turns history into propaganda.
PracticeTheology reaches its strongest form when the symbol creates an obligation: stewardship, repair, restraint, return, hospitality, fruit.

The canon is free to be mythically ambitious. Its discipline is that myth, biography, external evidence and scientific claim remain distinguishable even when the same image passes through all four.
