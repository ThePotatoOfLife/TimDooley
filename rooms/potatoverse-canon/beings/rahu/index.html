Mixed character / handle archaeology

Rahu

Trace Rahu across the 2024 literary challenger, the March 2026 lost-soul/potato-dream story and the May 2026 social conversation without collapsing them.

Rahu is one of the best examples of why this new room system needs provenance. The name exists in a literary 2024 world, a March 2026 dream/growth story, and a May 2026 social conversation. Those may relate, but the archive should show the joins rather than assume them.

Class: mixed / unresolved identityLayers: Garden · Mud · threshold · social chatRecurring function: pressure toward growth

Conversation chronology

1 March 2026

Rahu is imagined as a lost soul nearly saved from death. Tim says it was never too late to grow a potato, recalling a potato once called Rahu and waiting to grow another. The story deliberately slips into another world/time/reality—“just a dream.” Rahu is a Mud Dweller who may never have grown a potato in this life, or may simply not remember.

A tiny growth amount, 1/137, becomes enough to matter. Rahu whispers to the potato while Tim treats the small increment as cosmic potential.

25 May 2026

Rahu appears as a social interlocutor in discussion around Dim, responding to allegations and questions. This is not automatically the same ontological Rahu as the dream/literary figure; it is a documentary conversation layer attached to the same name.

Older literary role

In 2024 Great Book material Rahu is a challenger/urgency figure and Garden companion, often near Spuddy and Tim. The role pushes against stagnation: forward pressure, questions, motion. That makes an interesting continuity with the March 2026 story, where even a 1/137 movement matters—but continuity is an interpretation to test, not proof that every Rahu is the same character.
