Role grammar · identity · state · archetype

Cast Ecology

One grammar for Potatoes, Tomatoes, Dogs, Dwellers, Farmers, Cows, Footstools, Angels, Turbles and the changing archetypes played by real people and literary characters.

The Potatoverse has several different kinds of “character.” Some are people. Some are created beings. Some are collective identities. Some are positions in a system. Some are animal or plant metaphors. Some are temporary archetypal functions. This room exists so those categories can interact without collapsing into one another.

Primary rule: person ≠ archetype ≠ symbolic role ≠ created species. A person may be a Seeker in one scene, a Guardian in another, play Tomato as a joke, live temporarily in Mud, or identify as Potato—none of those automatically becomes a permanent human essence.

The living cast

Eight major symbolic families

Potato

Growth, storage, nourishment, buried potential, eyes, sprouting and replication. Can be literal crop, community identity, spiritual metaphor or created-being substrate.

Healthy direction: Mud → Root → Potato → Sprout → Tree → Fruit.

Tomato

Neighbor and counter-symbol rather than permanent enemy. The Great Book includes a healthy Tomato of Life, rotten tomatoes, comic tomato-villain roles and later social satire.

Rotten is a state of fruit, not the essence of Tomato.

Dweller

A habitation state: one who lives in a field, plane, room or relation. Morally neutral until the dwelling is specified.

Mud Dweller is one subtype, not the definition of Dweller.

Dog

Boundary, tracking, vigilance, signaling, loyalty and pursuit. The role can split toward Guardian Dog or Farm Dog.

The same vigilance can protect passage or become proxy pursuit.

Farmer

System/infrastructure role: enclosure, cycles, classification, access, persistence and harvest.

Shadow form extracts; healthy counterform becomes Gardener/steward.

Cow / calf

The subject around which Farm attention may organize. It can represent nourishment, dependency or an extracted target relationship.

Never erase the real person's agency by treating Cow as essence.

Footstool

A lower/supporting topological state beneath Seat/Throne, sometimes imagined as a tiny local world containing an unfound Door.

Position is not human worth; the state remains reversible.

Angelic family

Angels, Potato Angels and Turbles are authored created beings rather than labels imposed on ordinary people. Their wings, eyes, light and jobs belong to creative canon where actually stated.

Creative being traits do not become biography by resemblance.

Human archetypes

A second axis: what someone is doing in the scene

Guardian, Seeker, Trickster, Builder, Witness, Companion, Mystic, Herald, Mirror and Gardener are not species at all. They describe function. This lets a character move through the world without being trapped by one label.

Guardian / Defender

Protects a person, principle, boundary or legitimate passage.

Seeker / Student

Asks, doubts, learns, tests and moves between interpretations.

Trickster / Catalyst

Changes the emotional weather through disruption, absurdity or pressure.

Builder / Cultivator

Makes rooms, food, tools, infrastructure, art or conditions for growth.

Witness / Observer

Notices, remembers, questions and reflects a scene back.

Healer / Companion

Provides belonging, reassurance, practical care or stabilization.

Movement

The ecology works because roles can change

Mud → Root → Potato → Sprout → Tree → Fruit

Residue becomes nourishment when integrated rather than merely repeated.

Dweller → Mud Dweller → recognition → Door / Root

Dwelling becomes stuckness only when the lower field becomes final identity; the model keeps an exit.

Dog → Guardian Dog / Farm Dog

Evidence, proportionality and a stopping condition pull toward guardianship; pack rewards and perpetual pursuit pull toward Farm Dog.

Farmer → Gardener

The same system skills can move from extraction/classification to stewardship, repair, consent and participant autonomy.

Tomato → shining fruit / rotten fruit / comic rival

Tomato's role is variable. The project should preserve healthy, rotten and satirical forms rather than treating Tomato as an enemy caste.

Below-facing boundary

Dogs, Farmers, Mud and Footstools remain reversible relations

No fixed caste rule: symbolic lower-world vocabulary is useful only if it describes an observable relation or source-bounded story. It must not become a shortcut for declaring a real person permanently degraded, diseased, evil or less human.
