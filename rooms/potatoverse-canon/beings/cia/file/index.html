How to read a CIA file

A dossier is a source-bounded character record, not a verdict

The Character Archive combines several layers that must remain distinct. Timeline records dated appearances or events. Associations record documented co-presence or relationship evidence. Roles describe project/story functions that may change over time. Stories preserve literary or narrative material. Sources show what actually supports the record. The symbolic account is Potatoverse bookkeeping, not real debt, credit, criminal liability or objective human value.

Documentary

Public posts, chats, dated media, archive files and other recoverable source material.

Interpretive / archetypal

Archive synthesis about role, development or relationship. Dog, Farmer and other mythic labels should point back to dated exchanges and never substitute for them.

Creative / symbolic

Potatoverse roles, story scenes, karma/welfare and enhancements. These are project material rather than external biography.

Unresolved

Missing dates, uncertain identity, ambiguous wording or weak provenance remain open instead of being silently completed.
