Why Kale is in the Hall

A grounded Potato does not need to be the loudest figure in the room

The recovered Kale record is interesting because the archive repeatedly describes a grounding function. The old character files associate Kale with clarity, release and grounding, and place the figure in Garden / Saloon social space rather than a prestige-heavy upper role.

The current Potato classification comes from project canon as of 7 October 2026. It should therefore be read as a present project role laid over an older documentary/literary record—not as a claim that every 2024 source explicitly called Kale a Potato.

Great Book layer

The literary Kale is practical enough to pull cosmic talk back toward earth

In one authored Great Book scene, Kale interrupts lofty discussion with the practical question, What if an actual farmer read it? Elsewhere the literary voice uses an audio tapestry metaphor for people becoming different notes inside a shared whole.

Those scenes are useful because they agree with the later archival reading of Kale as reflective and grounded. They are still literature, not verbatim documentary transcripts.
