Person · changing social role

Marty Biz Music

Trace Marty from ordinary room-life through rival-orbit mythology and into grief without converting hostile source characterizations into permanent fact.

Marty is not useful as a frozen villain label. He is useful because the archive catches him changing function: ordinary companion, noisy center of gravity, rival orbit, subject of increasingly hostile Chronicle retellings, and finally a person whose absence becomes grief and music.

Arc

The social figure becomes a counter-center

2024 · inside the room

Companion before antagonist

Chronicle material first preserves Marty in the ordinary social texture: voice chat, Tanks, jokes and other recurring people around Tim. That matters because later “Drift King” mythology can otherwise erase the period in which Marty is simply part of the room.

November 2024 · distance begins

Chance & Taylor / separate direction

Later Chronicle entries describe Marty using the Chance & Taylor name, frustrated by bans and increasingly separate from The Great Book while still remaining close enough to keep interacting. The structural change is more important than the insult-language: he becomes a second center rather than merely another participant.

Late 2024 · hostile Chronicle layer

Raid, Garden-collapse and rival orbit

Authored Chronicle material increasingly attributes raids, disruption and Garden-collapse to Marty and people around him. Those are project-source allegations and literary/diary interpretations, not independently established facts. What can safely be extracted as story structure is the formation of a rival social orbit: Tim's room and Marty's room become competing centers of attention, loyalty and narrative.

Late 2024 · archetype inflation

Marty becomes “The Martys”

The text begins turning one person into a reusable class: “Martys,” Tomatoes, Drift King, Top Scholar. This is the precise point where the archive must resist flattening. Those names tell us how the project was interpreting conflict; they do not become permanent objective descriptions of Marty.

Early 2025

Death changes the grammar

The Story layer currently places Marty's death in 29 January 2025, while a separate external-data note preserves a January 29 date from a community wiki. The project therefore has a live date conflict. Until primary or stronger independent evidence settles it, the dossier should treat the exact date as unresolved rather than silently choosing whichever date fits the preferred chronology.

After death

Conflict becomes grief

The public creative archive contains a track titled Goodbye Marty. That changes Marty's final project-function again: the rival is no longer merely an opponent but a remembered person. This is one of the clearest examples in the archive of a role refusing to stay morally simple.

Evidence boundary

What this Room refuses to do

No permanent essence: hostile old-profile claims about motive, psychology, manipulation, criminality or intent remain historical source-characterization unless supported by direct evidence. Likewise, retrospective claims about the circumstances of Marty's death are not upgraded merely because they fit later theology.

Recovery queue

The material that would make Marty genuinely richer

  • Recover original Marty–Tim conversations before the Drift King label hardened.
  • Recover music-making exchanges, not only later summaries saying Marty was involved in creative work.
  • Propagate the now-verified January 29, 2025 date through every old Story/timeline file while keeping cause-of-death claims on a separate evidence track.
  • Recover the immediate notification and grief corridor before later theological interpretation.
  • Separate raids or conflicts that have direct logs from those surviving only as Chronicle narration.
  • Map Sammy, Port Monkey, Cyraxx, Capy and the wider orbit per scene rather than treating “Marty's crew” as a timeless block.
