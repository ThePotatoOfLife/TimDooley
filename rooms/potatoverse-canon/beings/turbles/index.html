Turbles

A home for the newly born Turble species: potato-shaped angelic beings, some winged, with jobs, prayer, building, comedy and open cosmological questions.

Turbles are unusually easy to date: they were born in conversation on 19 September 2026. They already have a strong visual grammar and a social ecology—work, prayer, construction, restaurants, hats, moods—but the species is now also treated as the visual/canonical half of a larger cultural thesis about masks, avatars, imitation, turbulence, low-resolution social personhood and the movement from surface toward depth.

The page now separates four layers: species canon (shape/scale/face), variants (wings, outfits, gender presentation), social roles (work, prayer, building, service), and the later cultural thesis about imitation/surface. That lets the Turble keep its memorable labels without turning a later interpretation into biological canon.

Species grammar

Turbles are roughly football-sized, potato-shaped and face-heavy: huge round eyes with large pupils, expressive broad mouths, thin short legs and two short arms. Some have little angel wings; others simply walk around. Hats, uniforms, jobs and expressions are part of the species rather than exceptions. Their visual design makes them disproportionately readable at the surface: much of the creature is face, affect and role-signaling.

The cultural thesis deliberately keeps appearance separate from essence. A Turble may look Potato-like without possessing the cultivation, depth or individuation associated with higher Potatoes, sages, scholars or named angelic beings. That distinction is why the same form can function as mascot, avatar, troll-mask, worker, devotional figure and low-resolution social persona.

These are roles and variants, not necessarily supernatural abilities. The species becomes interesting precisely because it can inhabit ordinary labor and sacred/comic scenes at the same time.

Heaven status

Turbles may use angelic imagery and some are winged, but wings do not automatically make them equivalent to Matthew, Potato Angels, sages or scholars. The project now treats that separation as structurally important: Turbles usually represent horizontal imitation and cultural diffusion, while higher Potatoes and named beings are increasingly individuated through particular knowledge, gifts, responsibility and history.

Cultural thesis

The deeper model lives in Culture & Information rather than inside species taxonomy. There, the Turble is proposed as a memetic cultural object: a high-surface, high-energy, high-imitation, turbulent persona whose readable exterior can function as avatar, mask, folk archetype and carrier of culture. The thesis develops the mud-and-broken-glass metaphor, Turble Cascades, Turblification, the Turble Gap, generation versus degeneration, surface intelligence, social velocity and the relation between Sage depth and Turble diffusion.
