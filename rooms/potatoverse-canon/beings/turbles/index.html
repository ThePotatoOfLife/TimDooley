Turbles

A home for the newly born Turble species: potato-shaped angelic beings, some winged, with jobs, prayer, building, comedy and open cosmological questions.

Turbles are unusually easy to date: they were born in conversation on 19 September 2026. They already have a strong visual grammar and a social ecology—work, prayer, construction, restaurants, hats, moods—but their cosmological powers are still mostly open.

Born: 19 September 2026Class: created speciesShape: potato/angel/gremlin/toddler tensionWings: some, not all

Species grammar

Turbles are potato-shaped and face-heavy: huge round eyes with large pupils, expressive broad mouths, thin short legs and two short arms. Their sizes vary. Some have little angel wings; others simply walk around. Hats, uniforms, jobs and expressions are part of the species rather than exceptions.

First social roles

  • Working Turbles
  • Praying Turbles
  • Church-building Turbles
  • McDonald's Turbles
  • Angel Turbles
  • Wizard / specialist archetypes
  • Women/girl Turbles
  • Construction and service roles

These are roles and variants, not necessarily supernatural abilities. The species becomes interesting precisely because it can inhabit ordinary labor and sacred/comic scenes at the same time.

Heaven status

Turbles were described as potato-shaped angelic beings, and some are winged, but the recovered conversation does not yet establish that every Turble lives in Heaven or can cross dimensions. That makes them a perfect test case for the room system: their appearance can be angelic while their actual powers remain open until stories grant them.
