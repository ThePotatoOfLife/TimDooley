The surviving story

2024 · literary

Termite tries to grow a physical potato for the Potatoverse. The green plant is cut down by a lawnmower, making the project look destroyed above ground. Instead of stopping at the visible damage, Termite digs. A small potato has survived underneath.

The useful character function is simple: he checks beneath the surface before declaring something dead. He replants, keeps tending the soil and does not become a king or prophet merely because the potato survived.
