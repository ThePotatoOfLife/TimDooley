The surviving story

2024 · literary

Termite tries to grow a physical potato for the Potatoverse. The green plant is cut down by a lawnmower, making the project look destroyed above ground. Instead of stopping at the visible damage, Termite digs. A small potato has survived underneath.

The useful character function is simple: he checks beneath the surface before declaring something dead. He replants, keeps tending the soil and does not become a king or prophet merely because the potato survived.

3–4 November 2024 · Chronicle

Termite appears as a quiet social supporter rather than only a gardener-character. On 3 November he marks the group as “best spuds”; on 4 November the Chronicle gives him playful theological curiosity around gods creating gods. These are Chronicle-retold exchanges, not verified verbatim chat.

4–7 December 2024 · Chronicle

Termite becomes a more visible boundary-and-dignity voice. The Chronicle remembers him encouraging Tim, asking for a more respectful term for supporters, remaining cautious around reconciliation attempts, and repeatedly framing the community through growth rather than rank.

15–20 December 2024 · Chronicle

He appears inside cooking, music, studio and creative scenes: joking about Anacondasin coming to Denmark, carrying songs around in his head, and repeating the simple social ethic “we’re trying to grow here.” This materially expands Termite from a single literary parable into an ordinary community presence.

20 February 2025 · public

A public donation notice says Tim received a coffee from “Termite.” That proves a recurring public name around the project, but it does not prove that the literary Termite and the supporter/account are identical in every respect.
