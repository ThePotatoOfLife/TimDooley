Heaven test

Are there potatoes in Heaven?

The recovered conversations do contain Potato beings in explicit Heaven language, but not all “potatoes” are automatically heavenly. Potato Angels are repeatedly placed in Heaven or described as growing/transcending into it. Matthew is explicitly both in Heaven and able to walk Earth. Turbles are angelic in design but their exact Heaven status is not settled. Rahu and Termite are more strongly attached to Garden, Mud, soil and threshold stories.

Useful distinction: instead of one flat Heaven cast, treat the upper world as inhabited by several possible classes—generic angels, Potato Angels, named winged potatoes such as Matthew, messengers/guardians, and perhaps Turbles if later stories establish that relation.

Character Archive

The whole cast without turning everyone into a Room

The old Chronicles and character manuscripts have now been absorbed as an archaeological cast layer. The archive currently tracks 56 named figures across 37 dated Chronicle segments, with aliases, changing archetypal roles, narrative regions, seeds/traces and current-status uncertainty.

Character ≠ archetype. A person can be Observer in one scene, Trickster in another, Guardian later, then disappear from the record. The archive stores role × time × source rather than assigning a permanent moral caste.

Backend discipline

How these rooms are allowed to grow

  • Every recovered interaction gets a date/window and source mode before it becomes lore.
  • Abilities are tracked as said in a story, recurring project ability, or open possibility; those are not interchangeable.
  • A person/handle and a literary character with the same name remain separate until a source actually joins them.
  • New beings start on the recovery shelf. A dedicated room is earned by recurring scenes, distinctive function, or enough unresolved questions to justify a persistent space.
