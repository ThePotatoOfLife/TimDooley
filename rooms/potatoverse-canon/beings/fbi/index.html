Figures, Bonds & Incidents — a Tim-centered archive for characters, relationships, conversations, stories and dated social history.

This is where the cast stops being a list. Every figure gets a persistent dossier; every important relationship can become a Bond; every scene that changes what we know becomes an Incident. The bureau is centered on Tim because the repository is centered on Tim, but people are not reduced to whether they supported, opposed, watched or followed him.

Name boundary: FBI here means Figures, Bonds & Incidents. It is a Potatoverse archival acronym and has no affiliation with the U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation or any real intelligence/law-enforcement service.

How to read a dossier

Identity says who/what the file represents. Chronology says when they appear. Bonds say who is present with them and how a relationship changes. Archetypes describe scene function. Symbolic roles are source-bounded Potatoverse labels. Source strata separate Chronicle, public chat, conversation recovery, Great Book and later synthesis. Recovery leads show what remains buried.
