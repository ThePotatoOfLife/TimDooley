Metalorian / Meta

Separate the thin primary Metalorian record from later Meta, karmic and foresight interpretations, while preserving the project's remembered role structure.

Metalorian is almost the inverse of Marty in the surviving archive. Marty has visible room-life and social motion; Metalorian is recovered mainly through later interpretation, old profiles, “Meta” language and foresight/karmic retellings. The primary event layer is therefore thinner, and the dossier makes that weakness visible.

Retrospective biography and old character manuscripts increasingly cast “Meta” as a figure involved in framing, monitoring and narrative distortion, sometimes alongside TXT. These are strong clues about how the project remembers the era, but they remain authored allegations unless the underlying posts, recordings or logs are recovered.

2023 · Cube interpretation

Metalorian returns as karmic meaning

The later Great Book timeline places Metalorian's suffering near the 20 September 2023 Cube-of-the-Moon marker. The current Story already handles this correctly: the karmic interpretation is later meaning-making, not proof that a mechanism was established on that date.

Later project language

Meta becomes a role more than a person

“Meta” starts functioning as shorthand for curating the old narrative, controlling context, or shaping how Tim is seen. That is precisely why a role-crosswalk is needed: the project can study meta-framing without declaring that every later claim about Metalorian's intent is factual.

The cancer problem

Obstruction metaphor must stay separate from medical causation

The older project has both literal disease language and a broader symbolic use of “cancer” for things that obstruct progress, spread distortion or interfere with becoming. Later biography then associates Metalorian with illness and earlier warnings. The archive should preserve that sequence as symbolic and retrospective interpretation.

Hard boundary: later illness, death or misfortune is not evidence that Tim caused it, predicted it accurately, or that a social “cancer” metaphor manifested as literal cancer. The current foresight ledger explicitly requires the original pre-event statement and independent chronology before the warning can even be scored.
