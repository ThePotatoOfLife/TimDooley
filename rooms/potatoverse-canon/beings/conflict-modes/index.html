Conflict Modes

Model social orbit, disruption, meta-framing, boundary pressure, attribution conflict and aftermath without turning people into permanent archetypes.

Conflict in the Potatoverse should be modeled as changing mechanisms, not permanent castes. A person may be a companion in one season, a rival center in another, an observer later, or absent entirely. These modes let the archive compare scenes without turning temporary roles into claims about someone's essence.

Unit: scene-level functionNot: diagnosis or moral rankingInputs: conversation · public post · Chronicle · Great Book · legal/source archive

Mechanisms

Six conflict modes worth tracking

Social orbitA person gathers people, attention, loyalty or shared activity around a second center. Marty is the clearest current example, but the mode is reusable.
Disruption / raid pressureA room is flooded, diverted or made difficult to govern. Every claimed incident needs its own source; the mode itself is descriptive.
Meta-framingConflict over what another person means, how their words are contextualized, or which narrative becomes authoritative. Metalorian/Meta and TXT are major recovery leads here.
Boundary pressureBans, filters, doors, servers, follow/unfollow relations and cross-community movement turn the abstract question “who belongs?” into observable mechanics.
Attribution conflictDisagreement over who said, authored, threatened, caused or intended something. This requires claim-level provenance rather than archetypal storytelling.
Aftermath / metabolizationConflict becomes grief, music, scripture, doctrine, humor, repair or a new boundary. The transformation is often more important than the original quarrel.

Crosswalk

How the named arcs differ

Marty: strongest in social orbit → boundary conflict → rival pole → aftermath/grief.

Metalorian / Meta: strongest in the archive's meta-framing → monitoring → attribution → later karmic interpretation chain, with major primary-source gaps.

TXT: strongest as a persistent conflict referent whose documentary chronology needs reconstruction between early old-profile claims and a firm July 2026 public marker.

Rahu / Termite / other cast: show why these modes are not “enemy classes.” The same architecture can track challenge, threshold-crossing, repair and growth without assigning anyone a fixed moral caste.

Institutional route

Why the Mai Mercado case is not a cast archetype

The 2016 Mai Mercado material belongs primarily to the project's evidence-led legal/timeline subsystem, not to a mythic opponent room. It concerns a real public political figure and a real criminal case, so claims about speech, attribution, police referral, prosecution, conviction and later interpretation must stay proposition-by-proposition and source-graded.

Routing rule: do not turn Mai Mercado into “Farmer,” “Dog,” “cancer,” “shadow,” or another Potatoverse essence. When the 2016 event is relevant to the story, link to knowledge/legal/mai-mercado-2016-research-index.md and the case-master synthesis. Mythic interpretation may be stored as later meaning, but it cannot replace the legal evidence chain.

Cancer / obstruction vocabulary

Keep three meanings apart

Literal medical cancerA health condition. It requires medical evidence and must never be inferred from moral or spiritual language.
Social “cancer” metaphorOld project language for spam, deception, copycat behavior or obstruction of progress. This describes rhetoric, not biology.
Retrospective karmic correspondenceA later event is read as fitting an earlier warning. This remains a correspondence unless a specific pre-event statement and independent chronology are recovered.

Data model

Every future incident should answer five questions

  • Who? Person/handle identity and alias confidence.
  • When? Exact date or bounded window.
  • What happened? Minimum source-supported event before interpretation.
  • Which mode? Orbit, disruption, framing, boundary, attribution, aftermath—or another explicit function.
  • What is the source distance? Direct conversation, public post, Chronicle retelling, Great Book literature, or later retrospective synthesis.
