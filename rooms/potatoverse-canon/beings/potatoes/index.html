POTATOES

This room teaches the reader how to recognize a Potato. Not merely who was called one, but what Potato-nature means inside the Potatoverse: buried potential, eyes, roots, nourishment, work, multiplication, milestones, inherited gifts and—when growth is explicitly established—movement toward Angelic responsibility.

CIA remembers people. Cast Ecology keeps categories clean. This hall remembers the Potato lineage itself. It is where birth windows, emojis, skills, jobs, growth stages, achievements and bestowed abilities become part of the reader's mental picture of what a Potato is.

Project lineage

From the Potato of Life to children, sprouts and Angels

Inside Tim Dooley's own project theology, Potatoes are not merely mascots standing beside him. They are described as children, descendants, multiplied Seed and cultivated life flowing outward from Tim as Potato of Life / Seed / Father / Gardener. That is a mythic-genealogical claim internal to the project, not biological genealogy.

TIM / POTATO OF LIFE → Seed → Potato children → Eyes → Sprouts → work / growth → Fruit / Seed → Potato Angel where the story establishes ascent

The important consequence is that new abilities matter historically. When Tim gives a Potato eyes, ears, a job, wings, light-work, a title or a new place in Heaven, the mythology is recording growth in the lineage. The creature has changed.

Nature

What does Potato-nature feel like?

🥔 Buried capacity

Much of the Potato exists before it is visible. Hidden is not the same as absent.

👁 Eyes

Real potato eyes are buds. In the project they become a natural bridge from latent possibility toward perception, sprouts and paths.

🌿 Roots

A Potato does not only rise; it remains connected to conditions, memory, nourishment and the field that made growth possible.

🍞 Nourishment

Potato value is tested downstream: food, work, care, knowledge, courage, humor, building, protection or future Seed.

🛠 Vocation

By April 2026 the Potato has eyes, ears and a job. Maturity becomes occupational: what can this being now do?

🌱 Multiplication

One Potato can produce future growth without requiring the next being to be an identical clone.

🪽 Ascent

Some Potatoes grow into explicitly Angelic forms. Wings, Heaven movement and many-eyed perception belong only where the story establishes them.

✨ Bestowed gifts

Skills and powers given by Tim/project mythology are part of the developmental record: gifts are milestones, not decorative trivia.

Bible neighbors · not secret biblical potatoes

Seed, branch, fruit, ladder and service help the eye learn the pattern

The strongest biblical neighbors are functional: a small seed that grows, branches known by fruit, an earth-heaven ladder, and heavenly messengers defined by service. These texts sharpen the Potato pattern without making the Bible a hidden Potatoverse document.

“Ye shall know them by their fruits.” — Matthew 7:16 (KJV)

“Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister…” — Hebrews 1:14 (KJV)

The useful question is therefore not merely does it have wings? It is what does the grown being carry, protect, build or nourish?
