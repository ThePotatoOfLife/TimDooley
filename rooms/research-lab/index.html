How the Forge actually works

Questions enter provisional; useful structures leave with owners

Research Lab reader →Open questions →

The Research Lab is not a warehouse for whatever is unfinished. It is a conversion space. A loose question may first need source recovery, then a clearer distinction, then a model, experiment or comparison. If the work survives criticism, its durable result is promoted into the Room that should own it; the Lab keeps the research history rather than becoming a second canon.

1 · Open QuestionsPreserve uncertainty and decide whether the next move is recovery, clarification, testing or contemplation.2 · Experiments & FormalizationTurn a candidate idea into explicit variables, assumptions, mechanisms, observables and failure conditions.3 · Research ProgrammesCoordinate work that crosses several Rooms, sources or implementation tracks.4 · House ArchitectureTest structural proposals before they become constitutional ownership or navigation rules.

Promotion test: a result leaves the Forge only when its owner, evidence class, scope and consequences are clearer than when it entered.

One conflict can occupy several layers

Separate the layers before you connect them

The Forge does not assume every lower-field problem is one war. It asks whether the same event has a cultural layer, an information layer, a spiritual/theological interpretation, a criminal/exploitation layer, an institutional layer—or only one of those. Connections are earned by evidence rather than by vocabulary.

Cultural conflictNorms, identity, taste, morality, status, belonging and competing stories. Ask who defines “normal,” what role labels do, and who is excluded or elevated.
Information conflictClaims, framing, amplification, deception, source laundering, coordinated influence, archive persistence and correction. A formal information operation requires evidence of planning/coordination and objective.
Spiritual warfareA religious/project interpretive layer about good/evil, temptation, deception, powers, judgment or inner struggle. In Christian scripture such language does not make human opponents literal demons; Ephesians 6 explicitly distinguishes the struggle from “flesh and blood.”
Predation / exploitationBoundary testing, coercion, dependency, grooming, forced labour, trafficking, sexual exploitation, stalking, threats or extortion. These are real-harm categories with specific evidence thresholds, not metaphors for people the project dislikes.
Political / institutionalPolicy, law, elections, state power, watchdogs, moderation, enforcement and public institutions. Keep documented political goals/actions separate from speculation about hidden motive.
Economic / platformAds, subscriptions, donations, labor, ranking, recommendation, archive/search and other incentives that make attention cheap or profitable to reproduce.

Information “trafficking” rule: use phrases such as information flow, laundering, redistribution, recirculation or influence operation for claims/data. Reserve human trafficking for the exploitation framework used in law and anti-trafficking practice.

What the reader wants to know

Who, since when, how active, how harmful, what goal?

Actors & roles

Who originates, carries, amplifies, funds, archives, moderates, targets, defends, investigates or repairs? Roles can change over time.

Targets

Individuals, vulnerable people, creators, communities, institutions, political audiences, reputation, markets, relationships or public trust. Never infer one universal target.

Goals / incentives

Entertainment, status, belonging, money, persuasion, ideological change, institutional power, exposure, protection, revenge, exploitation—or no shared goal at all.

History

First recoverable evidence, formation phase, peak attention, migrations, fractures, dormancy, reactivation and current posture.

Currentness

Active now? Dormant? Historical? Repaired? Escalating? Fragmented? Unknown? Current claims need current evidence.

Capability

Audience reach, archive depth, access, money, technical capacity, institutional authority and ability to impose costs—kept separate from motive.

Severity

Distinguish offensive speech or ridicule from privacy invasion, sustained harassment, coercion, threats, exploitation, violence or system-level harm.

Counterfactual

What evidence would show the model is wrong? What would make the conflict end? A framework with no falsifier or exit becomes self-sealing.

severity ladder
ordinary disagreement / satire / criticism → persistent target-centered attention → privacy/reputation harm → harassment / coercive pressure → threats / stalking / extortion → exploitation / trafficking / violence
Movement along this line must be evidenced; it is never automatic.

Outputs

The Forge should return fewer, stronger claims

Material entering the lower layer should leave as one of a small number of things: verified finding, bounded uncertainty, current-state assessment, correction, harm warning, intervention/repair design, referral to another owner, or rejection of the hypothesis. The goal is not to make every lower theory larger.
