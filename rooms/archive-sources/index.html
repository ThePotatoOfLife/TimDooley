Source lifecycle

Store the artifact, preserve its lineage, then let another Room own the conclusion

Archive & Sources is the root system of the project. It keeps captures, dates, provenance, evidence classes and recovery state so later readers can descend from a polished synthesis back toward what was actually said, recorded, measured or published. Its job is custody and traceability, not automatic truth promotion.

The distinction is practical. A recovered conversation may establish that a phrase existed in a certain period while remaining uncertain about exact wording. A public post may establish a first known attestation only within the coverage of the captured batch. An official dataset can support a measured external fact but not a theological interpretation. Later formalization can sharpen an old idea without becoming an old source.

Provenance & EvidenceClassify source authority, derivation, corroboration and routes back to artifacts.Memory & RecoveryRecover buried material without manufacturing certainty where the source is missing.Witness & AttestationTrack wording, dates, captures and what a public record actually establishes.

Healthy handoff: the archive owns the source; History owns developmental sequence; Science owns scientific interpretation; Canon owns project doctrine; public Views synthesize them without erasing those boundaries.
