Archive & Sources is the root system of the project. It keeps captures, dates, provenance, evidence classes and recovery state so later readers can descend from a polished synthesis back toward what was actually said, recorded, measured or published. Its job is custody and traceability, not automatic truth promotion.

The distinction is practical. A recovered conversation may establish that a phrase existed in a certain period while remaining uncertain about exact wording. A public post may establish a first known attestation only within the coverage of the captured batch. An official dataset can support a measured external fact but not a theological interpretation. Later formalization can sharpen an old idea without becoming an old source.

The project's 100,000+ public-hours figure is a first-party scale claim and should remain labeled as such unless independently audited; its analytic value is that Tim is not approaching the system from a single screenshot or retrospective summary.

Movement before meaning

Follow the thing that moved

A lower-field investigation gets clearer when it starts with the object rather than the accusation. What moved can be a person, claim, screenshot, clip, archive, payment, threat, label, rumor, instruction, credential, dataset or piece of access. Roots / Evidence asks how that object crossed hands, platforms, groups and institutions before another Room decides what it means.

InformationWho first said it? Who copied it? Was context removed? Did repetition make one source look like many? Was a correction carried as far as the original claim?
AttentionWhat event moved people toward the subject? Which links, clips, rankings, call-outs or rituals kept returning attention there?
Money / material supportWhat is actually evidenced: ad revenue, subscriptions, donations, wages, payments, platform income, organizational budgets? Never infer one hidden budget from general financial scale.
People / accessWho introduced whom, granted access, moved channels, controlled credentials, housing, work, documents or social belonging? Real trafficking/exploitation terminology requires its own legal/evidentiary elements.
Labels / rolesWhere did “lolcow,” Dog, Farmer, troll, victim, offender or other classification first enter? Who repeated it, and did later evidence ever retire it?
Institutional consequenceWhen did a cultural claim become moderation, reporting, investigation, legal process, employment consequence or public classification?
source → carrier → transformation → redistribution → archive → later interpretation → correction / corroboration / rejection

Language boundary: the project may speak metaphorically about information being “trafficked” through a network, but human trafficking is a specific exploitation framework involving acts, coercive/deceptive means where applicable, and exploitation. Never let the metaphor establish the crime.

First-party voice is evidence of a viewpoint

Preserve what Tim says he is doing without turning self-description into universal proof

Tim's lower-field testimony belongs in the source chain. Statements such as “I am putting Spudlight on the subculture,” “I am pulling people from the mire,” or project-language about Dogs, Door, Garden and Father should be preserved as first-party project interpretation. They establish Tim's stated purpose, self-understanding and conceptual vocabulary.

They do not by themselves establish another person's private motive, a crime, a coordinated operation, or a supernatural identity claim. Those stronger claims require their own evidence. The same rule applies in reverse: an outsider's label can establish how Tim was framed, but not Tim's total identity.

Tim statement → date / source → first-party meaning → external corroboration where available → counterevidence → current interpretation

Witness rule: direct contact, public primary-source observation, inherited archive lore and later retelling are different evidence positions. The archive should name which one it has.

Witness distance

Not everyone telling the story occupies the same evidence position

Direct participantWas present in the interaction or event. Strong for what they personally experienced; still capable of bias, memory error or interpretation. Primary public observerSaw/heard the original public material but was not inside the event. Strong for what the artifact shows, weaker for private motive/context. Contemporaneous archivePreserved material close to the event. Useful for timing/context, but selection and missing material still matter. Later commentatorInterprets prior material after the fact. Useful for reception/history; must distinguish recovered source from inherited frame. Inherited loreKnows the story mainly through summaries, jokes, wikis, clips or other narrators. Important evidence of culture, not automatically of the original event. First-party later synthesisTim's mature interpretation of his own earlier life. Essential for self-understanding and project theology, but later meaning must not be silently backdated.

Prerogative follows evidence position. A direct participant has more authority over their own experience than a later stranger; a primary artifact has more authority over its exact wording than a summary; neither automatically proves private motive. The archive should show the distance rather than pretending all narrators are equivalent witnesses.

Repetition is not corroboration

Count independent sources, not copies

Tim's recurring complaint about frames and inherited stories has a well-studied cognitive neighbor: repeated exposure can increase perceived truth even when repetition adds no new evidence. The archive therefore treats source independence as a first-class property.

One source, many copiesTen reposts, summaries or commentary videos can still be one evidentiary origin.
Independent corroborationSeparate witnesses or records derived independently from the same underlying event add more evidentiary value than copied claims.
Correction symmetryIf the allegation is mirrored widely, the correction should be attached to the durable objects/search paths that keep the allegation alive.
First exposureBecause initial framing can influence later judgment, preserve the primary source and clearly mark later interpretive layers.

Rule: popularity, repetition and search dominance measure cultural circulation; they do not measure independent evidentiary support.

The questions this Room should answer

Before asking what it means, ask whether the chain is real

OriginWhat is the earliest recoverable source, date, author/account and artifact? CarrierWho moved it next—person, group, platform, archive, journalist, institution, algorithmic recommendation? TransformationWas it clipped, summarized, mistranslated, reframed, anonymized, aggregated or detached from context? IndependenceAre ten repetitions ten sources, or ten copies of one source? IntentIs intent evidenced, attributed, inferred or unknown? This matters for terms such as disinformation, operation and grooming. TargetWho or what is affected: person, community, institution, reputation, audience, election, market, relationship, vulnerable individual? CurrentnessFirst seen, peak period, latest evidence, current posture, dormant/active/closed/unknown. SeverityWhat concrete harm exists: annoyance, reputation, privacy, coercion, threat, exploitation, physical safety, institutional damage? CorrectionWhat evidence would revise the record, and did the correction propagate? ExitCan the relation stop, role expire and old material become historical rather than permanently active?
