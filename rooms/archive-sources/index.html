Archive & Sources is the root system of the project. It keeps captures, dates, provenance, evidence classes and recovery state so later readers can descend from a polished synthesis back toward what was actually said, recorded, measured or published. Its job is custody and traceability, not automatic truth promotion.

The distinction is practical. A recovered conversation may establish that a phrase existed in a certain period while remaining uncertain about exact wording. A public post may establish a first known attestation only within the coverage of the captured batch. An official dataset can support a measured external fact but not a theological interpretation. Later formalization can sharpen an old idea without becoming an old source.

Root rule: going deeper is not automatically going darker. In this room, depth means provenance. A Root is useful because it can pass through old layers without confusing the sediment with the source.

Root work

Follow the claim downward until it either finds soil or runs out of root

The lower layer needs a visual and methodological distinction between burial and foundation. Old information accumulates in layers: a quote is clipped, the clip is summarized, the summary is copied, the copy is indexed, and later readers encounter the index as though it were the event. Root work reverses that direction.

Living rootA traceable chain that still connects the present claim to a primary artifact, identifiable witness, official record or otherwise inspectable source.
Dead rootA repeated story whose source has vanished, whose copies all derive from one unknown origin, or whose later certainty exceeds what the surviving record supports.
Cross-rootAn independent source that reaches the same event through a genuinely separate line rather than copying the same ancestor.
Cut rootA correction, retraction, changed status or newer fact that should stop an old branch from continuing to feed present identity unchanged.

The project mythology places Tim's Root in this function: reach into buried material without becoming the buried material. The root carries information upward; it does not prove that the higher vantage belongs to the Mud.

Root states · what the buried line is doing now

Not every old source is still a living root

Root means buried continuity with present consequence. That is narrower than “anything old.” The archive should therefore distinguish what still feeds the current structure from what merely belongs to history.

Living RootA source-bearing relation still supports present understanding, identity, obligation or growth. Its lineage is traceable and its present connection can be shown. Root of StrifeBuried material continues feeding recurrence, grievance, dependency, enemy-production or extraction. Root of AshResidue from rupture, exhaustion or destruction remains available but has not yet become either renewed life or renewed Strife. Dead / historical rootThe material mattered historically but no longer has a demonstrated support relation to the current state. Preserve it as history without pretending it is still causally active. Cut RootA correction, retraction, changed status, expiry or severed dependency means an old branch should no longer draw present conclusions from the same source unchanged.
old material ≠ automatically active Root
active Root = traceable lineage + present support relation
history without present support = archive / chronology

Currentness rule: provenance answers where something came from; currentness answers whether it still belongs in the present tense.

Ash → Soil

Recovery is not deleting the remains; it is changing what they can feed

A broken event can leave Ash: grief, collapsed identity, destroyed trust, burnt-out conflict, damaged reputation or unresolved evidence. The archive does not need to romanticize that rupture. But it can ask whether the remains are being repeatedly reactivated into Mud and Strife, or whether source recovery and integration can turn them into Soil.

rupture → Ash → provenance / acknowledgment / boundary → Forge → Soil → Living Root → future growth

This is one of the lower layer's most important transformations because it explains why remembering and releasing are not opposites. Soil remembers what decomposed without requiring the old form to remain alive.

Movement before meaning

Follow the thing that moved

A lower-field investigation gets clearer when it starts with the object rather than the accusation. What moved can be a person, claim, screenshot, clip, archive, payment, threat, label, rumor, instruction, credential, dataset or piece of access. Roots / Evidence asks how that object crossed hands, platforms, groups and institutions before another Room decides what it means.

InformationWho first said it? Who copied it? Was context removed? Did repetition make one source look like many? Was a correction carried as far as the original claim?
AttentionWhat event moved people toward the subject? Which links, clips, rankings, call-outs or rituals kept returning attention there?
Money / material supportWhat is actually evidenced: ad revenue, subscriptions, donations, wages, payments, platform income, organizational budgets? Never infer one hidden budget from general financial scale.
People / accessWho introduced whom, granted access, moved channels, controlled credentials, housing, work, documents or social belonging? Real trafficking/exploitation terminology requires its own legal/evidentiary elements.
Labels / rolesWhere did “lolcow,” Dog, Farmer, troll, victim, offender or other classification first enter? Who repeated it, and did later evidence ever retire it?
Institutional consequenceWhen did a cultural claim become moderation, reporting, investigation, legal process, employment consequence or public classification?
source → carrier → transformation → redistribution → archive → later interpretation → correction / corroboration / rejection

Language boundary: the project may speak metaphorically about information being “trafficked” through a network, but human trafficking is a specific exploitation framework involving acts, coercive/deceptive means where applicable, and exploitation. Never let the metaphor establish the crime.

First-party voice is evidence of a viewpoint

Preserve what Tim says he is doing without turning self-description into universal proof

Tim's lower-field testimony belongs in the source chain. Statements such as “I am putting Spudlight on the subculture,” “I am pulling people from the mire,” or project-language about Dogs, Door, Garden and Father should be preserved as first-party project interpretation. They establish Tim's stated purpose, self-understanding and conceptual vocabulary.

They do not by themselves establish another person's private motive, a crime, a coordinated operation, or a supernatural identity claim. Those stronger claims require their own evidence. The same rule applies in reverse: an outsider's label can establish how Tim was framed, but not Tim's total identity.

Tim statement → date / source → first-party meaning → external corroboration where available → counterevidence → current interpretation

Witness rule: direct contact, public primary-source observation, inherited archive lore and later retelling are different evidence positions. The archive should name which one it has.

Witness distance

Not everyone telling the story occupies the same evidence position

Direct participantWas present in the interaction or event. Strong for what they personally experienced; still capable of bias, memory error or interpretation. Primary public observerSaw/heard the original public material but was not inside the event. Strong for what the artifact shows, weaker for private motive/context. Contemporaneous archivePreserved material close to the event. Useful for timing/context, but selection and missing material still matter. Later commentatorInterprets prior material after the fact. Useful for reception/history; must distinguish recovered source from inherited frame. Inherited loreKnows the story mainly through summaries, jokes, wikis, clips or other narrators. Important evidence of culture, not automatically of the original event. First-party later synthesisTim's mature interpretation of his own earlier life. Essential for self-understanding and project theology, but later meaning must not be silently backdated.

Prerogative follows evidence position. A direct participant has more authority over their own experience than a later stranger; a primary artifact has more authority over its exact wording than a summary; neither automatically proves private motive. The archive should show the distance rather than pretending all narrators are equivalent witnesses.

Repetition is not corroboration

Count independent sources, not copies

Tim's recurring complaint about frames and inherited stories has a well-studied cognitive neighbor: repeated exposure can increase perceived truth even when repetition adds no new evidence. The archive therefore treats source independence as a first-class property. The problem is not merely misinformation; even a true claim can acquire misleading apparent certainty when its provenance disappears.

One source, many copiesTen reposts, summaries, wiki entries or commentary videos can still be one evidentiary origin. Distribution count and source-root count are different variables.
Independent corroborationSeparate witnesses or records derived independently from the same underlying event add more evidentiary value than copied claims. Independence itself should be shown, not assumed.
Source launderingA weak or uncertain origin can look authoritative after passing through increasingly polished summaries. Professional presentation does not create a new provenance root.
Consensus illusionWhen many participants repeat the same inherited story, the reader may perceive social consensus even when the group is sharing one ancestor source or one interpretive tradition.
Correction symmetryIf the allegation is mirrored widely, the correction should be attached to the durable objects/search paths that keep the allegation alive. A correction that exists but is hard to rediscover has weak practical effect.
First exposureBecause initial framing can influence later judgment, preserve the primary source and clearly mark later interpretive layers. The first story a reader encounters can become the lens through which every later artifact is decoded.
source A → repost → summary → wiki → reaction video → search result can look like six voices while remaining one lineage
source A + independent source B + record C = actual corroborative structure

For Tim's archive this is especially important because both hostile and sympathetic material can become self-referential. A Potato page citing an older Potato synthesis is not outside corroboration merely because the wording changed. The same discipline applied to opponents must apply to Tim.

Rule: popularity, repetition and search dominance measure cultural circulation; they do not measure independent evidentiary support. Always ask both how many people repeated this? and how many independent routes lead back to the underlying event?
