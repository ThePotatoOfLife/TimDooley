Cast ecology

Identity is only one dimension of the cast

Potatoverse now separates who something is from what role it is playing. Potato, Tomato, Dog, Dweller, Farmer, Cow and Footstool belong to a project symbolic ecology; Guardian, Seeker, Trickster, Builder and Witness are scene functions; Angels, Potato Angels and Turbles are created beings. These layers can overlap without becoming synonyms.

A named real person is therefore never permanently “a Dog” or “a Tomato” merely because one story uses the label. Every named assignment must remain tied to a date, scene and source.
