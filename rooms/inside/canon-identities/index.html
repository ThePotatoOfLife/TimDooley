Nested Room · Potatoverse / Canon

Core Identities & Roles

This Room answers a deceptively simple question: when the project says Tim, Father, Son, Spirit, Potato, Door, Axis, Gardener or House, which function is actually being named?

Local center

identity without collapse

The canon becomes clearer when titles are treated as differentiated roles rather than one expanding pile of synonyms.

Boundary: these are project-canon identities, religious self-descriptions and interpretive roles. They do not automatically become externally established empirical identities.

The identity grammar

One subject can occupy several functions

The Potatoverse uses identity relationally. A title is strongest when it tells you what position or function is active: source, manifestation, threshold, continuity, orientation, cultivation, containment, memory or growth.

Tim DooleyThe living/public subject at the center of the archive. The project stores documentary biography, public activity, self-description, theology, creative lore and later interpretation as different evidence classes.
Potato of LifeThe whole living organism-symbol: burial and emergence, storage and sprouting, nourishment, regeneration, humility and hidden capacity.
FatherSource-facing role: orientation, House, Root, memory, judgment, responsibility, Garden, Throne and upper center.
SonEmbodiment-facing role: personhood, ordeal, world-contact, suffering, Vessel, Door-specialization, death-language and return.
SpiritFlow and living continuity: breath, relation, animation, information and movement through the architecture rather than another static place.
DoorThreshold/interface between distinguishable fields or states. Door is relational and is strongly associated with Son/Christ/Vessel language.
AxisOrientation/reference relation connecting source and manifestation, above and below, root and fruit. Axis is not automatically a literal physical dimension.
HouseDifferentiated unity: one containing whole with Rooms, boundaries and access without requiring every part to become identical.
GardenerStewardship role: cultivate, protect, prune, water, repair and create conditions in which other life becomes more capable.
Tree / RootLiving continuity across depth and height: roots preserve support/history; trunk organizes continuity; branches differentiate; fruit and seed carry future capacity.

A title should answer “what function is active here?” before it answers “how impressive is the title?”

Tim and Father overlap, but the archive should still keep the words distinct

In mature canon, Tim identifies with the Father-side of the architecture. But “Tim” remains the broader archive subject: biography, public speech, works, conflict, politics, technical work and self-description. “Father” is a theological role inside that larger record. Keeping that distinction prevents every ordinary Tim event from becoming Father-theology by default.

Father and Son are differentiated by relation

The strongest mature reading keeps Father/source and Son/manifestation coupled without collapsing them. Father is associated with source, orientation, persistence and House. Son is associated with embodiment, passage, ordeal and world-facing manifestation. The Door binds the relation because access requires distinguishable sides.

That also resolves several apparent contradictions in older material. A role can share body, memory or narrative continuity with another role while still carrying a different function in the mature system.

Spirit should not become “the third circle” by force

Spirit works most coherently as continuity and movement: breath, flow, relation and animation. The mature triune structure therefore does not require three geometric containers. Two differentiated fields can support source and manifestation while Spirit names the living relation through and between them.

Potato of Life is the whole, not another member beside the roles

The project has older strata in which Potato, Tim, Source and Spirit were arranged differently. Mature canon treats Potato of Life as the whole organism/framework rather than a fourth member sitting next to Father, Son and Spirit. The older formulations remain historically important and belong in Developmental Genealogy rather than being erased.

Center is not one thing

The archive now distinguishes several center-types. A source-center is origin/reference. A geometric center is defined by symmetry. A relational center emerges from overlap. A dynamical center may be a barycenter or attractor. An attention-center is the node around which a social field organizes.

Tim maps most strongly to source/orientational center inside project theology, while Door/Son maps strongly to relational interface. This prevents the word “center” from doing contradictory jobs unnoticed.

King, Judge, Banker and Gardener are functions with different tests

King / King of Kings

Inside the mythology, this is meta-sovereignty or precedence—not an ordinary constitutional office. Its useful decomposition is responsibility, protection, judgment, coordination and service rather than civic legal authority.

Judge / Eye

Discernment and evaluation. The mature ethical boundary is that seeing or judging does not automatically confer coercive power or factual correctness.

Spiritual Banker

A metaphorical accounting role for obligation, repair, debt and value. Spiritual ledger figures remain symbolic unless tied to independently established legal/financial claims.

Gardener

The strongest practical constraint on authority: the center should help others grow, preserve meaningful exit and increase independent future capacity.

Identity, role, being and scene-function are different layers

The cast ecology makes this especially important. Potato, Tomato, Dog, Dweller, Farmer, Cow and Footstool are project symbolic roles; Guardian, Seeker, Trickster, Builder and Witness can be scene-functions; Angels, Potato Angels and Turbles are created beings inside the mythology. A named real person should not become permanently identical with a scene-bounded role merely because one story used that label.

Role assignments should therefore carry date, scene and source, and they should be able to expire when behavior or relation changes.

CIA and the Spiritual Bank belong beneath identity, not instead of it

The Characters, Incidents & Associations archive is a dossier system for recurring figures, appearances, relations and unresolved recovery leads. The World Spiritual Bank is a project-symbolic ledger. Both can consume identity information, but neither should redefine the core identity grammar by itself.
