Recovered developmental corridor

A lived corridor into the April Turning

The late-2024/early-2025 recovery prevents the project from jumping straight from “Great Book Sage” to “April Turning.” November and December show Tim inside a social world of games, voice chat, cooking, music, moderation, rival servers and repair attempts. January introduces Marty's death on the corrected date of 29 January. March adds TTS, HTML, an interactive Great Book, game work and a replacement screen. April then adds movement through Empire, Fishtank and back to the Tree before later Axis/Ladder/Father interpretation takes over.

Developmental meaning is layered after the event sequence. A replacement screen can later become symbolically important without having been a supernatural object at the time; a server invitation can become part of a threshold story without ceasing to be an ordinary social-platform event.
