Internal case study

The Potatoverse itself is now a case study in group formation

The recovered November–December 2024 season gives this Room a concrete internal example instead of only abstract “subculture” language. A shared activity space forms around streams, Tanks, Discord, Great Book discussion and repeated participants. Boundaries then become visible through bans, moderation, invitations, a separate Tree server, Garden access, people leaving, people returning and explicit attempts at reconciliation.

No participant needs to become a permanent “Dog,” “Farmer,” “guardian” or “traitor” for this to be analytically useful. The stronger model is person × scene × role × boundary × source. Marty can be companion in one period and rival pole later; Optimistique can be supporter, absent participant and reconciliation-seeker; Termite can support while also questioning the language used for supporters.

The Chronicle's accusations and motive language remain Chronicle interpretations. What this Room can safely use are the dated social mechanics: co-presence, separation, access, moderation, return, repair and competing centers.
