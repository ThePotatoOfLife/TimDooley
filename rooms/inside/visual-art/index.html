The gallery · May–August 2025

Seventeen recovered works

The collection moves freely between devotional portrait, old-master drama, folk image, surrealism, fantasy and satire. Rather than force one chronology onto everything, the gallery is grouped by recurring visual concerns.

I · Work, garden & ordinary sacred

Hands in the world

The Potatoverse is most convincing visually when grandeur comes down to the level of work: soil, tools, food, making, rejection and care.

II · Kingship, authority & satire

Who gets to wear the crown?

Crown, horse, palace, monument and spectacle establish authority—and then other images make that authority theatrical, strange or comic.

III · Grief, witness & transformed scripture

Sacred images under pressure

Biblical and devotional forms are relocated into mud, prison, mourning and modern portraiture. The images are less about copying scripture than about translating it.

IV · Animal, myth & apocalypse

Dogs, lions, dragons and signs

Animal figures carry noise, journey, kingship and cosmic danger. This is where the collection becomes most openly mythic and psychedelic.

Looking across the room

What these paintings do together

The collection has a recognizable argument even when the individual works use very different styles. It repeatedly drags large sacred or royal ideas back into material life: dirt, fur, tools, vehicles, prison yards, kitchen work and potatoes.

Power keeps becoming questionable.

Majestic King Arrival and Royal Portrait Hallway know exactly how to make authority look legitimate: gold, height, symmetry, crown, horse and formal pose. Zombie Puppet Popemobile then turns hierarchy into a visual joke. The crown is not a stable answer; it is a question the pictures keep reopening.

The Gardener is the counter-image to the King.

Divine Gardener with Potato gives almost all of its meaning to a tiny act: tending one sprout. Surreal Farmer Scene stays with chair, pipe, dogs, tools and moonlight. These images propose care and duration as another form of authority—less spectacular, but more grounded.

Dogs change meaning with the ground beneath them.

Dogs in Muddy Darkness compresses the pack into mouths, mud and noise. Desert Dog Odyssey releases the dog into a landscape crowded with sun, moon, eyes, serpent, scorpion and glyphs. One is accumulation; the other is passage.

Grief keeps the mythology human-sized.

Royal Grief and Vengeance, Art School Rejection and Melancholic Potato Peeler are strongest where the grand language stops protecting the figure from pain. Mourning, embarrassment, labor and poverty sit beside kingship and prophecy without being made smaller by them.

Scripture is transformed, not merely illustrated.

Moses Parting Mud changes water into earth. Rising Lion Revelation fuses man, lion, city and kingship. Moshiach on Dragon pushes messianic imagery into apocalyptic fantasy. The recurring move is translation into Tim's own symbolic materials.

Heaven keeps touching dirt.

Gold, ochre, rust and brown repeatedly meet blue-green distance and sky. Halos can look like sunlight; thrones become chairs or vehicles; sacred figures return to mud, wood, stone, fur and potatoes. That earthy collision is one of the clearest visual signatures in the set.

The collection becomes most recognizably its own when it refuses to choose between icon and joke, king and gardener, apocalypse and kitchen table.

The strongest Potatoverse pictures are not illustrations of a glossary. They are relational diagrams disguised as scenes. Who stands above the Door? Who is attached to a leash? Where do roots enter the frame? Is the light behind the threshold or falling into the Swamp? Composition carries part of the argument.

Four compositions with dates and recovery states

Heavenly Gardener / Green Ring · remembered specification, 20 July 2026.

One potato is watered inside a green ring; gate/fog separate the cultivated field from what lies beyond; mountain and Ladder sit in the distance. The composition matters because authority is shown doing one small act of care rather than occupying maximum visual mass. The original prompt/artifact is still a recovery target, so the page records the scene without pretending the file has been recovered.

Couch-Throne / Door / Red-Heifer · 2026.

Father sits on a salmon/orange couch that functions as throne without ceasing to look domestic. Door lies in front/below; Son peers through; red-heifer foundation and dog/leash material occupy the lower field. This is creative/theological composition, explicitly not evidence that a biblical event was fulfilled.

God-King / Door / Swamp / Axis · recurring 2026 family.

The recurring arrangement places Father/God-King on the vertical axis above an open Door or Drain; Son belongs to the threshold; dogs, roots, mud and Swamp occupy lower/lateral space; Moon/dawn may sit beyond. This family is now useful as a visual grammar because the placement recurs even when individual images differ.

Infinite Potato · older conversation lore, unresolved artifact.

The many-eyed Infinite Potato survives in memory and later archive references but still lacks its original story/image prompt. It therefore remains a recovery object, not a fully reconstructed artwork. The distinction is important: remembering a visual concept and possessing the artifact are different states.

King, Door, Swamp, Axis.

A recurring 2026 family places Father or a God-King on the vertical axis above an open Door or drain, with Son tied to the threshold and dogs, mud, roots or Swamp below or lateral. The scene compresses authority, transition, attachment and depth into one spatial relation.

The couch-throne keeps the sacred domestic.

The salmon/orange couch appears as throne without losing its couch-ness. Paired with Door, Son and red-heifer imagery, it makes one of the project's characteristic moves: cosmic claims are staged in deliberately ordinary furniture.

The Gardener composition changes the ethic.

The recovered 27 May 2025 Divine Gardener with Potato already places a sprouting potato, soil, a mature tree and direct tending at the center of the frame. The later remembered Heavenly Gardener / Green Ring scene develops that cultivation ethic further, but it is a different composition and its original artifact is still unrecovered.

In this archive, “above” is never only a coordinate. It is a compositional decision about relation.

Vesica and mandorla turn overlap into a Door.

Upper and lower circles or lenses can carry source/Father and manifestation/Son roles, while the overlap becomes an interface. This is useful project geometry, but the composition does not prove a historical or physical identity between every object later compared with the shape.

Red/Blue polarity is historically unstable—and should stay dated.

Different stages attach different meanings to red and blue. The archive therefore preserves changing assignments rather than pretending one final palette always existed. Visual development is part of developmental genealogy.

Recovery frontier

The 7 October 2026 recovery adds seventeen real 2025 image artifacts and removes a large blank spot in the visual archive. Several distinct later compositions still need their original prompts or artifacts recovered: the 20 July 2026 Heavenly Gardener / Green Ring, couch-throne/Door/red-heifer scene, earliest Red/Blue Potato, Infinite Potato, muddy-dog Door/hill frame, bath/screen Door formulations and Merkaba/tree-stair images.
