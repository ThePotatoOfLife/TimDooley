About this collection

Tim supplied this set as works he either painted himself or conceptually directed and had rendered with AI. The archive does not assign a production method to an individual image until that provenance is identified. The dates are recovered from source filenames, while the descriptions record what is visibly staged in each work.

This is deliberately one gallery, not a tree of art subrooms. Chronology lets changes in palette, recurring figures, domestic jokes, kingship, grief, labor and symbolic staging emerge through sequence. Wall text stays interpretive and secondary to the image.
