Open Questions & Hypotheses

The archive is allowed to say “we do not know yet.” This Room protects unresolved questions from being prematurely converted into doctrine, biography, science or historical fact.

Indexing a hypothesis does not promote it to canon. A hypothesis can be useful, plausible or structurally elegant while remaining unverified, under-sourced or explicitly speculative.

The question has to be dangerous to the answer

“What would make me stop believing this?” is sometimes the most useful question in the House

An open question is not decorative humility. It should be capable of changing something.

If the project asks whether a physical alignment exists, a coordinate calculation must be allowed to say no. If it asks when a title first appeared, an earlier source must be allowed to move the date. If it asks whether a role is ethical, a bad outcome must be allowed to damage the role's justification.

And some questions turn inward.

If Tim says “Gardener,” what would bad gardening look like?

Dependency that never decreases, correction that cannot travel upward, people who cannot leave without punishment, or a center that consumes more capacity than it grows would all count against the ethical success of the title.

If the archive says “development,” what would prove backdating?

A later vocabulary repeatedly appearing in summaries of earlier events without contemporary attestation would be evidence that the archive has smoothed history too aggressively.

If the project says “Spiral,” what would reveal a Ring?

Repeated return with no changed capability, evidence, relation or state. Calling recurrence growth does not make it growth.

If a beautiful comparison keeps surviving every possible result?

Then it may be poetry, theology or interpretation—and should be allowed to be those things instead of borrowing the authority of a test it cannot fail.

A good question is a Door because the answer on the other side is allowed to be inconvenient.

The FAQ question bank is larger than the answered FAQ on purpose. It preserves the frontier: questions about identity, theology, symbols, body mappings, history, science, archive evidence, creative works and project development can exist before the repository has earned a concise answer.

Some questions are source-recovery problems.

“What did this phrase originally mean?” may not need a new theory at all; it may need an older conversation, screenshot, stream segment or artifact. The right next action is recovery, not interpretation.

Some questions are distinction problems.

“Is this symbol literal, comparative, scientific, theological or creative?” can often be answered by separating categories rather than choosing one grand interpretation. Many archive errors begin when neighboring meanings collapse into identity.

Some questions are model problems.

A hypothesis becomes researchable when it can be translated into states, mechanisms, variables, observations or counterexamples. If no possible outcome could change the answer, the question may belong to theology or philosophy rather than empirical testing.

An unanswered question is not a hole if the archive knows exactly why it is still open.

Promotion requires an owner.

The question bank's rule is simple: move a question into the answer atlas only when an established subject record can support a concise answer and an evidence class. Demand alone does not create authority.

Contradictions are research inputs.

When old and new formulations disagree, the first task is to date and classify them. The disagreement may reveal genuine doctrinal change, different literary modes, a corrected factual claim or an unresolved conflict. Flattening it destroys information.

Every real open question now needs an execution contract

A question is only useful when the archive can say who owns it, why it is still open, what evidence would move it, and what counts as closure or downgrade. Otherwise “open question” becomes a polite name for indefinite vagueness.

Owner.

Which Room or canonical record is responsible for resolving the question?

Blocker.

What exactly prevents closure right now: missing source, category confusion, absent measurement, unresolved contradiction or inaccessible data?

Evidence needed.

Which artifact, timestamp, model comparison, dataset, derivation or independent observation would materially change the state?

Closure / downgrade.

What result closes the question—and what weaker result causes the claim to be narrowed rather than left hanging forever?

A good question is not a wound that must be closed immediately

Some questions want an answer; some want a better shape

There is a peculiar kind of intellectual panic that treats uncertainty as embarrassment. It rushes to fill the blank because an empty box feels less respectable than a wrong sentence.

This Room should cultivate the opposite reflex. Ask what kind of unknown you actually have. A missing screenshot is not the same problem as an ambiguous concept. A measurable physical claim is not the same kind of question as “what does a good life owe the future?”

RecoverSomething existed; we cannot currently find it.

The work is search, provenance and honest downgrade if recovery fails.

ClarifyThe words are tangled.

The work is distinction: split concepts until disagreement becomes visible.

TestReality can answer.

The work is to design an observation capable of disappointing the preferred explanation.

ContemplateThe value is in sustained articulation.

Not every meaningful question becomes better by pretending to have an empirical finish line.

Wisdom is partly knowing which questions deserve closure—and which deserve company.

What closes a question?

Closure can also mean the question was badly formed. Sometimes research succeeds by discovering that two things were never opposites, that a category mixed several domains, or that the requested number cannot honestly exist at the precision originally demanded.

Recover closes when the missing source is found or the search is exhausted enough to downgrade the claim. Clarify closes when categories are separated and the owner can state the distinction. Test closes only when the specified observation/model comparison has been run. Contemplate may remain permanently open; its success criterion is better articulation, not forced factual closure.

Four useful statuses

Recover: missing source or wording. Clarify: categories or definitions are conflated. Test: a model can meet observation. Contemplate: a philosophical or theological question remains meaningful without pretending empirical closure.
