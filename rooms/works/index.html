Fruit you can inspect

The work is where project ideas stop being labels and become artifacts

Works reader →Great Book of Potato →

Works collects what the project actually made: the 2024 Great Book, later essays and stories, music and lyric experiments, visual compositions, game and simulation concepts, performance writing and recovered creative arcs. These artifacts matter twice—first as things worth reading, hearing, seeing or playing, and second as historical evidence for how symbols changed through use.

That does not make every line of a poem doctrine or every simulation result a fact. The Great Book can preserve an earlier theology; a song can compress an idea through rhyme; an image can reveal visual grammar; a game can expose the consequences of a rule. Their creative status is a strength because it lets the project try relations before formalizing them elsewhere.

Put four things on the table

The Dwelling becomes easier to understand when the artifacts are allowed to speak first

Imagine entering Works and finding four objects already waiting for you. Not four categories. Four actual things.

A rock from the Great BookIn Chapter 12.1, a young Potato asks how to grow and Tim eventually hands the learner a rock as the image of needless burden. The teaching survives because somebody can picture putting the weight down.
“I AM THE STILLNESS AT THE CENTER”A restrained orchestral song makes “center” audible through pacing, low brass, lyrical violin and repeated root/seed/river/door imagery. The arrangement argues by returning rather than shouting.
The Couch-ThroneA salmon/orange couch functions as throne without becoming less absurdly domestic. Cosmic scale is forced to share a room with ordinary furniture. That collision is part of the project’s visual intelligence.
The Potato Growth GameWater, weather, inheritance, mutation, money and generations become rules. The philosophy has to stop sounding wise and reveal what its assumptions actually produce when they are allowed to run.

Those four objects do four different kinds of thinking. The story gives an idea a scene. The song gives it pulse and memory. The image gives it spatial relation. The game gives it consequences. That is why Works is not the decoration wing of the House. It is where ideas acquire bodies.

A useful creative test: if the idea changes when the medium changes, the medium has taught you something.

Art as another way of knowing

Sometimes a story discovers the structure before philosophy knows how to name it.

The Great Book can be contradictory and still be valuable because literature is allowed to move before doctrine has finished building fences. A song discovers which sentence survives repetition. An image discovers scale and relation at a glance. A game forces an idea to become rules, choices and consequences. A joke can puncture prestige faster than a treatise.

That is why Works should not feel like an appendix full of illustrations. It is one of the laboratories in which Potatoism learned itself. Divine Kneel, Holy Soil, burial, Garden, Tree, strange science, satire and social thought often appeared creatively before later pages separated theology, history and formal model.

Art does not become fact by being vivid. Its gift is different: it lets the reader inhabit a possibility long enough to discover what the possibility demands.

Form is not a container

The medium changes what the idea is capable of becoming

Prose can qualifyEssay and story can hold contradiction, sequence and second thoughts long enough for an idea to become more exact than its slogan.
Music can compressA chorus does something philosophy cannot: it discovers which part of an idea can survive repetition without explanation.
Images can spatializeA Door, Tree or Mountain placed in a composition acquires relations of scale, distance and orientation that prose only describes sequentially.
Games can expose rulesOnce an idea becomes a system of choices and consequences, hidden assumptions stop being rhetorical. The player has to live inside them.

A work is evidence that a work was made. It may illuminate doctrine, biography or history, but it does not become any of those merely by being vivid.

One idea · four bodies

Watch “Door” change when the medium changes

In proseDoor can remain ambiguous. A paragraph can ask whether passage is spiritual, psychological, temporal or social and hold several answers open.
In musicDoor becomes timing. Verse can approach it, silence can become threshold, and a chorus can make crossing feel irreversible through repetition.
In an imageDoor becomes spatial relation. Scale, light, position, what lies on each side and who can see through it become immediate claims.
In a gameDoor becomes rule. What state permits crossing? What cost is paid? Can the player return? The metaphor has to become executable.

Now compare the four versions. Whatever remains stable across them may belong to the project's deeper operator: separation, passage, changed state, cost, return. Whatever only works in one medium may belong to that artwork rather than to doctrine.

Works becomes a research instrument when changing the medium changes the question. The creative object is not merely an example of the idea; it is one of the places where the idea discovers its limits.
