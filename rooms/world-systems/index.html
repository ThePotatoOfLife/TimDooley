Relationship-first world analysis

A country, institution or asset becomes informative when you can follow what it depends on

World Systems is the empirical-facing Dwelling. Geography establishes where things are. Economy follows stocks, flows, ownership and production. Infrastructure asks whether resources can be converted into capability. Law and governance follow authority, procedure and accountability. The useful synthesis is not a master score; it is a chain that explains how one condition produces another.

A data centre depends on electricity, cooling, fibre, hardware and operators. A factory may depend on imported inputs, a port, rail, customs and credit. A legal rule may act through a regulator before firms change behavior. A security commitment may depend on industrial capacity and logistics. These are the relations the World Map and country dossiers should make inspectable without turning project symbolism into empirical fact.
