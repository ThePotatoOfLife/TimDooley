The five main Doors keep the entrance simple. The House has ten load-bearing Dwellings—major bounded contexts—each containing nested Rooms with more local vocabulary, evidence rules and specialist work. The three circular navigation planes below provide another way to stand inside the same Tree and decide which kind of material to enter.

Views and Programmes are shown separately below. A map, timeline, Axis or North guide may cross several Rooms without becoming one.

Three symbolic standing platforms

Heaven · World · Below

The same House and Tree can be entered from three navigation planes. The middle circle is the ordinary World Plane already used by the Axis; the new upper and lower circles let the reader stand in Heaven-facing integration or Below-facing roots/shadow without turning either one into a separate universe. Room placement here is a navigation emphasis, not a change of knowledge ownership.
