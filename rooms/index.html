DWELLINGS
& ROOMS

Start with the Potato of Life and its own project Rooms. The House has ten load-bearing Dwellings—major bounded contexts—each containing nested Rooms with local vocabulary, evidence rules and specialist work. Planes, compass and comparative atlases are secondary navigation tools for material that already belongs somewhere.

Views and Programmes are shown separately below. A map, timeline, Axis or North guide may cross several Rooms without becoming one.

Semantic center

Begin with the Potato, then move outward

Potato of Life is the conceptual center of the project. Axis is the spatial centerline of the map. Research Lab is the Forge around that center, not the center itself. Comparative gods, underworlds, esoteric systems and historical symbols belong farther out as mirrors and contextual inhabitants.

Population before orientation

What actually lives in these Dwellings?

A Room is useful only if a reader can enter it and find real material. The ten Dwellings below are not empty categories: they already contain biography, sources, dates, scriptures, scientific models, anatomy, countries, institutions, subcultures, books, songs, maps and unresolved research. These examples show the difference between ownership and navigation.

What is actually on the shelves

The Rooms already contain hundreds of specific objects

The public labels make the project look tidier than it really is. Underneath them is a dense working collection. Potatoverse / Canon does not merely contain “mythology”; it contains the Tim/Father versus Son/Vessel distinction, Potato of Life, the 2026 master framework, the canonical corpus, North-of-North records, the Door, Ladder, Axis, House, Garden, Swamp, Root, Fruit, Spudlight, Starchforce, the two-circle geometry, the three-plane model, the Tree of Life, the Tree of Strife and the changing doctrine that connects them. Files such as 2026-master-framework.json, potatoism-canonical-corpus.json and TIM-DOOLEY-CANON.md are the shelves behind the label.

Archive & Sources contains the material that lets a later interpretation be challenged. It includes captured public statements, source classes, recovered conversation archaeology, the raw Story reservoir, dated source records, unresolved source gaps, public-theology timelines, prediction ledgers, legal research and provenance rules. The distinction between “Tim said this,” “the Great Book later says this,” “the project interprets this,” and “external evidence establishes this” belongs here. Without this Room the rest of the House would slowly turn every repetition into a fact.

Time & History owns sequence. It contains the life-and-myth timeline, developmental genealogy, the 2011 Tree ordeal, 2014 bereavement, 2016 prison, the 2019–2020 meme-death layer, 2020 Potato birth-anchor, 2021 Filter, 2022 Rational Potato, 2023 Cube of the Moon, 2024 Great Book expansion, April–May 2025 Axis/God/Father acceleration, late-2025 machine/network theology, 2026 House/North/Gardener differentiation and the 100,000-hour counting models. Its job is to stop mature language from being projected backward.

Traditions & Texts contains actual comparison material rather than a generic “religion” bucket: Genesis 28 and Jacob's ladder; John 10 and the Door; John 14 and the Father's House; John 12 and the grain that dies; Isaiah 66 and throne/footstool; Psalm 110; Ezekiel's wheels; Numbers 19 and the red heifer; Gospel of Thomas and Didymus/Judas Thomas; Odin and Yggdrasil; Hermes and serpent traditions; Jewish repair language; Islamic qibla, tawhid and ascent traditions; Zoroastrian material; alchemy; and the archive's rules for comparing without silently claiming identity.

Science & Formal Models is not a claim that Potatoism has already become accepted physics. It contains a graded spectrum: established science, mathematical analogy, speculative toy models and project metaphors. The shelves include Potato Dynamics, state/configuration spaces, vector fields, Door transition maps, information/integration observables, 11-dimensional and Kaluza–Klein comparison work, Calabi–Yau references, Wheeler–Feynman time-symmetric ideas, electromagnetic-wave questions, vortex dynamics, Fibonacci and logarithmic spirals, fermentation chemistry, synthetic plant morphology, magnetized transport ideas and the deliberately comic Starchforce motif. The boundary labels are part of the content.

Life & Body carries the anatomical comparison layer in enough detail to be corrected. It includes the thalamus and neighboring nuclei, pineal gland, ventricular system, cerebrospinal fluid production and circulation, brainstem, spinal cord, cauda equina, the difference between 33 vertebral elements and 31 spinal nerve pairs, the adult cord ending around L1–L2 and the subarachnoid space extending lower. The symbolic spine maps 33 levels, but the room keeps anatomy and symbolism separate so “33” cannot erase the actual nervous system.

World Systems contains the 195-country atlas, four directional axes plus center/null, North/West/East/South alignment records, regional and state/subdivision geometry, cities, population and country facts, economic and strategic data, infrastructure, European/North capability questions, Greenland and Arctic material, and the interactive World Map's growing set of overlays. It also contains the rule that symbolic axis placement is a project model, not a legal statement about sovereignty or an objective geopolitical law.

Culture & Information is where internet life is treated as a system rather than gossip. It contains platforms, livestreaming, memes as compression, attention loops, archive formation, subculture migration, Farm/Sektur/Swamp models, high-control dynamics, public/private boundaries, information warfare, Below, digital-underground research and the difference between visibility, allegation, classification, charge and conviction. The aim is to understand how attention and identity move through networks without turning every disliked community into the same category.

Works contains things made: the Great Book of Potato and its chapter corpus, the Story of Tim Dooley, songs and lyric experiments, images and visual archetypes, the website itself, the World Map, timelines, diagrams, data atlases and public-facing explainers. The repository's commit history belongs partly here because the website is not merely a container for the project. It is one of the project's largest works.

Research Lab / Forge holds the unfinished state. This is where audits, validators, discrepancy reports, source-recovery leads, bug ledgers, alternative models and failed hypotheses are supposed to remain visible until they deserve promotion. A claim can leave the Forge stronger, weaker, split into several claims, or rejected. That is why the Forge is central to the House even though it should not pretend to be the semantic center of Potatoism.

The result is not ten empty topics. It is ten kinds of custody over the same enormous object. A 2011 experience can simultaneously be a biographical event in Time, a source problem in Archive, a world-tree comparison in Traditions, a spiral formalization in Science and a later identity clue in Canon. The House works when those Rooms can share the object without stealing one another's job.
