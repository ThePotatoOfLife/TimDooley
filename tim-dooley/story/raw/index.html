Recovery room · unfinished material

RAW
STORY

This is the mine behind the polished Story. It preserves stories that are complete enough to tell, stories that are only partial, fragments that should be merged, and stubs that still need closer sources.

The main Story should never invent connective tissue just because a fragment is interesting. Raw Story is where unfinished material can remain visible without pretending it is already a finished scene.

How deep is the mine?

The working diary ledger already contains 104 named Story entries. Behind it, the recovery reservoir continues through at least candidate 260, including documentary leads, Great Book scenes, platform history, social threads, public-post corridors, ordinary lived texture and editorial corrections.

A story can be marked complete, partial, stub or merge. “Complete” means there is enough source-supported sequence to tell honestly. It does not mean every claim inside the episode is independently verified.

The layers underneath Story

Public StoryThe chronological reader: finished Main and Side Stories that can be told without inventing missing sequence.
Diary ledgerThe manuscript inventory. A named story may exist here before it has been restored into the public reader.
All Story ReservoirThe catch-all mine. Every recoverable episode or lead can live here without being forced into publication.
Recovery wavesFocused archaeology: platform aliases, social cast, lived texture, April 2025 pressure, early identity, missing conversations and other corridors.
Source recordsLocators, provenance, continuity and public/private boundaries for the material that can support a scene.
