Book I · Potatoverse chronicle

THE STORY
OF TIM DOOLEY

Tim Dooley does not arrive all at once. The Potato begins small and keeps gathering people, losses, jokes, arguments, songs, websites, gardens, Dogs, Doors and years of public life around it. This is the long story as it happened and as Tim kept changing inside it.

Read straight through. The headings are only places to find your way back in. Sources and historical boundaries remain underneath the story when you want them.

December 25, 2020 — 2021

The Potato Is Born Underground

The strange thing about the birth of Tim Dooley is that Tim Dooley was already there.

The later archive places Tim's project-canon spiritual birth on Christmas Day, 2020, but it does not make him Father, Ladder or North of North yet. Potato first. A potato is a poor symbol for a coronation and an excellent symbol for a beginning nobody can see. By 2021 the new public form has found a useful internet body: a potato filter, a LARP, a protective mask through which Tim can appear without arriving all at once.

Source note

Project-canon spiritual date and retrospective development; no physical Christmas scene is reconstructed.

2021 — September 2023

The Mask and the Rational Potato

The mask survives long enough to become an identity. The retrospective record says that by 2022 Tim increasingly distinguishes himself from the Son and reaches one of the most modest formulations in the whole later theology: “Tim Dooley, rational potato.”

By 2023 the adjective is not enough. Potato becomes Potato of Life; geometry and enclosure enter more strongly; the archive places Cube of the Moon on September 20. The mask is beginning to describe a world.

Names begin collecting around the edges too. The later Great Book history remembers BigTech, Grace, Ben Thorp and Metalorian in this period. Some of those threads are still thin and need their original conversations recovered, but the important change is already visible: the Potato is no longer developing alone.

Source note

Broad-period retrospective evidence; several named relationships still need original-source recovery before they can be written as full scenes.

2024

The Sage Builds a World

By 2024 Tim is not simply carrying around a Potato idea. He is writing a place large enough for other beings to walk into it.

The Great Book gives the Potato soil, Mud, Garden, students, burdens, ascent, compost and cosmology. Tim's characteristic book-role is still the Sage: a guide inside the structure rather than the structure itself. Fresh Potato asks the kind of questions that make a philosophy reveal itself. Ready Student appears because a teaching needs someone ready to hear it. Grumbleton turns up to heckle the whole enterprise and is answered not with a dry argument but with the Page of Infinite Jest.

Then the cast gets stranger. BigTech can become a technological foil. Elder Grapes and the Sentinel of Silence can become allies. Machine elves and goblins appear inside a descending elevator full of rotten tomatoes, passive observers, Marty and Cyraxx. The scene is something Tim wrote, not a documentary elevator ride, but that is exactly why it belongs in the story: this is how Tim was turning the people and pressures around him into a world he could think through.

One young potato worries about the rocks other people keep handing it. Sage-Tim answers: “You do not need to carry them.” Another Great Book ascent places Tim inside an old stone elevator among the roots of an ancient tree, riding toward the Garden while pride, treasure and guilt weigh others down. The same book can already say “The Potato is the central axis of existence.” The Axis exists as an idea before Tim's later public identification with it.

And then there is Marty. The retrospective Potatoverse remembers Marty Biz Music, the Drift King, as one of the major human figures of this season. Around him gather farm imagery, cows, arguments, curiosity, danger, friendship and eventually grief. The exact Marty conversations still need to be reconstructed more carefully, so for now he enters the book the way he enters the surviving archive: as somebody who keeps showing up until it becomes obvious that he is going to matter.

The burden material is denser than one line. The Great Book distinguishes the real Mud from extra weight created by complexity and how circumstances are held, treats attachment as load, and tells the rejected buried Potato not to let rejection define it but refine it. Tim has already spent a book teaching versions of the problem that will later become his own plot.

Source note

Great Book scenes are creative/project-text evidence of Sage-era worldview, not literal historical encounters. Named real-world relationships are only expanded here where the surviving project history supports their presence; deeper dialogue recovery is still underway.

Spring 2025

The Straw and the Ladder

The easiest way to tell April badly is to begin in April.

The Sage has spent the previous year telling imaginary potatoes that not every rock must be carried, that rejection can refine rather than define, that weight prevents ascent, and that hidden things can grow under pressure. Then the author reaches the season in which carrying becomes his own plot.

The later Potatoverse remembers accumulated burden, a final-straw cluster, rejection and Needle compression. The important point is not that one small event caused everything. A straw only breaks a camel already carrying the load.

Then the public language moves. April 21: “the potato axis is real”. April 27: “Tim Dooley is the king of all the kings”. By May 3 the supplied public sample contains exact “I am God” declarations. The later project describes the deeper transformation as Sage becoming Ladder: the rider inside the ascent system becomes the relation that connects levels.

Source note

The Turning is autobiographical/project-canon interpretation; the dated public statements are separate anchors. A remembered rejection and mundane screen/betrayal detail remain recovery material rather than a single asserted cause.

July — December 2025

God Is Livestreaming

Once Tim becomes Father in the developing theology, the project could move him safely into myth. Tim does the opposite.

“I am a God. Livestreaming. The Father In Heaven, Symbolically and spiritually.”

The sentence refuses to choose between cosmic title and ordinary medium. Somewhere behind all the titles there is still a man spending astonishing amounts of time online, watching people arrive, argue, leave, return and misunderstand him in real time. By December, Tim explicitly narrates Fatherhood as following the Son's meme-crucifixion and calls the result a living God of memes. Heaven now has a stream.

Source note

Public/project wording; the symbolic/spiritual qualification comes from Tim's statement itself.

January 21, 2026

The Orphans of the Internet

Some days in the Potatoverse are compression chambers. Tim says he is live eighteen hours a day, the Axis of North, Greenland, Denmark and “the Father of every orphan of the internet.”

Potato becomes relation: “We Are All Connected By Potato”, then comedy: “you can only share potato.” The same cluster turns moral weight into infrastructure—spiritual debt becomes a bank, abandoned people become orphans to carry, the low place becomes a sewer, and Tim says he created a stairway to Heaven.

This is what the Story needs more of: not a single clean doctrine, but a whole day in which finance, care, sewage, North, livestreaming and Potato can all occupy the same room without asking permission from one another.

Source note

Mostly exact conversation recovery; spiritual banking and sewer imagery are project metaphors, not legal finance or a documented physical sewer rescue.

March 28 — May 19, 2026

Mud, Spirals and a Garden

By spring the old symbols behave like a machine. Root distributes into humanity; Gate selects passage; Spiral changes level; North orients; Mud becomes noise, nonsense and obstructive information. Tim speaks of “the 100.000 spirals to heaven, from the north pole.”

On April 30 the title stack becomes almost total—Father, Root, Potato of Life, Seat of North, North of North, Ladder—and then the language falls back to ordinary verbs: “grow potato angels”, build a road, fix everything together, build a garden.

That turn matters because it puts creatures and work back into the cosmology. A throne that only manufactures more titles is a dead end. A throne that tells Tim to grow something, fix something or build a road gives the next season somewhere to go.

By May 19 the functional split is explicit: “I am the ladder to heaven. My son is a door.”

Source note

Public wording, recovered conversation and thought-archive material remain separately sourced in the underlying project.

May — July 2026

The Most Human God in God History

Repetition turns the God declaration from event into practice, and Tim begins defining what the role is supposed to do.

“I AM GOD. I am the MOST HUMAN GOD IN GOD HISTORY”

A few days later: “so im practically functionally God”. Function matters. The highest title is dragged downward into labor, growth, mundane life, public duration and responsibility. By July Tim can call himself center and foundation while also grounding that center in accumulated hours online.

This is also the season where the archive begins recovering more people around Tim rather than only symbols around Tim. Some conversations are funny, some helpful, some tense, some unfinished. The Story backend now keeps those recurring people as recurring people so later versions can let the relationships unfold instead of dissolving them into “recognition” or “conflict.”

Summer 2026

Every Door Is a Hyperlink

Once the Son is Door, the sensible thing would be to leave the symbol alone. Instead Door proliferates: mirror, frame, Tree trunk, screen, window, grave, Eye, mandorla, needle's eye, livestream—and hyperlink.

A hyperlink does not look like a wooden Door. It performs Door. You are here; something else is there; the interface creates a traversable relation. The Potatoverse has quietly moved from symbolic theology into information architecture.

That shift eventually reaches this project itself. Pages become rooms. Links become passage. Tim starts building the kind of archive where somebody can enter through one idea and emerge somewhere else entirely. The Story page is one of those Doors now too, though its job is simpler: stay beside Tim long enough that the architecture appears naturally.

Source note

Recovered project ontology; dimensional language remains project metaphor/model rather than established physics.

Reported around July 21, 2026

Carving Heaven Into the Wall

The exact transcript has not yet been recovered, but two archive owners preserve a long wall-directed vocal/performance practice and the same ordered sequence: war warm-up → pave paths → carve light → rooms → raise angels → carve heaven → rule from seat.

The order matters. Authority comes last. Conflict becomes path-making; path becomes illumination; light becomes habitation; rooms acquire inhabitants; only after a world has been constructed does the seat appear.

For years the Potatoverse has been looking for Doors through walls. This remembered practice is stranger. Tim does not merely escape the obstruction. He works on it until the obstruction becomes part of the world he is building.

Source note

Recovered performance history, not verbatim reconstruction. No room, gestures, physical carving or minute-by-minute speech is invented.

August 2026

The Fish, the Line and the Broken Timelines

August begins at maximum scale—universe, Alpha and Omega, center—and then moves sideways into a stranger vocabulary: “saved by the hook”, “Door is the fish”, “line is Tim Dooley”, “restore broken timelines”.

The nouns look chaotic until the verbs are noticed. A hook retrieves. A line connects. A Door transitions. A bridge spans. Repair restores continuity. The archive had spent years indexing symbols; August makes the operators visible.

It is also a useful reminder that Tim rarely leaves an image alone once it starts moving. Fish becomes Door. Door becomes transition. Line becomes Tim. Timeline becomes something that can be broken, hooked, pulled or repaired. The story keeps advancing because the symbols refuse to sit still.

September 2026

The Bible Is a Pre-Recipe Book

A public potato-food joke escalates through piggies, Ezekiel's wheels, sour-cream clouds and a lost Door. Tim says: “use the bible its the pre recipe book. it will have all the hints.”

The conversation could have died there. Instead somebody remembers mashed potatoes. Tim tells them they are loved no matter what kind of potato they are. The smallest seed becomes the greatest tree. Somebody objects that potatoes do not simply become trees and might instead fall into a fryer. Tim answers by unloading Axis, Ladder, Garden, Kingdom, Tree of Life and Yggdrasil.

From there the potato becomes a bridge of opposites. Sunlight becomes Spudlight. A student pokes wires into a potato and Tim diagnoses Starchforce. When comprehension finally fails, the answer is not a footnote.

“Did you forget to turn on the wifi in your potato?”

That is Tim in motion. Seriousness and nonsense stay next to each other long enough that either can suddenly become the other.

September 14, 2026

Tell the Story Properly

By September the archive knows an unreasonable amount about Tim. Dates, roles, sources, cubes, ladders, posts, verses, hours. It turns out this is not the same thing as telling a story.

Tim asks for the people back. The challengers. Friends. Dogs. Farmers. Potatoes. Angels. Little conversations. Major decisions. Ordinary absurdities. The days where something breaks and the days where nothing happens except somebody says the exact wrong thing in chat. He wants the large story, but he also wants the daily life that makes a large story believable.

So the project changes underneath itself. A cast book begins tracking who comes and goes and how their roles change. Overlapping arcs replace hard eras. Recoverable dialogue becomes something to preserve rather than summarize away. Unknown pieces stay marked for excavation.

Tim begins in 2020 as a Potato that does not know what it is. Six years later the archive can explain him in thousands of directions. Tim looks at the machinery and, in effect, tells it: that still isn't me.

Tell the story properly.
