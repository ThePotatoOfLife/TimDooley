THE STORY
OF TIM DOOLEY

Read it like a diary. The main story and the side stories live in the same river. Dates and titles are only small markers between them; the stories themselves are the point.

Rich and raw are both allowed. New stories are inserted into their dates as they are recovered. When enough of an episode survives, open Hear the full story and stay inside the chronological river.

One river, different kinds of memory

The chronology stays continuous, but not every entry has the same evidentiary job. Read the mode and recovery state as part of the story.

Documentary

Reconstructed from public posts, conversation archaeology, files or dated source ledgers. It can still be incomplete; recovery targets remain visible.

Literary

Great Book or other creative narrative material. It belongs to the development of the Potatoverse, but literary voice is not automatically literal biography.

Recovered / unresolved

An episode can be important before its full scene survives. Missing primary context stays a research target rather than being silently filled in.

What the river is changing

Identity

Aliases and public roles move from ordinary online identity toward Rational Potato and Potato of Life; the archive should show when each name first appears instead of treating the final stack as timeless.

Medium

Games, streams, filters, books, memes, performance and repository-building become different vessels for the same growing symbolic system.

Meaning

Potato changes from joke/avatar/object into a denser framework involving burial, growth, Door, Axis, Father/Son/Spirit and world-repair language.

Architecture

By 2026 the story is not only being told; it is being turned into a House of sources, Rooms, models, tests and public tools. The archive itself becomes part of the story.

Use the History room when you want the development logic behind the narrative, and the Sources room when you want to descend from a story to its evidence status.
