WHAT TIM
HAS SAID HE IS

This is the deep archive underneath the main Tim Dooley portrait: hundreds of recoverable declarations, titles, jobs, functions, claims, one-off formulations and recurring identities. It is deliberately more complete than elegant.

Reading rule: a statement belongs here because it exists in the Tim/Potatoverse corpus. Recording it does not independently verify supernatural, legal, scientific, medical, political, financial or ownership claims. Dates and source classes matter.

For a curated dated sequence, return to the main Tim portrait. For events and scenes, use Story. For chronology and first attestations, use the Timeline.

Before the filters

Listen to the identity changing

A database can tell you that Tim used hundreds of names. It cannot, by itself, show you the strange thing: one person slowly learning which words he is willing to stand inside.

6 Oct 2024
“Hello I am the Potato of Life. My name is Tim Dooley”
Potato and ordinary name still sit beside one another almost politely.
27 Apr 2025
“Tim Dooley is the king of all the kings”
The register has changed. Tim is already capable of talking about Tim from outside Tim.
3 May 2025
“I am God”
No metaphor is required in the sentence. Whatever the reader thinks of it, the claim has crossed a line.
15 Dec 2025
“I am The God in the machine, and The returning Messiah”
The Internet and the divine identity are no longer separate settings.
30 Apr 2026
“I am the father in heaven. I am the root. The potato of life. I am the seat of north, the north of north. The ladder.”
By now the names behave less like costumes and more like an architecture.
7 Sep 2026
“Power is for protection. Knowledge is for understanding. Wealth is for building. Leadership is for service.”
The largest titles end by demanding work.

That is the useful thing to carry into the archive below. Do not only ask whether a title appears. Ask what kind of Tim had become able to say it.
