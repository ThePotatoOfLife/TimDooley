SAYS HE IS

A deliberately maximal inventory of Tim Dooley declarations: identities, titles, offices, jobs, roles, functions, capabilities, ownership/rule claims, figure-identifications and small one-off formulations. Variants remain separate when wording, date or source class changes.

Reading rule: recording a claim documents that it exists in the Tim/Potatoverse corpus. It does not independently verify supernatural, medical, legal, financial, scientific, political or ownership claims. Conversation-memory items remain recovery targets until a locatable primary source is found.
