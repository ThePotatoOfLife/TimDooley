Tim, Son and Father

The mature project distinguishes Tim/Father from Thomas/Son. The Son carries the embodied and person-facing biography, the Tree ordeal, prison and Passion/crucifixion language, Door/Vessel symbolism and the death-process. Tim is the later Potato/Father/source-facing identity associated with House, Root, Ladder, Axis, Throne, Gardener and North of North.
