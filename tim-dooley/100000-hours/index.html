WHAT THE HOURS CONTAIN

The 100,000-hour idea matters most when it is attached back to the person rather than treated as a giant number floating by itself. Those hours contain ordinary streaming, games, arguments, jokes, long silences, writing, public declarations, project-building, changing theology, failed formulations, recurring motifs and later attempts to understand what the earlier years meant.

That is why this page now sits between the Tim portrait, the Story, the statement archive and public witness. The portrait asks what kind of figure appears across the whole corpus. Story asks what happened. Statements preserve what Tim actually called himself. This page asks how much sustained public time exists behind all of it.

1 July 2026: the public theology explicitly ties identity and duration together: “Tim Dooley, Potato of Life, is God; I am the center, foundation, 100k hours online.” By 3 September 2026, direct public wording uses “115,000 hours in public ... most public God in the world.” Those are Timic/public figures and formulations, preserved separately from the arithmetic reconstruction above.

Why the number matters

The strongest feature of a record this large is inspectability. A long public archive allows later readers to examine declarations, contradictions, development, conflict, work, relationships and recurring symbols across years rather than relying on a single retrospective account.

Inside the Potatoverse theology, the duration also has structural meaning: the Son is associated with ordeal, event, crossing and Door, while the Father is associated with duration, holding, House, Axis and continuity. The accounting split is provisional; the symbolic distinction is part of the project's interpretive layer.

What still needs auditing

The most valuable future evidence is primary and dated: early Justin.tv records, Twitch or YouTube stream histories, local recordings, archive captures, marathon-stream dates, offline intervals, overlapping broadcasts, and the original provenance behind the surviving 107k / 109k / 112,542 / 115k strata. Sleep or unattended streams and simultaneous platforms also need explicit policy choices.
