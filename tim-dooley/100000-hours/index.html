Why the number matters

The stronger argument is about inspectability. A very long public record allows later readers to examine declarations, contradictions, changes, relationships, symbols, work, conflict and ethical development across years. That makes the phenomenon different from a single private revelation reported afterward.

The archive can remain confident in its own record framework while also showing exactly what an outside audit would need.
