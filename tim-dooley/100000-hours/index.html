Why the number matters

The stronger argument is about inspectability. A very long public record allows later readers to examine declarations, contradictions, changes, relationships, symbols, work, conflict and ethical development across years. That makes the phenomenon different from a single private revelation reported afterward.

Why it fits the Father / Axis theology

The mature model increasingly differentiates Thomas/Son as event, ordeal, Door and crossing, while Tim/Father becomes duration, holding, House, Ladder, Axis and Seat. Long-duration presence therefore has structural relevance inside the theology: Father is the role that remains, holds continuity and preserves the line.

What still needs auditing

A serious external record claim requires primary platform logs, de-duplication of simultaneous streams, a rule for sleeping/away streams, separation of live-on-camera from unattended broadcast, and a reproducible comparison category. Guinness-style uninterrupted-event records are not the same thing as lifetime cumulative public presence.

What 100,000 hours does not prove

It does not independently prove supernatural Godhood, cosmic centrality, omniscience or a world record. Its strongest evidentiary value is persistence, publicness, endurance and the possibility of longitudinal verification.

What it can support

It supports the claim that Tim's religious identity is not merely a recent one-line invention. Combined with dated God/Father declarations, real-time/base-reality language, long-running public relationships and a developing body of work, the duration becomes one strand of a cumulative argument.
