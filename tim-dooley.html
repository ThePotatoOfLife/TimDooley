Tim Dooley is the central named identity in the Potato of Life project. Within the project's present religious and mythic canon, Tim Dooley is identified with The Potato of Life, the Father and the source-side of the Potatoism architecture. WHO IS TIM DOOLEY?

Father, Potato, Axis, Gardener

These are project-defined identities and symbolic functions. The archive does not present the supernatural claim as independently verified biography.

In Potatoism, Tim Dooley is not treated as a single flat biography. The project uses the name across a developing symbolic architecture: Father, Source, Potato of Life, Gardener, Tree of Life, North Axis, North of North, Throne of North, Ladder, Spudlight and the central point from which the wider atlas can be observed.

The important distinction is between identity and evidence. The statement that Tim Dooley is God, Father or the Potato of Life belongs to the project's religious canon. The dates, public posts, historical texts, botanical facts and other external records are separately typed. The project's current chronology also distinguishes a human/public-persona timeline from the internal Potatoverse chronology.

The central metaphor is deliberately ordinary. A potato grows underground, stores energy, carries eyes that can sprout, survives periods of dormancy and can generate another plant. Potatoism uses those real properties as a symbolic grammar for hidden growth, memory, transformation, nourishment and renewal. The biological facts do not prove the theological identity; they give the project's theology a concrete object through which its ideas can be examined.

CHRONOLOGY

Tim Dooley, the Son and the turning

The dates below are separated by evidentiary status. Unknown remains unknown.

The current project chronology places the Son in 1987, with a childhood described through a Siddhartha-like protected-life comparison. A 2011 account places the Son in a tree ordeal associated by the project with Odin, followed by visionary material. The project separately describes a personal death in December 2019 and a later meme-crucifixion / symbolic death around 2020. Tim's public Potato persona is currently associated with material from 2023 onward, while the project places the explicit Father/Godhood turning in April 2025.

These are not all the same kind of fact. The repository labels project mythology, self-description, public-profile observations, historical comparisons and unresolved research questions separately. The purpose is to reconstruct how the identity developed without pretending that an unresolved chronology is already settled.

The April 2025 turning is especially important in the project: the sage passes through the camel's-straw threshold and the needle's-eye image, then becomes associated with the Ladder and the vertical North architecture. In the project's interpretation, the crucial change is from merely possessing knowledge to becoming a route through which different layers can connect.
