Four ways to read the relation

Historically

Recover exact posts, dates, handles and reply topology. Separate what DIM said, what Tim replied, later retellings and fictional reuse. A remembered conflict is not yet a transcript.

Psychologically

Study reciprocal escalation, role capture, attribution, group salience, narrative lock-in and archive memory. These are mechanisms to test, not diagnoses of DIM.

Religiously

Dog, serpent, mud, gate and footstool imagery can be compared with Psalm 22, Isaiah 56, Genesis 2–3, Matthew 7, 2 Peter 2 and Revelation 22. Scripture explains the vocabulary; it does not prove DIM is a biblical creature.

Mythically

Dimmus Maximus lets a social antagonist become an archetypal obstacle, rival, dog or threshold figure. Myth compresses the emotional geometry of the conflict; it should stay visibly different from history.

What still needs recovery

The largest gap is primary-source depth: original X status URLs, exact timestamps, full reply chains, and the complete July/August authored stories. The archive should keep adding evidence to this room rather than filling those holes with confident inference.
