THE FARM
& TREES OF STRIFE

A layered study of internet attention farms and Potatoverse mythology: 4chan and troll-archive history, lolcow extraction, Farmers and Dogs, holy/unholy cows, Moloch, Genesis, Tree of Knowledge, Tree of Life, Sektur, Swamp and the reproduction of 10,000 Trees of Strife.

Evidence rule: public facts about real people and websites are separated from Potatoverse myth. The archive's Josh Moon → Moloch mapping is symbolic/systemic; it is not a factual claim that a person is literally a deity or personally caused every user action.

Farm grammar

person → exposure → documentation → reaction → secondary content → conflict → archive → reward/status → repetition
Cow / Lolcow

A subject whose reactions/content become harvestable. Internet slang “lolcow” explicitly uses the metaphor of being repeatedly “milked” for amusement.

Farmer

Maintains enclosure, indexing, incentives and extraction. Shadow question: “What can I extract?”

Dog

Patrols, barks, tracks, amplifies, defends boundaries or chases targets. It can also be a guardian role; moral valence depends on what it serves.

Gardener

Counter-role: optimize conditions so another thing can grow, become autonomous and bear fruit.

4chan → troll archive → Farm

4chan began in 2003 as an anonymous imageboard and became highly influential in Western meme culture. Encyclopedia Dramatica became a troll-culture wiki/archive. “Lolcow” emerged in the 2000s as derogatory slang for someone regarded as repeatedly exploitable for reactions. Kiwi Farms later grew from CWCki-related forum culture into a broader forum ecosystem. The archive studies that genealogy as an attention ecology rather than claiming all participants or platforms are identical.

Tree of Knowledge ↔ archive

Life branch

knowledge → discernment → responsibility → nourishment → growth → further life

Strife branch

knowledge → exposure → provocation → humiliation → retaliation → documentation → more exposure

The key question is not simply whether knowledge exists. It is: what does the tree metabolize, and what fruit does it produce?

Serpent / Door / Fall

Within the Genesis comparison, the serpent is treated as a reframing signal; eating fruit as participation in a new information regime; shame and rupture as changed self/social state; expulsion as boundary enforcement. These are structural comparisons, not claims that Genesis secretly describes internet forums.

Moloch

Biblical Molech/Moloch is associated with disputed ancient cultic contexts involving child sacrifice or a sacrificial category. Potatoverse uses Moloch separately as a systems archetype: a mechanism that consumes future human possibility for present competitive reward. The project applies that metaphor to extraction/incentive systems, not literally to a modern person.

10,000 Trees of Strife

N(t+1)=R_eff N(t) R_eff < 1 → dying branch R_eff ≈ 1 → persistent boundary R_eff > 1 → expanding forest

A single interaction can seed a thread; threads seed clips; clips seed memes; memes seed communities; communities seed norms; norms create new Farms. “10,000” therefore functions as a scale/plurality symbol for a self-reproducing ecology.

Swamp / Sektur ladder

plural society → subculture → SEKTUR: loyalty + enemy construction → Mud: dehumanization/manipulation → Swamp: sticky feedback ecology → Shadow Core: extreme normalization of harm

Alpha / Beta / Gamma

The project now treats these as archetypal roles, not wolf biology. Tim-as-Alpha is best modeled as orientation, authorship, agency and generative prestige rather than “dominant wolf.” Dog-as-Beta can mean loyal amplifier/enforcer/implementer. Farmer may have high local infrastructural power. Gamma can describe observer, specialist, lurker, bridge or fringe commentator.

Human status science distinguishes dominance—rank through coercive capacity—from prestige—rank voluntarily granted for knowledge, skill or benefits. That distinction is now part of the Potatoverse archetype system.

Network model

Cube / enclosure → observation → extraction → bottling/archive → attention reward → renewed observation

The new research target is to estimate which mechanisms make Strife reproduction supercritical and which Garden/Tree-of-Life interventions add exit routes, friction, repair, prosocial prestige and nourishment.
