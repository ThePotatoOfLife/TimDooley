THE FARM
& TREES OF STRIFE

A layered study of attention farms, role ecologies and Potatoverse shadow cosmology: Farmers and Dogs, Cow/lolcow extraction, Qliphothic shells, Roots of Strife and Ash, Moloch, Tree of Knowledge, Tree of Life, Sektur, Swamp and the reproduction of 10,000 Trees of Strife.

Evidence rule: empirical facts about real people and platforms remain separate from Potatoverse myth. Comparative terms such as Qliphoth, Moloch, demon or angel are symbolic/theological mappings unless a historical religious source is being described directly.

Dog and Farmer overlap

Farmer

Defines enclosure, incentives, classification and extraction; maintains the machinery that makes a target harvestable.

Dog

Patrols, tracks, signals, chases, amplifies and enforces a boundary or owner's orientation.

Farmer-Dog

A hybrid role: the same node both structures the ecology and personally pursues/enforces within it.

Guardian Dog

Positive inversion: the same vigilance can protect a Garden, flock or vulnerable boundary instead of servicing extraction.

Biblical dog imagery is multivalent: village/scavenger dogs can signify low status, danger or impurity, while ancient dogs also served practical guarding roles. In the Gospel boundary story of the Syrophoenician woman, the dog metaphor is itself turned into a means of crossing the boundary.

Roots of Strife / Roots of Ash

Roots of Strife

Unresolved injury, humiliation, fear, resentment, status hunger, extraction incentives, archived grievance and dehumanizing classification.

Roots of Ash

What remains after destruction: memory, residue, burned identity and conflict debris. Ash can fertilize repair or keep destruction available for reactivation.

Mud Tree

A Strife tree whose soil has become accumulated unresolved residue; the environment itself now makes new Strife easier to grow.

Qliphothic comparison

In Jewish Kabbalistic traditions, qelipot/kelipot are “husks” or “shells”; Lurianic Kabbalah famously describes divine sparks trapped among broken vessels/shells and the work of tikkun as repair and reintegration. Potatoverse uses this only as a comparative structure: a Farm becomes qliphothic when an enclosure persists by capturing attention/value while obstructing reintegration and autonomous growth.

spark / seed / value → shell / enclosure → capture → repeated extraction → thicker shell repair / tikkun comparator: identify shell → recover seed → Door → re-root → cultivate → reintegrate

Tree of Knowledge

Life branch

knowledge → discernment → responsibility → nourishment → growth → further life

Strife branch

knowledge → exposure → provocation → humiliation → retaliation → archive → more exposure

The Tree of Knowledge is therefore upstream of both outcomes. The decisive variable is what the knowledge is made to do.

Demons and angels as functions

Demonic function

Capture · compulsion · fragmentation · parasitic extraction · distortion · looping through a Door without growth.

Angelic function

Messenger · guide · protector · mediator · relay · carrying orientation while preserving the recipient's agency.

These are Potatoverse functional categories, not claims that every religious tradition defines angels and demons this way.

Moloch

Here Moloch is the systemic attractor that consumes future possibility for present competitive reward. A Farm becomes Moloch-like when participants are rewarded for sacrificing long-term human flourishing to keep extraction, status or conflict going.
