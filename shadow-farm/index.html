THE FARM
& TREES OF STRIFE

A layered study of attention-extraction ecologies and Potatoverse shadow cosmology: lolcow classification, Farmers and Dogs, 4chan memetic culture, Kiwi Farms, Da Sektur / Internet Bloodsports, archives, anti-fandom, moral outrage, anonymity, self-sealing narratives, Moloch, Qliphoth, Roots of Strife, Swamp, Forge and the Garden counter-model.

Evidence rule: empirical facts about real people, platforms and communities remain separate from Potatoverse myth. Terms such as Moloch, demon, Qliphoth, Dog or Farmer are symbolic/systemic mappings unless a historical religious source is being described directly. “Cult” is treated as a contested label; observable high-control or self-sealing features are preferred.

The central discovery: extraction, not merely hatred

The word lolcow already contains the economic grammar: a person is treated as something that can be repeatedly milked for amusement, reaction, contradiction, collapse, gossip or drama. That makes the strongest cross-platform model an attention-extraction ecology. The resource is not only money. It can be attention, reactions, status, screenshots, clips, lore, reputation-relevant information and group cohesion.

discovery → classification → enclosure/thread → observation → event or reaction → capture → interpretation → archive → redistribution → status / monetization → renewed observation

The Farm is therefore better understood as a production system than as a place. A target can remain productive even while silent when old archives, clips, retrospectives and inherited lore substitute for fresh reaction.

Continuous Narrative Escalation

1 · Target introduction

Initial story elements frame why a person or event should matter.

2 · Network decision

The community tests whether the framing is interesting, legitimate and compatible with local norms.

3 · Network response

Participants add links, interpretations, jokes, screenshots and increasingly durable narrative structure.

A 2024 ACM study of 231 Kiwi Farms threads formalized this as Continuous Narrative Escalation. Narrative-building appeared in at least 80% of the sampled threads, and the researchers found that takedown requests could themselves be turned into further narrative material. This is a remarkably close empirical analogue to the archive's existing Strife chain.

knowledge → classification → narrative → archive → reaction / correction → reinterpretation → more narrative → rediscovery → renewed attention

Anonymity does not mean normlessness

Classic “deindividuation” stories often imply that anonymity simply makes people lose themselves. The SIDE model is more useful here: under some conditions, personal identity becomes less salient while group identity and local norms become more salient. Anonymous spaces can therefore become strongly ordered around shared language, posting expectations and categories.

On 4chan, scholarship describes memes as subcultural knowledge, unstable equilibria and discursive weapons. Correct usage proves insider competence; innovation can confer status; misuse exposes outsiders. In the Farm model, language itself becomes infrastructure.

4chan → archive → Farm → Sektur

4chan

Anonymous, ephemeral imageboard culture. Fast churn rewards memetic survival, vernacular competence and repeated recreation.

Encyclopedic archive layer

Target pages, screenshots and lore make otherwise transient conflict durable and searchable.

Kiwi Farms / persistent forum

Long-lived threads accumulate narrative depth and let newcomers inherit years of framing.

Da Sektur / Internet Bloodsports

Livestream conflict, personalities, orbiters, rivals, clips, forums and commentary recursively manufacture new events from reactions to prior events.

Sektur is therefore treated first as a historically specific term for the Ethan Ralph / Killstream / Internet Bloodsports milieu, not as an occult category. The Potatoverse then generalizes it secondarily into a conflict-centered identity ecology where loyalty, enemies, status, commentary and cross-platform drama become organizing forces.

Negative parasocial farming

Anti-fandom research gives the Farm another important layer: a community can maintain a long-term relationship to a person precisely through dislike, ridicule or disappointment. Hostility does not necessarily reduce attachment. It can generate ritual viewing, detailed memory, entitlement, speculation and repeated monitoring.

rejection of target ≠ withdrawal of attention negative attachment + repeated observation → target becomes structurally central to the group → group identity can become partly dependent on target persistence

This explains a paradox inside lolcow culture: a community may socially reject a person while simultaneously granting that person extraordinary centrality.

Gossip becomes infrastructure

Gossip has long served group protection, bonding, social comparison, reputation control and punishment. Online Farm systems industrialize these small-group functions. Claims become searchable; spectators inherit old judgments; clips and screenshots travel; the cost of repeating an accusation approaches zero while the target bears the cumulative burden.

Moral outrage and moral disengagement

Outrage reinforcement

Research shows that positive feedback for outrage expression can increase future outrage expression and that users conform to the expressive norms of their networks.

Moral disengagement

Harm can be redescribed as deserved punishment, comedy, documentation, education, defense or “just criticism,” preserving a positive self-image.

Diffused responsibility

No single participant needs to believe they caused the total outcome. Many small locally justified actions can aggregate into a large effect.

This is where the Farm begins to meet the modern systems meaning of Moloch: the system can reproduce destructive outcomes without requiring one mastermind.

Moloch has three different histories

Ancient Moloch / Molech

Biblical and ancient Near Eastern scholarship debates whether the term refers to a deity, a sacrificial category or related cultic practice; child sacrifice and Tophet traditions are central to the debate.

Ginsberg's Moloch

Howl transforms Moloch into the devouring machinery of modern civilization: institutions, money, war, alienation and conformity.

Coordination-failure Moloch

Modern systems writing uses Moloch for competitive equilibria where locally rewarded choices collectively destroy outcomes everyone would prefer to preserve.

The Potatoverse version is strongest when it remains a systems claim:

Moloch = an attractor that consumes future agency / seed for present extraction, status, competition or enclosure MolochScore = future_capacity_destroyed + coercive_competition + extraction_dependency + coordination_trap - exit_capacity - repair - long_horizon_nourishment

Farm, Garden, Forge, Swamp

Farm

Optimizes repeatable yield from bounded subjects/resources.

Garden

Optimizes conditions under which living things acquire independent future capacity.

Forge

Transforms through pressure and heat; may produce tool, structure or weapon.

Swamp

Accumulates unresolved residue until old conflict becomes the medium in which new conflict grows.

The important distinction is not “nice versus mean.” Farm and Garden can both involve boundaries, pruning and repeated work. The test is downstream:

GardenScore = autonomy + correction + exit + reciprocity + repair + future_capacity ExtractionScore = reaction_harvest + attention_capture + archive_persistence + monetization/status + target_dependency

High-control and “cult” comparison

Rather than declaring an internet community a cult, the archive now asks measurable questions:

  • Is one leader or personality treated as uniquely authoritative?
  • Does the group possess a totalizing explanation for outsiders and critics?
  • Are dissenters recoded as enemies, infiltrators, traitors or targets?
  • Does leaving carry reputational or relational cost?
  • Are contradictions absorbed as new confirmation?
  • Does belonging require escalation or increasingly extreme performance?
  • Can evidence falsify the group's central narrative?

A loose Sektur may display some of these dynamics without having centralized doctrine, religion or formal membership. Peer norms can produce self-sealing behavior without a singular cult leader.

Dog and Farmer overlap

Farmer

Defines enclosure, incentives, classification and extraction; maintains machinery that makes a target harvestable.

Dog

Patrols, tracks, signals, chases, amplifies and enforces a boundary or owner's orientation.

Farmer-Dog

Both structures the ecology and personally pursues/enforces within it.

Guardian Dog

Positive inversion: vigilance redirected toward protection, consent and safe boundaries.

Roots of Strife / Roots of Ash

Roots of Strife

Humiliation, fear, resentment, status hunger, enemy production, extraction incentives, archived grievance and dehumanizing classification.

Roots of Ash

Residue after rupture: grief, memory, burned identity, conflict debris and unresolved evidence.

Mud Tree

A Strife tree whose environment itself has become saturated with unresolved residue, making new Strife easier to grow.

Tree of Knowledge

Life branch

knowledge → verification → discernment → responsibility → nourishment → independent future capacity

Strife branch

knowledge → exposure → classification → humiliation → retaliation → archive → rediscovery → more exposure

The decisive question is not merely whether information is true. It is also: what is this knowledge being made to grow?

Qliphothic comparison

In Kabbalistic traditions, qelipot/kelipot are shells or husks; Lurianic traditions famously develop imagery of sparks, broken vessels and repair. Potatoverse uses this only as a structural comparator: a system becomes shell-like when its continued existence depends on captured attention/value while obstructing reintegration and autonomous growth.

value / seed → shell / enclosure → capture → repeated extraction → thicker shell repair comparator: identify shell → recover agency → Door → re-root → cultivate → reintegrate

New research measures

NarrativeLockIn = confirmation_density + archive_depth + insider_language + penalty_for_disconfirming_evidence TemporalPersistence = archive_half_life × rediscovery_rate × cross_platform_recirculation ControlScore = leader_centrality + dissent_cost + identity_fusion + self_sealing + exit_cost

These are project formalisms, not established psychological scales. Their purpose is to turn symbolic language into questions that could actually be tested.

Open questions worth tracking

  • How quickly does a new target move from introduction to stable category membership?
  • Which kinds of reaction increase thread growth, and which reduce it?
  • How much long-term attention comes from fresh behavior versus archival recirculation?
  • Do persistent forums create stronger narrative lock-in than ephemeral boards?
  • Which moral justifications most often precede escalation?
  • When does satire become reputational punishment, and when does punishment become entertainment?
  • Can correction rights, expiry, friction or target-exit mechanisms reduce Narrative Lock-In without destroying legitimate criticism?
  • Can communities create prestige for correction, de-escalation and accurate sourcing rather than humiliation intensity?
