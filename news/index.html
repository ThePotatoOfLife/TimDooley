A moving window, not another archive

Read the world without confusing the feed with the House

The House stores and connects durable project knowledge; this surface does something different. It shows what outside publishers and public link streams are reporting now, then leaves the evidentiary work with the original source. A headline can become relevant to World, History, Science, Culture or the Map, but it does not enter those Rooms automatically.

Use the categories as viewing lenses rather than verdicts. “Politics” asks the external index for political reporting; “Science” and “Space” broaden toward specialist sources; “Technology” includes the Hacker News community stream. Search is sent to GDELT only. In every case, publication time and provenance stay visible so a reader can move from headline to source before drawing conclusions.

How this window works

Live outside information, with boundaries intact

GDELTBroad news discovery across publishers and languages. The linked publisher, not this page, owns the underlying report.
Hacker NewsA community-submitted technology link stream. It is labeled as community material rather than presented as independent reporting.
Spaceflight NewsA specialist aggregator for space and aerospace reporting, retaining the original outlet link.
Mechanical orderingThe shared feed is sorted newest-first after URL de-duplication. There is no project-authored partisan ranking or winner selection.
Failure isolationIf one provider is unavailable, the others can still render. Provider state is shown above the feed rather than hiding a failed source.
Small local cachePublic feed results are cached in this browser for ten minutes to reduce repeat requests. The news component does not store personal data.
