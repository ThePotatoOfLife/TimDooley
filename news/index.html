Outside reporting stays outside reporting

Current World is a temporary view of external reporting, not a Room that owns claims. It can point toward the original publisher, World Map, Timeline or project Sources when deeper work is warranted.

Publisher RSS cards may contain publisher-supplied excerpts. Discovery cards may know only a headline and link. Similar headlines can reveal repeated coverage but do not establish truth or independent corroboration.
