PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE REPOSITORY

THE FRAME

A growing reference work for understanding the world as a connected system: people, places, institutions, history, economics, technology, nature, ideas, ownership, infrastructure and the relationships between them.

Explore the treeRead evidence

Read it like an encyclopedia.

Every record is an entry. Follow its sources. Follow its relationships. Compare it with neighbouring subjects. The unusual part is the connective structure: the archive is designed to show how facts fit together rather than leaving every subject on an island.

The 3D spatial model is available as a visualization. It is deliberately secondary to the readable archive.
