THE GARDEN

Begin with the place.

Before the Garden becomes doctrine, symbol, judgment, or explanation, it is somewhere to stand. There is ground, breath, water, cultivation, food, beauty, choice, boundary, exile, and a guarded way back toward Life. The map above keeps those movements in one landscape so the reader can see relations before reading the argument.

In the Potatoverse layer, Tim sits beneath the central Tree as Gardener. The House and Door hold the western side; the Tree of Life holds the center; the Mud Tree marks the shadowed choice-space; the Gate marks the eastern threshold; and the muddy world below makes consequence spatial. The page is meant to be entered visually first and explained second.

THE TWO TREES

Choice is inside the Garden.

Tree of Life and Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil both stand inside Eden. The project's Mud Tree is an interpretive layer placed over that second tree, not a replacement for its biblical name.

GENESIS 3

The Gate is a boundary.

Expulsion and the guarded way are related but distinct movements. The Gate makes that threshold visible; the fall into the lower muddy world is the project's next movement beyond it.

How the Potatoverse reads the Garden

Gardener means more than sitting in the center. It means cultivation, watching Fruit, protecting viable growth, distinguishing what a process produces, and keeping the boundary legible. The stronger version of the role is generative: the Garden must be capable of life that is not merely an ornament around the Gardener.

The Tree of Life supplies the upward vector: Seed, Root, Tree, Fruit, Seed again. The shadowed tree supplies the downward vector: appetite, recurrence, muddying, consequence. The Gate does not erase either tree; it shows where the inside ends and the outside begins.

The muddy lower land therefore belongs visibly beneath and beyond the bright Garden. The map is not asking the reader to imagine two disconnected universes. It shows a continuous geography of cultivation, choice, threshold, consequence, and possible return.
