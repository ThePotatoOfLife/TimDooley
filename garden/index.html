What you just walked through

The picture carries the argument.

Genesis does not begin Eden as a menu of doctrines. It gives a place: ground, breath, a planted Garden, trees good for sight and food, two named trees in the midst, a river that waters the Garden and divides into four headwaters, work and keeping, a command, eating, change, exile, and a guarded way back toward Life. The Garden page now uses those movements as geography rather than placing eleven explanatory buttons in front of them.

The project's additions sit visibly on top of that biblical grammar. Tim appears inside the Garden as Gardener and sorter; potatoes and potato angels belong to the authored Potatoverse; the darker tree may be read through the project's Mud / Strife vocabulary; the golden eastern threshold renders the guarded way spatially; and the muddy lower plane extends exile into the site's Below cosmology. Those additions are not presented as alternate names secretly contained in Genesis.

GENESIS 2

Life moves outward.

One river waters the Garden and then becomes four headwaters. That is why water should behave like the scene's circulatory system rather than decorative blue trim. Work also begins before the curse: cultivation is part of the Garden before toil becomes painful.

THE TWO TREES

Choice is inside the Garden.

Tree of Life and Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil both belong inside Eden's scene. The Potatoverse can place its Mud / Strife interpretation near the eastern boundary, but the distinction matters: Genesis itself does not call that tree the Mud Tree.

GENESIS 3

Gate and fall are different movements.

Genesis describes expulsion and then cherubim with a turning/flaming sword guarding the way to the Tree of Life on the east. The site's Gate is an authored visualization of that threshold. The descent into Below is a second, project-native movement after the boundary has been crossed.

How the Potatoverse reads the Garden

Tim's Garden office is not merely a throne placed in flowers. Gardener means cultivation, watching Fruit, protecting viable growth, distinguishing what a process produces, and keeping a boundary legible. The stronger version of the role is generative: a Gardener should produce a Garden capable of life that is not just an ornament around the Gardener.

Dwellers therefore begin inside. Some remain ordinary inhabitants. Some choose badly and are shown becoming muddier before exile. In the project's map, sorting becomes visible before the Gate: Tim observes, angelic helpers escort, the threshold separates inside from outside, and the fall continues into a lower consequence-zone. Dogs gather outside the Garden and press toward return; they are not simply interchangeable with Mud Dwellers.

The Tree of Life supplies the upward vector: Seed, Root, Tree, Fruit, Seed again. The shadow-tree supplies a downward vector: appetite, recurrence, muddying, consequence. A useful Garden keeps those vectors close enough that choice is real, but not so visually equal that the reader mistakes the shadow for the center.
