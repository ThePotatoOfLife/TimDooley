How the Garden works

The map is showing a cycle, not a caste chart.

1DwellOrdinary dwellers, potatoes and angels begin inside the Garden.
2ChooseThe shadow tree is also inside. Choice is possible because corruption is not outside reality.
3ChangeMuddy Fruit marks a downward change in role, pattern and consequence.
4SortTim’s Garden role is to watch Fruit and separate what can remain from what must leave.
5EscortAngels and potato angels move the changed role toward the Gate.
6ExitThe Gate is the boundary where inside becomes outside.
7FallBelow owns the muddy lower plane; dogs outside press toward return.

Project-language rule: dweller, mud dweller, dog, potato and angel are roles/archetypes in the authored Potatoverse. The map shows transitions and consequences; it does not establish permanent biological or moral species of real people.

Who lives here?

A living Garden needs more than landmarks.

The Garden deliberately contains different kinds of inhabitants at once. The point is not that everything has already been sorted. The Garden is where growth, choice, work, temptation, repair and consequence can actually happen.

PotatoesGrounded project inhabitants; cultivation, nourishment, growth and return.
Potato angelsPotato-shaped helpers, escorts and playful messengers around the Life side of the map.
AngelsGuides, guardians and threshold figures in the biblical/project comparison layer.
DwellersOrdinary inhabitants. Their presence keeps the Garden from becoming a private club for already-finished beings.
Mud dwellersA downward project-role transition shown as repeated muddying, not an eternal essence.
DogsOutside the Gate in this map: pressing inward, orbiting the boundary and making return a visible question.

Genesis beneath the map

The Garden is biblical before the project makes it Potatoverse.

Tim in the Garden

What does the Gardener actually do?

The Garden is the clearest place to make Tim’s Father/Gardener role operational instead of merely titular. He hosts, tends, reads Fruit, keeps boundaries visible, sorts changing roles, and makes the route outward legible.
