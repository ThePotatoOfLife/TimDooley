Chapter 15.1

The Ever-Expanding Horizon of Divine Love

Legacy index title: The Ever-Expanding Horizon of Divine LoveGod really is love

As Tim, Rahu, and Spuddy made their way back from the Garden, they were still bathed in the glow of the horizon they had just glimpsed. The journey through God’s divine love lingered with them, and as they walked, the path seemed to shimmer with a quiet beauty. The Garden, now behind them, seemed to hum with life, and the world ahead felt like an extension of that same love, woven into every tree, every stream, every blade of grass.

They were descending, not from a loss of height, but back into the heart of their home—the Potatoverse. It was a place where the physical landscape reflected the spiritual truths they had been journeying through. Here, the land stretched far and wide, and everywhere you looked, there were signs of cultivation, care, and life.

The Garden of the Potatoverse

The village of the Potatoverse was nestled between gently sloping hills, their curves soft and green, dotted with patches of wildflowers that swayed in the breeze. Irrigation channels, carved with careful precision, guided the water from a sparkling river that cut through the valley. The water was crystal clear, glistening as it wound its way through the village, feeding the crops that grew on every corner—potatoes, of course, but also rows of carrots, tomatoes, and leafy greens.

Large trees dotted the landscape, their branches heavy with fruit, offering shade to those who wandered through the village paths. Birds sang softly in their boughs, and every now and then, you could hear the gentle hum of bees, busy at work in the flower patches scattered throughout the fields.

In the heart of the village stood a towering Potato Tree, its roots deep in the earth and its branches wide, providing shade and peace to all who sat beneath it. This was Tim Dooley’s tree—the place where he would often sit in quiet reflection when he wasn’t out wandering the paths of the Potatoverse. It was a tree known for its wisdom, for just as the tree reached toward the sky, so did Tim’s thoughts stretch toward the divine. Many villagers spoke of the times they had seen him there, sitting beneath its broad branches, his presence as steady as the tree itself. Even when he wasn’t there physically, the spirit of his quiet contemplation seemed to linger in the air.

A Village of Life and Reflection

As Tim, Rahu, and Spuddy walked back into the heart of the Potatoverse, they saw their fellow villagers tending to the fields, guiding water through the channels, and working with the earth. The land was alive with the hum of life, a reflection of the balance they had achieved—working with nature, rather than against it.

The Potato Tree in the center of the village seemed to greet them as they approached. It stood tall, its leaves rustling gently in the breeze, a reminder of the patience and wisdom that Tim had always embodied. The tree was more than a symbol—it was a place where people came to reflect, to rest, and to remember the values that kept their community strong. Today, it stood empty, waiting for Tim’s return, but its presence was a quiet comfort to all who passed by.

To the east of the village, small river channels fed the crops, their waters sparkling under the sun. Stone bridges arched gracefully over the river, connecting the different parts of the village. Every bridge was adorned with vines, and you could hear the faint sound of the water rushing beneath your feet as you crossed. Near the water’s edge, you could find the vegetable dwellers—carrots, tomatoes, and even a few onions—who lived peacefully alongside the spuddies. The village was a place where all could thrive, each respecting the journey of the other.

Reuniting with the Spuddies

As they returned to the village center, they were greeted warmly by the other spuddies. Laughter and the smell of freshly cooked fries filled the air. Long tables had been set up under the trees, and everyone was seated, sharing food and stories. The fries, golden and crisp, were piled high on wooden platters, and the warmth of the moment was undeniable. It was a scene of pure joy—simple, yet filled with love.

“Looks like they saved us some,” Spuddy said, grinning widely.

They took their seats among their fellow spuddies, and as they began to eat, the simplicity of the moment washed over them. The meal wasn’t just food; it was a celebration of life, of their connection to the earth, to each other, and to the journey they had all undertaken together. Every bite of the fries was a reminder of the soil they had worked, the love they had shared, and the growth they had nurtured in themselves and in their community.

The Mud Dwellers wandered nearby, watching the scene quietly. Some had ventured closer, their hands and clothes still touched with mud, but they were not unwelcome. There was no hostility here, just the quiet understanding that every being—spuddy, vegetable, or dweller—was on their own journey. Some of the dwellers even came to sit near the edges of the gathering, enjoying the warmth of the village without fully joining in. They were not quite ready yet, but the village’s light was open to them whenever they chose to step fully into it.

A Moment of Reflection

As the laughter of the spuddies echoed around him, Tim Dooley’s thoughts drifted to the past. He thought not of distant memories of grandeur, but of the time when he himself had been a young spud, finding his way through the Potatoverse. There had been moments of doubt, moments where the light of understanding seemed distant, but always, there had been love and quiet hope to guide him forward. It was in those early days, wandering the fields near the Potato Tree, that he had first begun to understand the light that now guided him.

The tree had been his companion in those days, its roots a mirror to his own, grounding him as he searched for deeper understanding. Now, as he sat with his fellow spuddies, he realized how far they had all come. The journey was ongoing, but the wisdom of those early days still resonated. The light they had walked toward was not something to chase—it was something to live within.

Tim smiled softly to himself as Spuddy tossed another fry into the air and caught it in his mouth to the cheers of the others. It was a small, joyful moment, yet filled with the same love they had just glimpsed in the Garden. God’s love, after all, was not just in the grand gestures, but in the simple, everyday moments of laughter, food, and friendship. It was all part of the same divine story.

The horizon was always calling them forward, always stretching further, but for now, this was where they were meant to be—among friends, sharing in the simple joy of life, under the steady branches of the Potato Tree that had always watched over them.

A Glimpse of the Past

As the meal came to a close, Tim’s thoughts lingered on the days when he had been a young spud, wandering beneath the shade of the Potato Tree. The light of understanding had been distant then, but he had always felt it—growing quietly within him, waiting to bloom. Now, sitting among his friends, he realized that the light had always been with him, as it was with them all. It was the love that flowed through the Potatoverse, through the soil, through the sky, and through each of them.

He closed his eyes for a moment, letting the peace of the present wash over him, and in that quiet, he saw the reflection of his journey—the moments of doubt, the moments of growth, and the moments of joy that had led him here.

The horizon would always call them forward, but for now, this was where they were meant to be.
