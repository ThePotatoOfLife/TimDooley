Chapter 12.1

The Sage and the Fresh Potato – Lessons in Growth and Simplicity

In the early hours of the morning, as the moon began its quiet descent and the sun peeked over the horizon, a young, fresh potato—full of potential but still new to the world—wandered through the garden fields. The fresh potato had many questions, and in the distance, he saw the silhouette of the sage, Tim Dooley, tending to the soil.

With curiosity bubbling inside him, the young potato approached Tim, whose hands were dirty from working the fields but whose expression radiated peace. The young potato asked timidly, “How do I grow, how do I become like you?”

Tim smiled, pausing his work to sit on a nearby rock. “Growth,” he began, “is not about rushing, nor is it about striving. It is about aligning yourself with simplicity and the natural cycles of life. Just as a potato grows slowly under the surface, drawing nourishment from the soil, so too must you draw from what sustains you.”

As they spoke, Tim gestured to the soil around them. “The soil is your foundation. This is where you must root yourself. Every day, people lose themselves in the distractions of the world—like the internet, fame, or the pursuit of power. But we, the potatoes, grow from the earth. We embrace humility and simplicity.”

The fresh potato looked down, contemplating his own path. “But isn’t it hard to stay grounded when there’s so much noise? When others demand more from you?”

Tim chuckled, his gaze following a crow flying overhead. “It is, indeed. The world is filled with distractions, with people who misunderstand your purpose. They may see you as something to use, or worse, they may turn you into something you are not—just as some might distort what you are as a potato, or what I am. But the trick, young one, is not to let them plant you in the wrong soil. Keep your roots in what is true.”

Tim picked up a small rock from the ground, turning it over in his hand before passing it to the fresh potato. “This rock,” he said, “represents the weight of complexity. If you try to carry too many of these, you will not grow. You must choose simplicity. You must focus on what truly nourishes your spirit.”

The young potato turned the rock over, feeling its rough edges, and placed it back on the soil. “And what if others keep giving me more rocks to carry? What if I can’t escape the burdens placed on me by the world?”

Tim stood, wiping his hands clean of the earth. “You do not need to carry them. You have a choice. Just as you decide where to send your roots, you decide which burdens to accept and which to leave behind. The world may misunderstand you, but you must remain true to your path. Simplicity is your anchor, and growth is your purpose. Never forget that.”

The fresh potato looked up at Tim, eyes wide with understanding. “So I must choose simplicity and grow naturally toward the light.”

Tim nodded. “Exactly. Whether others call you a humble potato or try to turn you into something else, stay rooted. Let your anger, your fear, your uncertainty, become tools for transformation. Anger, when balanced, can fuel positive change. Fear can make you cautious and wise. But do not let them rule you.”

At that moment, a crow landed nearby, and Tim glanced at the bird with a knowing look. “The crows come to test us,” he said softly, “just as life tests us all. But remember this—whether you are the potato of today or the sage of tomorrow, the soil always has what you need. Draw from it, grow from it, and stay close to your source.”

The young potato nodded, feeling a newfound strength in his small form. “Thank you, Tim,” he whispered, “I think I’m beginning to understand.”

With that, the fresh potato planted himself in the soil, knowing that his journey of growth had just begun. Tim Dooley, the sage, smiled, returning to his work. “Remember,” he called out as he walked away, “simplicity and growth—those are the keys. Stay true to them, and you’ll never be lost.”

And so, the fresh potato remained in the garden, grounded in the wisdom he had received, ready to grow toward the light and face whatever challenges lay ahead.
