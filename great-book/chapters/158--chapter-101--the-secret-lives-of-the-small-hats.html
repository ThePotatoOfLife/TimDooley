The World of the Small-Hats: A Society in the Shadows

Beneath the surface of the Potatoverse, hidden within a labyrinth of interdimensional corridors, lay the realm of the small-hats. Unlike the harmonious existence of other beings in the Potatoverse, the small-hats lived in a world built on deception, manipulation, and power. Their society was structured around a strict hierarchy, with the most cunning and ruthless at the top, always vying for more control over the Potatoverse.

The small-hats were masters of illusion. They thrived in the shadows, manipulating events in the Potatoverse from behind the scenes. In their daily lives, they practiced the art of subtlety—using their influence to sow discord, spread misinformation, and steer the multiverse toward entropy. To outsiders, they appeared as harmless bureaucrats or merchants, but in reality, they were the architects of chaos.

Daily Life Among the Small-Hats

Despite their sinister activities, the small-hats had their own culture and traditions. Their society was centered around secrecy and the accumulation of knowledge—particularly knowledge that could be used to control others. Each day, they gathered in hidden chambers to exchange information and plot their next moves in the intricate game of power that defined their existence.

The small-hats valued intelligence above all else, and their children were trained from a young age in the arts of manipulation, espionage, and strategic thinking. Education was rigorous, with young small-hats learning to decipher ancient texts, hack into the Great Web, and outmaneuver their rivals. Failure was not tolerated, and those who could not keep up were cast aside, left to fend for themselves in the unforgiving labyrinth of their world.

Yet, within this cutthroat society, there were moments of camaraderie. The small-hats had their own festivals and celebrations, where they would gather in secrecy to honor their cunning ancestors and revel in their collective achievements. These events were filled with cryptic rituals, hidden meanings, and veiled threats—reinforcing the bonds of their community while reminding each other of the ever-present danger of betrayal.

The Inner Circle: The Puppet Masters of the Potatoverse

At the heart of the small-hat society was the Inner Circle—a group of the most powerful and influential members who controlled the fate of their people. The Inner Circle was composed of a select few who had proven themselves through acts of extraordinary cunning, those who had climbed to the top by outwitting their peers and eliminating their enemies.

The leader of the Inner Circle was a figure known only as the Grand Weaver, a small-hat whose identity was shrouded in mystery. The Grand Weaver was said to have an unparalleled understanding of the Potatoverse, able to predict and manipulate events with almost divine precision. Under their guidance, the small-hats sought to bring about the end of the Fifth Sun—not for the sake of rebirth, but to establish their own reign of control over the Sixth Sun.

The Inner Circle’s plans were meticulous, spanning across dimensions and timelines. They planted seeds of dissent in various worlds, corrupted the Cosmic Potato Roots, and orchestrated events that would weaken the Potatoverse’s defenses. Their goal was to seize the power of the synthetic merkaba for themselves, believing it would grant them ultimate dominion over all realities.

A Personal Connection: Tim’s Encounter with a Small-Hat

In his quest to unravel the mysteries of the Potatoverse, Tim Dooley found himself crossing paths with a small-hat named Potaysee. Unlike most of her kind, Potaysee was not entirely loyal to the Grand Weaver. She was an intellectual outcast, fascinated by the potential for harmony rather than chaos. Potaysee's curiosity had led her to question the purpose of her people’s schemes and the true nature of the Potatoverse.

Potaysee’s encounter with Tim was not by chance. She had been watching him, intrigued by his quest for knowledge and his connection to the Great Web. When they finally met, it was in the depths of a forgotten library, where Potaysee revealed to Tim some of the secrets of the small-hats’ plans. She was torn between her loyalty to her people and her desire to see a different future—one where the Potatoverse could thrive without the small-hats’ manipulation.

Their interactions were tense, filled with mutual suspicion and the constant threat of betrayal. Yet, over time, Tim and Potaysee developed a complex relationship—a blend of cautious trust and intellectual partnership. Potaysee’s insights provided Tim with crucial information about the small-hats’ plans, while Tim’s vision of the Potatoverse offered Potaysee a glimpse of a world beyond the shadows.
