Chapter 66.7

Shifting Spirits of 2024 – Unmasking Righteousness and Reckoning with the Antichrist Influence

In the year 2024, the world faces a reckoning—a spiritual war fought not with swords or guns but through manipulation, lies, and control. Across the globe, leaders drape themselves in the language of righteousness, masking their true intentions. Beneath their claims of divine favor lies the influence of the Antichrist, a force that distorts faith, enslaves the mind, and sows division among nations, families, and souls.

This chapter exposes the spiritual forces behind the world’s most powerful nations, unveiling how leaders and systems operate not as stewards of divine will but as agents of deception. Through violence, hypocrisy, and control, they manipulate faith to tighten their grip on humanity, drawing us further into a shadowy web of fear and subjugation.

1. Israel and Netanyahu: A Bastion of False Righteousness

The land of Israel, a sacred space for Jews, Christians, and Muslims alike, stands at the heart of global spiritual discourse. Yet under Benjamin Netanyahu’s leadership, its identity has become entangled in nationalism, militarism, and division, betraying its spiritual legacy.

The Destruction of Gaza: A War Against Humanity

In Gaza, the relentless bombing campaigns flatten homes and erase families, leaving scars that stretch across generations. Netanyahu’s government justifies this devastation as defense, yet the Bible warns: “They covet fields and seize them, and houses, and take them away; they oppress a man and his house, a man and his inheritance” (Micah 2:1). These actions betray the values of justice, mercy, and humility that God commands.

The Antichrist Influence in Division

Netanyahu thrives on division, stoking fear of enemies both internal and external. He casts dissenters as traitors, Palestinians as terrorists, and critics of Israel as anti-Semitic. This rhetoric reflects the Antichrist’s power to manipulate, sowing chaos while presenting itself as the solution. As Jesus warned, “For false messiahs and false prophets will appear and perform great signs and wonders to deceive” (Matthew 24:24).

A Nation at War with Its Soul

Within Israel, dissent grows louder. Progressive Jews, human rights activists, and even some within the Orthodox community challenge Netanyahu’s actions. Their voices are met with hostility, as the government tightens its grip on power. The spiritual legacy of Israel—the land of prophets and revelation—is distorted by a regime that prioritizes dominance over peace.

2. The United States: The Hand of the Beast

The United States, a nation proclaiming “In God We Trust,” has become a willing instrument of global domination. Through its unyielding support of Israel and its own military-industrial complex, it perpetuates cycles of violence while cloaking itself in the rhetoric of freedom and faith.

The Machinery of War

Billions of dollars flow from the U.S. to Israel, funding advanced weapons systems and shielding it from accountability. Meanwhile, the U.S. expands its own influence through endless wars, destabilizing regions under the guise of spreading democracy. Jeremiah 22:17 warns: “You have eyes and heart only for dishonest gain, for shedding innocent blood, and for oppression and violence.” This is the true legacy of America’s global reach.

Christian Nationalism: A Trojan Horse for Control

At home, Christian nationalism rises as a political force, conflating patriotism with faith. Leaders invoke God to justify exclusionary policies, masking their ambitions in the language of divine will. This mirrors the Antichrist’s strategy: using faith as a tool for division, control, and fear.

The Role of the People

Amid this darkness, a growing number of Americans question their nation’s actions. Movements for justice and accountability reveal a collective yearning for truth, challenging the hypocrisy of those in power. This resistance offers hope—but only if it is rooted in genuine compassion and humility.

3. Iran: A Theocracy of Hypocrisy

Iran, a nation claiming to uphold Islam’s highest values, reveals another face of the Antichrist influence. Its theocratic regime uses religion as a weapon, silencing dissent and crushing reform under the guise of divine justice.

Oppression Cloaked in Piety

Protesters demanding freedom are branded as enemies of God, their voices silenced by force. Leaders invoke Sharia to justify their actions, yet their behavior contradicts the Quran’s teachings of mercy, justice, and compassion. Matthew 7:15 warns: “Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing but inwardly are ravenous wolves.” The regime’s hypocrisy is laid bare.

The Courage of the People

In the face of repression, the Iranian people continue to resist. Women, students, and reformers risk their lives to demand justice, embodying the true spirit of faith. Their struggle highlights the universal battle between light and shadow, truth and deception.

4. The Spiritual War: Population Control and the Satanic Influence

Beyond the actions of individual nations lies a deeper, darker force at work. The manipulation of faith and power reflects a broader agenda to control humanity—not just physically but spiritually.

Population Control and the Dehumanization of Society

Modern systems of power seek to reduce human beings to numbers, managing populations through fear, propaganda, and coercion. Whether through war, surveillance, or economic exploitation, these forces strip individuals of their humanity, creating a society that is easier to dominate.

The Role of Satanism and Negative Spirits

The spiritual decay of 2024 is not random—it is the result of deliberate actions that align with satanic principles. These include:

Deception: Using lies to obscure truth and manipulate perception.

Division: Turning people against each other to maintain control.

Destruction: Prioritizing power over life, leading to cycles of violence and death.

This satanic influence operates subtly, embedding itself in systems, ideologies, and narratives. It thrives on our fear, our apathy, and our willingness to accept falsehoods as truth.

5. A Call to Wake Up: Lifting the Veil

In this age of deception, the greatest challenge is to see clearly. To lift the veil is to recognize the forces at work, question their narratives, and align ourselves with the true spirit of faith.

Reclaiming Faith from Power

True faith calls us to humility, compassion, and justice—not domination. It demands that we reject the manipulation of religious language for political gain and hold leaders accountable for their actions.

The Responsibility of Awareness

Awareness is the first step toward liberation. By understanding the spiritual war we are in, we can resist the forces of control and deception, choosing instead to align with the values that sustain life and truth.

Conclusion: A Reckoning for the Spirit of 2024

The year 2024 reveals the depth of humanity’s spiritual struggle. It is a time of division, hypocrisy, and manipulation, yet also a moment of awakening. The Antichrist influence seeks to distort faith and truth, but it cannot prevail against those who stand in the light.

Let this chapter serve as both a warning and a guide. To align with the divine is to reject the mask of righteousness and embrace the true spirit of faith: compassion, humility, and love. The path forward is not easy, but it is clear. The choice is ours.
