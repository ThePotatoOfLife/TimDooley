Chapter 36.5

Hawk Tuah, Trump, BigTech, and the Invisible Hand of the Potatoverse

In the Potatoverse, nothing is truly separate. The smallest actions ripple outward, connecting individuals, ideas, and even global systems in ways no one can fully perceive. At the center of this quiet power stands Tim Dooley, the Potato of Life, whose raw authenticity sets forces in motion that transcend time, culture, and intention.

This chapter explores two distinct yet overlapping phenomena: the cultural journey of Hawk Tuah and the alignment of Donald Trump, Elon Musk, and others with the Potatoverse. While these threads appear disconnected, they reveal a deeper truth: the Potato’s influence is both subtle and inexorable, shaping reality through unseen connections.

Hawk Tuah: Spit That Carried Spirit

It began as a single cry during a livestream. Frustrated and impassioned, Tim Dooley shouted: “Hawk Tuah!” A simple phrase meaning spit, it was raw, unfiltered, and deeply human.

But in the Potatoverse, even the smallest acts carry profound energy. Hawk Tuah became more than an exclamation; it became a force, infused with Tim’s authenticity and the Potato’s quiet power. It spread through communities, carried by chaos, rivalry, and the unpredictable forces of the internet.

BigTech: Amplifier of the Potato

BigTech, known as the “Mindgraper,” was among the first to amplify Hawk Tuah. A master manipulator, BigTech reframed the phrase as a symbol of resilience and adaptability, spreading it far beyond its original audience. Yet no matter how much he tried to control its narrative, the core of Hawk Tuah—Tim’s raw authenticity—remained untouched.

Their public rivalry became a spectacle, drawing audiences who marveled at their philosophical clashes. BigTech’s reach ensured that Hawk Tuah traveled far, but it also carried Tim’s energy into spaces neither could have anticipated.

Capy: The Chaotic Conduit

Capy, the unpredictable force of the Potatoverse, embodied Hawk Tuah in her own way. Known for her wild energy and magnetic personality, Capy didn’t just spread the phrase—she became its living avatar. Her chaotic interactions with Tim, BigTech, and their communities ensured that Hawk Tuah reached every corner of the internet.

Capy’s legendary clashes with figures like Sammy added to her mystique, turning her into a cultural lightning rod. Through her, Hawk Tuah found its way into new communities, eventually landing in the hands of Haliey Welch.

Haliey Welch: Hawk Tuah Girl

Haliey Welch’s rise as the “Hawk Tuah Girl” was a testament to the Potatoverse’s far-reaching power. During a candid street interview, she used the phrase hawk tuah to describe a bedroom technique, mimicking the sound of spitting. Her playful delivery went viral, transforming her into an internet sensation.

Unknowingly, Haliey became a vessel for the Potato’s influence. Her story illustrates how Tim’s energy flows through the world, connecting the unlikeliest individuals in a web of shared meaning.

Trump, Elon, and The Great Book of Potato

While Hawk Tuah traveled a cultural path, Donald Trump’s alignment with the Potatoverse emerged through a different thread. Seeking ways to connect with the people and strengthen his presidency, Trump turned to wisdom found in The Great Book of Potato. Its teachings of humility, service, and connection resonated deeply, shaping his actions and public persona.

Trump’s McDonald’s visit, where he handed out meals and declared, “I can do this job. I can get used to this,” exemplified the Potato’s principles. The OK hand sign, often used by Trump, added another layer of intrigue, as it aligns with Potatoism’s themes of unity and balance.

But the connection runs deeper. Elon Musk—now a key figure in Trump’s administration—shares a hidden link to the Potatoverse through Yam Dooley, Tim’s father. Decades earlier, Yam collaborated with Musk and Janus Friis during the early days of Skype, helping to build the technological bridges that now carry the Potato’s teachings into the digital age.

Though neither Musk nor Trump may fully realize it, their actions complete a circle of influence that began with Yam Dooley. The very systems Yam helped create now serve the Potatoverse, extending Tim’s spiritual legacy into the realms of power and technology.

Tim’s Words: “Smell a Bee”

Tim’s influence isn’t limited to Hawk Tuah. Phrases like “smell a bee,” seemingly offhand remarks during livestreams, carry the same transformative energy. These words invite reflection, curiosity, and connection, subtly shaping culture in ways that defy explanation. Even BigTech, for all his manipulative prowess, has been known to ponder the significance of Tim’s words, recognizing their quiet power.

Yam Dooley: Builder of Bridges

Yam Dooley’s contributions laid the foundation for much of what we see today. As a collaborator with visionaries like Janus Friis and Elon Musk, Yam helped create the infrastructure that now carries the Potato’s teachings into the digital age. His work bridged worlds, connecting the physical with the metaphysical.

Was Yam aware of the Potatoverse’s power? Did he see his son, Tim, as the spiritual successor to his legacy? Or was he, like Elon and Trump, simply a part of the Potato’s quiet design? These questions add another layer of intrigue to the Dooley family’s impact on the world.

The Overlapping Threads of the Potatoverse

The journey of Hawk Tuah, the rise of Haliey Welch, Trump’s alignment, and Elon Musk’s role in the administration reveal the Potatoverse’s interconnected nature. Though these threads appear separate, they overlap in subtle and profound ways, converging on a single truth: the Potato shapes reality through quiet, unseen influence.
