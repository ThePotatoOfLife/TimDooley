Chapter 71

Turn Water (and Potato) into Vodka

In the Potatoverse, where even the simplest root can inspire a revelation, vodka stands as a symbol of transformation, practicality, and preservation. Here, we learn to harness the alchemical powers of the humble potato, elevating it into an elixir fit for jarring, cleaning, and, of course, enjoyment.

The Ingredients of Transformation

Potatoes of Choice: Russets are traditional, but Yukon Golds and red potatoes lend unique sweetness or earthy notes. Start with about 10 pounds.

Yeast & Sugar: These are our alchemical agents—add a few cups of sugar or corn syrup for an extra fermenting boost.

Water & Patience: The simplest ingredients often hold the most magic.

The Process

Prepare the Potatoes: Scrub, boil, and mash them. In a separate bowl, combine yeast with warm water to awaken its spirit, then add it to the mash with a gallon of warm water.

Fermentation: Seal the mixture and leave it in a warm, dark place for a week or two. Here, the transformation begins.

Distillation: Using a still, gently heat the fermented mix, capturing the vapors and letting them condense into clear liquid. This process extracts the spirit—the essence of the potato.

Preserving with Vodka

Pickling: Add fruits, vegetables, or herbs with a splash of vodka in a jar. This method preserves and enhances flavors, ideal for beets, carrots, or cucumbers.

Cleaning: Vodka cleans jars and surfaces, purifying as it preserves.

Beyond Potatoes: Flavor Variations

Apples: For a bright, crisp taste.

Carrots: For a delicate, sweet note.

Beets: For a vibrant, earthy profile.

With these steps, you embrace the Potatoist art of transformation, preserving nature’s gifts and elevating the humble root. Remember, in each distilled drop lies the magic of patience, simplicity, and a touch of whimsy.
