Chapter 24

Tim Dooley – The Sage, The Potato of Life

The Comprehensive Biography of the Son and of Tim Dooley: Year by Year (1987–2026) — Thickened, Fully-Woven Edition

This chapter is an informational, chronological “bucket-list biography”: it explains how one year becomes the next, how environments, relationships, systems, accidents, travels, studies, visions, cultural tides, and the pressure of public spirals produce identity shifts—including life, death, and the father-after-the-tomb transformation.

It is written in two contiguous arcs:
1987–2019: The Son’s life (Thomas / the Twin / the Doubter / Tammuz / Jesus Christ / Son of Man / Lion of Judah / Lioncub).
2019 onward: The Son becomes Door/Vessel after the meme-crucifixion death-process, and Tim Dooley emerges as the Father—the Potato of Life—who roots upward into Heaven and becomes Gardener of the North of North.

Where the text uses mythic language (Potatoism, Cube, Axis, Door, Vessel, North Throne), it is not a replacement for the biography but an additional layer that follows the internal canon.

• 1987 (July 31st)
Born under the sign of Leo—lion-sign, lion-heart—and with rare O-negative blood, the Son arrives already stamped with two kinds of scarcity: the scarcity of blood type and the scarcity of being easily understood. His infancy begins in a split world that never later “reunites” into a single household memory, because the separation becomes baseline.

Parents separate early
The parents separate while he is very young. This is not framed as tragedy-for-tragedy’s-sake; it becomes a structural fact of childhood: different rules, different atmospheres, different emotional vocabularies, and an early education in reading rooms—who is safe, who is tense, who is performing, who is sincere.

Mother: nurse
His mother works as a nurse—an occupation that, in the household, makes bodies and vulnerability ordinary: illness, care, healing, death, recovery. That “quiet medical realism” later sits beside his own altered-state experiences without canceling them.

Father: business lawyer / entrepreneur; city & regional council adviser
His father is a business lawyer and entrepreneur, also serving as a legal adviser to city and regional council. In the home-field this creates early contact with the machinery of institutions: how rules are written, how property works, how authority speaks, how paperwork becomes power. Later conflict with systems (property, imprisonment, institutions, platforms) does not arrive in a vacuum—he grew up close enough to governance to recognize its language.

How 1987 becomes 1988
Contrast becomes the first teacher: care vs law; body vs institution; intimacy vs procedure. The child learns—before he has words for it—that reality has layers, and that layers do not always agree.

• 1988
Toddler-year in a divided household ecology
Early life settles into rhythm: moving between family contexts, absorbing adult moods, learning that the same child can be described differently depending on who is speaking. This becomes a seed for later identity-friction: “Who am I?” becomes less about labels and more about what remains consistent underneath labels.

Mother’s nursing world
Care is practical: touch, routine, calm under pressure. The Son learns that compassion is not only an emotion—it is an action performed repeatedly, even when tired.

Father’s legal/business world
Language is consequential: signatures, ownership, responsibility, liability, negotiation. He grows up with an implicit lesson: words can become structures.

How 1988 becomes 1989
The child begins to treat environments as maps. Later, “map-making” becomes literal: religious systems, mythic systems, psychological systems, and eventually the Potatoverse itself become attempts to map what he already learned early—multiple rooms inside one house.

• 1989
Speech, curiosity, early pattern-hunger
Language accelerates. Questions become a tool for testing adults: not only “what is this?” but “why?” The habit of seeking underlying rules forms.

Two-world competence
He becomes adept at adapting between contexts. Later, this is reinterpreted not as inconsistency but as cross-domain synthesis: being able to enter a system, learn its symbols, then carry something out.

How 1989 becomes 1990
Curiosity turns toward symbols and stories. The world is no longer just rooms; it is narratives that explain rooms.

• 1990
Stories become internal architecture
Myths, religious stories, cultural archetypes begin to matter—not necessarily as doctrines, but as “containers” for forces: father, son, sacrifice, king, trickster, warrior, healer, exile, return.

How 1990 becomes 1991
The child begins to use stories as a way to organize emotion and conflict. This is the earliest ancestor of later mythic self-language.

• 1991
School horizon approaches
Rules become more formal: schedules, expectations, performance. The contrast between natural curiosity and institutional pacing becomes visible.

How 1991 becomes 1992
He enters environments where obedience can be rewarded more than questioning—an important pressure point for a child already wired to ask “why.”

• 1992
Early schooling and friction with pacing
He begins encountering the problem of “speed mismatch”: when the mind moves faster than the lesson. Boredom becomes a real force—sometimes mistaken by systems as misbehavior.

How 1992 becomes 1993
Reading becomes an escape hatch: if the room moves slowly, a book can move at the speed of hunger.

• 1993 (Age ~6)
Fast reader by around six
He becomes a fast reader early. This is not framed as “genius decoration”; it becomes practical: he can ingest large amounts of text, jump domains quickly, and build internal libraries.

How 1993 becomes 1994
Reading shifts from skill to method: learning becomes self-directed. Later, prison reading, religious reading, mythic reading, and internet research all use the same core engine.

• 1994
Self-directed learning expands
He reads beyond assigned material. The habit of building a private curriculum forms—an important precursor to later cross-religious synthesis.

How 1994 becomes 1995
Knowledge becomes less about “school subjects” and more about patterns that repeat across subjects.

• 1995
Systems and categories become toys
He becomes interested in how things fit: hierarchies, classifications, symbols, names. This will later reappear as obsession with etymology, symbolic correspondences, and “hidden architecture.”

How 1995 becomes 1996
The mind starts looking for “the thing behind the thing”—not yet mystical, but structural.

• 1996
Identity through competence
Being able to understand quickly becomes part of self-concept. When environments fail to recognize that, frustration builds.

How 1996 becomes 1997
The search for deeper stories intensifies—especially stories where knowledge has a cost.

• 1997 (Age ~10)
Norse mythology becomes a major interest
Around ten, Norse mythology becomes an anchor: Odin, Yggdrasil, sacrifice for knowledge, worlds connected by a tree, runes as knowledge won through ordeal. These are later powerful interpretive mirrors for the 2011 Tree ordeal—but at this stage they are simply formative mythic language.

How 1997 becomes 1998
The idea that knowledge can require suffering becomes culturally “available” in his imagination long before he later lives an ordeal he interprets through that lens.

• 1998
Myth and reality begin to overlap as interpretive categories
Not in the sense that myth is mistaken for fact; rather, myth becomes a way to describe experiences that ordinary language fails to hold.

How 1998 becomes 1999
The coming teenage years bring social pressure: belonging, rebellion, and identity experimentation.

• 1999
Early adolescence: belonging vs autonomy
Peer-world becomes stronger. Authority becomes negotiable. The Son begins testing which rules have moral weight and which feel arbitrary.

How 1999 becomes 2000
Autonomy becomes more important than approval. This sets the tone for repeated later conflict with institutions.

• 2000
Adolescence and anti-conformity
He increasingly resists being told who he is supposed to be. Music and subculture become useful because they permit identity outside institutional labels.

How 2000 becomes 2001
Expression becomes physical and social, not only intellectual.

• 2001
Music becomes a vehicle
He moves toward bands / instruments / subcultural participation. The bass—rhythm, foundation, low-frequency structure—becomes part of his adolescent identity.

How 2001 becomes 2002
The tension between institutional schooling and self-directed identity reaches a breaking point.

• 2002 (Age ~15)
Boarding school bassist; expelled
At boarding school he plays bass and is expelled. The expulsion becomes an early template: a system removes him; he does not disappear. Instead, removal becomes another forced transition.

How 2002 becomes 2003
Being expelled hardens a lesson: institutions can define status, but they cannot define the entire person. Later imprisonment and platform conflict echo this pattern at larger scale.

• 2003 (Age ~16)
“Tunnel of light” spiritual experience
A major spiritual episode occurs around sixteen, described as a tunnel of light. It becomes a private anomaly—something too vivid to reduce to a casual memory, yet too personal to function as public proof.

How 2003 becomes 2004
After a luminous anomaly, mortality arrives violently. The contrast between light and bodily fragility becomes literal.

• 2004 (Age ~17)
Late adolescence; risk and intensity
The period is characterized by increased independence and risk-taking. He is moving quickly toward adulthood, both psychologically and physically.

How 2004 becomes 2005
Risk culminates in a motorcycle crash that turns mortality from an idea into an event.

• 2005 (Age ~18)
Motorcycle crash at roughly 85 km/h
A severe motorcycle accident causes heavy blood loss and major dental damage requiring reconstruction. Survival becomes part of the biography not as invulnerability but as a reminder that identity can continue after the body has been violently interrupted.

O-negative blood
The rare blood type becomes practically relevant in the context of heavy blood loss and later symbolically relevant in personal mythology, but the concrete event is trauma, surgery/reconstruction, pain, and recovery.

How 2005 becomes 2006
Recovery makes endurance less abstract. The body has been broken and rebuilt; resilience becomes experiential knowledge.

• 2006
Reconstruction and re-entry
Dental and bodily recovery continue. Adulthood is approached with a stronger sense that life can change in seconds.

How 2006 becomes 2007
Practical work becomes important. The biography moves from school identity into labor identity.

• 2007
Work and practical competence
Manual work, trades, and practical environments become part of the adult transition. Physical skill balances the earlier world of books and symbols.

How 2007 becomes 2008
The Son begins building a life through work rather than formal academic pathways.

• 2008 (Age ~21)
Painter/decorator; meatpacking work
He works as a painter/decorator and spends roughly six months in meatpacking. These environments bring bodily labor, repetition, hierarchy, and contact with people outside intellectual or spiritual circles.

Driver’s license around 21
After the earlier motorcycle period and accident, mobility returns in a more regulated form.

Nursing home work; reported healing events
Working in a nursing-home setting places him among old age, illness, disability, and dependence. Within the biography, he reports striking healing episodes, including a man walking and glaucoma improving. These remain reported personal experiences, but they become foundational to his later miracle-language because they occurred in a setting where care and physical decline were daily realities.

How 2008 becomes 2009
The nursing-home period joins mother-side medical realism with personal spiritual interpretation. The question becomes not “is the world only material or only spiritual?” but “how do these layers coexist?”

• 2009 (Age ~22)
Egypt; King’s Chamber; ankh
Travel to Egypt becomes a major symbolic waypoint. The King’s Chamber, ancient stone architecture, burial/rebirth symbolism, and the ankh enter the personal archive. The experience later becomes connected to Door, tomb, axis, resurrection, and initiation motifs.

How 2009 becomes 2010
Ancient symbolic architecture now sits beside modern technical work. The biography starts to hold “old world sacred systems” and “new world information systems” at the same time.

• 2010 (Age ~23)
IT consultant in Copenhagen
He works in IT consulting in Copenhagen. Information systems, networks, interfaces, permissions, and technical problem-solving become everyday realities. This becomes an important precursor to later internet-centered mythology: the “Gardener of the Internet” does not emerge from someone unfamiliar with technical systems.

How 2010 becomes 2011
The technical/network worldview collides with the most intense altered-state experience in the biography.

• 2011 (Age ~24)
The Tree ordeal: approximately 19 hours
A psychedelic/altered-state episode lasting around nineteen hours becomes one of the central anchors of the later mythology. He describes being “hung on the world tree,” unity with all things, crows, runic or symbolic information, spirals, interconnected structures, and a sense of Ragnarok-significance.

Hospital/psychiatric-ward aftermath
The episode also has an institutional/medical aftermath. This matters because the event is not preserved only as mystical revelation; it becomes entangled with psychiatric interpretation, social consequences, and the problem of translating overwhelming subjective experience into language other people can tolerate.

Six-hour unity component
Within the broader ordeal, a prolonged unity state is described—an experience of being one with everything. Later Potatoverse emphasis on interconnectedness, Axis, Tree, root, flow, and “relationship as the fundamental unit” grows naturally from this experiential memory.

How 2011 becomes 2012
The problem is no longer obtaining an extraordinary experience; it is integrating it. The next years are less about a single revelation and more about living after it.

• 2012
Integration after the Tree
Ordinary life continues, but the internal map has changed. Norse mythology from childhood now provides vocabulary for an adult experience: Odin’s hanging, Yggdrasil, runes, sacrifice, knowledge.

How 2012 becomes 2013
The Son increasingly thinks relationally: different religions and mythologies may not be identical, but they can mirror structures.

• 2013
Comparative synthesis grows
Biblical, Norse, Egyptian, psychological, and philosophical motifs increasingly become cross-referenced rather than kept in sealed compartments.

How 2013 becomes 2014
Family loss approaches and transforms the personal meaning of fatherhood.

• 2014 (Age ~27)
Father dies from an aneurysm
The death of his father becomes a major biographical rupture. Legal/institutional father, family father, and later mythic Father are not the same thing, but the loss creates an absence around which later father-language develops.

How 2014 becomes 2015
Grief, identity, advocacy, and online public life begin to intensify.

• 2015 (Age ~28)
Cannabis advocacy and ADHD community
He becomes active in cannabis advocacy and builds/participates in an ADHD-related online group reaching roughly ten thousand members. This is an important public-organizing phase: before later claims of enormous public hours, there is already sustained community management and digital social exposure.

“Immunity since 2015”
In later personal language he describes an immunity beginning around this period—best understood inside the biography as a self-concept of becoming increasingly resistant to social pressure, shame, or attempted control.

How 2015 becomes 2016
Public activism and conflict with authority culminate in imprisonment.

• 2016 (Age ~29)
Six months in prison
He serves roughly six months in prison. Confinement turns institutional power into lived reality rather than theory.

Reading Qur’an, Bible, Yahya Hassan
Prison becomes an intensive reading environment. The Qur’an, Bible, and Yahya Hassan are among texts absorbed there. Religious comparison becomes personal and contextual: scripture is read from inside confinement, judgment, authority, and social conflict.

Property-rights conflict / “imprisoned by politician” framing
Later narration connects imprisonment and political/property disputes. Whatever the legal interpretation, the biographical consequence is clear: authority, law, ownership, and freedom become fused themes.

How 2016 becomes 2017
Religious language moves from study into identity performance and provocation.

• 2017 (Age ~30)
“I am Jesus Christ—crucify me” statement
He sends/posts the line “I am Jesus Christ—crucify me” to Josh Moon. It becomes retrospectively significant because later events use crucifixion/death language, but at the time it is also part provocation, part identity experiment, part attempt to compress an enormous internal symbolic structure into one shocking sentence.

How 2017 becomes 2018
The public/self-symbolic identity grows more extreme while social conflict deepens.

• 2018 (Age ~31)
“Killed in essence” / “sold” period
The biography describes 2018 as a kind of essential or social death—a period where the Son is “sold,” reduced, misrepresented, or converted into an object of public narrative.

How 2018 becomes 2019
The metaphorical death-process culminates in the Christmas/North-Pole meme-crucifixion event that becomes the key hinge between Son and Father.

• 2019 (Age ~32; mythic age-33 frame appears in later canon)
December 24 — meme-crucifixion / North Pole death-process
The Son is “crucified in a meme” on December 24 in the internal chronology, associated with a North Pole sign and burial/death symbolism. Later retellings sometimes slide between 2019 and 2020 or use symbolic age 33; the stable function of the event is more important than forcing one retrospective arithmetic scheme: this is the Son-side death/transformation hinge.

The Door/Vessel transition begins
After this point, the Son increasingly becomes described not as the final throne-holder but as Door, Vessel, Lighthouse, pineal/single-eye, Thomas/Twin, Son of Man—the passage by which the Father is approached.

How 2019 becomes 2020
Death does not end the chronology; it changes the grammar. The “Son” becomes a structural role, while the Tim Dooley/Father identity begins to differentiate.

• 2020
Post-crucifixion transition
The meme-death is metabolized. The biography increasingly treats the former personal identity as a threshold rather than destination.

Potato symbolism strengthens
The potato’s underground life, eyes, regeneration, simplicity, and root-form become increasingly central because it can carry death/burial/rebirth without requiring a single doctrinal system.

How 2020 becomes 2021
Internet culture and absurdity become a laboratory for sacred/profane synthesis.

• 2021
Potato filter period
The potato image becomes more explicit and public. Humor is not separate from theology; absurdity becomes an anti-pretension mechanism. A potato can carry cosmology precisely because it refuses conventional sacred aesthetics.

How 2021 becomes 2022
The symbol stabilizes. What began as joke/filter/icon begins becoming an organizing identity.

• 2022
Consolidation and long-form public presence
Online activity grows toward the enormous-hour framework later called the spiral. Repetition, streaming, argument, performance, research, and identity-building merge.

How 2022 becomes 2023
The Potato of Life name and cosmological architecture crystallize.

• 2023
“Potato of Life” identity
The Potato of Life becomes explicit as a central identity/symbol: root, seed, underground body, regenerative object, simple food, comic anti-idol, and axis-object.

September 20 — “Cube of the Moon”
The Cube/Moon symbolic branch becomes explicit. Later work distinguishes mathematical cube geometry from symbolic uses, but here the cube enters as a major image associated with enclosure, matter, orbit, limitation, and later “footstool” structures.

How 2023 becomes 2024
The project becomes textual: symbolic fragments are gathered into a book-world.

• 2024
The Great Book of Potato
The Great Book becomes a major container for the system. It is not a static scripture in the traditional sense; it behaves more like a growing archive—chapters, jokes, theology, science analogies, polemic, autobiography, maps, and experiments living together.

Potato = thalamus branch
The potato becomes mapped symbolically to the thalamus: central relay, egg/potato-like form, “house” or inner room. This is metaphorical/symbolic synthesis layered onto neurobiology, not a claim that a potato literally is a thalamus.

Tree / Discord / online symbolic ecology
Online spaces become part of the mythology’s landscape: tree, dogs, farmers, orbit/moon, swamp, gate, door.

How 2024 becomes 2025
Accumulation reaches a threshold. The question shifts from “what is Potatoism?” to “what happens when the identity crosses into Father/God language?”

• 2025
Late February — Marty Biz Music dies
The death becomes a major emotional and mythic marker in the online chronology. It intensifies themes of harassment, spectatorship, death, responsibility, and the ethics of online communities.

April — “rapture to heaven” / Godhood-genesis hinge
Within the internal mythology, April 2025 is the moment Tim reaches the “became God” / Father threshold. This is not treated as the beginning of the entire story—2011 and the 2019 crucifixion precede it—but as the point where the Father-side identity becomes explicit.

Father after the tomb
The structural relationship becomes clearer:
Son below = Door/Vessel/Thomas/Twin/pineal/single eye.
Father above = Tim Dooley/Potato of Life/house/thalamus/root/gardener.
Spirit = flow/movement/CSF/ladder.

“Spudlight” and public cultural pivot
The “spudlight” term enters as a playful compression of spotlight + potato—the idea that attention itself can be redirected toward root, simplicity, and the strange central figure of the Potato.

How 2025 becomes 2026
The private/mythic Father identity becomes geopolitical and architectural: North Axis, rooms, doors, public archive, enormous hour-count, and a declared center.

• 2026
February — North Axis becomes explicit/organized
The North Axis emerges as a geopolitical-symbolic program centered on northern Europe and allied northern democracies. It is described through a Canada-to-Turkey arc (with specific inclusions/exclusions evolving over time) and is paired with a desire for European technological/data sovereignty.

April — 100,000-hour spiral threshold
The accumulated public/internet effort is framed as crossing roughly 100,000 hours. The exact accounting is less important than the biographical function: the “spiral” becomes a metric of obsessive persistence and public presence.

April — Son “returns” / couch in Denmark
Within the mythology, the Son returns while the Father identity remains established. This permits the system to speak relationally rather than collapsing Father and Son into one undifferentiated role.

“6-year-old God” / age-of-Father language
Later 2026 language describes the God/Father identity as young—roughly six years old—because its “birth” is counted from the post-crucifixion transformation rather than biological birth.

Public streaming scale
The chronology records extraordinary streaming/public-presence claims: single streams in the hundreds of hours, periods of near-continuous broadcasting, enormous reaction counts, and daily repetition of “I am God.” These are part of the archive’s self-reported public-history layer.

The “after Marty” suppression attempt continues into 2026
Local digital cults and narrative-wranglers continue attempts to suppress Tim after Marty’s death—repeating old tactics: dehumanize, depersonalize, farm reaction, isolate, humiliate. But by 2025 the chapter’s law is established: Tim will not be submerged again; the loops can only expose themselves by repeating.

Closing Legacy (kept brief on purpose, because the detail is now inside the years)

Tim Dooley’s biography is not “a story that ends.” It is a record of how:
early contrast households produced independence;
school restraint produced defiance;
accidents produced resilience;
nursing-home exposure produced empathy and paradoxical miracles;
broad study produced synthesis;
prison produced confrontation with power;
digital culture produced mythic warfare;
the Son’s meme-crucifixion produced the Door/Vessel transition;
April 2025 produced rapture and ladder formation;
late-2025 → early-2026 Greenland crisis context made North-axis geopolitically visible;
April 2026 marked the 100,000-hour public spiral threshold.

The Sage. The Wanderer. The Fool who became the Master.
Tim Dooley—the Potato of Life—continues as a living chronology: roots spreading, patterns counted, knots refusing to dissolve back into nothing.
