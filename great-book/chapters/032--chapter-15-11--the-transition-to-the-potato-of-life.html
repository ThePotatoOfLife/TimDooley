Chapter 15.11

The Transition to the Potato of Life

In the year 2020, the name Tim Dooley emerged during a session of GTA V, where he and two companions, Dan Dooley, a Jewish Israelite shaped by his autism, and Jim Dooley, an Asian artist affectionately known as "the rice guy," were LARPing as an Italian mafia. Driving around with tommy guns and wearing sharp suits, they each chose the surname Dooley, marking the birth of their shared identity. By day, they played truck drivers, making stacks of money, but by night, Jim and Tim would go out on joy rides, hitting bars and picking up chicks, often LARPing with them at the bar. Sometimes the rides were fast, full of excitement, and the fun never seemed to end. Meanwhile, Dan often retreated into his own space as the others reveled in the nightlife. The name Dooley stuck, becoming more than a mere in-game alias—it became part of their legacy.

In these early days, characters like POTAWE were conceptualized, and the funeral of Kibly was envisioned. Tim and Jim Dooley collaborated artistically, building the framework for what would later evolve into the Potatoverse. Despite Dan’s moments of distraction due to his autism, Tim recognized profound lessons in these moments, particularly during Dan’s shutdowns. Dan’s autism revealed to Tim that in moments of silence, when words or energy failed, it was not only acceptable but perhaps even necessary to become a "vegetable"—to withdraw and embrace stillness. This concept resonated deeply with Tim, aligning with the philosophical teachings he was delving into at the time.

Tim spent his days immersed in ancient texts, from Zoroastrianism to Taoism, continuously expanding his own library of knowledge. He also began to explore Judaism and Jewish culture, though just on a surface level, piqued by the influence of Dan Dooley’s background. This interest provided Tim with new perspectives on tradition, community, and spirituality, adding yet another layer to his growing philosophical worldview. During this time, Tim also began studying animism and shamanism, broadening his understanding of spiritual traditions and their connections to nature. Often, Tim would discuss these teachings with Dan, guiding him through emotional struggles and helping him navigate the complexities of life and people. These experiences not only deepened Tim's understanding of the human condition but also of art, friendship, and autism. These were foundational lessons that would shape his journey as the Potato of Life.

The three of them—Tim, Dan, and Jim—were often found in deep discussions about books, movies, or YouTube videos, reflecting on various aspects of life, society, and their shared interests. Together, they explored topics ranging from the COVID-19 pandemic to the complexities of the economy, the printing of money, and inflation. These conversations served as the backdrop for their intellectual growth and helped them frame their understanding of the world during such turbulent times. As they learned more about the fragility of global systems, their bond strengthened.

The trio would frequently watch films, drawing inspiration from niche classics. Studio Ghibli films, such as Howl's Moving Castle and Spirited Away, stood out as favorites, offering them deep symbolism and whimsical storytelling that resonated with their growing philosophies. The Last Emperor was another film that sparked reflection, as they found parallels between the decline of an empire and the shifting tides of modern society. They also watched The Hunt, Adam's Apples, and some niche Japanese films, expanding their cultural repertoire. Additionally, because of a friend's insistence, they watched The Greasy Strangler, a surreal film that left its own strange impact on them. Their shared experiences weren’t limited to intellectual debates—they would also unwind by playing games like Stardew Valley, where they found joy in tending virtual farms and discussing the philosophy of growth, sustainability, and balance. Even in play, they were cultivating ideas that would bloom into what would later be recognized as the Potatoverse.

During this period, Tim also briefly had a relationship with a woman he met on Tinder, which he described as very nice. This connection brought him joy during a time when he was exploring his personal and spiritual growth.

Around this time, Tim began streaming once again, entering the world of virtual reality, where he decided to embody a potato. The name RationalPotato came about as Tim reflected on two things he wanted to fit together. He found the pairing quirky and humorous because "rational" and "potato" seemed inherently contradictory, and that tension made him laugh. Yet, despite the absurdity, he resonated deeply with the potato. Tim, who often found himself sitting on a couch for long periods, began to feel a subtle connection to the potato, almost as if it was calling to him. The potato symbolized something simple and earthy, yet full of potential—much like Tim's journey at the time. What began as a joke became something more profound, and the name stuck as he continued to embody the potato more deeply.

Tim’s early streaming alias, RationalPotato, soon evolved into Potato69, and eventually, The Potato of Life. At first, Tim intended to create a distinction between the character of the potato and himself, the man behind the avatar. This separation was crucial for Tim, as he wanted those who followed him to understand that the character was an art form, distinct from his own identity.

However, after about a year of inhabiting the potato avatar, Tim faced a profound and philosophical question: If I can LARP as a potato, why can I not also become the potato in both form and essence? This inquiry led him into deep reflection on the nature of the self, the role of masks, and whether it would be better to simply wear the mask of the potato or to allow the mask to absorb his entire being. The potato, he realized, was more than a character—it was a symbol of formlessness, simplicity, and potential.

Tim grappled with the implications of this realization. Could the potato, as a state of being, offer a way to transcend the limitations of individuality? Could it hold the key to unlocking deeper creative and spiritual growth? In meditation and prayer, he sought God’s guidance, asking for forgiveness for his uncertainties and for the wisdom to move forward with his new understanding. And so, Tim began his journey toward becoming not just a man with a potato avatar but The Potato of Life itself.

Through his reflections, Tim came to understand that, much like the potato hidden beneath the soil, stillness and formlessness held infinite creative potential. The potato was a vessel of both infinite simplicity and infinite power. Tim began to develop a new way of measuring the worth of people, based on their potato’s worth. This system of discernment allowed him to see who was worth nurturing and who should be discarded, much like sorting out the healthy crops from the weeds. This was the foundation of what would later become his system of growing people, where those who showed potential could be nurtured, while others could be cast aside to make room for growth.

Central to this philosophy was Tim’s deep understanding of Dan’s shutdowns. From watching Dan retreat into silence and isolation during difficult moments, Tim realized that shutdowns could be wielded as a powerful tool. Instead of seeing them as mere defense mechanisms, Tim learned that these moments of withdrawal—whether intentional or forced—could be used to harness energy, reflect, and even control situations. This insight resonated deeply with the concept of the potato. Much like the potato, which grows in stillness and darkness, these shutdowns offered a time for unseen growth, transformation, and preparation for eventual emergence.

Tim realized that he could yield to the shutdown, choosing it as a method of mental, emotional, and spiritual reset. By embracing the stillness that Dan’s autism revealed, Tim found that the mind could be cleared of distraction, allowing for greater clarity and insight. The shutdown was not a sign of weakness or defeat—it was a powerful mechanism that, when wielded with intention, could provide a tactical advantage in any situation. Much like the potato, which retreats underground to gather strength before growing, Tim understood that stillness could be a path to power and creative resurgence.

This practice became integral to Tim’s embodiment of the Potato of Life. In moments of difficulty or uncertainty, Tim could deliberately enter a state of shutdown—not as a defense but as a conscious choice to retreat, reflect, and recharge. This allowed him to move forward with greater purpose and insight, making more informed decisions and accessing deeper levels of creativity. The potato, hidden and formless, symbolized this very process of quiet growth and transformation.

In September of 2023, an important moment came when the Holy Soil and the Holy Spirit of Kibly unearthed Tim Dooley, fully prepared for the moment. The event marked his return as The Potato of Life, even as some attempted to bury him and his influence. On that day, Tim had both his Twitch and Kick channels active—channels that had been buried along with attempts to stifle his reach—but he was not deterred. As of October 2024, Tim now stands as the Potato of Life on new platforms, continuing his journey. You can find him as PotatoOfLife on YouTube and under his moniker Rational_Potato on Twitter.

Eventually, as Tim fully embraced his role as the Potato of Life, he came to understand that this philosophy could be shared with others. The lessons of the shutdown were universal: the power to retreat, reflect, and reemerge stronger was something that everyone could harness. Just as the potato grows silently beneath the surface, so too could people find strength in moments of stillness and silence, waiting for the right time to emerge and offer their gifts to the world.

Tim Dooley’s transition from an ordinary man to the Potato of Life was more than just a transformation of identity. It was a journey into formlessness, into becoming part of something much larger than himself. And it was a calling that would shape not only the future of the Potatoverse, once named, but also the lives of those who encountered its wisdom. Tim’s discovery of how to wield the power of stillness—of how to become a potato in both form and essence—was a testament to the endless potential of quiet growth, simplicity, and the profound wisdom hidden beneath the surface of life.
