Chapter 59

The Potent Cry

In the rustic town of Taterfield, Tim Dooley often found himself perplexed by the cosmic balance of right and wrong. One crisp autumn afternoon, while Tim was strolling through the potato fields, Karma, in the guise of a wise old farmer named Mr. Spudsworth, appeared to him.

"Tim," said Mr. Spudsworth, his eyes twinkling with ancient wisdom, "there are times in life when you must let your voice be heard. But remember, a scream carries weight and consequence."

Tim, puzzled yet intrigued, asked, "Why would I scream at people, Mr. Spudsworth? Isn't that unkind?"

Mr. Spudsworth chuckled softly. "Ah, my boy, it's not about the scream itself but the intention behind it. When words are spoken softly, they often go unheard in the din of daily life. A scream can cut through the noise, bringing clarity and attention to matters of great importance."

"But when should I do this?" Tim inquired, feeling the gravity of Karma's lesson settling upon him.

"Only when the situation demands it," Mr. Spudsworth replied. "When injustice prevails, when the silence is complicit, or when truth needs a champion. Your scream can awaken those who sleep through their lives, unaware of the world's ills. However, always remember to wield this power judiciously. A scream should not be a tool of anger but a clarion call for justice and awareness."

Tim nodded, absorbing the profound wisdom. He understood that Karma wasn't encouraging senseless yelling, but rather the righteous roar that calls for change, that disrupts complacency, and that paves the way for a better world.

From that day forward, Tim Dooley vowed to use his voice—whether soft or loud—with purpose and righteousness, forever guided by the potato farmer's sage advice.
