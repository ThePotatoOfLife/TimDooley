Chapter 63

Be Like Potato.

Legacy index title: Be Like Potato

"Empty your mind. Be formless, shapeless—like potato. Now, if you put a potato in a pot, it becomes the pot. You put a potato in a sack, it becomes the sack. A potato can be mashed, boiled, roasted, or fried. Potato can become anything. You see, a potato is soft and yielding, but it is also strong and resilient.

Be like potato, my friend. Adaptable. Versatile. When life throws you into the boiling water of hardship, become soft, but remain whole. When you face the fiery trials of the oven, become golden, full of flavor. And when you are mashed, when you are pressed, let it make you better—smooth, creamy, and nourishing to all around you.

The potato doesn’t resist the forces of change—it flows with them. It doesn’t complain when it is cut into wedges or transformed into fries. The potato knows that in each form, it serves a purpose. So too, should you let yourself be shaped by life, without losing your essence.

In life, you must be flexible, yet firm. Be grounded, like the potato in the soil, but be ready to move, to transform, when the time comes. Be humble, like the potato, yet rich in the nourishment you give to others.

Remember: Potato does not fight the pot, the fire, or the knife. It becomes what is needed in the moment.

Be like potato, my friend."
