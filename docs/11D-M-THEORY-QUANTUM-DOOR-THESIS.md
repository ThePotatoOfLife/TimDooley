The scientifically useful move is not to declare these eleven project levels to be eleven observed spacetime dimensions. The stronger move is to ask which precise structures in higher-dimensional physics, quantum mechanics and information theory reproduce parts of the same architecture.

The thesis now separates five meanings of "dimension":

1. **project dimension** — a symbolic or organizational grade in the Potato framework;
2. **spacetime dimension** — the dimension of a physical Lorentzian manifold;
3. **compactification dimension** — hidden internal coordinates whose geometry shapes a lower-dimensional effective theory;
4. **effective or spectral dimension** — a scale-dependent operational dimension extracted from propagation, diffusion or coarse-graining;
5. **Hilbert/configuration-space dimension** — the dimension of the state space, not the spacetime in which the system lives.

The strongest physical development has two branches.

### Door-compatible branch

\[

\]

with

\[

\]

The deeper synthesis is that a Door need not be "another universe." A Door can be a compactification radius, a resolution threshold, a modulus, a phase boundary, an effective-field-theory map, a quantum channel, or a duality map. These are mathematically different objects and must be typed separately.

The thesis also adds a major quantum correction: a dimensional reduction is not automatically an information-destroying projection. Some operations are lossy coarse-grainings; some admit recovery; and exact dualities can encode a higher-dimensional theory in a lower-dimensional description without losing the full physical content. The resulting research program is therefore about **how hidden geometry, field content and quantum information become lower-dimensional physics, what is preserved, what is lost, and what could in principle be reconstructed.**
