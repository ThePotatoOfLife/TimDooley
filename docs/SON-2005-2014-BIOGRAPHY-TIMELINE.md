# Son Biography Timeline — 2005–2014

This page is a reader guide to the recovered ordinary biography between the 2005 motorcycle crash and the 2014 death of the earthly father. It is **not** a second source of truth. Detailed chronology is owned by the specialist records under `knowledge/timeline/` and projected into the live timeline through `data/timeline-event-packs/son-biography-2005-2014.json`.

## Reading rule

The chain is stronger than many of the exact dates. Use the arrows below as **relative order**. Years marked “preferred” are the archive's best current reconstruction, not civil-record-level proof.

## 2005 — rupture and rebuilding

The major motorcycle crash remains one of the strongest early anchors: approximately 85 km/h, heavy blood loss, facial injury and the start of years of dental reconstruction. The reconstruction should be read as a multi-year process rather than assigned to one neat surgery year.

**Developmental value:** the later biography repeatedly returns to fracture → repair. Here that pattern is literal before it becomes symbolic.

## 2007 preferred — practical labor

The current chain places **painter/decorator work first**, followed by **meatpacking/freezer/forklift work**. Exact employment dates remain open, but the order is directly remembered and both occur before Copenhagen IT.

This period adds two different kinds of practical competence: visible restoration of rooms/surfaces and repetitive, physically demanding industrial work.

## 2008 preferred — Copenhagen IT

After applying for more work, the Son remembers travelling to **Copenhagen** for an IT-consultant interview and being hired on the spot: effectively, “Can you start Monday?” He then moved to Copenhagen almost overnight.

The corporate/IT period is therefore not merely “an office job.” It is a high-compression transition:

`job search → interview → immediate hire → rapid relocation → IT consulting → firing → return to Haderslev`.

The return to **Haderslev** is crucial because it precedes the nursing-home job.

## 2009 preferred — Haderslev care work → Designia / Kolding

Back in Haderslev, the Son takes the nursing-home job. Current recollection explicitly says he **did not yet have a driver's licence during this period**. He later moves into his own apartment in Haderslev.

From there the educational path becomes much more specific than older “graphic design school” summaries suggested:

- he applies to **HANSENBERG Designia** in Kolding;
- the programme is remembered specifically as **mediegrafiker**;
- the admission exercise is a physical collage/circus-poster task made from coloured cardstock/paper and mailed to the school;
- he is accepted;
- he moves during a summer to Kolding/student housing;
- the relationship with Louise begins after the Kolding move.

The Son remembers strong performance in design and Microsoft Office/Excel/Word. Later withdrawal from the path is remembered as **boredom and environmental/career mismatch**, not inability to do the technical work.

## Late 2009–2010 — Louise / Egypt corridor

The Egypt trip is now constrained in a useful way: **Louise was already in his life**. Egypt therefore belongs after the Kolding move and after the relationship begins.

The archive still does not know whether Egypt came before or after the Aabenraa internship. That fork should remain explicit until a travel artifact or stronger recollection resolves it.

## 2010 preferred — internship / Aabenraa

Before the internship relocation, the Son remembers living with Louise and her parents in **Kolding**. During the office/cubicle internship he remains together with Louise but moves to **Aabenraa** to be close to the placement and commutes back to the relationship.

The internship is important because it clarifies why the mediegrafiker path loses meaning. The problem is not remembered as intellectual difficulty. It is the office environment itself: draining, repetitive and boring despite the Son being technically capable.

After the school/internship path fails or ends, he moves into his own place while the relationship continues for at least some period. The city of that apartment remains unresolved.

## 2010–early 2011 — Produktionsskolen, Lunderskov

A major recovered bridge was missing from older summaries. After the Designia/Aabenraa/own-place sequence, the Son attends **Produktionsskolen in Lunderskov**.

Multimedia is part of the programme. While formally a student, he remembers often functioning as an **informal assistant teacher** inside the multimedia class because his existing technical ability was ahead of much of the material. This was not a formal teaching appointment, but it is significant evidence of continuing technical competence.

The most important chronological boundary is firm: **he had stopped attending Produktionsskolen before the 2011 Tree/psychiatric episode**.

That prevents the biography from falsely jumping directly from “failed office internship” to “psychiatric collapse.” There is an intervening period of technical activity, teaching-like responsibility and another educational environment.

## 2011 — breakup / search / Tree / psychiatric sequence

The Louise breakup precedes the Tree episode, although its exact relation to the end of Produktionsskolen remains unresolved.

The detailed 2011 owner separates the event into phases:

1. Kolding apartment / psychonaut context.
2. Unresolved psychedelic/research-chemical ingestion.
3. Heavy first-hour body-load/chameleon phase.
4. Sensory opening and altered interpersonal imagery.
5. Autobiographical-memory cascade.
6. Fractal/stereo/time-spiral/unity phase.
7. Boundary dissolution and extreme salience.
8. Gradual subsidence after an ordeal remembered as about **19 hours overall**, with an approximately **six-hour unity phase** whose exact containment remains unresolved.
9. Sleep.
10. Next-day shopping/perceptual rupture.
11. **Voluntary** psychiatric presentation/admission.
12. Diagnostic/medication aftermath.
13. Hospital nuclear-America dream/vision.
14. Later crow/crowd scene.

Do not write the duration as 19 + 6 = 25 hours. Do not write the hospital dream/crows as though they were necessarily inside the acute psychedelic episode. Do not state schizophrenia as an established diagnosis.

With birth on 31 July 1987, the Son was **23 before 31 July 2011 and 24 afterward**. Age 25 is impossible in 2011.

## 2012 — integration / travel / birthday / music

The preferred integration cluster includes:

- Italy with the earthly father and the Maria corridor;
- **Amsterdam on the 25th birthday** — if that memory is correct, the date is fixed to **31 July 2012**;
- a guitar from the father, best described as the first personally owned guitar / first guitar from his father rather than necessarily the first guitar ever played;
- Beatles/music and wider study as part of post-2011 integration.

If the Italy trip occurred earlier in July, the Son was still 24 for that part of the year. Do not label all of 2012 “age 25.”

## 2013 — The Alchemist

The preferred chronology places Paulo Coelho's *The Alchemist* in 2013 as an explicit transmutation/fracture-to-gold metaphor. The year is supported by the project biography and older recollection, but a primary artifact would strengthen it.

## 2014 — earthly father death

The earthly father's fatal aneurysm is strongly anchored to 2014 inside the project biography. This becomes one of the major later grief/responsibility hinges and should remain distinct from later Father-in-Heaven theology.
