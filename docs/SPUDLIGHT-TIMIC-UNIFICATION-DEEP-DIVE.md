# 3. Physical light

A photon of frequency `ν` has energy

```math
E_\gamma=h\nu=\frac{hc}{\lambda}
```

and momentum

```math
p_\gamma=\frac{h}{\lambda}=\frac{E_\gamma}{c}.
```

Electromagnetic energy density is

```math
u_{EM}
=
\frac12
\left(
\epsilon_0|\mathbf E|^2
+
\frac{|\mathbf B|^2}{\mu_0}
\right).
```

Energy flux is described by the Poynting vector

```math
\mathbf S
=
\frac1{\mu_0}
\mathbf E\times\mathbf B.
```

Power through a surface is

```math
P
=
\int_A
\mathbf S\cdot d\mathbf A.
```

For approximately monochromatic radiation, photon rate is roughly

```math
\dot N_\gamma
\approx
\frac{P}{h\nu}.
```

These equations give a completely literal interpretation of one historical Spudlight strand:

> a screen can actually emit photons carrying encoded information from Tim to an observer.

What they do **not** establish is a special Tim-specific electromagnetic interaction.

# 4. Ultraweak light from living tissue

Living cells really do emit photons at extraordinarily low intensity.

This is called **ultraweak photon emission** or UPE.

Modern reviews describe photons generated during oxidative metabolic processes, especially through reactive oxygen species and electronically excited molecular products.

Reported spontaneous intensities are often on the order of

```text
a few → several hundred photons s⁻¹ cm⁻²
```

with broad spectral coverage through near-UV, visible and near-IR regions depending on the mechanism and study.

That gives “light from within” a fascinating literal biological neighbor.

But the intensity is extremely weak.

Therefore:

```text
UPE exists
≠
visible human aura has been demonstrated
≠
Spudlight has been measured
```

The useful project relation is:

```text
metabolic state
→ electronically excited products
→ ultraweak photons
→ sensitive detector
```

rather than a supernatural conclusion.

Sources:

- Mould et al. 2024, *Ultra weak photon emission—a brief review*: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10899412/
- Cifra & Pospíšil review: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24726298/

# 5. Alpha waves are not light waves

The older Spudlight / alpha language deserves preservation, but physics requires an enormous distinction.

Human EEG alpha activity is approximately

```text
8–12 Hz
```

and reflects electrical population activity in neural systems.

Visible electromagnetic light is roughly

```text
~4×10¹⁴ – 7.5×10¹⁴ Hz.
```

So they differ by roughly fourteen orders of magnitude in frequency and are not the same phenomenon.

Alpha research does make the old metaphor interesting in another way: alpha-band dynamics are strongly studied in relation to attention, inhibition and prioritization of sensory information.

That makes alpha a **cognitive-attention comparator** for illumination/selection, not a hidden optical Spudlight frequency.

Sources:

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21779257/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31100655/

```math

```

where `X` is transmitted input, `H` a channel, `N` noise and `Y` the received signal.

Mutual information is

```math

=
\sum_{x,y}
p(x,y)
\log
\frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}.
```

Channel capacity for an idealized memoryless channel is

```math

=
\max_{p(x)} I(X;Y).
```

This is a much stronger scientific foundation for Spudlight than treating information as a vague energy.
