# Potato Growth / Ladder / Axis / Heaven / Hall of Heroes

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: reader-architecture decision before further implementation

## The simple model

### Potato of Life = source symbol / central image

Question:
What does the Potato mean, and why a potato?

Primary owner:
- /potato-of-life/

Owns biology, hidden capacity, storage, nourishment, burial, Eyes, sprouts, and central theology.

It should not become the complete practice manual or Hall of Heroes.

### Potatoism = teaching / philosophy / religion / culture

Question:
What does this project teach, believe and practice?

Primary owner:
- /potatoism/

Potatoism is the system a reader can learn from without becoming a named figure in project canon.

### Grow a Potato = path / practice

Question:
How does a person grow?

Primary owner:
- /potatoism/grow/

This is not the Hall of Heroes.

Owns:
- Be simple.
- Grow toward light.
- Soil.
- Mud.
- Burial.
- Eye.
- Root.
- Sprout.
- Door.
- Fruit.
- Seed.
- Return.
- relationships.
- work.
- money and attention.
- rest.
- community.
- spiritual-growth exercises.
- future Potato Test / Are you a Potato?
- future diagnostic questions about what stage or condition needs attention.

Reader relationship:
The reader is the subject.

The page should help somebody practice, reflect and change even if they never appear anywhere else in the Potatoverse.

### Ladder = movement / transition

Question:
How does growth move between levels?

Ladder is not a membership list, room or reward.

It describes repeated passage, ascent and descent, connection between levels, learned pathways becoming repeatable, and return with what was learned.

### Axis / North = orientation

Question:
Which way is up in the project's symbolic system?

Primary public owner:
- /axis/

Axis is orientation, not achievement.

North / North of North can function as direction, source-facing orientation, highest reference and later Timic theology.

Do not turn North into a synonym for Potato, Angel, Heaven, Ladder or Hall.

### Heaven = upper domain

Question:
Where do integrated / source-facing / upper relations live?

Heaven is a place/domain, not the growth process itself.

It can contain Father's House, Garden, Throne, table, upper rooms, Angels and Hall of Heroes.

A Potato does not have to originate in Heaven for Heaven to be where the grown Potato is remembered.

### Hall of Heroes = exemplary memory / inspiration

Question:
Who or what shows us what growth looked like in practice?

The Hall belongs in Heaven.

This is different from /potatoism/grow/.

The Hall should not primarily teach stages or test the reader. It should display testimony:
- who grew;
- where they began;
- what changed;
- what they learned;
- what they built;
- what they survived;
- what they repaired;
- what they gave;
- what new ability or responsibility appeared;
- what Fruit remained;
- what later regression or correction complicates the story.

Reader relationship:
The reader is the witness / learner.

The Hall teaches indirectly:
Look at what happened here. What can you learn from it?

It should be inspirational without becoming a literal ranking of human worth or a court of favorites. Visually, the Hall may borrow the celebratory language of an old arcade high-score board—slots, high marks, initials, bonus stages, achievements and CONTINUE energy—so long as the 'score' remains the sourced transformation, Fruit and service rather than an invented moral number.

## Hall of Heroes design principle

The Hall is above the growth path, not identical with it.

GROUND / WORLD
→ Potatoism / practice
→ Root / Eye / Sprout
→ Door / narrowing
→ Ladder / Spiral
→ Heaven
→ Hall of Heroes

The Hall can contain Potatoes and Potato Angels because it is a memorial / honor space for the lineage.

That does not mean every Potato was born in Heaven.

## Potato vs Angel inside the Hall

Useful distinction:
- Potato = growth identity / stored life / rooted developmental form.
- Potato Angel = mature upper / service form where project canon grants Angelic function.

Both may be remembered in the same Hall.

The Hall can contain:
- honored ordinary Potatoes;
- learner Potatoes;
- builders;
- named Potato figures;
- Potato Angels;
- Angel Army material;
- incomplete / still-growing figures;
- historical milestones.

## What the Hall should teach without becoming the practice page

Every honored figure should answer some version of:

1. Where did they begin?
2. What condition or obstacle mattered?
3. What did they learn?
4. What changed in behavior or capacity?
5. What did they make, protect, nourish or repair?
6. What Fruit remained?
7. What should the reader notice?
8. What remains incomplete or contested?

Heroes should be exemplary without becoming flawless idols.

## Future Potato Test

Canonical home:
- /potatoism/grow/

Not Hall of Heroes.

Possible modules:

### Are you a Potato?
Questions about teachability, revision, rooting, patience, useful work, nourishment, humor, independence, service and ability to let others grow differently.

Possible outputs:

### Spiritual-growth check
Not how holy are you?

Instead:
- Are you more truthful?
- More capable?
- Less dependent on status?
- Better able to disagree?
- Better able to stop harmful recurrence?
- Better able to serve without owning?
- Better able to create conditions for others?
- Better able to return from insight to ordinary work?

The test should produce reflection and next steps, not spiritual rank.

## Core anti-confusion rule

Never ask one page to answer all four:
- What does Potato mean?
- How do I grow?
- How does the vertical map work?
- Who deserves remembrance?

Those are:
- Potato of Life;
- Grow;
- Axis;
- Hall of Heroes.

They connect strongly. They should not collapse.


## Hall of Shame design principle

The Dogs hall may function as the inverse arcade cabinet: **GAME OVER / failed run / loop / consequence / CONTINUE?** The purpose is to make failure memorable without creating a permanent caste of condemned people.

- Rank **failure modes**, not souls.
- A real person belongs in CIA when chronology and evidence matter.
- Hall of Shame language may describe a sourced role or pattern, but should always preserve an exit condition.
- The strongest dramatic contrast is Heroes = what became possible because growth happened; Shame = what kept reproducing because the loop did not change.
- A Dog who verifies, stops, repairs or changes function can leave the losing board. The mythology should make return visible.
