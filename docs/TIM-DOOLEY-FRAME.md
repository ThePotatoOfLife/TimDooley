# The Tim Dooley Frame

> **Source → Father → Father's House → Heaven → Throne of North → Ladder → Door → Son → Axis → Tree of Life → Plane → Roots → Potato → Source**
>
> The frame is recursive rather than a literal linear cosmology. The vertical line describes orientation; the Tree describes life; the Plane describes manifestation; the cycle describes return.

## 1. What the frame is

The Tim Dooley Frame is the canonical architecture for the Potatoverse project. It combines a mythological-symbolic layer with a separate empirical/research layer and a creative layer.

The governing rule is **relationships first**. A symbol, country, institution, story, or economic object is not treated as an isolated thing. Its meaning comes from the relationships in which it participates.

The repository must preserve a hard distinction between:

- **mythological** — internal canon and symbolic cosmology;
- **philosophical** — questions, principles, and interpretations derived from the frame;
- **empirical** — observable facts with sources and dates;
- **calculated** — reproducible transformations of sourced facts;
- **estimated** — explicitly uncertain values;
- **scenario** — analytical possibilities, not predictions;
- **historical** — claims about the past supported by historical evidence;
- **creative** — poems, songs, stories, images, speeches, and other artistic work.

A metaphor is never silently promoted into evidence.

## 2. The vertical architecture

### Source
The undivided origin of the architecture. Source is a metaphysical symbol, not an empirical claim.

### Father
The upper/source principle. In the mythology, Father is the orienting source-position from which the relationship with the Son is understood.

### Father's House
The interior center of integration: the symbolic house in which the scattered signals of the world become one ordered field.

### Heaven
The upper domain of integration, clarity, unity, relationship, truth, and love in the symbolic vocabulary.

### Throne of North
The point of ultimate orientation. North is used as a symbolic extreme and organizing direction, not as a claim of political sovereignty.

### Ladder
The traversable vertical connection between levels. It turns the Axis from a static coordinate into a path.

### Door
The threshold between states. Door is the interface: neither merely inside nor merely outside.

In the present interface model, **Door = Frame = Window**. The Door is not only something we pass through; it is the frame through which the system becomes visible. The Window gives the act of looking, the Frame gives the boundaries and relations that organize what is seen, and the Door gives passage between what is observed and what lies beyond the current view.

This produces the central learning principle:

> **We learn through the frame we look through.**

World and Axis therefore meet at the Door. **WORLD ↘ [DOOR / FRAME / WINDOW] ↖ AXIS.** World is the observable field opening outward and downward-right from the center; Axis is the organizing field opening upward-left. The Door remains in the middle because it is the interface between the two.

The distinction matters: the Window is not assumed to show the whole world, and the Frame is not assumed to be the world itself. What we see depends partly on the relations and boundaries through which we are looking.

### Son
The person-bearing centre of the mythic narrative. Within the canon the Son is associated with Thomas, Twin, Lion, Jesus Christ, Son of Man, and Lion of Judah. These are preserved as mythic correspondences within the project rather than asserted as historical identity claims.

### Axis
The vertical organizing principle. It gives the Plane orientation and connects the upper and lower structures.

### Tree of Life
The living expression of the Axis. Roots, trunk, branches, leaves, flowers, fruit, and seed form a model of continuity, differentiation, manifestation, and renewal.

### Tree of Strife
The fractured expression of the Tree. Branches become isolated, the fruit is detached from the roots, and relationships are replaced by competition, fear, extraction, and conflict.

### Plane
The horizontal world of manifestation: bodies, geography, society, institutions, technology, money, infrastructure, history, and ordinary experience.

### Roots
The hidden substrate: origin, memory, ancestry, history, potential, matter, and everything beneath ordinary visibility.

### Potato of Life
The inversion of the heavenly metaphor. Life is hidden in mud and matter, yet it can sprout, nourish, reproduce, and rise. The Potato is therefore the frame's emblem of buried life becoming visible.

## 3. The horizontal architecture

The Plane is organized through four directions:

- **North** — orientation toward the symbolic extreme and Axis;
- **South** — descent and return toward the lower/material field;
- **East** — emergence, beginning, sunrise, and outward becoming;
- **West** — decline, completion, sunset, and return.

The four directions form the world-field. The Axis crosses that field vertically.

At the interface level, the two principal directions are now rendered around the central Door:

**WORLD ↘   DOOR = FRAME = WINDOW   ↖ AXIS**

This is a view of the architecture, not a claim that physical space literally has these coordinates.

## 4. Tree logic

The Tree is the preferred model for representing relationships because it expresses both hierarchy and interdependence.

**Root → Trunk → Branch → Leaf/Flower → Fruit → Seed → Root**

The repository should use this grammar whenever it is useful to describe a system without pretending that every relationship is literally hierarchical.

- Roots preserve memory.
- The trunk preserves continuity.
- Branches express differentiation.
- Leaves expose the system to its environment.
- Flowers represent possibility and transformation.
- Fruit represents manifestation.
- Seed represents renewal.

## 5. Red and Blue Potato

### Red Potato
Creativity, love, expansion, emergence, sprouting, forward movement, and the upward/outward spiral of Life.

### Blue Potato
Memory, precision, fear, contraction, rootward movement, backward/downward memory, and the conserving spiral of Strife.

They are complementary archetypes rather than simple good/evil opposites. The frame is strongest when Life and Memory remain in dialogue.

## 6. The Vesica and the Door

The vesica piscis is used as a geometric metaphor for relationship. Two circles overlap; the overlap creates a third region that neither circle possesses alone.

Within the frame:

**Father + Son → Door**

The Door therefore represents relational emergence: something new becomes possible through connection.

The Door can also be read as a **Window in a Frame**. The overlap does not erase the two sides; it creates a shared region through which each side becomes legible to the other. In that sense, the Door is simultaneously:

- **threshold** — a place of passage;
- **frame** — the boundary that gives the passage shape;
- **window** — the opening through which something can be seen;
- **interface** — the place where observer and observed meet;
- **learning surface** — the point where a relation becomes visible enough to understand.

## 7. Crucifixion and transformation motif

In the Potatoverse chronology, the Son's Christmas/North Pole death is the mythic transformation event through which the Son becomes associated with the Door and Ladder.

This is part of the internal mythology. It must remain labelled as mythological/creative material in the data model.

The central transformation is:

**person → threshold → path → connection**

The meaning is that the route back toward integration is not merely an object. It is relationship embodied as a passage.

## 8. The cycle

The architecture is recursive:

**Source → Life → World → Death → Memory → Renewal → Life**

The end is therefore capable of becoming the beginning. The seed returns to the roots; the roots produce the tree; the tree produces fruit; fruit produces seed.

## 9. World-building and the empirical graph

The same relationship grammar is applied to the European Economic Graph, but without importing mythological truth claims into empirical analysis.

The graph maps:

- states and institutions;
- companies and ownership/control;
- debt and obligations;
- banks, funds, bonds, and capital markets;
- energy and infrastructure;
- procurement and public spending;
- trade and value chains;
- technology and research;
- labour and skills;
- geopolitical dependencies.

The graph's rule is the same: **map the connections, not merely the objects**.

## 10. North Programme

The North Programme is the applied policy layer. It uses the graph to study fiscal sustainability, productive investment, industrial capacity, energy security, infrastructure, technology, research, labour, and voluntary European cooperation.

The working North Axis geography is dynamic. Denmark and Greenland function as anchor nodes in the current conceptual architecture, with relationships extending through the North Atlantic and Europe toward Ukraine and Turkey. This is a project framework, not an assertion that a formal alliance already exists.

## 11. The Swamp

The Swamp is the shadow-system metaphor for accumulated dysfunction: extraction, corruption, institutional inertia, fear, manipulation, unsustainable obligations, and systems that consume the life they were intended to serve.

The Swamp is not synonymous with one real-world country or population. It is a reusable analytical metaphor.

## 12. Canonical rule for future additions

Every new element should answer five questions:

1. **What is it?**
2. **Which layer does it belong to?**
3. **What does it connect to?**
4. **What evidence supports the relationship, if any?**
5. **Is the relationship canonical, observed, calculated, estimated, scenario-based, interpretive, historical, or creative?**

This prevents the archive from becoming a pile of disconnected claims while allowing the mythology, philosophy, art, and empirical research to grow together.

## 13. One-line compression

> **The Tim Dooley Frame is a relationship-first architecture in which Source becomes Father, Father relates to Son, Son becomes Door and Ladder, the Door becomes Frame and Window, the Window makes World visible, the Frame gives relation and boundary, Ladder crosses the Axis, Axis lives as Tree, Tree manifests upon the Plane, roots preserve the hidden past, fruit carries life forward, and the whole system recursively returns toward Source.**
