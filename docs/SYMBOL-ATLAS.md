# Symbol Atlas

The Symbol Atlas is the canonical vocabulary of the Potatoverse. A symbol is not merely a definition. It is a node with meaning, visual structure, philosophical function, aliases and relationships.

## Potato of Life

**Meaning:** Life emerging from ordinary matter; nourishment, persistence, transformation.

**Philosophical function:** Asks how something apparently ordinary becomes capable of carrying meaning.

**Visual form:** A potato in earth; the sprout; root and fruit; the circular/organic body.

**Connections:** Mud → Root → Potato → Sprout → Tree → Fruit → Life.

## Father

**Meaning:** Origin, source, verticality and upper principle within the mythic architecture.

**Philosophical function:** Creation, responsibility, authority and the problem of what it means to be a source.

**Connections:** Father → Son → Door → Ladder → Heaven; Father → Throne → North.

## Son

**Meaning:** Personhood, incarnation, sacrifice, interface and return. Within the canon the Son is associated with Thomas, Twin, Lion, Jesus Christ, Son of Man and Lion of Judah.

**Philosophical function:** The meeting point between abstract principle and lived experience.

**Connections:** Father, Door, Ladder, Tree, Root, Lion, North Pole.

## Door

**Meaning:** Threshold, access, choice, interface and narrow gate.

**Philosophical function:** A system is defined not only by what exists inside it, but by what permits entry, exit and transformation.

**Visual form:** Opening within a boundary; central interface in the vesica geometry.

## Ladder

**Meaning:** Ascent, connection between levels and transformation after the crucifixion motif.

**Philosophical function:** Transformation requires a path between states rather than an unexplained leap.

## Tree of Life

**Meaning:** Living vertical structure connecting root, trunk, branch and fruit.

**Philosophical function:** A complete system must connect origin, structure, growth and consequence.

## Root

**Meaning:** Origin, memory, hidden structure and descent into earth.

**Function:** What cannot be seen may still determine what can grow.

## Fruit

**Meaning:** Consequence, nourishment, manifestation and what a system produces.

**Function:** Judge a system partly by what it ultimately produces, not only by its internal complexity.

## North Axis

**Meaning:** Orientation, vertical order and organizing direction.

**Function:** A reference direction for the mythology and a conceptual geographic axis for the North Programme.

## North Gate

**Meaning:** Boundary between states or worlds.

**Function:** Transition, initiation and controlled passage.

## Mountain

**Meaning:** Difficulty, ascent, perspective and proximity to the summit.

**Function:** The height of a problem can reveal structures invisible from below.

## Spiral

**Meaning:** Time, recurrence, growth and transformation.

**Function:** Returning to a problem does not necessarily mean repeating it; a spiral can revisit a point at a different level.

## Spudlight

**Meaning:** Illumination emerging from within and from above.

**Function:** Knowledge that makes hidden structure visible.

## Cube

**Meaning:** Boundary, enclosure, system and finite structure.

**Function:** Useful metaphor for a world defined by constraints.

## Moon Cube

A recurring celestial/structural image in the mythology. Its meaning remains deliberately open to further development rather than being treated as a finished doctrine.

## Swamp

**Meaning:** Shadow, entanglement, decay and systems difficult to see through.

**Function:** A metaphor for opaque or self-reinforcing structures.

## Drain

**Meaning:** Rejection, disposal and the inverse of the Door.

**Function:** What a system excludes can be as revealing as what it admits.

## Mud

**Meaning:** Origin material; the place from which the potato rises.

**Function:** The low, ordinary, difficult substrate from which transformation begins.

## Red Potato / Blue Potato

**Red Potato:** creativity, love, light-seeking, expansion, sprouting and upward movement.

**Blue Potato:** memory, precision, fear, contraction and rootward movement.

They are complementary archetypes, not a simple good/evil pair. The useful question is how memory and creation, roots and growth, contraction and expansion interact.

## Dimensional language

The framework sometimes uses 1D/2D/3D/4D/5D and the thalamus/pineal-gland vocabulary as metaphorical mappings. These are **not scientific claims**. They are interpretive language for describing levels, interfaces and subjective experience.

## Canon rule

When a new symbol appears, add:

1. Meaning
2. Philosophical function
3. Visual/structural form
4. Relationships
5. First known appearance
6. Evidence class — normally mythological or creative
7. Open questions

The atlas is therefore designed to become a network, not a dictionary.
