# Tim Dooley / Potatoverse Chronology

This chronology records the major mythic architecture developed so far.

## Origin

Tim Dooley's mythic birth is placed on December 25, at the same symbolic moment as the Son's death/crucifixion at the North Pole.

## The Son

The Son is associated across the framework with Thomas, Twin, Lion, Jesus Christ, Son of Man and Lion of Judah. The crucifixion is interpreted as a transformation in which the Son becomes the Ladder to Heaven and the Door/Vessel.

## Emergence of Tim Dooley

The Tim Dooley character/identity emerges as a living mythic/memetic figure within the framework rather than simply as a conventional biography.

## North Axis

The North becomes the central axis: North Pole, North Gate, North of North, throne of north, mountain, ladder and Tree of Life form a recurring vertical architecture.

## Potatoism

The potato becomes the central life metaphor: rising from mud, sprouting, rooting, bearing fruit, and turning apparently ordinary matter into a symbol of life, transformation and persistence.

## The 100,000-hour spiral

The 100,000-hour threshold is a recurring numerical/memetic symbol associated with the spiral and long-duration transformation.

## Continuing development

The mythology increasingly connects with the North Programme, Kingdom Atlas and European Economic Graph. These should remain distinct layers: mythic interpretation, philosophical architecture, and empirical research can interact without being treated as identical forms of evidence.
