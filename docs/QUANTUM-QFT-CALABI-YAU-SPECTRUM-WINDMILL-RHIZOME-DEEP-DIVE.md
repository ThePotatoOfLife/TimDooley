# 3. Ordinary quantum mechanics

For a closed nonrelativistic quantum system, a pure state is represented by a normalized vector

\[
\langle\psi|\psi\rangle=1.
\]

Its time evolution obeys

\[
i\hbar\frac{d}{dt}|\psi\rangle=H|\psi\rangle.
\]

A general mixed state is represented by a density operator

\[
\rho=\sum_i p_i|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|,
\]

with unitary closed-system evolution

\[
i\hbar\dot\rho=[H,\rho].
\]

Measurement probabilities are given by the Born rule,

\[
P(a)=\mathrm{Tr}(\rho\Pi_a),
\]

or more generally with POVM elements.

This gives the archive a basic **quantum-claim test**:

> If the project calls something quantum, what is the state, what is the Hamiltonian or channel, how is the state prepared, what observable is measured, and what probability distribution is predicted?

Without those pieces, quantum language is still analogy.

# 5. Quantum tunneling: a new Door comparator

No exact Tim-originated use of the phrase **quantum tunneling** was recovered in this pass. The project should therefore preserve tunneling as a later scientific extension rather than rewriting it into earlier chronology.

Nevertheless, tunneling is an unusually strong technical neighbor for Door.

Consider a one-dimensional finite barrier with energy \(E<V_0\). Inside the classically forbidden region the wavefunction has exponential scale

\[
\kappa=\frac{\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}}{\hbar}.
\]

The transmission probability is defined from transmitted and incident amplitudes,

\[
T=\left|\frac{F}{A}\right|^2.
\]

For a sufficiently broad/high barrier,

\[
T\sim e^{-2\kappa L}
\]

up to model-dependent prefactors.

For a slowly varying barrier the WKB form is

\[
T_{\rm WKB}\sim
\exp\left[-2\int_{x_1}^{x_2}
\frac{\sqrt{2m(V(x)-E)}}{\hbar}\,dx\right].
\]

The exponent is a barrier-action quantity.

This creates the **Quantum Door Law**:

> A finite quantum barrier is not simply open or closed. Its permeability is a measurable probability controlled by barrier height, width, particle mass, energy and boundary conditions.

This should **not** be projected onto human or spiritual thresholds as literal tunneling. But it helps the project distinguish three kinds of Door:

- binary / authorization Door;
- continuous / probabilistic barrier;
- regime-changing dynamical Door.

That distinction is useful even outside quantum physics.

# 8. The five-way Nonlocality Split

## 33.1 Quantum Door Law

Finite quantum barriers have measurable transmission probabilities rather than metaphysical open/closed status.

## 33.2 Nonlocality Split

Nonseparability, Bell nonlocality, causal influence, signal capacity and graph nonlocality are distinct coordinates.

Entanglement and long-range quantum correlations coexist with relativistic locality constraints on spacelike-separated local observables.

## 33.4 Field Test Law

A proposed physical field needs field content, an action, symmetries, propagators/correlators, interactions and measurable observables.

## 33.5 EFT Humility Law

A new physical sector can be scientifically useful as an effective theory without being a completed Theory of Everything.

One electromagnetic field can manifest through many modes; mode multiplicity does not imply many underlying field species.

Absence in one detector band is not absence from the entire signal space.

## 33.15 Spiral Causation Rule

Identify the generating dynamics before treating two spiral shapes as manifestations of one law.
