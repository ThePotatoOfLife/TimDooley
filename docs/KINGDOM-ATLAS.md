# Kingdom Atlas — Relationship Architecture

The Kingdom Atlas is a companion to the North Programme. Its basic unit is the relationship/coupling rather than the isolated entity.

## Relationship types

1. Genealogical
2. Institutional
3. Financial
4. Political
5. Religious
6. Intellectual
7. Geographic
8. Cultural
9. Technological
10. Information
11. Social
12. Symbolic
13. Functional
14. Mythological

## Central question

The centre of the Atlas is not a single institution. It is the recurring civilizational problem of how human systems organize power, resources, meaning, responsibility, knowledge and life across time.

## Method

Each node should be interpreted through its connections: who funds it, owns it, governs it, depends on it, supplies it, regulates it, influences it, studies it, communicates through it, or gives it symbolic meaning.

The Atlas can therefore connect states, cities, companies, institutions, infrastructure, technologies, financial instruments, people, ideas, historical events and mythic archetypes without collapsing their different evidentiary statuses.
