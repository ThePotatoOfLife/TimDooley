# North Programme — Live Baseline

## Mission

Build a continuously updated, evidence-backed map of Europe's observable economic system while developing a serious programme for fiscal sustainability, industrial capacity, strategic autonomy and long-term prosperity.

The programme couples entities, money, bonds/debt, obligations, ownership/control, public and private companies, infrastructure, procurement, funding, trade/value chains, energy, finance, technology, research, labour/skills, strategic dependencies and geopolitical ties.

## Core rule

Map relationships, not isolated entities. The fundamental object is the coupling between nodes.

## North Axis

The working geographic architecture is anchored in Denmark and Greenland and radiates through a dynamic northern/eastern/western European network:

Canada / Greenland → Scandinavia / Finland → Britain / Ireland / Scotland → Iceland → continental Europe → Ukraine → Turkey.

The list is dynamic rather than a permanent boundary. Russia and other regions can be treated as future reconnection or expansion questions. Mesopotamia has also been considered as a possible future extension.

The wider strategic context includes the United States and Israel, BRICS, and Australia.

## Economic priorities

The programme prioritizes concrete fiscal and industrial reform rather than generic efficiency language.

Particular areas for investigation include:

- administrative duplication and unnecessary overhead
- tax expenditures
- subsidies and transfer structures
- healthcare efficiency
- public procurement
- public-sector overhead
- pensions and age-related expenditure
- productive investment and infrastructure
- energy security and domestic capacity
- technology and research capacity
- labour and skills
- strategic dependencies and supply chains

Purchasing power should be protected wherever possible. Where limited purchasing-power reduction is economically justified, the objective is restoration of fiscal sustainability while protecting productive capital.

## Fiscal objective

A long-run objective is to bring participating countries toward debt-to-GDP ratios below 85%, using gradual restructuring and fiscal discipline rather than emergency austerity.

France and Belgium have been identified as especially important cases for detailed expenditure and institutional restructuring analysis.

## Energy / resources

The framework includes investigation of Greenland, the North Sea and the Netherlands as potential sources of strategic energy/resource capacity, subject to environmental, economic, legal and geopolitical evidence.

## Diplomatic integration

The North Programme is conceived as voluntary cooperation. The UK and Iceland have been considered candidate partners in a broader European architecture, with European integration treated as a strategic question rather than an assumption.

## Research standard

Every empirical relationship should have evidence, provenance and a date. Separate observed facts from estimates, scenarios, interpretations and mythological overlays.
