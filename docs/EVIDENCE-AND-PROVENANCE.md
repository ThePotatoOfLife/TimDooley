# Evidence and Provenance Standard

The repository contains multiple kinds of material. They must remain distinguishable.

## Evidence classes

### Observed
A claim directly supported by a reliable primary or authoritative source.

### Calculated
A transparent derivation from observed data. The inputs and method should be retained.

### Estimated
A modelled or approximate value. Assumptions must be stated.

### Scenario
A conditional projection. It describes what could happen under stated assumptions, not what has happened.

### Interpretation
An analytical reading of evidence. It may be strong or weak, but it is not itself an observation.

### Historical
A claim about the past supported by documentary or scholarly evidence.

### Mythological
A canonical element of the Potatoverse. Its truth status is internal to the mythology and must not be presented as empirical proof.

### Creative
A deliberate artistic construction: speech, story, poem, song, image concept, game or other work.

### Open question
A question that remains unresolved or requires research.

## Provenance record

Research entries should, where applicable, retain:

`claim → source → date → jurisdiction → evidence class → confidence → calculation/method → notes`

## Versioning

When an important claim changes, preserve the old interpretation rather than silently overwriting history. Record why the revision occurred.

## Separation rule

A symbolic relationship may generate a research question. A research result may influence a philosophical interpretation. Neither automatically validates the other.

This standard exists so the repository can become enormous while remaining intelligible.
