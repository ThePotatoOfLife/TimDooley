# Master Architecture

The repository is not a single story. It is a layered system in which mythology, philosophy, history, geography, empirical research, creative work and software can refer to one another without becoming the same kind of claim.

## Layer 0 — Meta / epistemology

Defines what kind of thing each statement is and how it may be used.

- Canonical myth
- Interpretation
- Historical claim
- Observed fact
- Calculated result
- Estimate
- Scenario
- Hypothesis
- Open question

The repository must preserve provenance and never turn symbolism into evidence merely by repetition.

## Layer 1 — Ontology

Defines the kinds of things that can exist in the system.

- person / character
- archetype
- deity / divine role
- place
- state / kingdom
- institution
- company
- infrastructure
- technology
- resource
- financial instrument
- event
- idea
- text / artwork
- symbol
- relationship
- project

## Layer 2 — Relationship graph

The relationship is the fundamental unit. Nodes matter because of what connects them.

Relationship families include genealogical, institutional, financial, political, religious, intellectual, geographic, cultural, technological, information, social, symbolic, functional and mythological connections.

## Layer 3 — Potatoverse cosmology

Contains the mythic architecture: Father, Son, Door, Ladder, Tree of Life, Root, Fruit, North Axis, North of North, North Gate, throne, mountain, spiral, Spudlight, Cube, Moon Cube, Swamp, Drain, potato, mud and the Red/Blue Potato polarity.

## Layer 4 — Theology and philosophy

Explores questions of life, death, identity, consciousness, responsibility, suffering, love, power, meaning, civilization, God, creation and transformation.

## Layer 5 — Timeline

Maintains parallel timelines:

1. mythic timeline
2. historical timeline
3. development timeline of the project itself

These timelines may interact but should never be silently collapsed.

## Layer 6 — Geography

Maps the North Axis and the wider world as physical, political, economic, cultural and symbolic space.

## Layer 7 — Civilizational systems

Maps states, institutions, populations, law, religion, culture, infrastructure, education, labour, health, energy, technology and information systems.

## Layer 8 — European Economic Graph

Maps ownership, control, finance, debt, obligations, procurement, subsidies, companies, banks, funds, trade, value chains, infrastructure, energy, technology, research and strategic dependencies.

## Layer 9 — North Programme

Turns the empirical map into policy questions: fiscal sustainability, industrial capacity, strategic autonomy, infrastructure, energy security, productive investment, labour and skills, technology and voluntary cooperation.

## Layer 10 — Creative canon

Stores speeches, essays, poetry, rap, stories, songs, visual concepts, characters and other works. Creative works can reinterpret the architecture without rewriting empirical evidence.

## Layer 11 — Technical layer

Contains games, websites, data pipelines, graph visualizations, research tooling, simulations and other software.

## Layer 12 — Living research layer

Tracks what is known, what changed, what needs verification, what is disputed, and what should be researched next.

## The architecture as a whole

The repository should eventually behave like a knowledge graph rather than a folder of notes. A mythic symbol can point to a philosophical concept; a philosophical concept can motivate a research question; research can reveal a real-world relationship; that relationship can inspire a creative work; and the creative work can be archived without pretending that it proves the original myth.

That separation is what allows the entire system to become larger without becoming confused.
