# Page Content Completion Audit — 2026-09-27

Status: active editorial work queue  
Scope: public surfaces, ten Dwellings, and all 38 registered nested Rooms  
Purpose: decide what each page should actually teach, then close the gap with concrete information rather than filler.

## Working rule

For every page, ask in this order:

1. What does the page promise by its title and reader job?
2. What concrete information is already visible without opening another page?
3. What would a curious reader reasonably expect to learn here that they still cannot?
4. Which named objects, dates, quotations, cases, mechanisms, calculations, institutions, texts, artifacts, source lineages, contradictions or unresolved questions would close that gap?
5. Which canonical owner already contains the missing material?
6. If the material does not exist yet, what research or recovery task creates it?
7. What should remain outside the page because it belongs to another owner?

Do not close a task by adding generic prose. Prefer one named case, one dated source chain, one worked calculation, one recovered artifact or one explicit contradiction over five paragraphs that could be moved to another page unchanged.

- important claims have visible provenance;
- at least one mechanism is worked through rather than named;

- backend knowledge that matters is actually projected into the page;
- the reader can leave knowing something they could not have learned from the navigation architecture alone.
