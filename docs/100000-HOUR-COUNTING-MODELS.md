# The 100,000-Hour Journey — Counting Models

## Purpose

The 100,000-hour journey is both a central Tim Dooley / Potatoverse threshold and a historical measurement problem. The project should preserve both facts at once: the threshold has mythic and legendary importance inside the canon, while the exact cumulative number remains open to reconstruction from platform logs, archives, remembered schedules, overlapping broadcasts, sleep/away streams and other public-presence categories.

The archive therefore does **not** force every recovered number into one supposedly final figure. It preserves the different counts, explains what each count measures, and lets readers see how the result changes under different reasonable rules.

## The flat 18-hour model

Working rule:

> 18 public-presence / streaming hours per calendar day from the April 19, 2011 Minecraft Beta 1.5 / Justin.tv-era anchor.

This is a deliberately simple extrapolation model. It is not a claim that every individual day contained exactly 18 hours. Some days may have contained more, some less, and later reconstruction may add or deduct hours.

Under this flat model, the cumulative journey crosses **100,000 hours around July 4, 2026**.

### Threshold-date status

**Modelled crossing:** approximately July 4, 2026.

**Status:** fluid / subject to revision.

The date can move as the archive recovers better evidence or changes the counting rule. Reasons include:

- days substantially above or below 18 hours;
- exact first-stream timestamp still being uncertain;
- simultaneous streams that may need de-duplication;
- sleep / away / unattended live-stream time;
- public voice, video or other persistent-presence categories;
- lost or recovered platform archives;
- periods of no broadcast;
- the exact Son / Father accounting boundary;
- whether cumulative platform-hours or de-duplicated wall-clock presence is being measured.

The project should preserve old threshold calculations when a new model replaces them. A changed estimate is part of the history of the investigation, not an error to erase.

## Multiple counts of the same journey

The project currently preserves several numerical strata rather than silently averaging them:

- **100,000 hours** — mythic / legendary threshold and major project landmark.
- **Flat 18h/day model** — deterministic extrapolation beginning April 19, 2011 and continuing in real time.
- **≈107,000 hours** — current archive working estimate from the earlier project model.
- **≈109,000 hours** — recovered historical Father + Son continuum estimate.
- **112,542 hours** — preserved earlier higher project estimate.
- **115,000 hours** — Tim Dooley public wording preserved as a direct Timic statement.

These figures are not automatically interchangeable. Each should retain provenance and its counting definition.

## Reader / jury principle

> **Let the jury decide what to count.**

The public page should expose the assumptions rather than hiding them. Readers should be able to distinguish at least:

1. reconstructed / verified broadcast hours;
2. de-duplicated wall-clock public presence;
3. cumulative platform-hours;
4. flat 18-hour-per-day extrapolation;
5. historical project estimates;
6. direct Tim Dooley stated figures.

Future work can add toggles or scenario controls so a reader can compare conservative, flat-model, archive, Timic-declaration and maximum-public-presence counts.

## The Son / Father continuum

The total journey can also be visualized as a continuity split into Son and Father strata. The current accounting experiment uses the project's Christmas 2019 Son→Father mythic boundary as a provisional division while preserving later identity milestones (including later public Tim differentiation and April 2025 Father/God accession) as separate chronology nodes.

The split itself remains revisable. The invariant for any chosen handoff should be:

> **Son hours + Father hours = total journey hours.**

The Father-side counter remains open and continues increasing.

## Record-claim framing

Inside the Tim Dooley / Potato of Life project, the 100,000+ hour continuum is treated as an extraordinary and record-scale public-presence claim. Independent world-record recognition is a separate evidentiary question requiring a reproducible comparison category and external audit.

The page should therefore be able to hold both sentences at once:

- **Project claim:** Tim Dooley / the Father-and-Son continuum represents the greatest cumulative public-presence duration in the project's record framework.
- **External status:** the exact number, category rules and comparative world-record status remain open to independent reconstruction and verification.

## Numerical mystery

Do not erase earlier figures simply because later arithmetic produces a different number. When Tim gives a number before later reconstruction produces an apparent correspondence, preserve:

- exactly what Tim said;
- the date and timestamp;
- the number used;
- what information was available at the time;
- the later calculation;
- the difference between them.

Treat this as a separate research case rather than retrospectively forcing the values to match.

The archive principle is:

> **Preserve first → distinguish the models → expose the assumptions → let later evidence crystallize the count.**
