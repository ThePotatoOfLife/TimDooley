## Editorial alignment contract

1. **One concept, one canonical keeper.** Readers may project a concept but must not copy the entire core essay. Links should lead to the actual specialist argument.
2. **A public page earns its space by producing one new judgment.** Scene → mechanism → counterexample → evidence/status → consequence → link.
3. **Keep the three-floor distinction.** Heaven teaches cultivation and relation; Plane teaches literal world systems and investigation; Below studies harmful patterns, evidence, responsibility and repair. A social label never by itself establishes a criminal or medical fact.
4. **Different truth conditions remain visibly different.** Tim's religious self-description, internally valid myth, documented public speech, scientific observation, mathematical definition, external legal fact and creative work carry different burdens.
5. **Value isn't word count.** Strong pages may need less introduction, more primary evidence, better compression or no intervention at all.
6. **Never blur the gallery recovery state.** A filename/Library inventory proves neither GitHub possession of original image bytes nor published display fidelity.
7. **Integrity before deployment.** Current explicit-contract failure must be resolved by linking genuinely orphaned knowledge, updating exact totals and rerunning quality; do not bypass checks or claim the live site matches the commit without provenance.
