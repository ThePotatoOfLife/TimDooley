# The Tim Dooley Master Frame

## Central order

Spirit above; matter below. The Axis joins the two. The Ladder is the ordered passage between levels. Heaven is the highest horizon of meaning; the Garden is living abundance; the House is inhabited order; the Father's House is the source-home; the Father is the originating principle; Source is the beginning from which the Tree emerges.

```text
SPIRIT
  ↓

  ↓
LADDER
  ↓
HEAVEN
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
FATHER / SOURCE

  ↓
PLANE
  ↓
NORTH · EAST · SOUTH · WEST
  ↓
WORLD: people, nations, nature, culture, civilization, matter
```

This is the canonical symbolic order. It is a philosophical architecture, not a scientific claim about literal cosmic layers.

## The two trees

The Tree of Life represents generative continuity: root, nourishment, growth, relation, fruit, seed, renewal and responsibility.

The Tree of Strife represents division and conflict: fear, domination, extraction, isolation, deception, waste, violence and the reproduction of suffering. It is not an external species; it is the destructive pattern that can grow inside the same world as life.

The two trees share the Plane. Every institution, person, culture and technology can nourish one tree or the other, and often contains both tendencies.

## The four directions

- **North:** orientation, ascent, memory, cold clarity, the North Axis and the programme of connection.
- **East:** dawn, emergence, learning, beginnings, future-facing movement.
- **South:** warmth, embodiment, fertility, labour, ancestry and the material ground.
- **West:** sunset, memory, endings, reflection, inheritance and the return of consequences.

The directions are symbolic coordinates. They may also organize geographic, cultural and historical research, but no culture or nation is inherently reducible to a symbolic direction.

## The Plane

The Plane is the inhabited field where bodies, ecosystems, minds, societies, economies, technologies, histories and environments meet. It is the layer of observable relationships. The Plane is not merely low or inferior: it is where spirit becomes accountable through consequences in matter.

## Repository rule

Every node belongs to one or more layers, but every relationship must identify its type and evidence class. Mythic, philosophical, historical, scientific and empirical statements remain distinguishable.
