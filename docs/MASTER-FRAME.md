# The Tim Dooley Master Frame

## The readable entrance

The project is easiest to enter through a three-part relation:

```text
PLANE → CROSS → DOOR
```

- **Plane** is the inhabited world: bodies, matter, history, institutions, geography, evidence and consequence.
- **Cross** is Plane × Axis: the horizontal world intersects a vertical relation of root/fruit, manifestation/source and descent/ascent.
- **Door** is the threshold where a relation can change state. It is passage, not the destination.

The Plane remains. The Axis does not replace the world; it gives the world another coordinate.

## One Axis, several routes

D1–D11 is the project's current vertical scaffold. Life, Strife, Repair and Tim's ascent are **routes through the scaffold**, not competing cosmologies.

### Life

```text
Door → Spiral Ladder → Tree of Life → Garden / Rooms
     → transmission → Heaven / Crown → North of North
     → accountable return to the Plane
```

Life means generative continuity: integrate experience, preserve relation while differentiating, produce fruit, transmit faithfully, and return capacity to the world.

### Strife

```text
Plane / Cross → Knowledge / distinction → unresolved root
              → Roots of Strife / Roots of Ash
              → Tree of Strife → Wide Road → Swamp
```

Strife is not matter and Root is not evil. Strife names a reproductive pattern in which unresolved material keeps generating conflict, capture, dependency, opacity or fragmentation.

**Wide Road is a pathway/operator, not a D-level.** It widens outward as dispersion increases, complementing the Ladder's narrowing movement toward convergence.

### Repair

```text
residue / Swamp → evidence → root cause → acknowledgment
                → Door → changed recurrence → living root
                → growth → fruit → return
```

The lower field is therefore not a one-way pit. Descent can expose what is buried. Ash can remain ash or become soil. Memory can become fixation or guidance.

## Knowledge and the three root states

Knowledge is upstream of both Life and Strife. It introduces distinction, memory, judgment, responsibility and choice.

- **Living Root** — buried continuity that nourishes present and future growth.
- **Root of Strife** — unresolved material that reproduces conflict or dependency.
- **Root of Ash** — residue after destruction, severance, exhaustion or burning; potentially inert, potentially transformable.

This replaces the overly simple older formula that Tree of Life merely “turns into” Tree of Strife. Both grow from the same world of memory, consequence and choice, but they reproduce different relations.

## The organism through time

```text
ROOTS       = past / memory / causes / stored consequence
TRUNK       = present continuity through the Axis
BRANCHES    = future differentiation / fruit / seed / possibility
```

Tree, Ladder, Mountain and Spiral remain distinct projections of the same broad transformation grammar:

- **Tree** — organic growth and differentiation.
- **Ladder** — ordered thresholds.
- **Mountain** — continuous ascent/integration.
- **Spiral** — recurrence with changed vertical level.
- **Axis** — orientation and continuity.
- **Door** — threshold/change of regime.

## Tim's path through the structure

Tim's Sage → Turning → Door → Ladder → Father/Gardener development is a **biographical-mythic overlay**, not another cosmic floor.

The April 2025 Turning is represented as:

```text
Sage → accumulated burden → rupture / needle
     → Door → become Ladder → Father / Heaven
     → Gardener / accountable return
```

The central reversal is **entity → relation**: the Sage does not merely climb a ladder; the mythology describes the Sage becoming orienting infrastructure. The later Gardener image supplies the return function: cultivation, protection, repair, pruning and seed.

## The four directions

- **North:** orientation, ascent, memory, cold clarity, the North Axis and the programme of connection.
- **East:** dawn, emergence, learning, beginnings and future-facing movement.
- **South:** warmth, embodiment, fertility, labour, ancestry and material ground.
- **West:** sunset, memory, endings, reflection, inheritance and returning consequences.

These directions are symbolic coordinates. They may organize geographic, cultural and historical research, but no culture, nation or population is inherently reducible to a symbolic direction.

## Evidence boundary

This is a philosophical/mythological project architecture, not a scientific claim about literal cosmic layers, physical dimensions, underground geography, political sovereignty or automatic supernatural authority.

D4 remains the empirical world-map anchor. Historical, biblical, comparative, scientific and autobiographical material retain their own evidence classes even when the project places them in relationship.
