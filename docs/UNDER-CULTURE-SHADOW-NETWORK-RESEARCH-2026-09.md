# Under-Culture / Shadow Network Research — September 2026

## Why this layer exists

The project has repeatedly approached AIPAC, Israel365, Palantir, NGOs, political movements, cult/high-control groups, Josh Moon, Kiwi Farms, the Farm, Sektur, the Swamp, Hollywood, Epstein, trafficking/exploitation, intelligence, private investigators, hate maps and controlled-opposition claims as if they might reveal one deeper object.

The strongest conclusion from this pass is that the deeper object is **not one proven hidden network**. It is a set of recurring mechanisms that can overlap:

- money and grantmaking;
- political spending and lobbying;
- religious or ideological mobilization;
- media and narrative production;
- technology and data integration;
- intelligence and private investigation;
- institutional reputation protection;
- abuse/exploitation and failures of protection;
- online attention extraction, archive culture and persistent identity capture;
- classification systems for hate, extremism or deviance;
- opposition management, infiltration or co-optation;
- secrecy, opacity, jurisdictional fragmentation and poor accountability.

The project becomes more powerful when it maps these mechanisms separately and then asks where they actually intersect.

## 1. Political-finance and advocacy are not the same thing

AIPAC PAC is an observable political-finance entity. FEC records identify it as C00797670 and report $50.06 million in receipts and $48.46 million in disbursements for 1 January 2025 through 31 July 2026, including $45.90 million in contributions to other committees.

United Democracy Project is a separate Super PAC, C00799031. Over the same period the FEC reports $107.54 million in receipts, $77.21 million in disbursements and $50.93 million in independent expenditures.

These records show concrete mechanisms:

**donor -> committee -> expenditure/contribution -> candidate/election**.

They do not by themselves show control of candidates, media, government agencies or other advocacy organizations.

Israel365 belongs in a different family. Its own site describes founder Rabbi Tuly Weisz, a 2012 founding date, and a mission focused on mobilizing Jews and Christians around Torah education, advocacy and support for Israel. Teach For Israel Inc, EIN 45-4041360, is a separate nonprofit legal entity whose FY2024 IRS-derived filing data shows $2.625 million in revenue and $2.697 million in expenses.

The key lesson is that **religious mobilization, media, charity, advocacy and electoral money are distinct mechanisms even when they concern the same country or issue**.

## 2. The nonprofit and donor layer is structurally important

The Vine & Fig Tree lead illustrates why entity resolution matters.

Vine & Fig Tree Institute I Inc, EIN 99-2090467, reported about $3.0 million in FY2024 revenue and an $850,000 grant to Vine & Fig Tree Fund Inc. Vine & Fig Tree Fund Inc, EIN 99-2100887, reported $850,536 in revenue and $742,095 in expenses. Its grant schedule reports downstream grants including Combat Hate Foundation, October 7th Coalition and Merona Leadership Foundation.

The correct graph is therefore not “Figtree = hidden network.” It is:

**Institute -> Fund -> downstream grantees**, with each legal entity, officer, filing year, amount and program purpose represented separately.

An additional claimed Stand Together Trust -> Vine & Fig Tree Institute grant remains in the research queue until the exact primary filing entry is independently verified in the repository.

Other organizations worth comparing as independently typed entities include Israel on Campus Coalition, StandWithUs/Israel Emergency Alliance, Maccabee Task Force Foundation and the Tikvah Fund. Their IRS filings show that there is a large, measurable nonprofit/educational/advocacy ecosystem around Israel and Jewish public life. That ecosystem should be mapped through money, programs, personnel and declared mission rather than collapsed into an accusation of centralized control.

## 3. Palantir shows the state-technology lane

Palantir's 2024 SEC filing says the company agreed to a strategic partnership with Israel's Ministry of Defense to supply technology assisting Israel in the ongoing war. The same filing says its Ukraine- and Israel-related operations were not material to the company's financial position or results at the time.

This is a documented edge:

**technology company -> strategic partnership -> defense ministry -> wartime technology support**.

It is not evidence that Palantir controls pro-Israel political advocacy or that advocacy organizations direct Palantir.

The Under-Culture atlas should therefore distinguish:

- political influence;
- public advocacy;
- procurement/contracting;
- intelligence/security capability;
- operational deployment;
- data integration;
- alleged misuse.

These are different power forms.

## 4. Turning Point USA is a mass-mobilization node, not automatically part of another network

Turning Point USA's FY ending June 2025 filing reports $115.45 million in revenue, $103.94 million in expenses and $114.46 million in contributions.

The important analytical point is that a large ideological or youth-mobilization organization can overlap with donors, political candidates, media figures and issue coalitions while still retaining internal disagreement and strategic autonomy.

The graph should actively record factional conflict and disagreement. **Counterevidence is not noise; it is part of network structure.** A coalition that publicly fractures over foreign policy, Israel, candidates or ideology is not well described by a single-control model.

## 5. Controlled opposition should be decomposed

“Controlled opposition” is usually too vague to enter the empirical graph as a fact.

But several underlying mechanisms are historically real and testable:

- infiltration;
- disruption;
- deliberate sowing of discord;
- co-optation through resources or access;
- concealed sponsorship;
- astroturfing;
- donor capture;
- strategic amplification of a weaker or more polarizing opponent;
- informant/provocateur allegations;
- opposition fragmentation;
- platform dependency.

The FBI's own history of COINTELPRO acknowledges infiltration, sowing discord and efforts to discredit groups, including cases where there was little or no evidence of unlawful activity. That is a documented historical anchor for some mechanisms commonly described in conspiracy language.

It does **not** make every modern opposition group “controlled.” Each modern claim still requires its own evidence.

The graph should therefore test a claim using:

**claimed mechanism -> actor -> funding/order/access -> dated action -> observable effect -> corroboration -> counterevidence**.

## 6. High-control groups should be measured through mechanisms

“Cult” is often rhetorically useful but analytically weak when applied as a binary label.

Janja Lalich's bounded-choice framework offers four dimensions: charismatic authority, transcendent belief system, systems of control and systems of influence. The project can combine this with observable indicators such as isolation, punishment of dissent, surveillance, dependency, labor/financial/sexual exploitation and high exit costs.

NXIVM is useful as an anchor case because Keith Raniere's racketeering, sex-trafficking and forced-labor-related conduct was established through a federal criminal trial. The lesson is not that unusual beliefs prove abuse. The lesson is that a high-control model becomes empirically meaningful when it is tied to documented coercive mechanisms and legal findings.

The repository should therefore store:

**group -> mechanism -> evidence -> consequence -> legal/institutional status**,

not simply `cult: true`.

## 7. Hate mapping contains multiple ontologies

SPLC's hate-group list and ADL's HEAT Map are not the same kind of object.

SPLC classifies organizations under its stated methodology. ADL's HEAT Map tracks incidents of hate, extremism, antisemitism and terrorism using multiple data sources.

This gives the project a general rule:

**classifier -> methodology -> classification event -> target entity**

and separately:

**incident -> place/date -> affected party -> bias/category -> source**.

A watchdog label should never become a permanent intrinsic property of a person, organization or country. The repository should preserve who classified what, when, and under which methodology.

## 8. The abuse-protection axis is one of the most important real-world layers

A stronger concept than “elite conspiracy” is **institutional protection failure**.

The DOJ Inspector General's Nassar report documents a sequence of failures: delayed response, failure to notify appropriate authorities, poor documentation and later inaccurate/incomplete representations. Importantly, the OIG explicitly said it did not find evidence that these failures were motivated by an intent to interfere with the investigation.

That distinction is essential.

A system can catastrophically fail to protect people without evidence of a centralized conspiracy.

Mechanisms worth tracking across cases include:

- delayed reporting;
- failure to transfer information;
- jurisdictional fragmentation;
- poor documentation;
- reputation protection;
- retaliation;
- legal intimidation;
- private investigation;
- destruction/loss of records;
- weak oversight;
- inadequate victim pathways;
- later institutional minimization.

The UK government's summary of the Savile policing review likewise describes investigative and intelligence-sharing failures that enabled prolonged abuse.

These are measurable structures of the “underneath.”

## 9. Weinstein / Black Cube demonstrates private-intelligence suppression

The Weinstein case is one of the cleanest examples of the bridge from entertainment power into private intelligence.

The New Yorker published the Black Cube contract and reported that one objective was to obtain information useful to stopping publication of a negative newspaper story. Another objective involved obtaining Rose McGowan's manuscript.

This yields a concrete mechanism:

**powerful client -> lawyer/intermediary -> private-intelligence firm -> deceptive/surveillance activity -> accusers/reporters -> attempted information suppression**.

This is not evidence of a generalized Hollywood/intelligence conspiracy. It is evidence that private intelligence can be purchased and deployed inside reputation and information conflicts.

Hollywood should therefore be researched through:

- studio/agency ownership;
- legal representation;
- PR/crisis management;
- private investigation/intelligence;
- talent management;
- insurers;
- financiers;
- labor unions;
- distribution and advertising;
- documented abuse complaints and accountability systems.

## 10. Surveillance companies belong in a capability graph

The U.S. Commerce Department added NSO Group and Candiru to the Entity List in 2021, stating that evidence showed their spyware had been supplied to governments that used it to maliciously target officials, journalists, businesspeople, activists, academics and embassy workers.

Cellebrite represents a different but adjacent capability class. Its 2024 SEC filing says it had about 7,000 customers, around 5,300 of them government agencies, and that public-sector customers accounted for more than 90% of revenue.

These examples show why the graph must distinguish:

**capability -> vendor -> customer -> deployment -> legal authority -> alleged misuse -> investigation/sanction**.

Possessing a surveillance or forensic capability is not the same thing as documented misuse.

## 11. Epstein should become a corpus, not a list of suspect names

DOJ announced in January 2026 that nearly 3.5 million pages had been released under the Epstein Files Transparency Act, along with more than 2,000 videos and 180,000 images. DOJ also warned that the production could contain fake or falsely submitted material because responsive public submissions were included.

This means the repository needs strict edge types:

1. appears in a document;
2. appears in a contact record;
3. appears in a travel record;
4. documented business/financial relation;
5. witness/allegation;
6. investigation;
7. charge;
8. conviction or adjudicated finding.

No graph traversal may silently turn stage 1 into stage 8.

The victim/survivor layer should prioritize privacy, aggregated patterns, public court records and institutional lessons rather than spectacle.

## 12. The Farm and Sektur become the micro-social counterpart

The existing Farm theory is strong because it describes how a human being becomes a reusable source of attention:

**person -> visible subject -> recurring narrative -> archive -> reusable attention node**.

The Under-Culture layer extends this by treating archive infrastructure, reaction content, doxxing/tracking claims, platform migration, monetization and group identity as measurable relations.

The strongest empirical question is not “who is a Dog?” It is:

**who watches, archives, frames, monetizes, responds, migrates, moderates, litigates, provokes or preserves?**

Sektur should retain two levels if provenance is confirmed:

- a historical/subcultural referent around the Killstream/Ethan Ralph milieu;
- Tim's broader generalized Sektur concept: a social territory in which attention communities, adversarial archives and recurring roles become culturally self-reproducing.

## 13. The Timic interpretation becomes clearer when kept separate

The mythic layer remains useful precisely because it is not asked to do the work of empirical proof.

- **Farm** = cultivation/extraction of attention.
- **Sektur** = social territory of repeating attention roles.
- **Mud** = accumulated reputational, informational and legal residue.
- **Swamp** = deeper institutional and incentive environment where flow and accountability can become blocked.
- **Rubble Baby** = vulnerable life inside wreckage and protection failure.
- **Epstein Axis** = symbolic concentration of exploitation, secrecy, elite access and failed protection.
- **Tree of Strife** = root incentive -> mechanism -> actors -> outputs -> consequences -> seeds of reproduction.
- **Garden / tikkun** = transparency, protection, restitution, repair and restored capacity to exit the conflict loop.

The empirical system tells us **what happened and through which mechanisms**.

The Timic layer tells us **why Tim keeps looking at those mechanisms together**.

## 14. What may be “under culture”

A useful preliminary model is:

**surface culture**

media narratives, elections, celebrity, public institutions, public ideology

↓

**organizational layer**

campaign committees, nonprofits, donors, foundations, studios, agencies, think tanks, ministries, contractors, platforms

↓

**infrastructure layer**

payment rails, advertising, hosting, data vendors, law firms, PR, private intelligence, surveillance/forensics, procurement, archives

↓

**subcultural layer**

forums, group chats, reaction channels, pseudonymous communities, anti-fans, fandoms, gossip networks, rumor markets, street-level intermediaries

↓

**residue layer**

screenshots, dossiers, legal filings, reputational claims, buried complaints, unresolved victims, archived conflict, institutional memory

This is much closer to what the project calls **Mud / Swamp** than a single secret cabal model.

## 15. Research direction

The next highest-value work is to follow actual edges:

- donor -> nonprofit -> project -> downstream grantee;
- PAC -> expenditure -> candidate -> issue;
- company -> ministry/agency -> contract -> capability;
- lawyer/PR/private intelligence -> client -> target -> tactic;
- complaint -> institution -> response delay -> harm -> inquiry -> reform;
- platform -> community -> archive -> creator -> subject -> monetization;
- classifier -> methodology -> classification -> public/political consequence.

Then search for repeated mechanisms across domains.

That lets the project become smarter over time without needing to decide in advance that everything connects.
## 16. Information funnels: how signals move upward, downward and sideways

The project has often used phrases like “information war,” “narrative control” or “hidden network.” Those phrases become more useful when replaced by a chain of transformations.

A raw event rarely reaches a decision-maker unchanged. A post, clip, transaction, sensor reading, complaint or incident may first be collected, then archived, categorized, summarized, scored, modeled, briefed, reported, litigated, campaigned on or turned into a public message. The public response to that message then becomes new data.

A generic funnel looks like this:

**event / post / signal -> collector -> archive or dataset -> analyst / classifier / vendor -> report / dashboard / brief -> institutional or media decision -> public message or action -> reaction -> new signal**.

The important analytical question is not “is the information fake?” but **what happened to it at each handoff?**

Possible improvements include corroboration, deduplication, context recovery and professional verification.

Possible distortions include selective sampling, inherited categories, commercial incentives, duplicated sources that look independent, context loss, political messaging and the compression of ambiguity into one headline or score.

This is a better model of “top” and “bottom” than a pyramid with one controller. Different actors have different partial powers over collection, interpretation, distribution and consequence.

## 17. AIPAC / UDP: visible political influence, not invisible omnipotence

AIPAC belongs in the atlas because it is an unusually important and measurable U.S. foreign-policy advocacy organization. Its own public mission is to encourage and persuade the U.S. government to adopt policies strengthening the U.S.-Israel relationship. AIPAC PAC and United Democracy Project are legally distinct electoral-spending vehicles whose FEC filings make receipts, contributions and independent expenditures measurable.

That gives a concrete influence chain:

**donor -> PAC / Super PAC -> contribution or independent expenditure -> election -> officeholder / policy environment**.

This is real political power. It is also narrower than “control.” Campaign spending can change incentives, visibility and electoral risk without proving that an organization secretly commands candidates, intelligence agencies, media companies or unrelated Jewish or Israeli institutions.

AIPAC should therefore be compared with other large advocacy and electoral networks rather than treated as a mystical exception. The point is to understand lobbying and political finance as mechanisms.

## 18. Unit 8200: institutional skill can migrate without proving permanent command

Unit 8200 belongs in a different lane. The IDF describes it as a central collection unit within Israeli Military Intelligence with signals-intelligence, cyber, analysis and technological functions. The IDF and outside reporting also describe a substantial alumni presence in Israel's technology sector.

This creates a genuine network effect:

**intelligence institution -> training / selection / relationships -> alumni -> private technology -> commercial and government markets**.

That pathway matters because institutions can seed technical culture, trust networks and talent pools long after individuals leave formal service.

But the edge must be typed correctly. Former service is not evidence that every later company remains directed by the state. Alumni networks can produce influence, access and shared expertise without constituting a secret chain of command.

The project should therefore map:

- service history;
- founders and senior personnel;
- company creation;
- funding;
- government contracts;
- documented partnerships;
- publicly acknowledged intelligence relationships;
- and specific allegations of misuse separately.

## 19. Psy-Group: the private market for influence operations

Psy-Group is one of the clearest examples of why “information operations” should not be reserved for states.

Investigative reporting describes the Israeli firm as a private intelligence/influence company that recruited people with intelligence backgrounds and used covert personas and targeted influence techniques. The firm also discussed proposed election-influence work with people connected to the 2016 Trump campaign; reporting and later investigations must distinguish those proposals from actual completed operations and from measurable effect on the election.

The mechanism is nevertheless important:

**client -> private influence firm -> personas / targeting / content / collection -> audience -> measurement / report**.

That means capabilities historically associated with intelligence, propaganda or political campaigning can be purchased as services.

Psy-Group should therefore sit beside Black Cube, not beside AIPAC: one is a private influence/intelligence capability case; the other is an overt political-advocacy network.

## 20. Israel365 Action: overt religious-political mobilization

Israel365 Action belongs in yet another lane because its politics are public rather than hidden.

The organization describes itself as mobilizing Jews and Christians in support of Israel, Judeo-Christian values and Israeli sovereignty claims in Judea and Samaria. It also explicitly discusses advocacy against what it regards as misinformation and reports winning representation in the 2025 World Zionist Congress election.

That creates a visible mobilization chain:

**religious worldview -> media / education -> audience -> conference / rally / action alert -> political or institutional participation**.

This is useful for understanding how theology becomes politics without requiring a covert-network theory.

The project should preserve the organization's own wording, opponents' descriptions and independently observable activity separately.

## 21. NCRI, SPLC and ADL: classification is itself a form of power

The Network Contagion Research Institute, SPLC and ADL differ substantially, but all illustrate a layer the project should take seriously: **classification power**.

NCRI describes and studies cyber-social contagion, manipulation, extremism and misinformation; Rutgers has partnered with it in threat-analysis research and training. SPLC classifies organizations under its own hate-group methodology. ADL maintains incident and extremism datasets and publishes movement research.

The common mechanism is:

**raw public material -> methodology -> category / report -> journalist, platform, institution or policymaker -> downstream consequence**.

A classifier can therefore shape the field it studies even when it has no legal authority. A label can change discoverability, reputational risk, platform attention, donor behavior or institutional response.

This does not make the classifier a truth oracle or a conspirator. It means methodology, funding, error correction, category definitions and institutional uptake become part of the power map.

The April 2026 federal indictment involving SPLC's alleged use of paid informants is a particularly strong reason to keep two layers separate: SPLC's classification outputs are one analytical object; the criminal allegations and SPLC's denials are another. An indictment is not a conviction.

## 22. Groypers: distributed ideological networks can route around platform restrictions

The Groyper / America First milieu is relevant because it shows how a political subculture can operate as a distributed memetic network rather than a formal membership organization.

Current watchdog research describes networks of affiliated or sympathetic accounts clipping, reposting and collaborating around Nick Fuentes' content even when Fuentes himself faces platform restrictions.

The mechanism is familiar from older troll culture but politically sharper:

**figure / ideology -> clips / memes / affiliated accounts -> platform distribution -> audience -> replication -> recruitment / factional identity**.

This is a useful bridge between the earlier Sektur analysis and contemporary political culture. The techniques—irony, clipping, adversarial identity, distributed amplification—can persist while the ideological content changes.

Affiliation still needs careful typing. Watching a stream, sharing a meme, appearing on a show and being an organized participant are not the same edge.

## 23. Palantir: information power becomes operational when data can be joined and acted on

Palantir belongs in the infrastructure layer.

The company's public filings document extensive government business and a strategic partnership with Israel's Ministry of Defense after October 7, 2023. Palantir has also publicly disputed allegations that it built or participated in specific automated targeting systems in Gaza; those denials should be stored as company claims rather than treated as independent resolution of contested reporting.

The important general mechanism is:

**many data sources -> integrated model / operational interface -> institutional user -> decision or action**.

That is qualitatively different from lobbying or propaganda. Data-integration firms can make already-existing government information easier to query, correlate and operationalize.

The relevant questions are therefore about procurement, data provenance, permissions, model design, human review, deployment context, auditability and documented misuse—not whether “Palantir” is a synonym for an intelligence agency.

## 24. Epstein: elite access is a network problem, but adjacency is not guilt

The Epstein corpus belongs in the atlas because it demonstrates how wealth, social access, intermediaries, travel, finance, institutions and exploitation can coexist in a real criminal network.

It is also one of the clearest examples of why careless network visualization can become defamatory.

Every edge must preserve its type:

**contact record != travel record != business relation != witness allegation != investigation != charge != conviction**.

A name appearing in released material establishes only the relation actually shown by the material.

The strongest research questions are institutional:

- who enabled access;
- where complaints or warnings failed;
- which financial, legal or social structures reduced accountability;
- which claims were established in court;
- and how victim protection can be improved.

The weak question is “who appears near Epstein?” The stronger question is “what documented mechanism converted elite access into reduced friction for exploitation or reduced accountability?”

## 25. Shivat Zion: a useful dead end because it teaches entity resolution

“Shivat Zion” currently illustrates a different research lesson.

Publicly identifiable organizations under that name include an aliyah/integration nonprofit and a separate charitable/security-support organization. The shared name does not establish common legal identity, political coordination or an information-war role.

This is exactly the kind of lead that should sometimes remain a dead end.

A good hidden-network atlas becomes more trustworthy when it records **failed connections** as well as successful ones. The absence of an evidenced edge is information.

## 26. What the reader should now be able to see

These cases do not resolve into one master network. They resolve into several recurring power forms:

- **money power** — donors, PACs, grants, procurement;
- **intelligence power** — collection, analysis, secrecy, institutional expertise;
- **influence power** — persuasion, covert personas, targeted messaging, narrative testing;
- **classification power** — defining categories that institutions and media later reuse;
- **infrastructure power** — joining data, controlling access, hosting, payment and technical continuity;
- **mobilization power** — turning religious, political or cultural identity into coordinated public action;
- **archive power** — deciding which past remains easy to retrieve;
- **network power** — moving through personal, professional and alumni relationships;
- **criminal power** — exploiting secrecy, access, money and institutional failure;
- **state power** — law, intelligence, procurement, coercion and adjudication.

The phrase **hidden network** is most useful when it means: a set of real edges that are not obvious from the public surface but can be reconstructed from filings, contracts, personnel, grants, technical infrastructure, court records and documented communications.

It becomes least useful when it means: everything disliked belongs to one invisible controller.

The project's job is therefore to make hidden edges visible without inventing edges that are not there.
