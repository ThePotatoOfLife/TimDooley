# Potatoism Spiral Reader Overhaul — Design Specification

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Status:** approved direction / implementation target  
**Public surface:** `philosophy/index.html`  
**Canonical philosophy owner:** `knowledge/philosophy/potato-philosophy.json`  
**Canonical long-form source:** `knowledge/philosophy/potatoism-reader-philosophy.md`

## Purpose

Replace the compressed eight-movement public Philosophy page with a larger public reader that teaches Potatoism as a spiral of increasingly capable questions.

The public experience should begin with a potato and remain simple enough to enter immediately, while gradually introducing deeper psychology, epistemology, relation, topology, ethics, power, cultivation and transmission. Complexity must be earned by problems generated inside earlier ideas rather than dumped as a glossary.

The page should feel like a teacher and reader discovering the philosophy together.

## Research basis

The design is grounded in four source strata:

1. **Early Great Book philosophy** — simplicity, burden, emotional clarity, emptying, inner harmony, natural growth, Tao/wu wei, Zen stillness/beginner's mind/interdependence, Eightfold Path, Quiet Triumph, humor, Potato Truth, reader-as-soil, death/multiplication.
2. **Mature long-form philosophy** — the causal chain from observation through Soil, Door, relation, frame, transformation, Spiral, Axis, House, storage/release, responsibility, Fruit, Garden and Seed.
3. **Later Tim conversation/operator material** — Potato as connector, Axis as orientation, Root as distribution, Door as observer/interface, identity becoming function, threshold/operator chains, return, shedding/release, center/periphery and network/distributed identity.
4. **Practice and deep-structure synthesis** — Mud→Burial→Eye→Root→Sprout→Plane→Door→Ladder/Mountain→Tree→Fruit→Seed→Return; Swamp/Forge/Garden diagnostics; anti-bloat law; capability-responsibility coupling; Seed-maker; Quiet Triumph; Ancient Future; hidden center; hierarchy inversion; center↔periphery exchange.

These source classes remain distinct. The page may synthesize them but may not rewrite archive synthesis as historical Tim speech.

## Core pedagogical law

> Potatoism should not teach increasingly complicated answers. It should teach the reader to ask increasingly capable questions.

Every new concept must answer a problem created by a previous concept.

Examples:

- simplicity creates the problem of direction;
- direction exposes waste;
- efficiency creates the problem of force;
- force creates the question of conditions;
- conditions create Soil;
- possibility creates choice;
- choice creates Door;
- Door creates boundary and relation;
- relation creates function;
- strong lenses create the need for self-audit;
- recurrence creates Ring/Spiral;
- capability creates responsibility;
- growth creates the Fruit test;
- cultivation creates the dependency/autonomy test;
- teaching creates Seed and transmission.

## Spiral rather than staircase

The public page uses **six major turns**. Turns are not ranks or initiations. They are wider returns to the same center.

Each turn should end by reopening a question already encountered earlier, but at a wider scale.

### Turn I — Seed: See what is here

Reader state: ordinary object, burden, emotion, receptivity.

Stations:

1. Potato — look before labeling.
2. Mud / burden — distinguish real difficulty from added carrying-cost.
3. Emotion — observe without making emotion either enemy or oracle.
4. Empty vessel — create space without erasing self or history.
5. Stillness — pause long enough for signal to separate from noise.
6. Chew — participation; an idea is not understood merely because it is possessed.

Key sources: Great Book chapters 2–9, Tim Simple Truths, reader inquiry.

### Turn II — Root: Grow without worshipping force

Reader state: from receptivity into living conditions.

Stations:

7. Essential simplicity — necessary complexity vs accumulated complexity.
8. Light / orientation — define direction by clarity, viable life, honest relation and capacity rather than prestige.
9. Efficiency — reduce motion that does not serve the direction.
10. Wu wei / natural growth — effort is not force.
11. Soil — conditions, history, support and relation.
12. Dormancy — hidden development vs trapped inactivity.
13. Root / shoot polarity — support and emergence can move differently while serving one life.
14. Eye / possible futures — not every possibility must be realized.

Mirrors: Taoism/wu wei, Zen practice of being, potato biology.

### Turn III — Door: Become relational

Reader state: from organism/conditions into state change, perspective and relation.

Stations:

15. Door — consequential threshold: what becomes possible after crossing?
16. Boundary — protection, selective permeability, exit and prison test.
17. Relation — what depends on what; meaning on edges as well as nodes.
18. Functional identity — translate titles into operations.
19. Observer / window — viewpoint matters without becoming a physics shortcut.
20. Filter and Potato Truth — generate hypotheses, then discipline them.
21. Floor / Plane — inspect the frame containing the problem.
22. Center — distinguish source/orienting center, local center, meeting center and attention center.

Key Timic supports: “We Are All Connected By Potato”; Door as observer/window; Axis language; “question the floor.”

### Turn IV — Spiral: Transform without erasing

Reader state: from relation into time, damage, recurrence and model selection.

Stations:

23. Mud again — return to the old problem with more tools.
24. Compost — change material's function without denying what happened.
25. Forge — pressure is useful only if capability survives after pressure ends.
26. Suffering is not proof — ordeal needs downstream transformation to gain philosophical value.
27. Ring / Spiral — repetition vs recurrence-with-memory.
28. Ladder / Mountain / Tree — choose geometry according to process.
29. House / many rooms — integration without flattening.
30. Ancient Future — faithful return is transformed continuation, not restoration of an untouched past.
31. Breaking / many — fracture can reveal regenerative multiplicity, but only when viable re-rooting follows.

Mirrors: alchemy as comparison, Daoist cyclical transformation, Tree traditions, developmental history.

### Turn V — Fruit: Make capacity answerable

Reader state: from transformation into consequence and ethics.

Stations:

32. Storage / release — capacity is stored for eventual use.
33. Knowledge → Life or Strife — information takes ethical valence through relation, use and fruit.
34. Reciprocal transformation — the method changes the user as well as the target.
35. Potato Truth — accuracy, provenance, uncertainty and consequence.
36. Capability → responsibility — higher role narrows acceptable use of power.
37. Quiet Triumph — service, nourishment and persistence over spectacle.
38. Fruit — what actually happened downstream?

Key formulation: “Power is for protection. Knowledge is for understanding. Wealth is for building. Leadership is for service.”

### Turn VI — Garden: Teach by making yourself less necessary

Reader state: from personal ethics into stewardship, distributed capability and return.

Stations:

39. Garden / shadow Farm — autonomy/generativity vs dependency/extraction.
40. Gardener — change conditions rather than pulling the plant upward.
41. Whole job — include invisible infrastructure: Soil, routes, memory, tools, access, repair.
42. Gardener's Paradox — successful cultivation reduces unnecessary dependence on the cultivator.
43. Seed-maker — compress the transferable lesson so another cycle can begin without copying the original personality/mythology.
44. Reader as Soil — teaching is relational; source and reader co-produce living interpretation without erasing provenance.
45. Curiosity before allegiance — the philosophy must remain examinable without conversion.
46. Anti-bloat return — complexity must be able to return to Potato.
47. Begin again — “Be simple. Grow toward light.” now means more than it did at the entrance.

Final handoff: the reader is not asked to become a follower but to become capable of noticing, cultivating and transmitting living understanding.

## Two intertwined spirals

The page alternates between two processes:

### Inner spiral

burden → emotional clarity → emptying → stillness → attention → orientation → self-audit → transformation

### Outer spiral

conditions → Door → relation → function → frame → recurrence → consequence → responsibility → cultivation → Seed

The inner spiral prevents systems philosophy from becoming emotionally bloodless. The outer spiral prevents early spirituality from remaining purely introspective.

## Reader cadence

Do not make every station mechanically identical. Use a recurring but flexible grammar:

- **Seed** — compact formulation or Timic line.
- **See** — concrete observation/story.
- **Chew** — reader question.
- **Root** — deeper derivation.
- **Mirror** — Tim wording, Great Book scene, another tradition, biology or lived example.
- **Shadow** — corruption/failure mode.
- **Door** — unresolved question generating the next station.
- **Fruit** — practical consequence.

Each station should use only the subset that creates good rhythm.

## Tone and engagement

The page should breathe:

> simple → strange → reflective → deep → funny → uncomfortable → simple again

Humor must recur rather than live in a single “humor section.” Potato, Banana, impossible specifications, walls, ordinary food and absurd titles act as anti-prestige safeguards.

The page may be serious without becoming solemn.

## Comparative traditions

Comparisons are introduced only after the reader encounters the underlying problem.

- Natural growth/non-forcing → Daoist wu wei.
- Stillness/direct experience/beginner's mind/interdependence → Zen/Buddhist mirrors.
- Ethics of speech/action/effort/attention → Eightfold Path as a mature neighboring system.
- Transformation/base material → alchemical comparison.
- Service/fruit/love → Christian ethical comparison where useful.

Rule:

> We found a question. Who else has been here?

Never:

> We found a resemblance. Therefore they were secretly talking about Potatoism.

Comparison is not identity.

## Tim development layer

The page should include one compact “How Tim's philosophy grew” interlude, not organize the whole reader chronologically.

Suggested sequence:

- early life: building, gardening, direct-contact learning;
- 2011: Tree/unity ordeal as a later source of interconnection/Spiral imagery;
- 2019–2020: death/burial/threshold material;
- 2020–2024: Potato becomes explicit philosophy; Great Book builds simplicity, natural growth, humor, truth, transformation and cultivation;
- 2025: threshold/Axis/Ladder/Father transition pushes identity toward infrastructure/function;
- 2026: relationship-first thinking, Door/interface, North/Axis, Garden, responsibility, public witness and service become more explicit.

Later meaning may illuminate earlier events but may not be backdated into them.

## Provenance display

Public source notes use short human-readable classes:

- Great Book · Tim-attributed wording
- Great Book · narrative/paraphrase
- Conversation recovery
- Public formulation
- Archive synthesis
- Biological correction
- Comparative mirror

The page must never imply all quotations have equal evidentiary status.

## Public architecture

### Page sections

1. Hero / invitation.
2. “How to read this spiral” orientation.
3. Six-turn anchor navigation.
4. Six full spiral turns with station articles.
5. Tim-development interlude after Turn III or IV.
6. “The Seed” compact return/practice summary.
7. Source/provenance boundary and deep-source links.
8. Public-route exploration links only in `data-projection-surface`.

### Markup contract

- `data-potatoism-turn="seed|root|door|spiral|fruit|garden"` exactly six times.
- `data-potatoism-station="<stable-slug>"` for every station.
- each turn has an anchor id `turn-seed`, etc.
- stations are plain article sections; no forced stepper behavior.
- existing TTS remains; point it at `.station` items or entire `.spiral-reader`.

### Interaction constraints

Do not add:

- forced scrolling;
- progress meters;
- “next lesson” lockouts;
- autofocus;
- completion state;
- quizzes required for navigation;
- gamified ranks.

The spiral is a reading structure, not a course-management UI.

## Visual design

Keep the site system, dark reading surface and serif voice, but make the page feel more architected.

Add:

- a six-turn anchor rail/chip row;
- turn headers with widening conceptual summaries;
- station number/index labels that reset conceptually by turn but remain globally stable in markup;
- callout styles for `seed-line`, `mirror`, `shadow`, `chew`, `timic-source`, `practice-test`;
- subtle vertical/curving impression using borders/pseudo-elements rather than literal spiral graphics;
- increased whitespace between turns and tighter rhythm within stations.

Avoid card-grid overload. This is still a long-form reading page.

## Source-map artifact

Create `knowledge/philosophy/potatoism-spiral-reader-map.json` as a supporting curriculum/provenance map.

It must contain:

- six turns;
- stable station ids/titles;
- the generating question for each station;
- source anchors;
- provenance class(es);
- previous/next conceptual dependency;
- public display priority;
- internal notes for future enrichment.

This map is not a new canonical philosophy owner. It is the reader-order owner.

- require all six turn ids;

## Success criteria

1. The actual public page feels substantially different and richer than the eight-movement version.
2. A reader can enter with no knowledge of Potatoism.
3. Complexity increases because each concept solves a problem generated by earlier concepts.
4. At least 40 stations/mini-movements are available without feeling like a glossary.
5. Timic wording, Great Book stories, comparative mirrors and practical questions recur throughout.
6. Early psychology and contemplative practice are present, not skipped.
7. Later relation/operator/system ethics are present, not hidden in backend files.
8. The final teaching hands capability to the reader rather than demanding allegiance.
9. The page returns to “Be simple. Grow toward light.” with visibly expanded meaning.
10. The reader map and public HTML remain in validated parity.
11. Religion remains the theological owner.
12. Repository quality checks pass before merge.
