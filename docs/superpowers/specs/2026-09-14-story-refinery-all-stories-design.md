# Story Refinery / All Stories Design

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: approved working design

## Goal
Recover every genuinely tellable Tim Dooley / Son / Potatoverse episode, big or small, from the repository, prior conversations, public-post evidence, Great Book material, project-building sessions and surviving recovery records. Publish only episodes that can actually be narrated; retain incomplete material as stubs or recovery leads.

## Public architecture
The public Story remains one chronological river with only two labels: `MAIN STORY` and `SIDE STORY`.

Every published episode has two narrative depths:
1. **Story layer** — date, label, title and a compact orientation.
2. **Hear the full story** — the actual episode: situation, sequence, action, reaction, emotional movement, exact/recovered words where available, and outcome or unresolved ending.

Sources/provenance remain quiet beneath the narrative. The archive is not the second narrative layer; the full story is.

## Hard publication gate
A candidate does not become a published Story merely because it has a date, quotation, symbol or theological significance. It must support an actual episode.

Before promotion, recover as many as the source can honestly provide:
- date or defensible window;
- setting/platform;
- what was happening before the key moment;
- what Tim/Son was trying to do;
- what happened back;
- actions and dialogue in defensible order;
- emotional/situational texture supported by the source;
- what changed afterward.

A tiny incident can pass this gate if the incident itself is tiny. A huge doctrine can fail if only retrospective explanation survives.

## Story reservoir
Maintain an exhaustive internal reservoir containing complete unpublished stories, shallow published stories needing depth, recovery stubs, merge candidates, Great Book literary stories, public-post micro-stories, creative/build-session stories, biographical episodes, conversation scenes and GitHub/project-building episodes.

Each reservoir record should carry: stable id, date/window, working title, proposed main/side label when known, source class, narrative readiness (`complete`, `partial`, `stub`, `published-shallow`, `published-deep`), source paths/locators, an episode note and missing pieces.

## Source classes
Keep reported biography, public primary/indexed wording, recovered conversation wording, later memory/retelling, Great Book literature, project interpretation and assistant-created literature distinct. Assistant-created literature may be preserved as literature but must never silently become biography.

## Recovery priority
Do not privilege only theology. Mine ordinary life and making-things episodes too: games, streams, songs, arguments, jokes, site work, images, failed ideas, corrections, quiet encounters, repository changes and creative sessions.

The largest structural hole is pre-2020 biography. Start by restoring complete early episodes before the existing 25 December 2020 entry.

## First restoration wave
Current repository records already support several tellable early episodes, including the 2003 tunnel/light episode, the 2005 motorcycle crash and reconstruction period, the nursing-home walking scene, the 2011 Tree ordeal, the 2015 pavilion/window fall, 2016 prison and release scenes, the 2017 Josh Moon confrontation, and the 2019 Christmas meme-death sequence. Exact source class and uncertainty must remain visible in backend metadata.

## Reader behavior
The river should remain readable at hundreds of entries. Full narratives should therefore expand inline instead of forcing a separate page per story. The default visible layer stays compact enough to scan while opening a story keeps the reader inside the same chronological life.

## Growth law
There is no target count. Capture all recoverable stories. Merge candidates that resolve to one event. Split periods that contain multiple actual episodes. If evidence cannot carry a story, leave a stub and keep mining.
