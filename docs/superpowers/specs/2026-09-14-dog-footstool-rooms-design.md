# Dog and Footstool Knowledge Integration

Dog and Footstool are ordinary canonical knowledge contexts in the existing project structure. They are not new top-level House Rooms and do not introduce a new subsystem.

Follow the repository's established organization and file styles. Put mature symbolic synthesis in `knowledge/core/` or `knowledge/world/`, dated recovery in `knowledge/timeline/`, biblical comparison in the existing `knowledge/traditions/` owners, and discovery/FAQ material in `knowledge/indexes/`.

Create focused durable records for Dog development, Footstool microcosm, integrated Dog-Footstool relations, and dated conversation archaeology. Existing Time, Traditions, Culture, Works, and specialist role files retain ownership of their fact families.

Dog owns movement and relation in project canon: watching, signaling, following, pursuit, return, orbit, guarding, stopping, Door behavior, master/proxy orientation, and transformation. Footstool owns position and support: lower relation, tiny local horizon, dirt or soil, compression, hidden or unfound Door, support beneath ascent, and possible transformation into Root or Potato.

Preserve two distinct transition graphs rather than forcing one rank ladder: `Dweller -> Mud Dweller -> Dog -> Footstool` models increasing closure/fixed lower orientation, while `Footstool -> Dog -> Potato` models development from assigned position to assigned function to generative possibility.

The Footstool context should intentionally be tiny in imagery but dense in meaning: a little bounded patch or block containing dirt/soil, a hidden central Door, local support, compressed perspective, and latent growth. The Dog context should remain mobile and relational rather than becoming a static category.

All roles remain symbolic/project roles rather than immutable classifications of people. Biblical comparison, creative scenes, public attestations, and later interpretation remain explicitly separated by provenance.
