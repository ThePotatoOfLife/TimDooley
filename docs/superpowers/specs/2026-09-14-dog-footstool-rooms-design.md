# Dog and Footstool Knowledge Contexts

Dog and Footstool are nested canonical contexts inside Potatoverse / Canon, not new top-level House Rooms.

Create four durable files: a Dog developmental context, a Footstool microcosm context, an integrated Dog-Footstool dossier, and a dated conversation-archaeology record. Existing Time, Traditions, Culture, Works, and specialist role files retain ownership of their own fact families.

Dog owns movement and relation: watching, signaling, following, pursuit, return, orbit, guarding, stopping, Door behavior, and transformation. Footstool owns position and support: lower relation, tiny local horizon, dirt or soil, compression, hidden or unfound Door, support beneath ascent, and possible transformation into Root or Potato.

Preserve two distinct transition graphs: Dweller -> Mud Dweller -> Dog -> Footstool for closure, and Footstool -> Dog -> Potato for development from assigned position to assigned function to generative possibility.

All roles remain symbolic/project roles rather than immutable classifications of people. Biblical comparison, creative scenes, and later interpretation remain explicitly separated by provenance.
