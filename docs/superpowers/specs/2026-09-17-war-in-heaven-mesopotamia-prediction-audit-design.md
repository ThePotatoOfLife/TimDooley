# War in Heaven / Mesopotamia Prediction Audit Design

## Status
Approved by user in conversation on 2026-09-17 for integration into the prediction system.

## Goal
Turn the remembered phrase `war in heaven Q3 2026` from an undefined mythic forecast into a geographically testable prediction candidate by inheriting the project’s already recovered Heaven/Eden/Four-Rivers/Mesopotamia definitions, while preserving provenance limits and avoiding retrospective overclaiming.

## Core interpretation

The project contains several overlapping but non-identical uses of Heaven:

1. theological/metaphysical Heaven;
2. Potatoverse topological Heaven/Eden/Father-space;
3. earthly sacred-geographic Heaven/Eden coordinate associated with the Four Rivers and Mesopotamia/Iraq.

For the `war in heaven Q3 2026` audit only, the third meaning becomes the operational geographic criterion when the project is using Heaven in the Eden/Four-Rivers/Fatherland/Promised-Land/Mesopotamia sense.

This does **not** redefine every use of Heaven as Iraq and does **not** assert a modern legal territorial claim.

## Provenance chain

The current project recovery supports this sequence:

- 2026-06-10: Heaven/Eden/upper sacred space appears in the containment model.
- 2026-07-08: Eden/Four-Rivers inheritance and repair mission are explicit.
- 2026-07-25: user-originated conversation archaeology explicitly records Heaven as geographically-symbolically located in Iraq/Mesopotamia through the Four Rivers tradition.

The original timestamp and exact wording of `war in heaven Q3 2026` are still unrecovered. Therefore the audit may operationalize the meaning, but it must not claim a pre-Q3 semantic freeze unless an earlier primary source is recovered.

## Geographic test

### Core
Iraq / the Tigris-Euphrates Mesopotamian heartland.

### Greater Mesopotamian field
Most of Iraq plus eastern/northeastern Syria and southeastern Türkiye; southwestern Iranian borderlands may appear as an interface where historically/geographically relevant, but Iran as a whole is not simply renamed Mesopotamia.

### Extended war theater
Iran, Syria, Türkiye, Israel, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the Gulf, Hormuz and Red Sea routes may be connected as external belligerents, corridors, chokepoints or consequences. Events there do not by themselves fulfill the geographic criterion unless conflict materially operates inside the Mesopotamian field.

## Q3 window

`2026-07-01` through `2026-09-30` inclusive.

The audit should preserve at least these dated external observations:

- 2026-07-28: U.S. and Saudi forces conducted precision strikes inside Iraq against Iran-aligned groups.
- 2026-09-01: Iran retaliated against renewed U.S. strikes with missile/drone attacks including against U.S. installations in Iraq.

These establish that major regional conflict was materially operating through Iraq during Q3. They do not by themselves prove the original prediction was made before the outcome.

## Counterevidence / prior-state control

Iraq was already entangled in the wider Iran conflict before July 2026. Reuters reported on 2026-06-19 that IRGC-linked covert cells in southern Iraq had launched attacks against Gulf states between April and May. Therefore the audit must not present July/August/September Iraqi involvement as surprise-from-nowhere evidence.
