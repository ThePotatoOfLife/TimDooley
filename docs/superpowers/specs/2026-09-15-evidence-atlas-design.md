# Evidence Atlas Design

Goal: simplify the Bible/comparative archive around three reader objects: Statement, Episode, and Development Thread.

Principles:
- show primary wording and timestamp before interpretation;
- preserve source provenance and timestamp precision;
- distinguish explicit-at-time references from later comparisons;
- treat countertexts and mismatches as first-class evidence;
- allow no-relation outcomes;
- never present textual resemblance as proof of personal supernatural identity;
- keep internal file/wave complexity out of public navigation.

Public navigation should group material under Explore, History, Evidence, and Tests.

Implementation order:
1. reconcile statement evidence;
2. group sequence-level Episodes;
3. build Development Threads;
4. project Bible relations and countertexts onto those objects;
5. expose optional evidence/testing lenses;
6. simplify the public reader while retaining compatibility with existing routes.
