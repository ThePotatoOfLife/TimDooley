# 2011 Kolding Chronology Integration Design

## Purpose

Reconstruct the 2011 Kolding psychedelic / Tree / psychiatric sequence as a precise, source-layered chronology without collapsing intoxication, later hospital events, diagnostic uncertainty, and retrospective Potatoverse interpretation into one event.

The reconstruction belongs first to **Thomas / the Son's material-human timeline**. Later Tim/Potato/Father interpretation may inherit and reinterpret it, but must not backdate post-2020 or post-2025 language into 2011 as contemporaneous doctrine.

## Core architecture

Use one detailed canonical owner:

`knowledge/timeline/son-2011-kolding-psychonaut-psychiatric-sequence.json`

All other timelines should project from this owner at the level appropriate to their purpose instead of reproducing the entire narrative independently.

### Canonical owner responsibilities

The owner must preserve five distinct layers:

1. **Biographical setting** — Kolding apartment, friend group, experimental drug/chemistry culture, unemployment / psychiatric contact, pursuit of ADD/ADHD assessment, and broader psychonautic search.
2. **Acute psychoactive episode** — ingestion, difficult somatic come-up, sensory opening, memory/self dissolution, fractal/stereo/time-spiral phenomenology, unity/interconnection, and eventual subsidence.
3. **Post-acute rupture and admission** — next-day overwhelming noise / apparent internal voices, recognition that ordinary perceptual boundaries had not returned, and voluntary psychiatric admission.
4. **Hospital / diagnostic aftermath** — medication exposure, later refusal of drugs, schizophrenia considered but not established according to recollection, continued ADD/ADHD assessment, and a longer period of diagnostic disentanglement.
5. **Hospital visionary sequence and later interpretation** — nuclear-destruction dream, crow/crowd awakening, Ragnarok/Odin/Yggdrasil comparisons, and later Tree/Axis/Spiral/Father interpretation.

## Event order

The current working order is:

1. Thomas lives in Kolding and is experimenting with psychoactive substances with friends interested in drugs, chemistry and altered-state exploration.
2. First-time ingestion of an unresolved psychedelic research chemical. Current recollection identifies **2C-E or 2C-I** as candidates and remembers a dose around **20–25 mg**. An older recollection instead described **mushrooms**. These are conflicting memory variants and must both be preserved until a primary source resolves them.
3. Approximately the first hour is dominated by a difficult, wave-like somatic / autonomic phase: heat and cold, dryness and moisture, nausea, gastrointestinal and urinary sensations, anxiety/thrill sensations, differing limb sensations and a strong background body load. The user calls this a "chameleon" phase.
4. As the somatic turbulence diminishes, sensory perception intensifies. Music is remembered as Goa and dubstep; sound appears as visible spectrum / rainbow-like structure and mechanical vibration becomes visually salient.
5. While friends are still present, Thomas reports flames / demons / unusual visual-emotional imagery around people and an unusually strong sense of reading mood or information from them.
6. Before or around the friends' departure, autobiographical memories flash rapidly. Thomas reports that the memories contain events but no ordinary central self.
7. After the friends leave, the episode becomes strongly internal and unitary: fractal carpet patterns, self-transforming / internally rotating objects, time perceived as "stereo," a continuously moving spiral associated with time, recursive scale, world-as-ocean / continuous field, room breathing with him, objects and matter experienced as informational / communicative, and a collapse of the ordinary self/world boundary.
8. Auditory and relational salience becomes extreme: he reports hearing / sensing vibrations, wind, conversation and mood beyond normal boundaries and experiencing trees and objects as communicative. These are preserved as phenomenological reports, not independently established extrasensory facts.
9. The acute ordeal gradually subsides. The archive currently preserves an overall duration of about **19 hours** and roughly **six hours of unity**, but it remains unresolved whether the six-hour unity phase is contained within the nineteen hours or was described as additional time in some retellings.
10. After sleep, Thomas goes shopping. Approaching / entering the store, noise becomes overwhelming and he experiences what seem to be other people's internal voices. He interprets this as evidence that ordinary perceptual boundaries have not fully returned.
11. Thomas voluntarily presents to psychiatric care so his brain can rest and return toward baseline.
12. During psychiatric care, clinicians consider psychotic-spectrum explanations because he presents in a highly altered / disorganized state. According to the user's recollection, schizophrenia is considered but never established as the final diagnosis. He is simultaneously attempting to obtain ADD/ADHD assessment. The diagnostic question remains entangled with recent drug use and later medication exposure for roughly a year or more.
13. Psychiatric medications are offered / administered. Thomas initially agrees despite reluctance, later turns medication down, and retrospectively treats this period as part of his broader experiential investigation of psychoactive effects. This should not be framed as controlled pharmacological research.
14. During the hospital period, Thomas has a dream / vision of catastrophic destruction in America involving a nuclear explosion. An older recollection describes an aerial or drone-like perspective. This event must be separated from the acute psychedelic phase unless primary evidence shows otherwise.
15. He later wakes to an extraordinary crow scene, remembered in some accounts as a dense murder of crows filling / obscuring sky, trees and ground, with a crowd of people also present. Later Potatoverse material relates this to Ragnarok / Odin imagery.

The file must never imply that a later interpretation was the language Thomas necessarily used in 2011.

## Substance and dose handling

Do not identify the substance as fact. Store:

- current candidate: `2C-E or 2C-I`
- older variant: `mushrooms`
- remembered dose: approximately `20–25 mg` **if** the 2C candidate is correct
- chemical verification: `none recovered`
- confidence: `unresolved autobiographical reconstruction`

Scientific context may note that 2C-series oral doses are normally discussed in milligrams rather than micrograms, but pharmacological context must not be used as proof of identity.

## Psychiatric-history handling

Do not write that Thomas "had schizophrenia" or that clinicians definitively "misdiagnosed schizophrenia" unless a primary record proves either proposition.

Preferred wording:

> During the 2011 drug-related psychiatric episode, schizophrenia or another psychotic-spectrum explanation was considered because Thomas presented in a highly altered state. According to later recollection, it was not established as the final diagnosis, while ADD/ADHD assessment continued and the effects of recent substance use and medication complicated interpretation.

The archive may preserve the user's later view that relatively little remained wrong once the episode resolved, but that is retrospective self-assessment rather than an independent clinical conclusion.

## Psychonaut / expertise framing

It is appropriate to describe the period as **psychonautic experimentation** or **serious lay psychonaut practice** if sourced as self-description. It is not appropriate to equate Thomas's technical expertise with Alexander Shulgin's professional chemical expertise without evidence.

Comparisons with Shulgin or Terence McKenna should be framed as cultural / methodological parallels: direct investigation of altered states, attention to subjective effects, and attempts to build language around difficult-to-describe experiences.
