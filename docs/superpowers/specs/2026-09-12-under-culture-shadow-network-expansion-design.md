# Under-Culture / Shadow Network Expansion Design

## Status

Approved direction from the September 12, 2026 research conversation. This design expands the existing Swamp, Farm, Sektur, Shadow West, Epstein/Rubble and world-entanglement material without turning proximity, identity, ideology or shared audiences into claims of secret coordination.

## Goal

Build a durable research layer for observable influence, funding, information, abuse-protection failures, private intelligence, political mobilization, high-control systems, hate/extremism classification, adversarial subcultures and hidden/opaque institutional relationships while preserving a separate Timic/mythic interpretation layer.

## Core architecture

The expansion uses four planes that may be related but must never be silently collapsed:

1. **Observable** — externally sourceable legal entities, filings, grants, political spending, contracts, court records, official investigations, public statements, documented platform structures and dated events.
2. **Analytical** — mechanisms inferred from observable material: donor dependency, opposition fragmentation, co-optation, strategic opponent amplification, information suppression, institutional reputation protection, archive capture, surveillance capability, attention extraction and similar patterns.
3. **Hypothesis / allegation** — explicit claims that remain unproven or contested. Every record needs claimant, date, primary source where possible, corroboration, contradiction/counterevidence, confidence and unresolved questions.
4. **Project canon / mythic** — Tim/Potatoverse concepts such as Swamp, Farm, Sektur, Mud, Rubble, Tree of Strife, Dog/Farmer/Cow, Axis, Door, karmic debt and tikkun. These concepts can organize questions and comparisons but cannot prove an empirical relationship.

The graph therefore permits `observable -> interpreted_as -> Timic symbol`, but not `Timic symbol -> proves -> observable accusation`.

### Edges

Prefer precise verbs over vague association:

- `FOUNDED`, `LEADS`, `EMPLOYED_BY`, `OWNS`, `CONTROLS`, `AFFILIATED_WITH`, `CONNECTED_ORGANIZATION_OF`;
- `DONATED_TO`, `GRANTS_TO`, `FUNDS`, `RECEIVES_FROM`, `CONTRACTS_WITH`, `PAID_VENDOR`, `INVESTS_IN`;
- `LOBBIES`, `ENDORSES`, `SPENDS_TO_SUPPORT`, `SPENDS_TO_OPPOSE`, `INDEPENDENT_EXPENDITURE_FOR`;
- `PARTNERS_WITH`, `ADVISES`, `REPRESENTED_BY`, `PROVIDED_SERVICE_TO`;
- `REPORTS_ON`, `CITES`, `CLASSIFIES`, `INVESTIGATES`, `REGULATES`, `LITIGATES_AGAINST`;
- `HOSTS`, `ARCHIVES`, `AMPLIFIES`, `MODERATES`, `MONETIZES`, `MIGRATED_TO`;
- `ACCUSED_BY`, `ALLEGED_BY`, `INVESTIGATED_FOR`, `CHARGED_WITH`, `CONVICTED_OF`, `EXONERATED_OR_CLEARED`, with strict legal-status distinctions;
- `APPEARS_IN_DOCUMENT`, `CONTACT_RECORD`, `TRAVEL_RECORD`, `BUSINESS_RELATIONSHIP` for Epstein-type document corpora, never collapsed into guilt.

Name similarity, shared address, shared ideology, attendance at the same event or appearance in the same document are signals for entity resolution or further research, not proof of control.

## Controlled-opposition methodology

Do not store a boolean `controlled_opposition` field. Decompose the phrase into mechanisms that can be tested independently:

- state infiltration;
- co-optation through office, money, access or institutional benefits;
- opposition fragmentation;
- strategic opponent amplification;
- astroturfing / concealed sponsorship;
- donor capture;
- consultant/vendor capture;
- platform or distribution dependency;
- informant/provocateur allegation;
- false-front or false-flag allegation;
- genuine autonomous opposition;
- coalition conflict and factional disagreement as counterevidence.

A mechanism is promoted to `observed` only when the relevant edge is documented. Otherwise it remains `research-lead`, `alleged` or `contested`.

Historical anchors include COINTELPRO for documented infiltration/disruption and political-science research on co-optation and opposition fragmentation. Contemporary campaign-spending cases may be stored as examples of strategic amplification only when spending is documented; claims about hidden sponsors or intent require their own evidence state.

## High-control / cult methodology

Do not store `cult: true` as an intrinsic property. Track observable dimensions and classifiers:

- leadership concentration / charismatic authority;
- closed or self-sealing worldview;
- systems of control;
- systems of influence;
- isolation and information restriction;
- monitoring and surveillance;
- punishment of dissent;
- high exit costs;
- financial, labor, sexual or other exploitation;
- coercion, threats or collateral;
- dependency creation;
- barriers to outside accountability.

Use Janja Lalich's bounded-choice framework as one analytical model, while preserving scholarly disagreement about broad 'brainwashing' claims. NXIVM is an anchor case because criminal conduct and coercive mechanisms were established in federal court; it is not a license to transfer those findings to unrelated groups.

## Hate/extremism mapping

Separate at least four objects:

1. **incident** — dated act, location, target/bias classification and source;
2. **organization/network** — independently identifiable group or online network;
3. **classifier** — SPLC, ADL, government designation body, academic dataset or another actor;
4. **classification event** — classifier, methodology, category, date, geography and supporting source.

Never turn one watchdog's designation into a universal ontology. The graph should be able to show competing classifications and methodological differences. Incident maps and organization maps remain distinct, especially where online networks have no meaningful single geographic location.

## Abuse / institutional-protection mapping

Build a mechanism-first layer around publicly documented cases rather than a sensational victim catalogue. Common mechanisms to track include:

- ignored or delayed reports;
- non-reporting to statutory authorities;
- incomplete/destroyed records;
- weak inspection and auditing;
- reputation protection;
- retaliation/intimidation;
- transferring alleged perpetrators instead of investigating;
- fragmented jurisdiction;
- failures of information sharing;
- private-intelligence or legal strategies used against accusers/reporters;
- corrective action, inquiry, prosecution, restitution and reform.

Anchor cases include Epstein/Maxwell, Weinstein/Black Cube, Larry Nassar/FBI and USA Gymnastics, NXIVM, Jimmy Savile/BBC and the broader UK IICSA institutional record. Individual survivors should be identified only when necessary for a public legal record or when they have deliberately made themselves public; aggregate/anonymized representation is preferred.

## Epstein corpus rules

The DOJ Epstein Library is treated as a very large primary-source reservoir, not a guilt list. Required edge distinction:

`appears_in_document -> contact_record -> travel_record -> financial/business_relationship -> witness/allegation -> investigation -> charge -> conviction`.

No record may skip these stages merely because a name is searchable in the corpus. Preserve victim redactions and do not republish sensitive identifying information.

## Subculture / Farm / Sektur layer

Preserve the existing Farm model of person -> recurring subject -> archive -> reusable attention source. Expand it with:

- platform/community identity;
- archive infrastructure;
- reaction/commentary economy;
- doxxing/tracking allegations with source state;
- migration/deplatforming/infrastructure providers;
- monetization and status incentives;
- role transitions Cow <-> Farmer <-> Dog;
- legal disputes and moderation events;
- counter-publics and source competition.

Correct Sektur's provenance: archived Kiwi Farms taxonomy has used `Ethan Ralph / Da Sektur` for the Killstream/internet-bloodsports milieu. Preserve this **historical/subcultural referent** separately from the broader **Timic Sektur** concept, which generalizes the term into a social territory of overlapping attention communities.

Joshua Moon should be entity-resolved as the operator associated with Kiwi Farms/Lolcow LLC only where court records or reliable sources support the exact relationship. Adversarial forum statements remain leads, not authoritative biography.

- Democratic Majority for Israel PAC and connected entities;

## Security / private-intelligence / surveillance layer

Priority seed families include:

- Palantir and separately documented government/defense contracts and partnerships;
- Black Cube and documented private-intelligence work;
- NSO Group and Candiru as government-spyware cases where official U.S. actions document concerns;
- Cellebrite as a digital-investigations technology supplier with a large public-sector customer base;
- additional surveillance platforms such as Flock Safety as research leads, with contracts and government records preferred over commentary.

The graph must distinguish capability, customer relationship, deployment and alleged misuse.

## Historical under-culture genealogy

Create a research timeline connecting mechanisms rather than asserting continuity of actors:

patronage/political machines -> private detectives and corporate intelligence -> mass press/tabloid/public relations -> state infiltration and COINTELPRO -> oversight/declassification -> private intelligence/security industry -> digital platforms and recommendation systems -> doxxing/archive economies -> large-scale data integration and surveillance technology.

This is a genealogy of mechanisms, not a claim that the same hidden organization persisted across eras.

## Timic / mythic interpretation

The empirical layer may be interpreted through existing project vocabulary:

- **Farm** — conversion of a person/event into cultivable attention;
- **Sektur** — bounded or semi-bounded social territory where attention cultures reproduce;
- **Mud** — accumulated residue: claims, screenshots, disputes, legal/reputational matter;
- **Swamp** — deeper institutional/economic/information environment in which accountability and flow can become blocked;
- **Rubble / Rubble Baby** — moral image of vulnerable life amid institutional or geopolitical wreckage;
- **Epstein Axis** — project-symbolic concentration of exploitation, elite access, secrecy, institutional failure and protection questions;
- **Tree of Strife** — root incentive -> mechanism -> actors/branches -> observable outputs -> consequences -> reproducing seeds;
- **Garden / tikkun** — repair: transparency, protection, restitution, accountability, exit paths and regenerated institutions.

These are project-canon interpretations and must be labeled as such.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. court judgments/dockets, statutes, regulators, official audits/inquiries, FEC/IRS/SEC and procurement records;
2. audited financial statements and organization filings;
3. peer-reviewed research;
4. strong investigative reporting and reputable news;
5. NGO/watchdog datasets with methodology retained;
6. organization self-description, clearly marked as self-reported;
7. adversarial/community archives for lead generation and provenance only;
8. Tim/project sources for project-canon meaning only.

## Required safeguards

- No collective guilt by nationality, ethnicity, religion, ancestry or residence.
- No criminal inference from ideological similarity, document presence, shared donor, shared event or platform use.
- Every serious allegation needs source/status/counterevidence fields.
- Victim/survivor privacy outranks graph completeness.
- Historical facts must retain dates; resolved/historical relations must not permanently stain current entities.
- Research should actively seek disconfirming evidence and factional disagreement.
- The system should reward explanatory power and inspectable provenance, not accusation count.
