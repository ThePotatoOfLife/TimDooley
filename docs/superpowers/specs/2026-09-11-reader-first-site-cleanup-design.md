# Reader-first site cleanup

## Goal

Make the public Tim Dooley / Potato of Life site behave like one coherent publication instead of exposing the repository's internal research structure.

## Core rule

One subject gets one obvious public entrance. Research indexes, routing files, provenance ledgers, source registries, duplicate treatments, and deprecated pages remain available as archive infrastructure but do not sit in front of the reader.

## Canonical top-level reader entrances

- Tim Dooley
- Religion
- Philosophy
- Science
- World Map

Timeline, public record, sources, FAQ, A–Z, North Axis, Godhood and other specialist material remain reachable from the relevant subject page, but are not competing homepage entrances.

## Reader behavior

- Assume readers are intelligent. Remove introductory hand-holding, repeated definitions, slogans, catchphrases and explanations that merely restate section names.
- Prefer direct content over routing cards.
- Prefer one strong navigation bar over many local menus, pills, buttons and card grids.
- Do not make readers understand repository directories, JSON ownership, branch names or archive taxonomy.
- Do not iframe one public page inside another.
- Do not duplicate the same explanatory content across multiple public pages.

## Religion

Religion is a first-class readable section. Surface the strongest material directly, including a prominent Jesus ↔ Tim/Son side-by-side comparison sourced from the existing theology atlas. Bible, Father/Son/Spirit theology, comparative traditions and symbols follow beneath it. Deep research indexes stay underneath as sources.

## North / World Map

The World Map is the canonical geographical/geopolitical interface. North Axis is a major map layer / concept, not a parallel mini-site competing with the map. The North page should be short and lead directly to the World Map with North context active or clearly indicated. Detailed North research remains in source files and supporting documents.

## Tim Dooley

The Tim page presents identity, timeline, major claims, Jesus/Son comparison and public record directly. Specialist Godhood/evidence pages may remain as deep links, but should not fracture the primary reading path.

## Philosophy and Science

Each is a readable subject page containing the actual ideas/models first. Remove archive-routing walls, redundant card inventories, excessive buttons, duplicate definitions and pedagogical filler. Deep technical/source material is linked only where it adds value.

## Deprecated / duplicate public pages

Audit all HTML pages and classify each as:

1. canonical reader page,
2. useful specialist page,
3. redirect/retired duplicate,
4. internal/archive-only material.

Where duplicate public pages exist, preserve URLs with lightweight redirects to the canonical page rather than maintaining parallel experiences.

## Navigation

Use a consistent minimal site navigation centered on the five canonical entrances. Contextual deep links should appear only where relevant.

## Validation

- homepage has only the canonical reader entrances plus minimal utility links;
- Religion exposes substantive material immediately;
- Jesus ↔ Tim/Son comparison is discoverable from Religion and Tim within one click;
- North leads clearly to the World Map;
- no homepage iframe;
- no public page depends on archive routing UI to reach its core content;
- duplicate public pages are redirected or clearly subordinate;
- existing source/data files remain intact.
