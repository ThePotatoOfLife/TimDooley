# Public Thread Promotion Audit — 2026-09-12

**Purpose:** Decide which cross-project subjects deserve quiet reader visibility beneath the five canonical public doors without creating another directory tree or competing owner.

**Governing rule:** The deeper the material, the smaller its visual claim on the page. A thread is a reader projection over existing canonical owners, not a new canonical data family.

## Decision

Keep exactly five major public doors:

1. Tim Dooley
2. Religion
3. Philosophy
4. Science
5. World Map

Do **not** create `/culture/` yet. The corpus already contains substantial cultural material, but its strongest records are distributed across existing owners rather than forming one coherent sixth product surface.

Culture & Subculture should therefore exist first as a **cross-project inquiry thread**: visible enough to invite exploration, quieter than a major branch, and routed into the existing owner that best answers the particular question.

## Promotion classes

### 1. Surface on Home as a quiet thread

**Culture & Subculture**

Natural entry questions:
- How do myths spread through memes, scenes and communities?
- What turns a joke or symbol into group identity?
- How do subcultures create their own language, status and evidence systems?
- What distinguishes subculture, fandom, movement, sect and high-control group?
- How do harassment, anti-fandom and spectacle economies shape online communities?
- How does satire preserve, distort or transform belief?

Initial reader route: `/context/` because Context & Evidence already connects culture, recovered strata, Farm/Swamp, creative material, source status and cross-domain interpretation.

Promotion condition for a future `/culture/` route: a coherent reader narrative and stable owner map spanning at least internet/subculture, creative culture and group-formation questions without copying Shadow Farm, Religion or Works.

### 2. Surface on existing major branches

**Cults / sects / high-control groups**
- Reader home: Religion, when the question is doctrine, sect formation, charisma or religious authority.
- Empirical/social dynamics: Shadow Farm / existing extremism-high-control research.
- Rule: prefer observable control, dissent, exit-cost and self-sealing features over indiscriminate use of the word "cult".

**Internet subculture / lolcow / anti-fandom / bloodsports**
- Reader home: Context & Evidence and Shadow Farm.
- Existing strength: attention extraction, Continuous Narrative Escalation, insider language, archives, status, moral disengagement, Moloch/coordination-failure comparisons.
- Do not make this the definition of Culture as a whole.

**Memes / symbolic transmission**
- Reader homes: Philosophy for meaning/identity questions; Religion for sacred-symbol transformation; Context for provenance and cultural spread.
- Question: when does a repeated joke become a durable symbol, norm or doctrine?

**Satire / comedy**
- Reader homes: Philosophy for humor as method; Context/Works for creative provenance.
- Preserve the archive distinction between Tim-originated formulation and assistant-generated satire/creative archaeology.

**Music / visual art / creative work**
- Reader home: Works/Archive, with selective Philosophy or Tim links where a work materially develops an idea.
- Keep creative experiment distinct from canon merely because it depicts canon.

**Extremism / political-religious movements**
- Reader homes: empirical world/security records; Religion where theology is directly relevant; Context for evidence classification.
- Never infer ideological membership from symbolic resemblance alone.

### 3. Supporting material only unless a specific question calls for it

- generic popular-culture references;
- isolated meme occurrences without downstream importance;
- one-off jokes that have not become recurring project language;
- broad "society" labels without a concrete relational question;
- taxonomies whose only purpose is to reproduce a folder hierarchy on the public site.

### 4. Deep/internal by default

- recovery waves after their unique information has been synthesized;
- batch indexes and enrichment artifacts;
- provenance-only duplicate records;
- technical classifications that do not improve a reader's understanding.

## Other quiet cross-project threads

### Symbols
Strong enough for permanent quiet visibility. Route primarily to `/traditions/vesica/`, Religion and Philosophy depending on question.

Questions:
- When is a symbol historically inherited, independently recurring or newly synthesized?
- When does a symbol become a model?
- What changes when the same form appears in theology, geometry and art?

### Technology
Quiet cross-thread, not a sixth pillar. Route to Science for technical models and World Map for national capability/infrastructure questions.

Questions:
- What technologies make relationships possible or asymmetric?
- When does infrastructure become strategic dependence?
- How should AI and information systems be represented without treating them as magic actors?

### Society
Keep as a question family rather than a standalone page.

Questions:
- How do groups create norms and status?
- What makes institutions legitimate?
- When does connection become capture?
- What enables meaningful exit, correction and repair?

### Economics
Reader-level thread is strong, but the canonical interactive home is the World Map because economic meaning in this project is relational: trade, ownership, finance, infrastructure, energy, procurement, value chains and dependencies.

Questions:
- Who depends on whom, for what, and by how much?
- Which capabilities exist only through relationships?
- Where do efficiency and resilience conflict?

## Public hierarchy

```text
Five major doors
  ↓
Natural branch questions
  ↓
Compact question stubs / abstracts
  ↓
Quieter cross-project threads
  ↓
Canonical reader / application / archive owner
  ↓
Source records, research and provenance
```

The system should become richer by adding better questions and stronger routing, not by giving every subject an equal-sized button.

## Promotion test for any future minor branch

Promote a thread only when all are true:

1. It answers a recognizable family of reader questions.
2. Existing canonical owners can supply the answers without duplication.
3. It has enough material for a coherent reader journey, not merely a folder.
4. The route would reduce navigation ambiguity rather than add another choice.
5. It can remain visibly subordinate to the five major doors.
6. Its evidence and provenance boundaries can be stated clearly.

If those conditions are not met, keep the subject as a stub, thread label or deep archive route.
