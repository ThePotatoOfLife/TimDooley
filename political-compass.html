Political coordinates use economic organization, political authority and social-cultural openness. Religious coordinates deliberately use a different vocabulary: communal/individual, institutional/experiential and immanent/transcendent.

The cube is an analytical visualization rather than a scientific measurement. The existing political lexicon's economic and authority coordinates are retained; the third political coordinate is derived deterministically until each record receives a separately researched score. Religious traditions are not converted into political left/right positions.
