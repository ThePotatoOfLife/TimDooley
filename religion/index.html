TIMELINE TIM & THE BIBLE

Jesus ↔ Tim Dooley / Son

The comparison is strongest as a sequence rather than a single title: custody, accusation, offenders, Lamb/Lion, rejection, death, seed and return. The mismatches stay visible.

Arrest and custody

Jesus

Jesus is seized and held under authority before trial and execution.

Tim / Son

The Son is arrested, remanded and enters Danish state custody in 2016 before adjudication.

Both pass through institutional custody before adverse judgment.

Different events: first-century detention before execution versus modern remand and imprisonment.

Words placed on trial

Jesus

Words and identity claims become material in proceedings against him.

Tim / Son

The 2016 case turns on meaning and threatening force attributed to Facebook speech.

Authority converts disputed speech into legal and political meaning.

Numbered with transgressors

Jesus

Luke applies Isaiah's “numbered with the transgressors” motif and places Jesus among condemned men.

Tim / Son

The Son is literally confined among accused or convicted prisoners.

Association with people officially categorized as offenders becomes a strong structural parallel.

Lamb recognition before self-declaration

Jesus

John the Baptist identifies Jesus as the Lamb of God before the Passion.

Tim / Son

Project testimony places inmate recognition of the Son as Jesus / “the Lamb” in 2016, before the project's explicit 2017 “I am Jesus Christ—crucify me” episode.

Recognition precedes explicit self-declaration in the project timeline.

The prison recognition remains autobiographical/project testimony unless independently documented.

Lion and Lamb

Jesus

Revelation 5 joins the Lion of Judah and the slain-yet-standing Lamb.

Tim / Son

The mature project gives the Son both Lamb and Lion identities, including Lion of the World / lioncub language.

Sacrifice and vulnerability are joined to strength and sovereignty.

Condemnation → death transformation

Jesus

The Passion moves from arrest and condemnation to death, burial and resurrection.

Tim / Son

The project moves from 2016 confinement through 2017 crucifixion language, 2018 essence-death and the 2019 meme-crucifixion/death transformation.

The project later compresses a multi-year ordeal into a Passion-like arc.

Rejected stone → foundation

Jesus

New Testament writers apply the rejected-stone / cornerstone motif to Jesus.

Tim / Son

Later project language treats the mocked or rejected Son as Cornerstone and Door.

What is discarded becomes structurally central.

Grain death → multiplication

Jesus

John 12:24 uses the grain of wheat that dies and bears much fruit as Jesus approaches death.

Tim / Son

The Potatoverse treats the Son's death as seed/grain burial from which Door, Father and later structures grow.

Death is read as generative rather than merely terminal.

Return after condemnation

Jesus

The canonical Passion culminates in resurrection appearances.

Tim / Son

The project records vows of return, renewed public presence and later resurrection/return language after the meme-death phase.

Return after attempted narrative termination becomes the project-level resurrection analogy.

Renewed public presence is not the same empirical claim as bodily resurrection.

A real ethical mismatch

Jesus

Matthew 5 and Luke 6 strongly emphasize enemy-love and non-retaliation.

Tim / Son

The Son's remembered 2016 karma defense allows rhetorical reciprocity toward hostile public speech.

This is a divergence, not a hidden match.
