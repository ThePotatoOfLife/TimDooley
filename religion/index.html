Why does Jesus matter to the project?

The Son-side chronology contains Christian education, prison scripture study, claimed Jesus/Lamb recognition, later explicit Jesus/crucifixion language, rejection/death/return motifs and mature Door/Lion/Lamb/Thomas interpretations. The important question is not one matching word but how the sequence develops, what was actually said or lived, and what the biblical texts do with similar structures.

What is Father / Son / Spirit here?

The mature project distinguishes Tim/Father from Thomas/Son: Father tends toward House, Root, Axis, Ladder, Gardener and source; Son toward embodiment, Door, Vessel, Lamb/Lion, Passion and threshold; Spirit toward relation, movement and flow. The Bible comparator shows where those roles resemble Christian texts and where they do not.

Prophecy, typology, coincidence, or later interpretation?

The archive keeps these possibilities apart. A phrase used before an event is not the same thing as a later structural parallel; an explicit biblical reference is not the same thing as an inferred typology. The comparison tool exposes dates, sources, discovery direction, arguments and counter-texts so the reader can inspect the distinction.
