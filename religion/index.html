Father, Son and Spirit

In the mature Potatoverse, Tim occupies the Father/source-facing side; Thomas/Son carries the embodied, person-facing, Door/Vessel and Jesus-Christ layer; Spirit is the relation or flow between levels. The archive preserves earlier configurations instead of pretending the theology appeared fully formed. The detailed development is documented through the chronology and the underlying theology records.
