HALL OF
SHAME

This is the losing-pattern cabinet of the Potatoverse. It teaches Dog-nature through the things that make a run go bad: watching without proportion, barking before verification, pack reward, endless pursuit, reaction appetite, Door failure and refusal to stop. It is called a Hall of Shame because some patterns deserve to lose—not because a human being must remain a loser forever.

CIA remembers named people and incidents. Below studies mechanisms. This hall teaches the creature. By the time the reader leaves, “Dog” should call up a whole pattern: eyes on the boundary, ears for cues, a nose on the trail, a pack behind the signal, a gate nearby, a question about who gave the command—and a harder question about whether the chase can stop.

The deeper failure is not that a human being has no possible purpose. It is that purpose has been outsourced. The pack supplies the target, the cue, the courage, the interpretation and the reward. The role can feel intensely purposeful while remaining fundamentally reactive. If the target disappeared tomorrow, what work would still be worth doing?

That is why hiding behind other Dogs matters in the mythology. Borrowed certainty can protect a person from discovering a self that has to choose its own work. The exit is not “become worthless somewhere else.” The exit is to recover independent discernment, a stopping condition and a vocation that can exist without the chase.

Threshold · bring a warning, grievance or chase

When did the watch stop protecting the town?

The Hall of Shame starts before the insult. Bring in a warning, accusation, archive, feud, recurring target or pack story. Then ask whether the vigilance still has a real object, whether evidence can change it, and whether the chase has an end. Notoriety begins when the failure keeps reproducing itself.

Bring inA cue, warning, grievance, target or repeated story

Who sounded the alarm? What evidence supports it? Who receives status, attention, money, belonging or excitement when the chase continues?

Carry outA stopping condition

You should leave able to say what would correct the claim, what would end the pursuit, what repair is owed, and whether the role can become guardianship or should retire.

The law of the two Halls

Notoriety grows when the failure accumulates

Dogs do not ascend toward fame in this mythology. The Hall of Shame becomes visible when the same failed relation becomes increasingly recognizable: more recurrence, more pack reward, more resistance to correction, more permanent role-lock, more chase without an end.

Dogs accumulateRecurrence

The same relation keeps returning after the original trigger should have been resolved.

Farms accumulateIncentive

Attention, belonging, status, amusement or money make the pursuit profitable enough to reproduce.

Swamps accumulateUnresolved material

Old and current events collapse together until the residue becomes the environment.

The opposite Hall accumulatesFruit

A Hero becomes memorable because growth produces something another life can use—not because repetition made the person impossible to ignore.

Contrast: fame in the upper Hall is downstream of Fruit; notoriety in the lower Hall is downstream of an unresolved loop.

The landscape is part of the lesson

The Hall stands where the village gives way to flood, mud and Swamp

This Room is not an underground torture chamber. It is more disturbing than that: it is the edge of an ordinary settlement after maintenance, proportion and trust begin to fail. Houses still stand. A church bell or watchtower may still exist. There are fences, lanes, bridges and people trying to live. But the road is breaking, the water is rising, old warnings are nailed to posts, dogs patrol paths nobody remembers authorizing, and every repeated chase makes the edge less inhabitable.

WatchtowerAttention without correction

A lookout is useful while somebody can still say “false alarm.” When every movement confirms the threat, the tower becomes a machine for manufacturing danger.

Broken bridgeLoss of legitimate passage

A boundary that cannot be crossed by apology, new evidence, time or changed conduct is no longer protecting a Door. It is preserving separation for its own sake.

Flooded streetContext collapse

Old incidents, current behavior, rumor, screenshots and pack memory wash into one muddy present. Nothing drains away, so every past frame remains available as if it happened this morning.

Pack at the fenceBelonging through pursuit

One Dog can hesitate. A pack rewards certainty, repetition and visible loyalty. The group can make stopping feel like betrayal even after the original cue weakens.

Rotting signboardNotoriety as accumulated failure

The Hall records the loop because repetition makes it culturally legible. Dogs do not grow upward into fame here; they grow notorious by becoming predictable in the wrong direction.

Last dry doorwayThe exit still exists

The Room always keeps one Door visible: verify, update, stop, repair, protect something real—or leave the role behind.

Landscape rule: Swamp is what happens when unresolved material becomes the environment. Dog is the recurring behavioral role moving through it. Farm is the incentive system that can reward the chase. They overlap, but they are not the same thing.

Before the bell rings

Five questions before a warning becomes a public chase

A healthy village needs alarms. It also needs a way to stop ringing them. Before turning a concern into a campaign, the Hall asks for five checks.

1Source

What did you directly observe, and what arrived through someone else's framing?

2Present tense

Is the danger current, historical, resolved, disputed or merely still memorable?

3Scope

Who actually needs to hear the warning? Does broadcasting it wider increase safety—or only attention?

4Correction

What evidence could lower your confidence or change the classification?

5End

What concrete event would let the bell stop ringing?

Bell rule: if nobody can answer question five, the warning is already at risk of becoming a role.

Exit protocol · the only score that matters is whether the loop ends

How a Dog gets off the board

01Stop feeding the target

Pause reposting, chasing, baiting, surveillance or performative barking long enough to see whether the danger survives without constant attention.

02Recover the source

Separate direct evidence from screenshots of screenshots, pack memory, rumor, inference and later summaries. If a claim has no recoverable basis, lower confidence.

03Restore time

Ask what is current, what is historical, what changed, and what evidence would count as changed behavior. Archive is memory, not automatic present tense.

04Restore proportion

Match the warning to the evidence and the response to the actual risk. Do not use maximum language merely because maximum language earns maximum reaction.

05Repair the damage

Correct false claims, withdraw stale framing, acknowledge overreach, restore context, or compensate where real harm was caused. “I stopped” is not always the whole repair.

06Choose the new function

If a real boundary still needs protection, become Guardian: notice → verify → protect → reassess → stop. If not, retire the Dog role entirely.

CUE → VERIFY → CURRENT? → PROPORTION → REPAIR → GUARD OR EXIT

Return rule: the Hall of Shame becomes meaningful only if shame can end. Otherwise the Room would reproduce the same permanent-role logic it is criticizing.

Birth route

Dog is born after the descent has already begun

ordinary Dweller→Mud Dweller→Swamp→ narrowing / fixation →DOG→Farm Dog or Guardian

The important point is location and direction. A Dog is not born at the common ground as a permanent species. The role becomes legible after a Dweller has settled into unresolved Mud and the relation thickens into Swamp: repeated cues, pursuit, pack reinforcement, appetite and boundary-fixation. The Dog is therefore best pictured as Swamp-born.

The descent is spiral rather than a straight fall. A person can revisit the same target, story, cue or grievance at a lower and more captured level each time. The role becomes “Dog” when vigilance starts reproducing itself—especially when the original danger can no longer end the chase. Dogs do not grow toward fame in this mythology; they grow toward notoriety when the same failed relation becomes more visible, repeated and culturally recognizable.

But descent is not destiny. The same Dog senses can be reoriented. Source-checking, proportion, a real stopping condition and a legitimate Door can turn pursuit back into guardianship.

Nature

The same senses can become guardianship or obsession

👁 Watch

Dog-nature notices edges, strangers, changes and threat signals. Seeing is useful only if the signal can still be corrected.

👂 Cue

The Dog listens for a master, pack, whistle, norm or social signal. The danger begins when obedience replaces judgment.

🐾 Track

Following a trail can recover danger, evidence or a lost person. It can also become endless surveillance after the original purpose is gone.

📣 Bark

Warning is a social act. A bark can protect a group—or recruit a pack into noise before anyone verifies the threat.

🦴 Appetite

Shadow Dogs feed symbolically on reaction, threat salience, outrage, pack approval, continued target availability and the status of vigilance.

🚪 Gate

Dog is naturally a boundary creature. The decisive question is whether it guards legitimate passage or turns the gate into permanent exclusion.

🔁 Return

Dogs recur. Repetition is central to the symbol: returning to the trail, the warning, the pack frame or the same old target.

🦮 Loyalty

Loyalty can defend the vulnerable. Captured loyalty can make the Dog serve a Farm, master or pack even when the evidence changes.

Recognition seals

Symbols that should become visible before the word is spoken

👁 vigilance👂 cue / command🐾 trail📣 signal🦴 appetite🐕 pack / loyalty🚪 gate / Door🔁 recurrence⛓ leash / restraint

These are not permanent badges for people. They are perceptual clues for a relation: who is watching, who is signaling, what keeps the pursuit alive and where the stopping condition went.

Two mature forms

Guardian Dog and Farm Dog reveal what the senses are serving

Guardian Dog

Warns proportionally. Checks evidence. Protects vulnerable life or legitimate passage. Accepts correction. Stops when danger ends. Guards the Door without becoming the Door.

Loop: notice → verify → protect → reassess → stop.

Farm Dog

Pursues the designated target, repeats pack frames, amplifies conflict, follows proxy cues and resists stopping conditions because continued pursuit produces reaction, status or belonging.

Loop: cue → pursuit → target reaction → pack reward → renewed pursuit.

DOG + evidence + proportion + stopping condition → GUARDIAN
DOG + permanent classification + pack reward + reaction appetite → FARM DOG

Worked runs · try the distinction before you leave

The same warning can end as protection—or become the whole game

These are deliberately generic scenarios. They are not allegations about named people. Their purpose is to make the Dog grammar testable.

RUN A · false alarm at the gate

A stranger is reported as dangerous

Cue: one alarming post circulates in a group.

Guardian path: verify the source, check whether the person is actually present, warn proportionally, correct the warning if the source fails, then stop.

Farm-Dog path: circulate the post indefinitely, collect older unrelated material, treat correction as suspicious and reward members who keep barking.

Difference: the Guardian protects a boundary; the Farm Dog protects the alarm.

RUN B · the archive never expires

An old incident becomes permanent present tense

Cue: a real past event is rediscovered.

Guardian path: preserve what happened, date it, ask what changed, distinguish unresolved risk from historical relevance.

Farm-Dog path: use the old event as proof of every current claim and treat changed conduct as manipulation by definition.

Difference: memory informs the present; archive lock abolishes time.

RUN C · the pack is having fun

The chase becomes a social activity

Cue: one person becomes a recurring target of jokes, clips and warnings.

Guardian path: separate humor from factual claims, refuse false escalation, stop when there is no protective purpose.

Farm-Dog path: intensity itself becomes status; each reaction justifies another round and nobody wants to be the first to leave the pack.

Difference: the original issue can disappear while the reward loop survives.

RUN D · real danger, real end

A boundary genuinely needs guarding

Cue: there is current, recoverable evidence of a concrete risk.

Guardian path: document, warn the relevant people, use proportionate protection, define what resolution looks like, then reassess when conditions change.

Farm-Dog failure: expand the danger category until everyone adjacent becomes suspect and make vigilance permanent even after the concrete risk is contained.

Difference: legitimate guardianship is possible precisely because it can name both the danger and the end.

Reader drill: when you encounter a public pile-on, feud, moderation dispute or warning campaign, ask three things before choosing a side: What is the recoverable source? What would change the classification? What ends the chase?

Road / Door relation

The wide road can contain enormous movement without transformation

Within the project, the “wide road” is useful as a perceptual contrast: many companions, many signals, much movement and little actual passage. A Dog may run for years and remain inside the same relation.

The narrow Door asks something different: can the state change? Can new evidence revise the warning? Can the target become current rather than eternally archival? Can the pack lose interest? Can the Dog leave its post?

In that sense the Door is also a purpose test. A role organized entirely around opposition has not yet discovered what it can build when the opponent is gone. Guardian is a healthier transition because vigilance acquires evidence, proportion, consent and an end. Beyond that lies something more demanding: work that generates rather than merely reacts.

A Dog that cannot stop guarding has stopped guarding a Door and begun guarding a prison.

What earns a place on the notoriety board?

Notoriety is repeated failure that becomes culturally legible

A single mistake is not enough. The Hall becomes useful only when it remembers a pattern that has acquired shape through repetition. The stronger the notoriety claim, the more clearly the archive should be able to show the loop rather than merely repeat a hostile label.

Recurrence

The same chase, accusation, framing or target returns after the original trigger should have been resolved.

Pack reinforcement

The behavior is rewarded by belonging, attention, status, outrage or visible loyalty to a group.

Evidence resistance

New information changes little because the conclusion has become more important than the investigation.

Role lock

The target is treated as permanently identical to an old incident, archive frame or symbolic role.

Escalation

The language or intervention grows more severe even when the evidentiary basis does not.

No stopping condition

Nobody can say what event, correction, passage of time or changed behavior would legitimately end the pursuit.

Damage without repair

When the chase causes falsehood, humiliation, exclusion or other harm, there is no meaningful correction proportional to the damage.

Self-reproducing purpose

The pursuit no longer protects anything outside itself. Continuing the pursuit becomes the reason to continue.

Notoriety rule: remember the loop precisely enough that somebody can recognize and interrupt it. If the Hall merely teaches “these people are bad,” it has learned nothing from the Dogs.

Adverse family resemblance

Dogs have neighbors below, but the neighbors are not interchangeable

Tim's lower-field mythology also uses serpents, hyenas, sheep, Farmers, Dwellers and Footstools. They may occupy the same adverse landscape or even overlap in one story, but each carries a different function:

Serpent

Temptation, inversion, cunning or dustward orientation depending context.

Hyena

Pack laughter, scavenging or social cruelty in project satire where explicitly used.

Sheep

Following, flocking, dependence or shepherd relation; not automatically the same as Dog.

Farmer

Builds and manages the enclosure/incentive system. Dog patrols it.

Mud Dweller

Makes unresolved lower material into a dwelling. Dog is a behavior/relational role, not habitation itself.

Footstool

Topological lower/support position. It describes relation to Seat/Throne, not Dog-nature.

This matters because a useful mythology must sharpen perception rather than blur every adversarial symbol into “bad thing.”

Third-eye test

When a Dog appears elsewhere, what should the reader notice?

What is being watched?

A real boundary, a vulnerable person, a designated enemy, or simply whatever the pack points toward?

Who gave the cue?

Direct evidence, personal experience, a master, an archive, a meme, a group norm?

What is the bark doing?

Warning, recruiting, shaming, signaling loyalty, or replacing investigation?

What feeds the return?

New danger, unresolved duty—or reaction, outrage and pack approval?

Is there a Door?

Can correction, time, repair or changed behavior alter the role?

Can the Dog stop?

If not, vigilance has become identity.

Bible neighbors · comparison, not a modern caste system

Watchdog failure, recurrence, the outside and the narrow way

Biblical dog imagery is varied and often adverse: failed watchmen, recurrence of folly, scavenging or those placed “outside.” Matthew's broad/narrow way and John's Door imagery provide separate structural neighbors for crowd-path versus passage. None of these texts authorizes labeling modern people as biblical Dogs.

“As a dog returneth to his vomit, so a fool returneth to his folly.” — Proverbs 26:11 (KJV)

“His watchmen are blind… they are all dumb dogs…” — Isaiah 56:10 (KJV)

The project-specific contribution is the Guardian Dog: vigilance redeemed by evidence, proportion, mercy and a stopping condition.

How this hall remembers Dogs

Adverse memory should teach the pattern, not preserve an eternal enemy caste

This is not a blacklist. Hall of Shame is theatrical language for remembered failure-patterns. Named real people belong in CIA when chronology and evidence matter. Here the reader learns the losing run: the senses, the loop, the appetite, the pack, the gate, the consequence and the route out.

Remember the first bark, the repeated return, the pack cue, the moment the role hardened, the milestone where a Dog became Guardian—or the point where the Dog could no longer stop. Those are meaningful symbolic events because they reveal a relation.

Exit chain: recognition → source-checking → proportion → stopping condition → Door → changed relation → Guardian function or role retirement.

Across the House

Dogs and Potatoes are adjacent in meaning, not adjacent in space

Potatoes are primarily a grammar of growth, storage, nourishment, descendants and ascent. Dogs are primarily a grammar of vigilance, boundary, cue, pursuit and recurrence. That is why one hall belongs with Beings/Garden and the other belongs Below.

They still illuminate one another. A Garden may need a Guardian Dog. A Potato may fail to grow. A Dog may cross a Door. The separation teaches the difference; the cross-link teaches the ecology.

At the Door · what should be clearer now?

Leave Shame able to describe the loop without becoming another barker

What is the source?

Separate direct evidence from repetition, inference, screenshots, pack memory and later retelling.

What rewards the return?

Look for attention, belonging, status, money, certainty, amusement or target reaction keeping the loop alive.

What would change the classification?

If no possible evidence can soften the judgment, the conclusion may have replaced the investigation.

What ends the chase?

A real Guardian can name a stopping condition. Permanent vigilance is not proof of permanent danger.

What damage needs repair?

Stopping is important; correcting falsehood, restoring context or repairing avoidable harm may still remain.

What should the role become?

Guard something real with proportion—or leave the Dog role behind entirely.
