Model a system as a chain of capacities

A system is not just a list of institutions. The useful object is the chain connecting actors, resources, rules, infrastructure, dependencies and outcomes.

ActorState, company, household, institution, alliance or other decision-maker.
ResourceCapital, labour, energy, data, land, technology, legitimacy or material input.
RuleLaw, contract, standard, norm, mandate, budget or governance constraint.
InfrastructureGrid, port, network, factory, cloud, road, payment rail or other enabling system.
DependencyWhat the actor cannot do alone, replace quickly or lose without meaningful cost.
OutcomeCapability, service, price, resilience, exposure, distributional effect or failure mode.

System question: if one node disappears or changes terms, which downstream capacities fail, substitute or become more expensive?
