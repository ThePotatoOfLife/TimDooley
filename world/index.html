From a place to a consequence

A world-facing investigation should be able to move from something locatable to something answerable. These five objects keep geography, power, time and outcome connected.

1 · WHEREPlace

Country, city, corridor, territory, grid, port, data center, mine or other locatable asset.

Locate it →
2 · WHOInstitution

Government, company, bank, regulator, alliance, university or organization with authority or capacity.

Find the system →
3 · HOWRelationship

Ownership, law, finance, trade, energy, information, culture, security or dependency linking the objects.

Trace relations →
4 · WHENEvent

Treaty, election, merger, law, crisis, investment, conflict, reform or other dated change.

Put it in time →
5 · SO WHATConsequence

Capacity gained or lost, cost shifted, resilience changed, dependency created, right altered or population affected.

Test the conclusion →
