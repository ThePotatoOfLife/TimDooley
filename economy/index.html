How to read an economy

The Economy Room now treats the observable system as linked balance sheets, flows, contracts, productive capacity and dependencies. The basic discipline is simple: stock ≠ flow · debt ≠ deficit · ownership ≠ control · commitment ≠ payment · liquidity ≠ solvency.
