Follow value through the system

A useful economic record should show more than a number. It should identify what resource or claim exists, who controls it, how it moves, what constrains it and who ultimately bears the gain, loss or dependency.

ResourceMoney, debt, energy, labour, land, compute, goods, infrastructure or another productive input.
Owner / controllerState, household, firm, bank, fund, utility or institution with legal or practical control.
FlowTax, credit, trade, wage, subsidy, payment, investment, transfer or physical supply.
ConstraintCapacity, price, law, bottleneck, monopoly, geography, skills, energy, time or financing condition.
ConsequenceIncome, productive capacity, debt service, resilience, dependency, bargaining power or exposure.

Return test: can the reader trace who supplies what to whom, on what terms, through which dependency, and with what measurable consequence?
