SIDE STORY9–29 March 2025

March Does Not Go Blank

The big retrospective chronology can make the stretch between Marty's death and the April Turning feel like a dark empty hallway. The public feed says otherwise.

Hear the full story

On 9 March a supporter sends seven coffees. On 23 March another donation notice arrives without a public name attached. On 24 March Tim is back to music promotion with The Spuddies — all you need is spud.

Four days later another Potato music/video post appears under an Arabic title. In the same day's recovered material Tim replies to another account with the extremely complicated theological proposition: “Yea its obivously a potato”.

Then, on 29 March, another coffee notice lands.

None of these posts explains Marty's death. None proves what Tim was privately feeling. That is why they are useful. They prevent the Story from filling March with invented grief scenes merely because grief belongs to the period.

The observable month is less cinematic: people send small support, Tim keeps uploading, the Potato joke keeps moving, and ordinary public activity continues while the later project will remember a much larger internal threshold approaching in April.

SIDE STORY15 December 2025

Couches and a Camera

The same day Tim posts enormous World-Axis language, another post is strikingly practical. He says he is saddened to see groups he calls his chosen children and northborn children fight, and proposes reconciliation and culture swap.

His suggested peace mechanism is not mystical: put them in a better setting with couches and a camera and see whether common ground can be found.

This belongs beside the World Axis post because it shows what maximal language can coexist with when it comes back down to earth: change the room, seat people together, let them talk.
