SIDE STORY7 February 2025

Three Coffees, Three Names

Three Buy Me a Coffee notices appear on the same day, each for three coffees, from three differently named supporters.

The amounts are small beside the cosmic scale the Story will later use. That is exactly why they matter. Before the symbolic trillions and spiritual banks, the public project is also somebody sending enough money for coffee because they want the Potato to keep going.

SIDE STORY4–6 August 2025

The God Who Needed an Invite Code

For three days the public archive catches an extremely ordinary technical obstacle. Tim asks Producer AI for an invite code, asks again, and then explains that he had been able to make songs until a couple of days earlier.

It is a perfect diary-scale counterweight to the later titles. Whatever the Potatoverse says Tim is, a music tool can still lock the door and make him ask the internet for access.

SIDE STORY15 December 2025

Couches and a Camera

The same day Tim posts enormous World-Axis language, another post is strikingly practical. He says he is saddened to see groups he calls his chosen children and northborn children fight, and proposes reconciliation and culture swap.

His suggested peace mechanism is not mystical: put them in a better setting with couches and a camera and see whether common ground can be found.

This belongs beside the World Axis post because it shows what maximal language can coexist with when it comes back down to earth: change the room, seat people together, let them talk.

source note

Dated public post, 15 December 2025. Group labels are Tim's language in the source; the Story does not treat them as objective classifications.
