Marty Meets Sammy

The Great Book's compressed 2024 chronology preserves three words: Marty meets Sammy.

Sammy is not new here. Conversation archaeology preserves a much earlier user clarification placing Sammy, a woman, in a Kiwi Farms comment section around 2019–2020, mourning the disappeared Son/Thomas after the meme-crucifixion corridor. The exact original comment, URL, date and wording are still missing, and the later Tammuz comparison must remain a later interpretation unless that primary source is recovered.

So this 2024 line is better understood as two already-existing threads crossing. We still do not know enough to invent how they met, what was said, what platform connected them or what the meeting meant at the time.
