Right now the precision comes from later project chronology, so the Story preserves the date while refusing to fabricate the missing scene around it.

Later Potatoverse work will accumulate several different cubes and cube-like structures: Moon/Saturn material, other sacred-geometric enclosures, Heaven/cosmology diagrams and later numerical interpretations. Those later systems should not be projected backward onto this day.

A separate autobiographical Great Book chapter says Tim had both Twitch and Kick channels active in September 2023 and describes the month as a Potato-of-Life return/emergence period. That gives plausible platform context, but not proof that the Cube appeared on either platform or that the channel event and the September-20 marker were the same scene.

The later Great Book timeline also places Metalorian and karmic interpretation near this marker. That is later meaning-making, not independent evidence that a cosmic mechanism was established on September 20.

So the honest story is smaller than the later cosmology: the archive preserves a strong later date marker, but the original same-day event remains missing. Until that primary source is recovered, this is a retrospective dated anecdote rather than a full documentary scene.
