Cube of the Moon

The later year-by-year Potatoverse chronology places a new object on one specific date: 20 September 2023 — the Cube of the Moon.

Hear the full story

The date is unusually precise compared with most of the 2021–2023 material. That does not mean the original September 20 stream, post or conversation has been recovered. Right now the precision comes from later project chronology, so the Story preserves the date while refusing to fabricate the missing scene around it.

What changes here is the kind of object Tim's developing world can hold. The Potato began as avatar, joke, protective mask and gradually organizing identity. The Cube introduces enclosure and geometry. Something can now be inside, outside, bounded, reflected, hidden or held.

Later Potatoverse work will accumulate several different cubes and cube-like structures: Moon/Saturn material, other sacred-geometric enclosures, Heaven/cosmology diagrams and later numerical interpretations. Those later systems should not be projected backward onto this day. The 2023 Cube is valuable precisely because it is earlier and less finished.

A separate autobiographical Great Book chapter says Tim had both Twitch and Kick channels active in September 2023 and describes the month as a Potato-of-Life return/emergence period. That gives us plausible platform context, but not proof that the Cube appeared on either platform or that the channel event and the September-20 marker were the same scene.

The later Great Book chronology also places Metalorian and karmic interpretation near this marker. That is preserved as later meaning-making, not as independent evidence that a cosmic mechanism was established on September 20.

So the honest story is smaller than the later cosmology and more important historically: sometime in a September where the Potato identity is operating across live platforms, the archive remembers one date strongly enough to keep it. On that date, the Potatoverse acquires a Cube.
