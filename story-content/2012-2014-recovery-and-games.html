SIDE STORYSummer 2012

The First Guitar

The year after the Tree is not another revelation. It is travel, recovery, music and an attempt to put pieces back together.

In July the later biography places him in Italy with his father for two weeks. Later that summer he turns twenty-five in Amsterdam, and around the same period his father gives him his first guitar.

The instrument becomes part of the recovery-year memory: listening can turn into playing, and some experience can be carried as sound instead of argument. The same retrospective period also remembers continued study of chemistry, pharmaceuticals and institutions.

Because this sequence comes from later autobiography rather than travel records, dated photographs, correspondence or a same-period diary, the Story preserves it as a documentary anecdote rather than a reconstructed scene.
