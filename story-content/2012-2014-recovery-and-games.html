SIDE STORYSummer 2012

The First Guitar

The year after the Tree is not another revelation. It is travel, recovery, music and an attempt to put pieces back together.

Hear the full story

In July the biography places him in Italy with his father for two weeks. The year has a lighter motion than 2011: bars, beaches, travel, conversation, distance from the place where everything had broken open.

Later that summer he turns twenty-five in Amsterdam. Around the same period his father gives him his first guitar.

The instrument becomes another way to think. The Beatles are already part of the recovery-year soundtrack, and now listening can turn into playing. Instead of trying to solve every experience as an argument, some of it can be carried as sound.

The rest of the year is still investigative. He studies chemistry, pharmaceuticals and institutions, trying to make a coherent worldview out of pieces that do not naturally fit together. But the guitar adds something the files full of ideas cannot: a way for the hands to do the thinking.

One Dollar of Dota

Not everything in the father-loss year is grief. The biography also preserves a tiny internet story: Tim pays a dollar to play Dota with AngryTestie.

Hear the full story

The setup survives in one compact line. Tim pays a dollar for the game. AngryTestie feeds mid. Tim carries.

It is a wonderfully small artifact beside the rest of the year. There is grief, reading, the rescued cat, and then there is somebody on the internet paying one dollar for a Dota session and turning the match into a story worth remembering.

He is also making Photoshop tutorials around this period. Long before the website becomes a House full of theology, part of the public identity is simply somebody who plays games, understands digital tools and shows other people how to make things.
