Buried Does Not Mean Dead

Another Great Book story treats rejection and isolation as burial. The potato is under hard earth, not visible, not celebrated, but still capable of rooting upward.

The line the book keeps is blunt: do not let rejection define you; let it refine you. A year later, rejection will become part of the project's account of the Turning. Here it is still advice to a buried potato.
