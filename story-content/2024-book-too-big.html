The Book Gets Too Big

By 2024 the joke has become too large to abandon. Tim begins building the Great Book of Potato in earnest, not merely as a collection of potato jokes but as a place where biography, theology, biology, internet culture and myth can all collide.

This is the year the Potatoverse becomes populated. Tim stops merely explaining the Potato and starts writing a world around it.
