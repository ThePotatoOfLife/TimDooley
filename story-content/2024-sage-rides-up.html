The Sage Rides Up

Tim stands inside an old stone elevator among the roots of a tree. This is Great Book fiction, but the image matters because of where the later story goes.

The Sage rides. The structure carries him. Other figures are stopped by pride, treasure or guilt. Tim reaches the Garden because he is inside an ascent machine, not because he has become the machine.

A year later the project's grammar changes completely.
