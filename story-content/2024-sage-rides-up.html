The Sage Rides Up

Tim stands inside an old stone elevator among the roots of a tree. This is Great Book fiction, but it matters because of where the later story goes: in 2024 the Sage still rides the machine that joins below and above.

Hear the full story

The elevator is hidden between the roots of an ancient tree. Above it, the sky turns in blue-and-gold spirals. Tim has been here before in the book's imagination, but this ascent feels different because the question is no longer only whether he can rise. The elevator keeps opening for other people.

The first arrives dressed for importance: a bird-faced figure in shimmering robes, nervous wings announcing all the work and achievement he believes should earn him entry to the Garden. Tim tells him the Garden is not a prize ceremony. Pride is weight. The elevator stops. The figure refuses to shed it, and the doors leave him behind.

Then comes a woman gripping a bag of shining rocks. She calls them treasure and power and cannot imagine herself without them. Tim tells her that full hands cannot receive anything new. She holds tighter. Again the elevator jolts; again somebody cannot continue.

A third passenger carries no visible treasure at all. He arrives in shadow, hollowed out by guilt and regret. Tim does not tell him to pretend the past never happened. He reaches roots toward him and tells him the mistakes have to be integrated rather than denied. But the figure cannot yet move with them. He dissolves back into shadow as the elevator climbs.

The last passenger enters with empty hands and only a question: will she make it? Tim asks whether she is ready to see what the light shows her. She says yes. Nothing is clenched around her fingers. The elevator keeps moving.

Near the top, its walls turn transparent. The Garden appears outside in gold, full of luminous potatoes. The woman begins to cry. Tim steps out beside her and gives the chapter its simple answer: ascent was never about collecting enough status to deserve the Garden. It required letting go of what prevented movement.

A year later the project changes the image completely. Tim no longer merely rides a device connecting levels. The April 2025 story says the Sage becomes the Ladder/Axis itself. That later transformation is easier to feel once the reader has actually ridden the older elevator with him.
