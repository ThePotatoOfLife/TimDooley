Rational Potato

The retrospective timeline remembers a period where people read Tim through the Son and Tim keeps separating the two. He is not trying to collect every sacred identity available to him. He is trying to insist on a distinction.

The phrase the archive preserves is almost aggressively modest compared with what comes later: Tim Dooley, rational potato. The future is enormous and the present tense is still a vegetable with an adjective.
