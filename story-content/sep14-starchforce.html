SIDE STORY14 September 2026

The Starchforce

Tim answers a joke with another joke: "Nah you must be reffering to the starchforce."

Not every joke is hidden scripture. That is part of the Story too.

Hear the full story

The surviving Story manuscript preserves a much longer thread around the punchline. It begins with a deliberately ridiculous theological problem: if Potato is sacred, why does a potato apparently improve when somebody covers it in bacon, sour cream, chives, cheese and butter?

Tim does not retreat from the toppings. The conversation starts treating them as clues. Scripture becomes a "pre recipe book" full of possible hints; food imagery starts bouncing against biblical imagery; a tiny potato thought becomes a seed, the seed becomes a tree, and the tree becomes the mature stack Tim has been building all year: Axis, Ladder, Garden, Kingdom, Tree of Life and Yggdrasil.

The thread then asks the kind of question that only this theology could generate: one potato, or several distinct spuds? Tim answers through opposites rather than a formal creed—earthly and spiritual, Father and Son, material and ethereal—and compresses the relation into a line: "The potato are all opposites in one. It is a bridge."

From there the associations accelerate. Potato skin moves toward philosopher's-stone imagery. Mud resembles rust. Potato becomes stored sunlight. Sunlight becomes Spudlight. Potato eyes become places from which the light can shine.

Then somebody brings electricity into it. A potato with wires is enough to knock the entire system sideways, and Tim answers: "Nah you must be reffering to the starchforce."

The joke does not end the theology. It keeps mutating. The manuscript preserves another line: "The spudlight is the aura. Its the feeling you get when you have potatoes at home vs when you have no potatoes at home." And when understanding finally gives out altogether, the thread lands on its natural technical-support question: "Did you forget to turn on the wifi in your potato?"

That is the real shape of the episode. Food becomes scripture; scripture becomes symbolic anatomy; seed becomes Tree; Tree becomes Ladder; opposites become bridge; sunlight becomes Spudlight; and then the whole machine cheerfully falls into potato Wi-Fi. Editing out the nonsense would make the thought process less accurate, not more.
