The Mud Dweller's Burden

One of the early Great Book stories is not about enlightenment at all. It is about weight. The Mud Dweller is carrying real mud, but the story keeps asking whether the way he carries it is making the burden heavier.

The point is not that difficulty is imaginary. The point is that complexity, ego and the manner of holding a problem can become additional cargo. Tim is already writing about load long before the later story says the load finally becomes too much.
