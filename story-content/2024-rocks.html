You Do Not Need to Carry Them

A fresh young potato finds the Sage and asks how to grow. Tim gives it soil, simplicity and patience. Then the potato asks what happens if people keep handing it more rocks.

The Great Book gives Tim a sentence that will become difficult to forget: "You do not need to carry them."

At this point the line is advice. The story has not yet caught up with the adviser.

source note

Great Book literary dialogue.
