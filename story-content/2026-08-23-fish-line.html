The Fish, the Hook and the Line

Tim's public language suddenly becomes almost cartoonishly mechanical: fish out of water, saved by the hook, the Door is the fish, the line is Tim Dooley.

The metaphor is messy, which is part of why it belongs raw. Door is no longer a single architectural object. It can become whatever performs passage in the moment, while Tim casts himself as the line that keeps the movement connected.
