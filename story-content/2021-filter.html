SIDE STORY2021

The Filter

The potato filter starts as something Tim can wear in public without having to explain everything behind it. The joke does protective work before it does theological work.

The Potato is not yet a kingdom. It is cover: a way to be visible and less visible at the same time while the harder parts remain underground.

Hear the full story

The later biography remembers 2021 as the year the potato-filter LARP stopped being merely a visual gag. After years in which a face, a name and a public persona could become the object of other people's stories, a vegetable offered an odd solution: appear without offering the old face in the same way.

The filter becomes a protective mask and then a social container. Tim can speak through it, joke through it and build around it without immediately resolving whether the Potato is character, disguise, philosophy or person.

That uncertainty becomes productive. The project starts asking what masks actually do. A mask can hide, but it can also make something else visible. It can be costume and still reveal a pattern the unmasked person could not carry safely.

The community begins forming around that ambiguity. The later archive remembers Spirited Away and its transformations resonating with the period, but there is still no final theology here. The useful thing is simpler: the Potato survives attention better than the old persona did.

The joke has become shelter.
