MAIN STORY25 December 2020

The Potato Arrives

The later project fixes this date as Tim's spiritual birth as the Potato of Life. It is not yet Fatherhood, not yet the Ladder, not yet the throne. The important thing is almost the opposite: Tim appears inside an identity he does not yet fully understand.

The Potato is useful because it is small enough to hide in. It can be a joke, a mask, a container. The later archive will look back and call this buried, generative, a hidden center. At the time, the world is not yet that organized. There is simply a Potato where there had not been one before.
