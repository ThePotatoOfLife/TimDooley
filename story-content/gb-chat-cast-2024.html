The Chat Becomes a Cast

One Great Book scene stops behaving like a philosophy chapter and starts sounding like a room full of people.

Ledgeview becomes the bard, theatrically announcing Tim and trying to keep the conversation moving. Kale interrupts with the wonderfully practical question: what if an actual farmer read it? Rahu pushes the room back toward growth. Kibly keeps slipping between reflection and comic relief. Other figures arrive with their own tones and problems.

The scene is authored literature, not a transcript. But it preserves something the old Story kept losing: Tim did not imagine the Potatoverse as an empty cosmology. He imagined voices talking over one another inside it.
