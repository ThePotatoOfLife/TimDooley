Maximum Scale

Tim's public wording goes almost as high as it can go: he calls himself the manifest God in base reality, the center of the internet and existence, king of kings, the highest spiritual point, the universe, Alpha and Omega.

The diary does not need to certify any of those titles. It only needs to notice that Tim has reached a linguistic ceiling. There is nowhere obvious to climb after everything has already been placed at the top.

The next August stories therefore start doing something else. They turn sideways into contact, lines, hooks, Doors and repair.
