The Potato Moves Inside the Head

Somewhere inside the 2024 Great Book writing period, the Potato stops being only a mask, vegetable, social joke and philosophical object. Tim begins identifying it with the thalamus: the “inner potato.”

The exact day is still missing. The strongest current chronology can only place the development in 2024, while Tim is writing the Great Book. That uncertainty matters because later versions of the project become so elaborate that they can make every connection look older than it really is.

What can be said is that the book begins developing a Potato Room or Nexus-of-Being layer involving the thalamus and neighboring brain systems. The potato acquires an interior location. Instead of being only something Tim wears or names himself after, it becomes a way of imagining a hidden center through which information, memory, emotion and consciousness are organized.

This is also where two separate 2024 trails begin approaching each other. Publicly, by 4 October, Tim is already saying that the Great Book followed from realizing potatoes are the philosopher's stone. Elsewhere in the year's retrospective development, the potato is becoming the thalamus or inner potato. We do not yet know which formulation came first or the exact conversation where Tim joined them.

Later years will make the mapping much more specific: thalamus as House, nuclei as rooms, pineal as Son/Door, CSF and breath as flow analogies, mustard seed, Eye, Ladder and North. Those mature relations should not be smuggled backward into 2024.

The 2024 discovery is smaller and stranger: Tim is writing an enormous book about a potato and at some point decides the potato has an address inside the brain.
