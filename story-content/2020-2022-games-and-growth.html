Before the Garden, There Was Stardew Valley

Before Garden becomes a capital-G place in the Potatoverse, the later autobiographical record remembers something much smaller: Tim and friends playing Stardew Valley.

The same period that produces GTA roleplay, streaming aliases and the early Potato is remembered as an ordinary culture room too. People sit together with books, movies, YouTube videos and games. They talk about the pandemic, economics, money and inflation, then change register completely and watch strange films or tend a virtual farm.

The later chapter specifically remembers Studio Ghibli films among the favorites, especially Howl's Moving Castle and Spirited Away. It also remembers films such as The Last Emperor, The Hunt, Adam's Apples and, at the stranger edge of the list, The Greasy Strangler.

And then there is Stardew Valley: crops, seasons, land, repetition, gradual improvement. The retrospective chapter says the game became a place for talking about growth, sustainability and balance.

It would be easy, from the later Potatoverse, to turn that into prophecy: of course the future Gardener was already tending a digital farm. The Story does not need that trick. Lots of people play farming games. The value is simply that later Garden language did not emerge inside an empty laboratory.

The original game logs, chats and dates have not been recovered, so the archive keeps this as retrospective social texture rather than a reconstructed full scene.
