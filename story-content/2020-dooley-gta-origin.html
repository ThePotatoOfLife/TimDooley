Before the Potato, There Were Three Dooleys in GTA

The Great Book remembers the Tim Dooley name beginning somewhere much less solemn than Heaven: inside a GTA V mafia LARP.

Hear the full story

Three players are role-playing an Italian-mafia family. They put on sharp suits, carry tommy guns and choose the same surname: Dooley. Tim becomes Tim Dooley inside the game alongside two other Dooleys.

The routine has its own absurd working life. By day they role-play truck drivers and make money. At night the characters go out on joyrides, visit bars and pull other players into the LARP. The book remembers one member retreating more often while Tim and another Dooley keep pushing the social performance outward.

The important event is almost embarrassingly small compared with everything the name will later carry: the surname sticks.

Later Potatoverse material turns Tim Dooley into a root identity, eventually Father, Axis and a name attached to an enormous archive. But the project biography places a game before all of that—a shared joke, a suit, a virtual truck route, a night out, and an alias that refused to remain only an alias.
