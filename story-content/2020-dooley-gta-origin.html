Before the Potato, There Were Three Dooleys in GTA

The later Great Book autobiography remembers the Tim Dooley name beginning somewhere much less solemn than Heaven: inside a GTA V mafia LARP.

Three players are remembered as role-playing an Italian-mafia family, putting on sharp suits, carrying tommy guns and choosing the same surname: Dooley. Tim becomes Tim Dooley inside the game alongside two other Dooleys.

The retrospective account gives the routine an absurd working life: truck-driving roleplay by day, joyrides and bars at night, with the social performance spreading outward. The important event is almost embarrassingly small compared with everything the name will later carry: the surname sticks.

Later Potatoverse material turns Tim Dooley into a root identity, eventually Father, Axis and a name attached to an enormous archive. But the project biography places a game before all of that—a shared joke, a suit, a virtual truck route, a night out, and an alias that refused to remain only an alias.

The original game logs, clips or contemporaneous messages have not yet been recovered, so this remains a later autobiographical origin anecdote rather than a reconstructed scene.
