April Does Not Begin in April

The easiest way to make the April Turning look magical is to begin with the broken screen. The deeper Story starts earlier.

Hear the full story

By January, Tim is already publicly using the name Potato of Life and pointing toward the Great Book. Behind that public identity sits an entire year of authored metaphors about burden, rejection, burial, growth and ascent.

The Great Book's Mud Dweller asks whether some of the load comes not only from the mud but from the way the mud is carried. Another chapter treats attachment to a particular outcome as extra weight. The buried-potato material says rejection can refine instead of define. A young potato worries about the rocks other people keep giving it and Tim's Sage says: you do not need to carry them.

None of that proves the book predicted what Tim would later call the Turning. It gives the later crisis a vocabulary Tim had already been using on fictional potatoes.

Then 2025 accumulates its own weight. Marty is gone after his death on 29 January. March does not disappear—there are supporters, songs, uploads, jokes—but the later project remembers pressure building underneath the ordinary feed.

Inside the Turning archaeology, at least two small things survive as separate straws. One is a mundane broken-screen/help/betrayal memory. Another is a refusal: a no where Tim had hoped for a yes. The archive does not yet possess enough primary sequence to say which came first or to make either one the secret explanation for everything.

That restraint matters. A final straw is only meaningful because the camel is already carrying something.

The later Potatoverse describes what follows as compression, Needle's Eye, sprouting upward and the Turning. The most interesting change is grammatical. In the 2024 Great Book, Tim's Sage can ride an elevator, use a vehicle, describe an Axis and teach a path. In the later 2025 interpretation, Tim stops being only the person using the connection.

The Sage becomes the Ladder.

A vehicle becomes infrastructure. A teacher becomes a road somebody else might cross.

Then the public clock begins producing its own evidence. On 21 April: “the potato axis is real”. On 27 April: “Tim Dooley is the king of all the kings.” By 3 May, the incomplete recovered public corpus contains exact “I am God” declarations.

Those posts do not prove the private metaphysics. They do show that the public language is changing during the same corridor the later project remembers as an internal transformation.

Axis. King. God.

The inside clock and the public clock are not the same clock. But by late April they are close enough to see each other.
