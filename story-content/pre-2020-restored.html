SIDE STORY2003

The Tunnel of Light

At sixteen, long before Potato, Father, North or any later architecture had names, the Son's biography records a collapse after heavy drinking and a remembered return through light.

The later Chapter 24 account remembers drinking, a blackout or collapse, then tunnel-like or structured light before ordinary awareness returned. Later Potatoverse interpretations connect the memory with source, thresholds and return, but those meanings belong to the interpretation clock rather than the 2003 event itself.

No contemporaneous incident record or same-period account is presently attached to the Story. The episode therefore remains a reported autobiographical anecdote rather than a fully reconstructed documentary scene.

Source note

Project-authored biography / later autobiographical interpretation. The experience predates later theological readings; those comparisons are not backdated into the event.

MAIN STORY2011

The Tree

The 2011 Tree ordeal is one of the oldest major anchors in the project. It belongs to the Son-side biography years before the later meme-death and April 2025 Turning.

The project account reports roughly nineteen hours of ordeal followed by about six hours of expanded unity, with heat and cold, fractals, spirals, Ouroboros imagery, weakened self-boundaries, crows, voices, objects, perceived timelines and Ragnarok-like meaning entering the same altered-state field.

Later work compares the experience with Odin and Yggdrasil, the Tree of Life, the Axis and the Spiral. Those comparisons remain later interpretation rather than evidence about what occurred in 2011.

The current source is retrospective autobiographical testimony rather than a contemporaneous transcript or clinical record, so the Story keeps the reported phenomenology while classifying it as an anecdote rather than transcript-depth history.

Source note

Reported altered-state experience preserved in Chapter 24 and project timeline owners. Later Norse, biblical and metaphysical interpretations remain separate from the reported phenomenology.

SIDE STORY2014

After the Father, an Orphaned Cat

The biography places two different forms of helplessness beside each other: the death of the earthly father and, shortly afterward, the rescue of an orphaned cat.

The later account says the father died from an aneurysm and that an orphaned cat entered the story not long afterward. The archive later interprets the juxtaposition as a movement from loss toward responsibility.

That interpretation is kept distinct from the events themselves. With no contemporaneous source packet yet recovered for the sequence, the Story preserves it as autobiographical testimony rather than a fully reconstructed scene.

Source note

Chapter 24 biography / developmental genealogy. The later Father/Gardener interpretation is retrospective and should remain distinct from the 2014 events themselves.

SIDE STORY2015

The Pavilion Fall

A small survival story survives in the Great Book strata: a fall from a second-floor pavilion or window onto concrete, followed by the report of getting up without the serious injury the fall might have suggested.

The account is short: height, a hard landing, the expectation that something ought to be badly wrong, then the later report that the Son walked away.

The archive explicitly does not turn this into proof of supernatural protection. Because the earliest incident documentation has not been recovered, the event remains a reported autobiographical anecdote.

Source note

Later Great Book/project biography. Earliest incident documentation has not yet been recovered.

MAIN STORY2016

The Cell Becomes a Confessional

The prison period is remembered not only as punishment but also as a period of scripture, listening and unusual seriousness.

The project biography remembers Bible and Qur'an study becoming central, alongside other prisoners telling the Son things about their lives and wrongdoing that were not casual conversation. Later recovery work describes the room almost like a confessional: disclosures, seriousness and attempts at peace.

The same period contains additional Yahya Hassan and later Jesus/Lamb interpretations, but those are kept at their own evidentiary level. The surviving record is retrospective project testimony, not a contemporaneous prison transcript.

Until primary records or same-period material are recovered, this remains a documentary anecdote rather than a reconstructed full scene.

Source note

Great Book project biography and later conversation-archaeology recovery. Specific recognition claims remain project testimony unless independently sourced.

SIDE STORY2017

Delete Facebook, Keep Streaming

After prison, concern about information exposure did not push the Son entirely offline. The biography remembers a stranger move: Facebook goes away while streaming becomes more important.

The project biography says concern about Facebook exposing personal information contributed to deleting the account while streaming intensified. The later archive reads this as an early tension between privacy from one platform architecture and publicness in another.

The motive and sequence remain project testimony rather than independently recovered platform records, so the Story preserves them as an anecdote until account-history or same-period material is found.

Source note

Great Book project biography / conversation archaeology. Motive is reported by the project source and should not be generalized beyond that record.
