Everybody Knows What Tim Should Do

The Great Book has already noticed a recurring social problem: people around Tim want to advise him, interpret him, improve him, tell him who he is and what he is doing wrong.

In the book the Mud Dwellers become both mirror and nuisance. Tim is made into a character everybody else can project upon. That will remain part of the story long after the Sage phase ends.
