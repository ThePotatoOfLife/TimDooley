INTERPRETIVE JUSTICE

A description can contain truth about a subject without becoming the subject.

This reader asks what happens when people, institutions, archives, diagnoses, audiences, cultures or models describe another subject—and what responsibilities appear when those descriptions begin to shape reputation, memory, access and future possibility.

Canonical owner: the law-family is maintained in interpretive-justice-laws.json. Provenance owner: interpretive-justice-source-map.md distinguishes older Potatoist roots from the September 16, 2026 law-family crystallization. This page is a reader-facing teaching surface, not a separate canon.

Interpretation needs a Door back to reality

Potatoism already says relation is not identity, the Filter is not evidence, authority is not exemption, and return is not reset. Interpretive Justice applies those disciplines to descriptions of living subjects. The goal is not to abolish judgment, criticism, diagnosis, investigation or disagreement. The goal is to keep them scoped, sourced, revisable and capable of hearing what they may have missed.

The subject can be wrong. The observer can be wrong. The crowd can be wrong. The archive can be wrong. The model can be wrong. A healthy system preserves enough structure to find out.

I

Person Before Dossier

Do not confuse the representation with the represented.

A map can be useful without becoming the territory

Diagnosis, allegation, conviction, biography, reputation, meme, archive record, audience interpretation and self-description can all contain information. None of them is automatically the whole subject.

The problem begins when one useful description expands until every other dimension is interpreted through it. A local frame becomes a total frame. Potatoism calls that identity compression and jurisdiction creep.

What relevant dimensions disappear when this description is treated as the whole person?

First-person authority is situated, not magical

A person is a primary source for the intended meaning of their own words, their self-description and their internally held identity. That matters. It does not automatically settle external empirical facts, law, medicine, consequences, history, hidden motives or another person’s experience.

The discipline is therefore neither “believe the subject about everything” nor “outside interpretation outranks the subject.” It is: identify what kind of claim is being made, then use the source class appropriate to that claim.

II

Hear Before You Close the Case

Strong characterization should follow serious hearing rather than replace it.

Reply is not immunity

A right of reply does not guarantee that a criticism disappears. Hearing can strengthen a criticism as well as revise it. A subject may answer badly, contradict evidence or reveal something new. The point is not automatic acquittal. The point is that a correctable interpretation remains open to relevant information.

What evidence or reply could cause this interpretation to change?

Count provenance, not echoes

Ten pages repeating one allegation may still represent one provenance root. Distribution can become large without evidentiary independence becoming large. Repetition matters for cultural reach and reputational effect, but it is not the same thing as independent corroboration.

That makes source independence, context retention and correction reach distinct variables rather than one vague measure of “how many people say it.”

Severity increases burden

The more severe, persistent and identity-affecting a characterization is, the stronger its source and correction burden should become. This is an archive and philosophy rule, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal standards.

III

Memory That Can Update

Return should carry correction as well as accusation.

An archive can remember too well in one direction

Digital memory can preserve an old role after the person, evidence or context changes. A healthy archive records the original claim and also the date, reply, correction, adjudication, changed status and later development where those exist.

Memory + update + context → learning.

Memory without update → identity lock.

Correction has to travel

A correction that exists but never reaches the audience exposed to the original claim may have low practical effect. That is why this project tracks ideas such as CorrectionReachRatio, ContextRetention and LabelPersistenceAfterCorrection.

These measures describe information ecology. They do not independently prove that the original claim was false, that anyone acted maliciously, or that the subject is innocent of every criticism.

Do not reproduce the mechanism in reverse

Reciprocal Transformation asks what a method turns the actor, target and shared environment into. If a system dehumanizes, the repair is not simply to choose a new target for dehumanization. Correction should dismantle the mechanism rather than preserve it with reversed polarity.

Compression

Interpretive Justice in one pass

See the person before the dossier. Identify the scope of the frame. Hear relevant first-person meaning and relevant external evidence. Count independent provenance rather than copies. Preserve the sequence around reactions. Keep a Door for reply and correction. Remember changes as durably as accusations. Let authority increase burden. Refuse both outsider totalization and self-sealing self-description.

Observe → distinguish claim type → hear → source → test → reply → correct → remember proportionately.
