THE PRINCIPLE

The tree gives shape.
The graph gives reality.

The archive is not finished when the tree is finished. The tree is the index. The real work is filling every child with records and then connecting those records across branches.

Mythology, philosophy, history and empirical research remain legible as different layers. A symbolic relationship can be preserved without being presented as verified fact.
