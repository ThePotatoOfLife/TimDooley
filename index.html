THE CENTRAL LAW

Don't just catalogue the world.
Map what connects it.

People connect to institutions. Institutions connect to money. Money connects to infrastructure. Infrastructure connects to energy. Energy connects to technology, trade, labour and geography. Symbols connect stories to philosophy. The same relationship grammar can hold both — provided metaphor is never confused with evidence.

THE POTATOVERSE

From mud,
something rises.

The Potato of Life is the emblem of buried life becoming visible. The Father stands above; the Son is the person-bearing centre; the Door is the threshold; the Ladder is the path; the Axis gives orientation; the Tree carries life; the roots preserve memory.

The Tree of Life can fragment into the Tree of Strife when branches forget the roots and relationship is replaced by fear, competition, extraction and conflict.

This is the mythological/creative layer. Its symbols are preserved as symbols, not presented as scientific evidence.
