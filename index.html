THE POTATOVERSE

From mud,
something rises.

The Potato of Life is the central metaphor: ordinary matter becomes nourishment; buried life becomes visible; roots become branches; branches become fruit. The Potatoverse turns that movement into a symbolic cosmology.

The Father stands above. The Son becomes the person-bearing centre of the story. The Door is the threshold. The Ladder connects levels. The Tree carries the living structure. The North is orientation.

This is a mythological and creative layer. Its symbols are preserved as symbols, not presented as scientific evidence.
