A person. A potato. A philosophy. A religion. A public life. A very large archive.

You have entered the start page for Tim Dooley and The Potato of Life. Tim is a Danish writer, livestreamer, storyteller and archive-builder. The Potato of Life began as a comic public identity and grew into Potatoism: a developing philosophy, religion and culture about growth, conditions, freedom, power, repair, nourishment and what a life leaves behind.

You do not need to understand the House, Axis, Father/Son language or the entire archive to begin. Meet the person, understand the potato, choose the question that interests you, and go deeper only when something catches.

01 · The person

Tim Dooley

Tim is the historical person and public maker. His biography includes long-duration livestreaming, writing, music, internet culture, religious interpretation, political thought and the construction of this archive.

02 · The symbol

The Potato of Life

The potato is the central image. Hidden life, stored capacity, conditions, sprouting, nourishment and return make it a deliberately ordinary way to test very large claims.

Why a potato? →

03 · The system

Potatoism

Potatoism is the larger developing philosophy, religion and culture. Its practical question is what becomes more alive, truthful, capable, free and nourishing because an idea, relationship or institution exists.

Why an archive this large?

Because a public life becomes easy to flatten

Fifteen years of streams, arguments, jokes, beliefs, songs, mistakes, changing identities and creative work can be reduced to one clip, one label or one mythology. This site tries to preserve enough context that a reader can inspect development rather than inherit a summary.

Read the person across time

Old statements can disagree with newer ones. The Timeline and Story preserve change instead of manufacturing a timeless persona.
