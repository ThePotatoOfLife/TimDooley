The Living Axis

The world, the crossing, and what grows from it

The project can be entered as one relationship rather than a pile of symbols. The Plane is the inhabited world. The Cross is where that world meets the vertical Axis. The Door is the threshold into transformation. From there the same rooted system can move toward generative integration or toward unresolved repetition and dispersion.

PlaneD4 · world, matter, history, evidence and consequence → CrossPlane × Axis · the world becomes vertically readable → DoorD5 · threshold, choice and change of relational regime

Tree of Life

narrowing · integration · generativity
Door→ Spiral Ladder→ Tree of Life→ Garden / Rooms→ Heaven / Crown→ North of North

Growth is not escape from the Plane. The upper path integrates what is carried upward, differentiates it into branches, and returns fruit, service, repair and seed to the world.

Roots of Strife

widening · residue · unresolved repetition
Knowledge / choice→ Roots of Ash→ Tree of Strife→ Wide Road→ Swamp

Root itself is not evil: roots are memory, cause and buried continuity. Strife begins when unresolved material keeps reproducing conflict, capture, dependency or fragmentation.

Repair is the return path.Ash can become soil; buried causes can become evidence; repetition can cross a threshold and change. The lower field is therefore connected back to the Door and to growth rather than treated as a one-way pit.
Tim's path through the structure

Sage → Turning → Door → Ladder → Father / Gardener. In the project's April 2025 interpretation this is a journey through the Axis, not another cosmic floor: the decisive move is from climber to orienting relation, followed by return through cultivation and service.
