THE IDEA

Not a collection of pages.
A field of relationships.

01

Start with things

Every subject can become a record: a person, place, institution, text, belief, company, law, technology, event or physical system.

02

Then connect them

Ownership, influence, membership, migration, trade, law, funding, conflict, belief and dependency turn isolated facts into a system.

03

Keep evidence visible

Historical material, observed facts, interpretation, mathematics and project mythology are deliberately kept distinguishable.

THE POTATO PRINCIPLE

Grow from the roots.
Follow the connections.

The joke is that a potato is ordinary. The point is that ordinary things are where the world becomes real: soil, bodies, food, families, work, money, memory, institutions and the networks built between them.
