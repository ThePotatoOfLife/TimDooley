THE ATLAS AT A GLANCE

Know what each number means.

The homepage now separates the major layers instead of presenting every count as if it were the same kind of record.

—core atlas recordsNodes + tree-child records + support records.
—relationshipsExplicit graph edges between records.
—evidence factsResearch claims with evidence metadata.
—sourcesRegistered research sources.
—extended studiesProject lore and interpretive material.
195canonical nationsThe world directory baseline.
—enriched nationsEffective country enrichment coverage.

Important: “core atlas records” is deliberately not called “all records.” The repository contains several layers — country files, enrichment overlays, graph manifests, evidence, lore, events and sector studies — and many of those are not counted in the single core-record figure. The old “165 records” label was therefore easy to misunderstand even though the underlying data layers were present.

THE POTATO PRINCIPLE

Start with something ordinary.
Keep following it.

A potato leads into soil, water, agriculture, labour, food, transport, markets, money, families, land, institutions and history. The point is not that everything is secretly a potato. The point is that almost anything becomes a map when you refuse to stop at the name.
