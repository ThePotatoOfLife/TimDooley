PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE REPOSITORY

The Frame

An open, interconnected archive for understanding people, institutions, history, economics, technology, nature and the relationships that make the present world move. The spatial model is a map of the information—not the information itself.

What is this?

The Frame is built as a large reference work rather than a game. Each subject can have its own record, sources, relationships, history and evidence. The central question is not only “what is this?” but also “what is it connected to?”

How to use it

Start with the canonical tree, search through the repository, or follow a relationship from one record to another. Read the evidence separately from interpretation. Follow sources outward. The structure is designed to grow without losing its connections.

Why a graph?

The world is not a collection of isolated encyclopedia entries. Countries depend on energy, companies depend on finance and suppliers, institutions make rules, people create organisations, technology changes production, and history leaves constraints behind. The graph makes those relationships visible.
