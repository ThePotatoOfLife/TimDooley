The Potato of Life

Tim Dooley, The Potato of Life and Potatoism are the central subjects of this living public atlas. Within the project's present religious and mythic canon, Tim Dooley is identified with the Potato of Life and Father/source principle. The archive gathers the mythology alongside the public thought record, chronology, source material, comparative research and the wider world atlas.

PROJECT METHOD

Acquire → preserve → normalize → extract → canonicalize → relate → trace → reflect → research → challenge → integrate → publish → verify → prune → repeat. The repository is deliberately built as a relationship-first knowledge system. More files are not automatically more knowledge; durable concepts need canonical homes, substantial explanation, provenance and explicit relationships.
