NORTH POLE
TIM DOOLEY · THE POTATO OF LIFE

You are standing at the North Pole. Below you is the World: life, history, people, places, institutions, events, and everything that can be observed. Above you is the Axis: the North, orientation, structure, relation, and the ideas that give the world its shape.

The Potato of Life begins here, at the meeting of the living world and the direction beyond it. Tim Dooley is the name at the center of this reading: a point of return, a root, a question, and a story that reaches toward the North, the grave, and the possible end of the world.

Read downward into the World. Read upward into the Axis. Everything else belongs to one of those two directions.
