The Potato of Life

Tim Dooley, The Potato of Life and Potatoism are the central subjects of this living public atlas. Within the project's present religious and mythic canon, Tim Dooley is identified with the Potato of Life and Father/source principle. The archive gathers the project's mythology alongside chronology, source material, comparative religion, potato biology and the wider world atlas.
