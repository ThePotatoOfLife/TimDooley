Governance method

Politics is not only positions; it is also how authority becomes action

The World layer now separates political positions from the machinery of governance: selection → mandate → resources → constraints → accountability → implementation → feedback. A policy can therefore be followed from problem definition through design, authorization, funding and implementation to monitoring, review, revision or exit.

This method does not produce a partisan score or an overall verdict on a political system. It keeps formal rules, observed practice, public capacity, outcomes and disagreement separately inspectable.
