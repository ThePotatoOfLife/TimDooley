Do not start with left or right

Start with the machine somebody wants to move.

Tim’s political corpus is most useful when it stops being a pile of labels and starts behaving like government. Take any proposal and push it until something concrete resists.

“Audit defence spending.” Fine. Who has statutory authority to compel the records? Which spending is classified? What happens when the audit finds a programme that is wasteful but strategically difficult to cancel?

“Tax billionaires.” Fine. Income, wealth, unrealized gains or estates? At what valuation date? What happens to illiquid ownership? What prevents avoidance? What revenue is realistically expected after behavior changes?

“Reduce debt.” Fine. Through taxes, spending cuts, growth, inflation, monetary changes or some mixture? Who bears the transition? Which services are protected? What happens in recession?

This is how the page should be read. An explicit position tells you what Tim presently prefers. A proposal tells you what intervention he has imagined. A programme such as North tries to connect many interventions into one direction. A prediction can later be checked against what happened. A research lead is permission to investigate, not permission to believe. Symbolic East/West/North/South language may organize a worldview without becoming a literal treaty map.

Political thought becomes serious at the point where the desired outcome meets authority, incentives, budgets, institutions, law, implementation, second-order effects and somebody who disagrees. The archive therefore preserves changes of mind and unresolved questions instead of manufacturing a complete ideology Tim never stated.

Reader test: after reading a Tim proposal, you should be able to say not only what he wants, but what mechanism would have to move, what evidence would tell us it worked, and who would carry the cost if it failed.

~2015 · explicit position · civil liberty / drug policy

Drug policy begins with harm differentiation rather than one moral category

The recovered corpus records cannabis advocacy and later opposition to treating all drug use as one criminal or moral category. The position emphasizes prevention, rehabilitation and harm differentiation, while explicitly distinguishing psychedelics and other substances from heroin, fentanyl, heavy narcotics and sedatives. The archive does not generalize this into an unrestricted all-drug legalisation doctrine.

15 January 2026 · explicit position · sovereignty

Greenland and Danish sovereignty are treated as boundaries, not tradable abstractions

The compendium records Tim framing sovereignty, country and soil as non-negotiable under external pressure. Strategic access, security or economic arrangements may still be questions for negotiation, but the attributed position does not treat sovereignty itself as an ordinary transferable asset.

February–May 2026 · programme design · Europe / digital capability

European capability becomes a systems programme

Across several 2026 entries, North and European autonomy move from symbolic orientation toward practical questions: defence burden-sharing, interoperable cloud and compute, encrypted data exchange, multiple vendors, energy security, production and institutional memory. The important archive distinction is that this is a programme design, not evidence that the proposed institutions or alliances already exist.

2 June 2026 · explicit position · state capacity

The recurring distinction is capable versus wasteful government, not simply big versus small

The recovered statement set combines preference for an efficient state with support for healthcare, education, welfare, infrastructure, defence, technology, industry and energy security. That creates a tension the page should preserve rather than flatten into a conventional left/right label: the attributed view asks whether public capacity produces durable outcomes and whether spending can be justified, measured and corrected.

27 August 2026 · explicit position · mixed political economy

Public services and markets are both retained

The recovered material supports public schools, healthcare/hospitals, unions, public prisons and a social safety net while also retaining markets, private property, entrepreneurship and contribution expectations. The page should therefore model the position as a mixed political economy rather than forcing it into a single doctrinal category.

2 September 2026 · policy proposal · institutional audit

“Reverse Jenga” is a sequential audit idea, not an instruction to demolish the state

The proposal is to examine public entities, programmes, NGOs, companies or branches for measurable value and then simplify, merge, repurpose, pause or remove structures sequentially. The core mechanism is reversibility and testing: change one load-bearing element, observe consequences, and avoid assuming a replacement will work merely because the existing structure is disliked.

Proposal → authority → implementation

A political proposal is incomplete until the machinery is named

The archive now treats implementation fields as part of political research rather than optional decoration. A proposal can be interesting without yet being legally or administratively specified; the missing fields should stay visible until researched.

Reverse Jenga · institutional audit

Jurisdiction
Not one fixed jurisdiction; the proposal is a method that could be applied to a particular government, agency, programme or public body.
Legal authority
Depends on the target. Some changes may be managerial; others require statute, appropriations, collective bargaining, procurement changes or constitutional limits.
Implementer
Relevant executive/administrative owner plus legislatures, auditors or independent review where required.
Financing
Audit/restructuring capacity must itself be budgeted; claimed savings should be measured after transition costs rather than assumed in advance.
Outputs
Service quality, processing time, unit cost, error rate, frontline staffing, redundancy removed, and whether the original public function still works.
Tradeoffs
Transition failure, loss of institutional memory, false identification of “waste,” political capture of the audit, and short-term savings that create larger downstream costs.

European digital sovereignty · federated capability

Jurisdiction
Would span EU-level law/programmes and member-state procurement, infrastructure, security and public-administration choices.
Legal authority
Depends on the instrument: EU legislation generally requires the applicable Commission/Parliament/Council process; procurement and national implementation have additional authorities.
Implementer
EU institutions, national governments/agencies, public buyers and qualified infrastructure/cloud/compute providers according to the chosen architecture.
Financing
Potential mix of EU programmes, member-state budgets, procurement and private investment; Tim's current corpus does not settle a complete financing formula.
Outputs
European compute/cloud capacity, interoperability, vendor concentration, switching ability, data-control guarantees, procurement competition, resilience and service performance.
Tradeoffs
Duplication, cost, fragmentation, protectionism, slower deployment, security inconsistency, and the risk that “sovereignty” becomes a label without real portability or capacity.

Implementation boundary: these fields are analytical requirements, not a claim that a proposal has been adopted. For EU law-making, the European Commission describes proposal, Parliament/Council adoption and later delegated/implementing stages as distinct parts of the process. Reader questions

Quick answers about Tim Dooley's politics

What political label does Tim Dooley use?

The strongest recovered self-description is “radically centered.” Other phrases such as “Nordic civic developmentalism” are archive syntheses and remain labeled as such.

Is Tim Dooley left-wing or right-wing?

The recovered corpus is multidimensional rather than a conventional left/right package: markets and private property coexist with public healthcare, education, welfare and unions; fiscal discipline coexists with strategic public investment; liberty is paired with civic responsibility.

What is the North Programme politically?

In the practical political layer it is a voluntary cooperation and capability programme involving sovereignty, defence, energy, industry, technology, infrastructure and resilience. Symbolic North and real institutional membership remain separate.

Are forecasts treated as political positions?

No. Forecasts are dated separately and routed to prediction auditing. A prediction about alliance movement or troop retrenchment does not automatically become a policy preference.

What political questions are still unresolved?

The archive still lacks sufficiently specific positions on several issues including abortion, detailed LGBTQ/trans law, exact gun-control design, full tax policy, electoral-system design, NATO end-state and several conflict-settlement questions.
