The highest-order political principle

Power, legitimacy and sovereignty

The most consistent high-level principle is that power is not the same as legitimacy. Wealth, military superiority, surveillance capacity, technological reach or institutional scale can make an actor powerful without making its actions justified. Legitimacy comes from some combination of law, consent, reciprocity, accountability, protection, proportionality, participation and good outcomes.

That is also why Tim’s mythic “king,” “God,” “throne” and “rule” language cannot simply be translated into a literal preference for monarchy or dictatorship. In the May 2026 practical European discussion he explicitly denied having formal authority and framed his work as an offer that governments could take or leave. The mythic layer expresses orientation, integration, burden or sacred authority; the constitutional layer still needs ordinary political legitimacy.
