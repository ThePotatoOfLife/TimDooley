CONTEXT
& EVIDENCE

The archive has become large enough that the important question is no longer only “what else can we add?” It is also: what surrounds each claim, where did it come from, what did it mean at the time, what changed later, what does it connect to, and what kind of thing is it?

Context changes the question before it changes the answer

The same sentence, image, bodily sensation or political claim can mean something very different depending on when it occurred, who said it, what kind of source preserves it, what had happened immediately before, what the speaker believed at the time, and what later interpretation was added afterward. Context is the archive's protection against flattening.

“I am the Potato of Life”

Identity statement or complete theology?

A public Potato identity statement from 2024 is evidence for the identity language used at that time, not for every theological meaning that developed later. Sources keeps the exact wording/date and provenance; Context asks what changed around it.

Context is interpretation around evidence, not another archive directory

Use Sources when you need provenance, evidence class or authority. Use Explore when you need to find a subject by question, name or path. Stay in Context when the problem is how date, surrounding event, domain, later interpretation or reception changes what a source can responsibly mean.

The distinction matters because the same artifact can be strong evidence for one question and weak evidence for another. Context does not upgrade the source; it tells you what question the source can answer without flattening chronology or domain boundaries.

Seven words the archive should not blur

Evidence

What is preserved?

An artifact, observation, record, measurement, testimony or other source object relevant to a question.

Claim

What is being asserted?

A proposition extracted from or supported by evidence. One source may support some claims strongly and others weakly.

Interpretation

What meaning is added?

An explanation or reading placed around evidence/claims. It can be insightful without being identical to the source.

Comparison

What resembles what?

A typed relation between separate objects or traditions. Resemblance does not by itself establish identity, transmission or cause.

Reconstruction

What is inferred from incomplete remains?

A best-current recovery assembled from fragments, memories or later references. It must remain distinguishable from a surviving original.

Reception

What did others do with it?

How audiences, communities, institutions or later writers represented, reused, rejected, distorted or remembered an object.

Canon

What does the project currently treat as governing?

The current project synthesis or definition. Canon can change through dated revision; it should not be silently backdated into earlier sources.

Context is successful when the reader can still separate the layers

A good synthesis does not erase the source object, the date, the evidence class, the domain or the fact that an interpretation was added later. The practical rhythm is simple: recover the object, classify what it can support, date it, explain the relation, and remove duplicated or misleading summaries.

That means richness is not measured by how many files surround a claim. It is measured by whether the reader can understand what happened, what the source establishes, what was inferred, what changed later, and where to inspect the underlying record.
