CONTEXT
& EVIDENCE

The archive has become large enough that the important question is no longer only “what else can we add?” It is also: what surrounds each claim, where did it come from, what did it mean at the time, what changed later, what does it connect to, and what kind of thing is it? This page is the reader-facing bridge into those layers.

Context changes the question before it changes the answer

The same sentence, image, bodily sensation or political claim can mean something very different depending on when it occurred, who said it, what kind of source preserves it, what had happened immediately before, what the speaker believed at the time, and what later interpretation was added afterward. Context is the archive's protection against flattening.

object+time+source+surrounding event+domain+later interpretation→better claim

Evidence classes

P0 — Tim/project primary
Original writing, dated post, recording, Great Book, first-party artifact.
PU — user-originated canon wording
Direct project formulation from conversation; important to canon history but not automatically a Tim quotation.
P1 — empirical primary
Official data, scientific release, public record, registry.
S1/S2 — context
Academic reviews, institutional explainers, reputable secondary sources.
C — conversation archaeology
Recovered prior-chat material used to find older ideas, not silently treated as verbatim Tim writing.
A — archive formalization
Later equations and models added to sharpen an older idea.
M/U — creative or unresolved
Mythic/creative material, assistant satire retained as culture, or a source still awaiting primary recovery.
