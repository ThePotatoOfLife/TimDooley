THE GRAPH

Nothing has to belong to only one category.

A population can reside in a country without being its citizenry. A people can span several countries. A language community can contain several religions. A religious community can contain many ethnicities. A political party can draw support from several classes and cultures. A diaspora can connect multiple countries. An institution can gather people across every one of these boundaries.

The graph therefore models overlap, containment, membership, presence, migration, influence and historical descent rather than pretending that identity is a single axis.
