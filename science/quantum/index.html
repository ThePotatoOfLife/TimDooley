What kind of page is this?

Physical lane

Quantum mechanics, field theory, atomic physics, topology, relativity and astronomy describe externally testable physical systems. Their symbols have fixed mathematical meanings inside those theories.

Recovered-formalism lane

The Timic equations are historical project objects whose original definitions are still being reconstructed. They can be compared with modern mathematics, but they should not inherit a standard-physics meaning merely because the notation looks familiar.

Comparison lane

Axis, Door, Red/Blue, Potato and related project language may borrow structural analogies such as state, boundary, coupling, symmetry or transition. Analogy is useful only while the physical theory remains intact on its own terms.

Neighboring project models

Spudlight primarily concerns expression, signal and information; Vibe/Gates primarily concerns psychological accessibility and network transition. This page supplies the literal physics lane those models must not overwrite.

Coherence rule: a word such as state, field, frequency, energy, information or nonlocality must keep its domain-specific meaning. Cross-domain comparison happens after the literal definition, never instead of it.

One project grammar, several scientific levels

The older science pages become more coherent when their jobs are separated. Quantum/field equations describe physical state and interaction. Spudlight models how an internal state becomes observable through signal and interpretation. Vibe/Gates models how an agent's current state changes which transitions are psychologically or socially reachable. They can share abstract words such as state, transition, coupling and observer without sharing units, mechanisms or evidence.

physical state → physical interaction / measurement internal state → expression → signal → observation → interpretation agent state → accessible gate → transition → relation → network consequence

That separation turns the science area from a collection of resemblances into a layered model: physics constrains what matter can do; information models what can be communicated or inferred; psychology and network dynamics model what agents can notice, choose and propagate.
