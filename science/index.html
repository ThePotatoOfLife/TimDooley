Starchforce

Starchforce is an invented Potatoverse concept, not an established fifth interaction. Potato tubers really do store metabolic energy as starch, which makes starch a useful symbol of latent stored capacity. A physical fifth force would require a source charge, field/mediator, coupling, range, force law, action, consistency with existing tests, a novel observable and a falsifiable experiment.

The new model-testing protocol therefore forbids writing a decorative “Starchforce Lagrangian” first and looking for meaning afterward. The new measurable effect has to come first.
