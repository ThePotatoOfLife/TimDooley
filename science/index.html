02 / Evidence & status system

Every idea gets a visible scientific status.

The evidence matrix is deliberately more important than visual polish: it tells a reader what was actually recovered, what was formalized later, what is established external science, and what remains open.

Primary / project-attested

Direct public/project material or a recovered formula with strong first-party attestation.

Book-derived project material

Scientific language, equations or models preserved in the 2024 Great Book stratum.

Conversation archaeology

Recovered from dated conversation history; wording or notation may still have unresolved primary-source details.

Project formalism

A model developed in the project as a mathematical language for a recurring structure.

Archive formalization

A later reconstruction or synthesis made to organize older material; not retroactively treated as original notation.

Established external science

Standard mathematics or physics used as a comparator, constraint, repair option or test framework.

Speculative comparator

A structured analogy or research possibility that has not been established as a physical claim.

Open / unrecovered target

An equation, transcript, derivation, parameter or test that the archive explicitly does not yet possess.

10 / Model testing

Scientific value increases when failure is possible.

The repository's testing protocol gives every speculative model a route toward sharper mathematics or rejection.

1. ProvenanceWho wrote the equation, and when?
2. VariablesDefine every symbol and mathematical type.
3. UnitsPhysical equations must be dimensionally consistent.
4. DomainDo not mix spacetime, graph, probability and symbolic ontology.
5. ObservableWhat could actually be measured or computed?
6. FalsifierWhat result would count against the model?
7. BaselineWhat established theory already explains the effect?
8. RevisionKeep corrections visible instead of erasing them.
