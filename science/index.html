06 / Completion queue

What would actually make the science stronger next?

Completion means fewer undefined variables, fewer parallel ansätze and more baselines, observables and predeclared failure conditions—not simply more equations.

Consolidate

One canonical model contract per model family; supporting records remain evidence and history.

Derive

Choose minimal actions and derive consequences before introducing another sector or analogy.

Calibrate

Fit parameters or bound them against real data wherever a physical or biological claim is intended.

Compare

Every empirical model competes with the simplest established baseline that already explains the effect.
