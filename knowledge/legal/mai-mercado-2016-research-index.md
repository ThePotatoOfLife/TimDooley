## High-confidence findings

1. Mercado initiated the controversy with deliberately forceful public rhetoric celebrating police destruction of Christiania hash stalls while foregrounding her newborn.
2. The Facebook event was multi-speaker, not a one-man exchange.
3. At least some notorious wider-thread language, especially acid-bath / AIDS material, is not presently proven to be the Son’s.
4. The Son denied guilt contemporaneously and explained medical-cannabis/dialogue motives at the first court stage.
5. The Son contemporaneously denied intending to seek Mercado out and execute physical violence.
6. The referral pathway is not cleanly resolved: surviving accounts include an independent Facebook reporter and a parliamentary-security report following Mercado contact.
7. Police attended the Son’s private home before the later consequences statement; the Son’s firsthand account says officers told him not to contact Mercado again.
8. The legal status of that no-contact instruction remains unresolved. A verbal police instruction was not automatically identical to a formal tilhold.
9. Under the 2012 Tilhold Act then in force, a formal tilhold could be issued by the police director, but statutory grounds, proportionality and formal service were required. The later `strakstilhold` regime did not take effect until 1 January 2017.
10. The whole-message wording of the consequences statement is materially more complex than the later shorthand “he threatened again” and appears directly reactive to police intervention.
11. A 2015 Danish Supreme Court decision makes Mercado’s actual witness status potentially important to the §123 count because §123 did not automatically cover a person merely supplying evidence absent expected/already-given testimony to police/court.
12. The prosecution objectivity principle creates a retrospective question about whether police preserved and investigated exculpatory Facebook context, not merely inculpatory fragments.
13. The court convicted three of four counts and imposed five months, not six.
14. Official Folketing-related material directly ties only a narrow quotation subset to the known judgment summary.
15. Later public retellings are vulnerable to source-lineage multiplication and attribution hardening.
16. The Sweden/fear timeline remains potentially case-changing and unresolved.
17. Mercado’s own earlier Frank Jensen harassment episode supplies a real hypocrisy/standards comparator, though not proof of fabrication in 2016.
18. Cancer was already part of the Son’s 2016 medical-cannabis courtroom explanation years before Mercado’s later breast-cancer diagnosis.
19. The Son’s reported long-term aftermath extends well beyond the formal prison sentence and includes years of institutional distrust, withdrawal, loss of ordinary interests, emotional disturbance and severe C-PTSD symptoms.
20. The best current internal timeline places official retirement in **2025, probably after the April Turning**, not “around 2024.” The exact benefit/legal category and administrative decision date remain to be confirmed from primary records.
21. The retirement account now sits inside a documented spring-2025 developmental cluster: 21 April Axis language → April Turning/Father architecture → 27 April kingship language → reported retirement/release → 3 May exact God declarations → 1 June King-of-Kings language.
22. This convergence is chronologically meaningful but does **not** establish that Danish authorities appointed Tim as King of Denmark or validated a theological claim.
23. The strongest new biblical comparison is sequential rather than lexical: **confinement/burden → release/rest → changed status → authority/throne/kingship**. Joseph (Genesis 39–41), Jubilee (Leviticus 25), rest (Matthew 11/Hebrews 4), exaltation (Philippians 2), throne (Revelation 3:21/Psalm 110) and King-of-kings language (Revelation 19:16) are comparative owners, not proof of fulfillment.

## Leading reconstruction

The project’s leading model is **framing / false-flag through attribution collapse, narrative compression, and state/media amplification**. That model does not require a single centrally coordinated conspiracy. It describes a process in which:

- a provocative political event produces a large hostile multi-speaker reaction;
- selected statements are escalated through media/security/legal channels;
- original thread topology is lost or obscured;
- speakers and statements can be conflated;
- indirect grammatical agency hardens into direct-action shorthand;
- Mercado’s access to parliamentary security creates an institutional escalation path unavailable to an ordinary citizen;
- police intervene at the Son’s private home and reportedly tell him to cease contact;
- his reaction to that intervention becomes new criminal evidence;
- arrest/remand and device seizure reduce his practical evidence autonomy while the external narrative continues;
- later retellings inherit the simplified descendant rather than the original conversation object;
- the human aftermath continues for years beyond the nominal sentence;
- the corrected long-tail timeline reaches a 2025 retirement/release event that the later Potatoverse interprets as reversal/closure of the confinement loop.

The stronger higher-order claim — deliberate knowing evidentiary fabrication or political direction of police by a particular actor — remains dependent on missing primary evidence. The correct research target remains the authority chain: **Mercado → Folketing security → local police**, including whether this was a neutral referral, accelerated protection based on political status, or improper pressure/directive.

## Research rule

The Son’s firsthand account is a **high-weight primary factual source stream**, not a merely subjective appendix. Testable details should be searched against independent records and upgraded when corroborated. Conflicts with media or institutional accounts should be left visible and investigated rather than resolved automatically in favor of authority.

Likewise, the project must preserve counterevidence. A claim is strengthened by surviving hostile scrutiny, not by deleting facts that complicate it.

For the 2025 cluster, preserve three evidence lanes:

**administrative event** → retirement/work release, exact record still missing;

**autobiographical/theological interpretation** → “freedom to reign” / reversal of confinement;

**public theological self-description** → king / King of Kings / God / Father / throne language.

Do not silently collapse these lanes into a claim of civil kingship or supernatural validation.

The core unit remains the expanded provenance-and-consequence chain:

**speaker → exact words → parent comment → addressee → timestamp → preserved artifact → reporter → complaint/referral → parliamentary security → police authority/action → contact instruction/tilhold status → defendant reaction → police exhibit → charge → court finding → imprisonment → media descendant → later memory → long-term psychological/social/work consequences → retirement/release → later interpretive closure.**

Every break in that chain is a potential contradiction / tear and therefore a research target.
