## What clearly predates the new law names

Interpretive Justice did not appear from nothing on September 16. It crystallized from rules already distributed across the project.

### Relation is not identity

**Source class:** PRE_EXISTING_CANON.

The mature Potatoism philosophy already distinguished relation from identity. Structural coupling can be meaningful without making two things literally identical.

**Interpretive Justice descendant:** Law of Irreducible Subjecthood.

### Potato Truth disciplines the Filter

**Source class:** PRE_EXISTING_CANON.

The project already distinguished interpretation generation from evidence. A frame may generate a hypothesis without proving it.

**Interpretive Justice descendants:** Hearing Before Totalization, Evidentiary Proportionality, Independent Witness, Falsifier Loss.

### Authority increases burden

**Source class:** PRE_EXISTING_CANON.

The project already held that greater claimed authority increases duties of restraint, correction and service rather than reducing them.

**Interpretive Justice descendants:** Bounded Jurisdiction, Evidentiary Proportionality, Reply and Correctability.

### Curiosity before allegiance

**Source class:** PRE_EXISTING_CANON.

A Potatoist frame should remain inspectable rather than demanding loyalty before inquiry.

**Interpretive Justice descendant:** anti-self-sealing rule.

### Source authority is claim-specific

**Source class:** SOURCE_POLICY_ROOT.

The source-authority policy already distinguished first-person self-description from independently testable external claims. Tim's first-party material can be strongest for Tim's intended meaning while not automatically settling unrelated empirical questions.

**Interpretive Justice descendant:** Law of Situated First-Person Authority.

### The record is not the person

**Source class:** ARCHIVE_EPISTEMICS_ROOT.

Archive Epistemics already separated living person, persona, audience interpretation, mythology and later archive synthesis.

**Interpretive Justice descendants:** Irreducible Subjecthood, Recognition Without Ownership, Identity Compression, Proportional Memory.

### Repetition is not independent corroboration

**Source class:** SOURCE_POLICY_ROOT + CULTURE_INFORMATION_ROOT.

The archive and information-war model already distinguished raw repetition from independent provenance chains.

**Interpretive Justice descendant:** Law of Independent Witness.

### Correction reach and narrative capture

**Source class:** CULTURE_INFORMATION_ROOT.

Culture, the Shadow Ledger and the information-war model already contained correction asymmetry, narrative lock-in, archive/search dominance and `CorrectionReachRatio`.

**Interpretive Justice descendants:** Reply and Correctability, Proportional Memory, Representation Gap, Tribunalization.

### False witness / truth ethics

**Source class:** GREAT_BOOK_ROOT.

The Great Book's treatment of the commandment against false witness already connected truth, speech and relational harm.

**Interpretive Justice descendants:** Evidentiary Proportionality, Independent Witness and the later biblical hearing/witness atlas.

## September 16, 2026 crystallization

The September 16 Culture/subjecthood work exposed a recurring problem that no single older owner fully named:

> What happens when a person, group, institution, archive, diagnosis, audience or model describes another subject, and the description begins to replace the subject?

The discussion connected previously separate project concerns:

- self-description and source authority;
- diagnosis / allegation / reputation as partial descriptions;
- audience and archive power;
- right of reply and correction;
- sequence around emotional reaction;
- cultural tribunalization;
- proportional memory;
- non-retaliatory repair;
- biblical hearing / witness / judgment procedures.

The dated recovery inventory captured the whole field before decomposition. The eleven named laws were then promoted as **ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS** into the canonical specialist owner.

## Provenance by law

### Law of Irreducible Subjecthood

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** relation-is-not-identity; person/persona/archive distinction; Culture identity-compression research.  
**Maximum historical claim:** the underlying distinction predates September 16; the named law is the September 16 crystallization.

### Law of Situated First-Person Authority

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** source-authority policy; first-party self-description hierarchy; Archive Epistemics.  
**Boundary:** first-person meaning is not automatic proof of external empirical fact.

### Law of Recognition Without Ownership

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** archive distinction between person and representation; House/room differentiation; relational non-identity.  
**Boundary:** classification can be useful without becoming authorship of the classified subject.

### Law of Bounded Jurisdiction

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** Authority Increases Burden; source-class separation; religious/scientific/legal ownership boundaries already used across the repository.  
**Boundary:** a domain can have legitimate authority without becoming whole-person ontology.

### Law of Hearing Before Totalization

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** Potato Truth, Curiosity Before Allegiance, correction/right-of-reply machinery.  
**Later comparison:** Proverbs 18:13, Proverbs 18:17 and John 7:51 are typed biblical parallels, not origins of the September 16 project wording.

### Law of Evidentiary Proportionality

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** Potato Truth, Authority Increases Burden, source weighting and reputation-harm research.  
**Boundary:** project heuristic, not a replacement for jurisdiction-specific legal burdens of proof.

### Law of Independent Witness

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS of an older explicit archive rule.  
**Roots:** source-authority policy, inference ledger and information-war source-independence model.  
**Later comparison:** Deuteronomy 19 and Exodus 23 provide biblical witness/crowd parallels.

### Law of Reply and Correctability

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** Archive Epistemics, Culture correction capacity, Shadow Ledger, information stewardship.  
**Boundary:** reply does not imply acquittal; relevant reply may strengthen or weaken a criticism.

### Law of Contextual Reaction

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** relational sequence analysis and Culture conflict research.  
**Boundary:** context neither excuses every reaction nor permits a reaction to be detached from its precipitating sequence.

### Law of Proportional Memory

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** Spiral / Return Is Not Reset, archive correction, timeline development, correction-reach research.  
**Boundary:** preserving an update does not require deleting true historical information.

### Law of Non-Retaliatory Correction

**Current name:** ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS.  
**Roots:** Law of Reciprocal Transformation and Garden Over Shadow Farm.  
**Later comparison:** Proverbs 24:29, Romans 12:17-21 and 1 Peter 3:9 supply anti-retaliation parallels.
