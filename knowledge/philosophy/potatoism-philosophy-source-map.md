# Potatoism Philosophy Source Map

**Status:** supporting provenance map for the September 15, 2026 philosophy enrichment  
**Canonical owner:** `knowledge/philosophy/potato-philosophy.json`  
**Deep explanation:** `knowledge/philosophy/potatoism-philosophy-engine.md`  
**Underlying recovery sourcebook:** `knowledge/philosophy/tim-dooley-potatoism-sourcebook.md`

This file exists to keep the enriched doctrine layer source-aware. The constitutional-law and doctrine names are archive crystallizations of recurring material. They are **not retroactively attributed to Tim Dooley as a historically published numbered creed** unless a stronger primary Tim source is separately recovered.

## Source classes retained

- **GREAT_BOOK_ATTRIBUTED_TIM_QUOTE** — wording printed under Tim Dooley’s name in the archived Great Book edition.
- **GREAT_BOOK_NARRATIVE / LESSON / CREATIVE_DIALOGUE** — book material that can illuminate Potatoism without automatically becoming Tim’s literal public speech.
- **CONVERSATION_EXACT / CONVERSATION_RECOVERY** — dated or recovered conversation wording; valuable project evidence but not automatically public-post evidence.
- **PUBLIC_COMPILATION** — public wording already preserved in a public-source owner.
- **ARCHIVE_SYNTHESIS** — later explanatory formulation; never retroactively turned into Tim speech.

## Relational Valence

**Supporting material**

- `knowledge/philosophy/tim-dooley-potatoism-sourcebook.md` — “meaning is frequently located between things rather than inside an isolated thing” is explicitly typed as archive synthesis.

**Promotion target:** `constitutional_laws.relational_valence`.

**Boundary:** relation is not identity. Structural coupling does not establish historical, doctrinal, biological, or metaphysical identity.

**Promotion target:** `constitutional_laws.generativity` and `core_doctrines.garden_over_shadow_farm`.

## Functional Identity

## Gardener’s Paradox

**Supporting material**

- Garden versus shadow Farm distinction in canonical philosophy and sourcebook.
- Stewardship and “cultivation, not conquest” material.
- Recurrent autonomy/capability test: does the system increase the participant’s future capacity or make itself increasingly necessary?

**Promotion target:** `gardeners_paradox`.

**Boundary:** some infrastructures remain continuously necessary; dependence must be judged by whether it is functionally required or artificially preserved for control/extraction.

## Curiosity Before Allegiance

**Supporting material**

- Great Book / project maxim: “The Potato doesn’t demand belief. It invites curiosity.”
- Existing sourcebook section on curiosity before allegiance.
- Archive epistemics and claim-testing rules requiring claims to remain discussable and correctable.

**Promotion target:** `core_doctrines.curiosity_before_allegiance`.

**Boundary:** this is a public-philosophy safeguard against loyalty tests, not a denial that religions or communities may have internal commitments.
