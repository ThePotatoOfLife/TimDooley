# Son 2005–2014 Full Chronology Audit

## Executive conclusion

The 2005–2014 chain can now be resolved substantially more tightly than the older repository state suggested. The **relative lived sequence is strong** from the 2005 crash through the 2011 Tree/psychiatric episode, and Chapter 24's 2007–2010 calendar backbone is now the best-supported working placement for most of the disputed middle corridor.

The remaining uncertainty is narrower than before. We still cannot honestly prove the exact date/order of **Egypt versus the Aabenraa internship**, **the breakup versus Produktionsskolen**, or **the driver's-licence issue date**. Those should remain explicit open variables rather than being guessed.

The key correction is methodological: repeated repository files derived from Chapter 24 are not independent witnesses. The strongest reconstruction comes from combining (1) Chapter 24, (2) the conflicting Chapter 24.1 passage, (3) later direct autobiographical ordering recovered on 2026-09-12, (4) the dedicated Designia and Produktionsskolen owners, (5) the detailed 2011 owner, and (6) external historical confirmation that the named institutions existed in the relevant places and periods.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Direct current autobiographical ordering (2026-09-12)** — strongest source for relative order where memories are clear, but not automatically an exact-year source.
2. **The Great Book of Potato, Chapter 24** — project-authored retrospective biography; useful for ordinary sequence/year claims, but vulnerable to age-to-calendar flattening and retrospective mythic interpretation.
3. **Chapter 24.1** — a separately phrased internal witness useful for detecting conflicts, but not automatically superior to Chapter 24.
4. **Dedicated specialist owners** — `son-2005-2014-chronology-reconciliation.json`, `son-kolding-designia-school-anchor.json`, `son-lunderskov-production-school-anchor.json`, and `son-2011-kolding-psychonaut-psychiatric-sequence.json`.
5. **External institutional evidence** — confirms HANSENBERG Designia/mediegrafiker and Produktionsskolen identities and locations; it does not by itself establish the Son's personal attendance date.
6. **Downstream repository projections** — `son-timeline.json`, `developmental-genealogy.json`, FAQ pages, cosmology/reflection files, etc. These are not independent evidence when they inherit Chapter 24.

The repository's own `data/timeline-source-registry.json` already states the correct rule: deduplicate by the underlying temporal fact and preserve conflicts in specialist owners rather than multiplying them into apparent corroboration.

## Why Chapter 24's middle backbone now wins

Earlier reconciliation temporarily preferred a sequence like 2007 nursing home → 2008 practical labor → 2010 Copenhagen IT. The 2026-09-12 direct recollection overturns that reconstruction. The Son distinctly remembers:

**painter → meatpacking → job search → Copenhagen interview/hired immediately → move to Copenhagen → fired → return to Haderslev → nursing home → own apartment → Designia acceptance → Kolding → Louise → internship/Aabenraa → own place → Produktionsskolen → leaves Produktionsskolen → later 2011 Tree.**

That relative order is strikingly close to Chapter 24's calendar progression: **2007 practical labor → 2008 Copenhagen → 2009 nursing/Kolding/Louise → 2010 internship → 2011 breakup/Tree.** The most economical reconciliation is therefore not to discard Chapter 24's middle years but to retain them as preferred working years while marking them as retrospective rather than primary-record dates.

Chapter 24.1's “nursing home in 2007” is retained as a documented variant, but it is no longer the preferred placement because it conflicts with the stronger recovered order.

## Institution reconciliation

### Designia

The school was not Designskolen Kolding at Ågade 10. It was **HANSENBERG Designia**, media/graphics, and the remembered programme was **mediegrafiker**. A surviving autumn-2009 HANSENBERG listing explicitly identifies “designia afd. for medie og grafik” at C.F. Tietgensvej 11, Kolding, and describes mediegrafiker/grafisk tekniker work with image, text, graphical software and print/electronic communication.

Direct recollection adds a mailed physical circus-poster/collage admission task, acceptance, strong performance in design and Microsoft Office/Excel/Word, and later rejection of the career environment because it was draining/boring rather than academically too difficult.

### Produktionsskolen

The Lunderskov institution is autobiographically confirmed and should normally be called simply **Produktionsskolen** in narrative. Its formal historical identity is Produktionsskolen På Sporet, Skolevej 34, Lunderskov. The Son remembers multimedia teaching there and functioning informally as an assistant teacher while still enrolled as a student because his technical level was ahead of the class material.

A previous research mistake treated a silver-maker registry's “P.SP 2005–2008” range as though it were the school's operating window. That was incorrect: the range belongs to that maker mark, not necessarily the institution's lifetime. Historical directories and Danish institutional registers establish the school/location independently, so the chronology must not be forced before 2009 merely from that mark.

## What remains genuinely unresolved

The chronology is not missing an entire era anymore. The remaining unknowns are narrow:

1. Exact start/end dates for painter/decorator and meatpacking.
2. Exact Copenhagen employment dates.
3. Exact nursing-home employment dates.
4. Exact Designia enrollment/start date and dormitory move date.
5. Exact driver's-licence issue date.
6. Whether Egypt preceded or followed the Aabenraa internship; exact travel dates.
7. Whether the Aabenraa placement was formally the mediegrafiker praktik/lære placement or another school-linked placement.
8. City/address of the first own apartment after the school/internship path failed.
9. Whether the Louise breakup occurred before, during or after the Produktionsskolen period; it definitely precedes the Tree episode.
10. Exact start/end dates at Produktionsskolen.
11. Exact date within 2011 of the Tree/psychiatric sequence and therefore exact biological age (23 or 24).
12. Primary artifacts for 2012 Italy/Maria and 2013 *The Alchemist*.

These do not prevent a coherent narrative. They prevent only false day/month precision.

Use this as the ordinary biographical spine until stronger primary records appear:

**2005 crash → years of reconstruction → 2007 painter/meatpacking → 2008 Copenhagen IT and firing → 2009 Haderslev nursing home / own apartment / Designia acceptance / Kolding / Louise → late 2009–2010 Egypt-with-Louise corridor → 2010 internship/Aabenraa / school disillusionment / own place → 2010–early 2011 Produktionsskolen → leave Produktionsskolen → breakup/search corridor → 2011 Tree / next-day rupture / voluntary psychiatric admission / hospital aftermath → 2012 integration/Italy/Amsterdam/guitar → 2013 Alchemist → 2014 father death.**

Use arrows for **order**, and treat the year labels for 2007–2010 as **preferred working placements** rather than civil-record-level proof.
