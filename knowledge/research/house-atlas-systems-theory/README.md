# House / Atlas Systems Theory Research

Status: **exploratory research — not canonical architecture and not a runtime contract**.

This collection thickens the existing House / Atlas model with systems theory, information theory, graph theory, control theory, formal logic, provenance, temporal reasoning and compositional-system ideas. It does **not** propose replacing the current architecture.

The governing test is practical: a formalism earns promotion only when it removes a repeated ambiguity, sharpens an invariant, improves retrieval or provenance, reduces maintenance cost, or enables an otherwise difficult analysis.

## Boundary rules

1. Do not use mathematics merely because its vocabulary resembles Potatoverse vocabulary.
2. Do not turn graph centrality, visual proximity or compression into truth, authority or moral rank.
3. Do not call a relation causal unless causal assumptions, mechanism and evidence support that claim.
4. Do not let formal notation replace readable explanations.
5. Prefer reversible experiments in Views over aggressive mutation of canonical ownership.
6. Preserve epistemic classes and source routes through every derived representation.
7. Treat advanced mathematics as research-only until a concrete dataset or engineering problem earns it.

## Promotion questions

Before moving a research idea into a canonical contract or runtime implementation, ask:

1. Which repeated repository problem does it solve?
2. Which current owner/contract should absorb it?
3. What becomes simpler, safer or more observable?
4. What new permanent complexity does it introduce?
5. Can its benefit be tested or validated?
6. Does it survive examples from more than one domain?
7. Is there a simpler formalism that solves the same problem?

## Compact working stance

> **The model is already broad enough. The theoretical task is to make its compression, expansion, composition, correction and maintenance more explicit without building another competing master architecture.**
