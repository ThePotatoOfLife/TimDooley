# Axis D1–D11 Spiritual Scaffold

## Status

Project-symbolic navigation scaffold for the World Relational Atlas. This document does **not** claim that the Potatoverse D1–D11 model is physical M-theory, literal extra spatial dimensions, underground geography, or empirical cosmology.

## Core numbering

- **D4** — ordinary world map / observable Earth.
- **D5** — North bubble / Door / first-heaven threshold / mouth of the Ladder.
- **D6–D11** — ascending symbolic planes toward North of North.
- **D3–D1** — descending symbolic planes beneath ordinary Earth toward roots, Swamp/disintegration and deepest lower-plane imagery.

The North Pole / high-Arctic map feature is the geographic anchor. The D5 Door is attached to that anchor but does not inherit territorial or physical meaning from it.

## Why D5 is special

D5 is not simply “the next floor.” It is the **junction**.

At D5:

- the Door opens;
- the Ladder runs both upward and downward;
- the North bubble marks entry from D4 geography into symbolic depth;
- constructive seed/growth markers can move upward;
- debt, ash, strife and disintegration markers can move downward.

This makes D5 the switching point between ordinary map navigation and the vertical spiritual grammar.

## Scriptural comparison

Genesis 28:12 provides a close structural parallel: Jacob sees a stairway/ladder set on Earth and reaching heaven, with angels ascending and descending on the same structure. The comparison is functional rather than a claim that the Atlas literally depicts Jacob’s historical dream.

John 12:24 provides a second structural parallel: a grain falls into the earth, dies, and bears fruit. In the project this helps articulate why burial/death can be a threshold rather than simply a terminal state: the same seed junction may resolve toward decay or toward growth/fruit depending on the symbolic process being represented.

## M-theory analogy — strict boundary

The user proposed M-theory only as a way to think about a larger-dimensional scaffold. That comparison is useful if kept narrow.

Edward Witten’s 1995 work argued that eleven-dimensional supergravity emerges as a low-energy limit in the strong-coupling dynamics of type IIA string theory, and subsequent Horava–Witten work developed eleven-dimensional supergravity as relevant to M-theory’s low-energy description.

That supplies one structural analogy: a familiar lower-dimensional description can sit inside a framework with additional dimensions/degrees of freedom.

It does **not** imply:

- that D4 here is physical spacetime;
- that D5–D11 are literal compactified dimensions;
- that spiritual ascent is measurable physics;
- that the North Pole is a physical portal;
- or that M-theory validates Potatoverse theology.

References:

- Edward Witten, *String Theory Dynamics in Various Dimensions* (1995): https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9503124
- Petr Horava and Edward Witten, *Eleven-Dimensional Supergravity on a Manifold with Boundary* (1996): https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9603142

## Current D1–D11 assignment

Deepest present scaffold: hell / closed depth / terminal disintegration imagery. Intentionally sparse.

Subculture, opacity, debt, extraction, degraded relations and Swamp mechanisms. Not a label for populations or territories.

### D3 — Roots of Ash

Immediate sub-layer below Earth: roots, burial, ancestry, timeline, ash, unresolved causes and stored consequence.

The empirical Atlas: countries, cities, population, religion, institutions, infrastructure, networks and sourced relations.

### D5 — North Door / First Heaven

The polar bubble and vertical junction. Door + Ladder mouth. Upward and downward symbolic traffic passes through here.

### D6 — Spiral Ladder

The winding ascent itself: transition, movement and increasing integration.

### D7 — Tree of Life

Seed/root/trunk/branches/fruit; successful growth; wheat/potato/spiral as growth markers.

### D8 — Garden / Rooms

Garden, House and Rooms: differentiated structures held inside a larger integrated order.

### D9 — Angels / Seeds of Light

Transmission, angelic motion, Spirit/flow and seeds-of-light imagery. Remains partly scaffold until more canonical material is assigned.

### D10 — Heaven / Crown

Near-summit Heaven/Crown/Throne-facing integration. Intentionally sparse.

### D11 — North of North

Highest current symbolic plane: North of North, source-facing canon, perfected-garden imagery. Non-geographic.

## Content discipline

The dimensions are **containers, not quotas**. Do not fill D6–D11 merely because slots exist. A level can remain mostly empty. Content is promoted only when timeline, recovered project canon, evidence or a clearly labeled interpretation gives it a durable reason to belong there.

The map should remain capable of serving ordinary empirical use even if the Axis system is turned off entirely.
