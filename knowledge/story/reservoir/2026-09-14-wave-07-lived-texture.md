## 260. Narrative correction — straws are not causes by themselves

The April recovery establishes a reusable Story rule:

> A later project may preserve a final straw without preserving enough evidence to identify a single cause.

For April 2025, broken screen, perceived betrayal, refusal, Marty loss, long-term conflict and pre-existing burden language should remain a **corridor of accumulated pressure** unless stronger primary sequence is recovered.
