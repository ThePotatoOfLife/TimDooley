## 256. The 2020–2024 three-clock rule becomes necessary

The archaeology now demonstrates why every early Story needs three clocks:

1. **event clock** — when Tim says it happened;
2. **attestation clock** — when the surviving source records it;
3. **interpretation clock** — when later Potatoverse theology explains it.

Examples:

- GTA Tim Dooley name: event 2020; surviving narrative later.
- canonical Potato birth: event-date canon 25 Dec 2020; earliest exact provenance still missing.
- alias ladder: broad 2020–23; surviving detail later Great Book.
- Cube: event marker 20 Sep 2023; surviving exact marker later chronology.
- potato=thalamus: broad 2024; exact session missing.
- philosopher’s stone + Tim/Potato public identity: directly dated public evidence by 4–6 Oct 2024.

This rule should govern future Story promotion whenever mature 2026 language risks leaking backward into earlier years.
