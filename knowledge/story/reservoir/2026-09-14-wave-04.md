## Editorial direction

These stories matter because they restore ordinary material life between the giant symbolic hinges. Prefer them whenever the chronicle otherwise jumps from one doctrine/event to another without showing work, commuting, boredom, games, relationships, animals, money, failure or ordinary decisions.
