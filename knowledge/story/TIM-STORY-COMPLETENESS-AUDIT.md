# Tim Dooley Story — Chronological Completeness Audit

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: living Story-Refinery control document
Scope: **Tim Dooley from canonical project birth (25 December 2020) forward.** Earlier Son/vessel material is prehistory and should enter the public river only when it materially explains Tim's emergence or later development.

## Completion rule

A period is not covered merely because the archive knows its doctrine. Tim-side Story coverage asks six separate questions:

1. **What was Tim calling himself / becoming?**
2. **Who was present?** People, recurring chatters, friends, rivals, collaborators, witnesses.
3. **Where was Tim?** Platform, stream, server, book, game, project, public account, website.
4. **What actually happened?** A sequence with action/reaction/change, not merely a concept or quote.
5. **What was Tim making or doing?** Songs, streams, games, images, books, AI systems, maps, arguments, jokes, archive work.
6. **What changed afterward?** Identity, relationship, project, vocabulary, platform, public practice or direction.

A year/month with theology but no people/actions/projects remains **partially covered**.

## Source hierarchy for completeness

- **A — dated old-conversation exact/near-exact:** strongest source for reconstructing what Tim was thinking/doing inside a session; not automatically public.
- **B — dated public primary/indexed:** strongest source for what Tim publicly said/did on a given date.
- **C — project/autobiographical retrospective:** useful for chronology and relationships, but later interpretation must not be backdated.
- **D — Great Book literary/project stratum:** valid creative history and literary Side Stories; not documentary biography unless separately sourced.
- **E — assistant-created/adopted creative artifact:** archive as creative history only, never silently convert to biography.

## Efficiency rule

Do not write isolated stories directly from search results. Work period-first:

`period → source sweep → people/projects list → episode candidates → merge overlapping fragments into sessions/corridors → source boundary → full narrative → public river`

This minimizes duplication while maximizing fullness. A dense day can contain several memorable quotes but remain one full story with smaller beats inside it.
