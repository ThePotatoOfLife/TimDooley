# Tim Dooley Story — 2020–2024 Gap Map

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: active Story-Refinery control document
Purpose: separate **event time**, **first recovered attestation**, and **later interpretation** across the years where Tim/Potato emerges.

## Why this map exists

The 2020–2024 period is especially easy to flatten because the surviving sources are uneven. Some details are preserved in later autobiographical Great Book chapters, some dates are canonical project dates whose original source has not yet been recovered, some 2024 material has dated public posts, and some later theology describes earlier years with language Tim did not necessarily use at the time.

For Story authoring, every candidate should answer three different questions:

1. **Event clock** — when the event is said to have happened.
2. **Attestation clock** — when the surviving source actually says it.
3. **Interpretation clock** — when later Potatoverse theology explains what the older event supposedly meant.

A later interpretation may illuminate an earlier event. It must not silently become contemporaneous evidence.

## 2020 — the Tim Dooley name appears before the Potato chronology stabilizes

### What survives

Great Book Chapter 15.11 explicitly says that **in 2020** the name `Tim Dooley` emerged during a GTA V Italian-mafia LARP shared by three in-game Dooleys. The chapter remembers daytime truck-driving roleplay, nighttime joyrides/bars, and the surname sticking beyond the session.

This is currently the strongest story-level source for the **Tim Dooley name/persona** appearing before later Potato theology.

### What does not survive yet

- exact GTA date;
- server/session logs, screenshots or clips;
- original wording at the moment the surname was chosen;
- clean chronology for when `RationalPotato` begins relative to the GTA sequence;
- primary source for the later canonical **25 December 2020** Potato-birth date.

### Important distinction

`Tim Dooley name emergence` ≠ `Potato birth` ≠ `Father realization`.

The current mature project keeps 25 December 2020 as the canonical spiritual birth of Tim/Potato, but the exact earliest source for that date remains a recovery target. The Story should therefore treat the date as **project canon**, not invent a Christmas room or scene.

### Current public Story

`story-content/2020-dooley-gta-origin.html` tells the GTA/name episode and intentionally omits unnecessary private descriptors of the other players.

## 2020–2023 — the alias ladder

Great Book Chapter 15.11 preserves a sequence that is more useful than the generic phrase “the Potato identity developed”:

`RationalPotato → Potato69 → The Potato of Life`

The same later autobiographical chapter says Tim returned to streaming / virtual reality and decided to embody a potato. `RationalPotato` is explained as an intentionally funny collision between `rational` and `potato`. At first, the Potato is described as an **art/avatar kept distinct from Tim**.

After roughly a year of inhabiting the potato avatar, the chapter says the distinction itself becomes a philosophical problem: if someone can LARP as a potato, can the mask become more than costume? The later chapter interprets this as the move from wearing the potato to becoming the Potato of Life.

### Source boundary

This is **later autobiographical/project testimony**, not a contemporaneous stream transcript. The alias order is worth preserving. Exact transition dates still need recovery.

### Story consequence

The Story now needs a dedicated alias-transition episode rather than making 2021 Filter, 2022 Rational Potato and 2023 Potato of Life look like unrelated labels.

## 2021 — avatar / filter / protective container

### Current strongest reconstruction

The project remembers the potato avatar/filter as a joke that becomes protective architecture: Tim can appear publicly through a vegetable while keeping distance from an older face/persona and from the demand to resolve the Potato’s meaning immediately.

Chapter 15.11 strengthens the **avatar/VR/streaming** side of this period. Other retrospective project history supplies the `filter` wording.

### Missing primary material

- first potato-filter/VR appearance;
- first platform/session where `RationalPotato` is visible;
- exact account/channel names at that moment;
- first audience reactions;
- first time Tim explicitly says the Potato is protective rather than only funny.

### Coverage judgment

Developmental account: good.
Documentary scene: thin.

## 2023 — Potato of Life becomes a larger public identity

### Broad transition

Retrospective timeline says Tim moves from `Potato` toward `Potato of Life` in 2023 and names BigTech, Grace and Ben Thorp as people in the period. Their original conversations remain unrecovered.

### September 2023 platform clue

Chapter 15.11 says that **in September 2023** Tim was `unearthed` / returned as the Potato of Life and that both **Twitch and Kick** channels were active. This is useful platform chronology, but the chapter is a later retrospective account and does not supply an independently preserved stream transcript.

### 20 September 2023 — Cube of the Moon

A separate later year-by-year Great Book chronology gives **20 September 2023** as the Cube-of-the-Moon marker.

Important: Chapter 15.11’s broad September Potato-of-Life/platform return and Chapter 24’s exact September-20 Cube marker should not be collapsed into one event unless a primary source connects them.

### Biggest unresolved 2023 people

- **BigTech** — current original interaction missing; Great Book later turns BigTech into `Mindgraper`, Cybersoil technology titan and other literary forms.
- **Grace** — named in retrospective chronology; scene, platform and role unresolved.
- **Ben Thorp** — named in retrospective chronology; scene, platform and role unresolved.
- **Metalorian** — appears in later project interpretation around the Cube; first actual event/source needs separation from later karmic reading.

### Coverage judgment

Identity/platform skeleton: improving.
Human/social chronology: very thin.

## 2024 — first period where later project material and dated public material overlap usefully

### Retrospective / literary layer

- Great Book grows into a large world and Tim becomes Sage/guide inside it.
- Marty, Sammy, Monkey/OPS/Tree, Cyraxx, Robotosan, BigTech and Capy appear in varying retrospective/literary forms.
- Potato/thalamus or `inner potato` development is placed somewhere in 2024.
- Great Book authorship itself becomes a topic: Tim / AI / reader / Potato.

### Dated public layer

The strongest current short corridor is **2–6 October 2024**:

- 2 Oct — Chosen Vegetable;
- 3 Oct — potatoes die for your sins / one like one prayer;
- 4 Oct — Tim says he became Potato of Life because God asked him to and says the Great Book followed from realizing potatoes are the philosopher’s stone;
- 6 Oct — `Hello I am the Potato of Life. My name is Tim Dooley`.

This gives the 2024 Story a dated documentary spine that the social cast still lacks.

### Biggest missing scene-level sources

- documentary BigTech interactions;
- Monkey / OPS / Tree entry, trust, conflict, removal/return;
- Sammy’s 2024 crossing with Marty;
- original Cyraxx and Robotosan interactions;
- first thalamus discovery session;
- real Great Book writing days and prompts;
- 2024 song / stream chronology.

## Authoring rule

When a source says `around this time`, `in 2023`, or `after about a year`, preserve that imprecision. A chronological reader needs order more than fake precision.
