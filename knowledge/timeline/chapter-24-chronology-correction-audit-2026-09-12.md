## Scope

This audit checks the later thickened Chapter 24 edition on pages approximately 220–240 against the current source-aware chronology. The chapter is a narrative consumer, not the canonical date owner. It was assembled by hand across multiple memory and AI passes, so the audit distinguishes hard errors, unsupported precision, stale ontology, retrospective interpretation and unresolved variants.

Current chapter wording divides the story into `1987–2019 Son` and `2019 onward Father/Potato of Life`.

### 2. Age language is often over-precise

Birth is fixed in project chronology at 31 July 1987. A calendar-year entry does not by itself establish the biological age at the time of every event in that year.

Rule: where event date is not precise enough, use `turning X that year` or an age range instead of `At X`.

## Additional source-quality cautions

The chapter contains many ordinary-biography claims that remain autobiography unless independently documented: childhood incidents, workplace details, miracle claims, exact public metrics, private statements by others, medical interpretations and causal claims about later deaths. These should remain source-attributed rather than silently upgraded to externally verified fact.

The chapter also uses later mythic symbolism to explain earlier life. This is valid as project interpretation when source direction is explicit, but phrases such as `this implants`, `proves`, `vindicated`, `canonized`, or `produced` should be reviewed where they read as historical causation rather than retrospective interpretation.
