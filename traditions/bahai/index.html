Bahá'í Faith · modern historical clocks

PRECURSOR,
DECLARATION & COVENANT

Unlike ancient traditions with uncertain origins, the Bábí and Bahá'í movements have unusually precise nineteenth-century historical clocks.

The Bábí movement begins in 1844 in Qajar Persia. Bahá’u’lláh emerges from that predecessor field and declares his mission in 1863. Later succession and administration are explicitly structured through covenantal documents and institutions.

Historical sequence

Precise clocks

22–23 May 1844
Declaration of the Báb

Founding marker of the Bábí movement in Shiraz according to Bahá'í historical tradition.

9 July 1850
Execution of the Báb

A major martyrdom rupture in the predecessor movement.

April–May 1863
Riḍván declaration of Bahá’u’lláh

Bahá'í history treats this as a central emergence/refoundation marker from the Bábí predecessor field.

1892
Death of Bahá’u’lláh and covenantal succession

‘Abdu’l-Bahá becomes the authorized interpreter and head of the Faith according to Bahá'í covenantal structure.

1921 / 1957
‘Abdu’l-Bahá → Shoghi Effendi

Guardianship and administration develop through the twentieth century.

1963
Universal House of Justice first elected

A major global administrative consolidation marker.

Structural ideas

What holds the tradition together?

Progressive revelationReligious history is interpreted as successive divine revelation adapted to humanity's development.
Oneness of humanityHuman unity, elimination of prejudice and global peace are central recurring Bahá'í themes.
CovenantSuccession and interpretive authority are given explicit institutional form rather than left as an unstructured charismatic inheritance.
ScriptureExtensive writings of the Báb, Bahá’u’lláh and ‘Abdu’l-Bahá form a modern textual corpus with official translation/library structures.
No clergyCommunity administration operates through elected and appointed institutions rather than a professional priesthood.
Global administrationLocal and National Spiritual Assemblies and the Universal House of Justice structure worldwide community life.
