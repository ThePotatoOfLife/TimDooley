VESICA
MANDORLA

The useful question is not whether every old tradition secretly taught the Potato of Life. It is which relations genuinely recur: source and manifestation, two centers and a shared field, Mountain and transformed light, Sun and Moon, inner chamber and threshold, river and Tree, ascent and descent. This page keeps the historical evidence and the modern Potatoverse synthesis visibly separate.

The exact vertical geometry

H_top — Heaven crown │ C_F — FATHER / upper center top point of Door │ MANDORLA │ P_L ───────── O / PLANE ───────── P_R │ MANDORLA │ C_S — SON / lower center bottom point of Door │ E_bottom — depth C_F = (0,+r/2) C_S = (0,-r/2) O = (0,0) Plane: y = 0 Axis: x = 0

In a standard Vesica Piscis rotated vertically, the two equal-circle centers are separated by one radius. This creates a precise result important to the project: the Father center is exactly the upper endpoint of the shared lens, and the Son center is exactly the lower endpoint. Each center also lies on the circumference of the other circle. The midpoint O is a third, relational center and should not be confused with either Father or Son.

What the Christian mandorla really adds

Christian art gives the lens shape its strongest documented sacred neighbor. Mandorlas surround Christ especially in scenes of transformed or boundary-crossing presence: Christ in Majesty, the Transfiguration, Ascension, Last Judgment and Anastasis/Descent into Hell. The historically safer inference is therefore not “mandorla literally meant an interdimensional portal,” but that it functions as a field of sacred manifestation or transformed visibility.

The Transfiguration is particularly important because later Byzantine icons place Christ in a mandorla on Mount Tabor while apostles remain below. That gives a real historical composition of Mountain → summit → Mandorla → radiant transformed figure → rays/witnesses below. Mountain becomes a perception/integration gradient; Mandorla marks changed mode of appearance.

Door-image over Door-object

Romanesque Christ-in-Majesty images often occupy tympana above actual church portals. Sacred threshold-image and physical doorway are therefore superimposed in the worshipper's experience. That does not prove Potatoverse Door theology was intended by medieval builders, but it makes Door ↔ Mandorla a stronger historical comparison than a mere modern visual pun.

Sun, Moon and the lower world

Physical astronomy supplies a simple distinction: the Sun radiates visible light; moonlight is reflected sunlight; lunar phase depends on relational geometry. Renaissance occult writer Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa provides a historically explicit esoteric analogue: Sun as source/radiance and Moon, nearest the lower world, as receiver/mediator. Medieval astrological magic such as the Picatrix likewise treats lunar condition as important to manifestation, while modern Hermetic Qabalah places lunar Yesod close to Malkuth/Earth.

The project therefore gains a strong comparative pattern:

SOURCE / SUN ↓ MOON / REFLECTOR / MODULATOR ↓ EARTH / MANIFEST WORLD

This does not validate astrology scientifically. It does support the symbolic decision to place Moon as a lower circumferential mediator rather than at the Father's source-center.

Scripture also goes beyond Sun and Moon

Revelation 12 provides a visionary Sun/Moon vertical image, but Revelation 21–22 goes further: the renewed city does not require ordinary Sun or Moon because divine glory/light supplies illumination. That gives Spudlight a useful theological correction: Sun is an image of source-light, not the final identity of source-light.

Garden → River → Tree → Fruit

Genesis 2 describes a river flowing from Eden and dividing outward. Ezekiel 47 and Revelation 22 develop related sacred-center → river → trees → fruit/healing patterns. For the Potatoverse, this makes the upper Garden a generative interior rather than a static paradise:

FATHER / HOUSE / THRONE ↓ SPIRIT / FLOW ↓ RIVER ↓ TREE ↓ FRUIT ↓ HEALING / WORLD REPAIR

This also yields an ethical center rule: the legitimacy of Source is visible in what flows from it.

Mountain has more than one moral meaning

Isaiah and Micah connect the high Mountain of the Lord's House with teaching, judgment, peace and the transformation of weapons into cultivation tools. The Transfiguration makes Mountain a revelation field. But Matthew's temptation narrative also uses a very high mountain to stage an offer of world domination. So the archive should never use a crude equation higher = better. The summit is judged by its fruit.

New Jerusalem: ascent and descent at once

Revelation 21 takes the seer upward to a high mountain, but the city itself descends from God. That creates a bidirectional verticality:

observer / vision ↑ city / order ↓ WORLD

The mature Axis is therefore stronger as ascent for perspective + descent for manifestation/service than as permanent escape from Earth.

Swamp, mire and stable footing

Biblical poetry and narrative repeatedly use mire, muddy pits and deep water for sinking, danger and loss of foothold. Rock/firm ground gives the opposite image. The project's Swamp becomes clearer when understood as loss of stable reference and unresolved entanglement, not simply as “evil below.” Mountain supplies perspective; Rock supplies footing; Axis supplies direction; Door supplies transition.

Thalamus: the “inner chamber” is a real linguistic neighbor

The Greek word behind thalamus means an inner chamber and often a bridal chamber. Histories of neuroanatomy discuss Galen's house/chamber language around brain anatomy. This gives the Potatoverse Thalamus=House motif a genuine linguistic/history-of-anatomy neighbor. It does not prove the thalamus is literally God's House.

The pineal has a different history: Descartes famously proposed it as a central interface/seat of soul in his obsolete physiology, and later occult traditions connected it to third-eye symbolism. The project can therefore compare Thalamus/House/inner chamber → Pineal/Door/Eye → manifestation/presence while keeping modern neuroscience separate.

Serpent and Spirit: compare functions, not substances

Genesis serpent, bronze serpent, John 3's lifted-Son typology and South Asian Kundalini serpent traditions have different histories and functions. A useful cross-tradition abstraction is coiling = stored recurrence/potential; lifting/uncoiling = directed transformation; gaze = changed relation; shedding = changed form. The archive should not claim Holy Spirit, Kundalini, CSF and literal snakes are one physical substance.

The strongest current Potatoverse synthesis

SOURCE-LIGHT / SPUDLIGHT │ HEAVEN / GARDEN / SUN │ FATHER / HOUSE │ SPIRIT / FLOW │ MANDORLA / DOOR │ SON / THOMAS │ PLANE / WORLD │ EARTH / MATTER / OUTER ⟲ MOON │ ROOT / MEMORY │ SWAMP / MIRE

The value of this diagram is not that ancient traditions secretly drew the same Potato. It is that it gives a precise modern geometry to an old family of questions: How does an inner source become present in an outer world? How can the two remain distinct yet connected? What carries flow? What changes at the threshold? What comes back down after ascent?

Research boundaries

  • Do not claim every esoteric school knew one universal Vesica secret.
  • Do not claim every cathedral is built from a hidden Vesica master grid.
  • Do not present Hermetic Qabalah as identical to historical Jewish Kabbalah.
  • Do not present pineal/third-eye or thalamus/House as neuroscience facts.
  • Do not treat Michelangelo hidden-brain hypotheses as settled consensus.
  • Do not equate biblical serpents with Kundalini historically.
  • Do not use Moon symbolism to infer facts about people named Moon.
