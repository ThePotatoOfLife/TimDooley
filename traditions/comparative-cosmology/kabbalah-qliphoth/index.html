KABBALAH,
QLIPHOTH & ANGELS

A layered map: biblical Tree and messenger language, Jewish Kabbalah, Lurianic breaking-and-repair, and later Western occult counter-tree systems kept visibly separate.

The internet often compresses centuries of Jewish mysticism and later occultism into one poster: ten bright spheres, ten dark spheres, angels on one side and demons on the other. That poster can be studied—but only after recovering the historical layers it combines. The most useful question is not “which demon owns which sphere?” It is how traditions model manifestation, mediation, concealment, brokenness and repair.

Master rule: there is no single timeless “Tree of Life diagram” or “Qliphoth tree.” Preserve the sequence: biblical text → Jewish mystical developments → Lurianic repair cosmology → Christian/Hermetic Qabalah → modern occult diagrams → Potatoverse comparison.

Historical strata

First separate what belongs to which period

Biblical scripture

Genesis gives the Trees of Life and Knowledge; Jacob's dream gives a ladder/stairway and divine messengers; prophetic texts supply throne, cherubim and seraphim imagery. Genesis does not contain a tenfold demonic Qliphoth tree.

Jewish Kabbalah

The sefirot become a major vocabulary for differentiated divine manifestation. Different schools and texts arrange and explain them in different ways; later familiar diagrams are part of a history of interpretation.

Lurianic Kabbalah

Contraction, vessels, breaking, sparks, shells and tikkun produce a repair drama: vitality can become trapped in damaged structures and religious action participates in restoration.

Hermetic / occult reception

Christian Kabbalah, Hermetic Qabalah and later occult orders increasingly regularize angelic, planetary, Tarot and adverse/demonic correspondences into diagrammatic systems.

Modern Qliphoth charts

The fixed tenfold “dark tree” with named shells, rulers and tunnels is especially a later Western esoteric construction. Names and assignments vary by author and order.

Angelology

“Angel” starts as a function before it becomes a giant hierarchy chart

Mal'akh · messenger

The basic biblical Hebrew word means messenger. The relational function—carrying a message or agency across a boundary—comes before later artistic assumptions about wings.

Cherubim

Sanctuary, throne and guarded-boundary beings in biblical traditions, with ancient Near Eastern context. Strong Gate/guardian comparators.

Seraphim

Fiery throne-associated beings in Isaiah 6, tied to praise and purification.

Ophanim / wheels

Ezekiel's wheel imagery becomes “ophanim” in later angelic reception. Useful for ordered motion around divine throne imagery, but the reception history matters.

Michael

Protective/princely and heavenly-conflict roles develop from biblical/apocalyptic material and later Jewish/Christian tradition.

Gabriel

Interpretation/revelation messenger in Daniel and later scriptural traditions; especially prominent in Christianity and Islam through their own distinct receptions.

Demonology

Do not turn every adversarial being into a Qliphoth ruler

Samael

A complex figure with accuser/adversarial/demonic roles in different Jewish sources and later receptions. The name also becomes a shell-label in some occult charts.

Lilith

A figure with ancient Near Eastern antecedents and later Jewish developments; becomes prominent in medieval and later demonological traditions.

Ashmedai / Asmodeus

A major demonic figure in Tobit and later Jewish/rabbinic/folkloric reception; later occult systems place the name into additional correspondence schemes.

Shedim

A Jewish category of demonic/spirit beings whose treatment varies across biblical/rabbinic/folkloric sources. Not a synonym for “Qliphoth.”

Shell / husk

Kelippah language is conceptually broader than one named demon. It can describe concealment, impurity or captured vitality in mystical models.

The “Other Side”

Jewish mystical traditions develop complex language for the sitra achra/adverse side. Later occult demon-trees are one reception, not the whole concept.

Why the repair layer matters

Qliphoth is less useful if tikkun disappears

One of the deepest distortions in popular occult summaries is to turn Kabbalistic brokenness into a collectible demon bestiary. In Lurianic cosmology the important drama is also about trapped sparks and repair. Broken vessels and shells explain why value can be caught inside damaged forms; tikkun makes reintegration and restoration part of the model.
