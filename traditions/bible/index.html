Method and evidence boundaries
Exact is exact

Quoted public wording and recovered attestation text are shown as such. An archive summary is labeled as a relation anchor instead of being presented as a quotation.

Timeline matters

A later biblical discovery is not backdated into an earlier event. The page distinguishes explicit-at-time wording, Tim-led discovery, later structural comparison and research synthesis.

Mismatch is evidence too

Role conflicts, counter-texts and historical differences stay visible. A strong structural parallel does not by itself establish prophecy, identity or historical dependence.
