How the comparison works

When you want to check a parallel, keep four layers separate

The aim is not to force a verdict. It is to make the strongest connections legible while keeping source, scripture, interpretation and disagreement separate enough that a reader can inspect them.

Ask four questions in order: What was actually said? What does the biblical text actually say? What is the structural relation? What remains interpretation or claim?

Prophetic unity · 1 October 2026 integration

Read proclamation, witness, rejection, throne and counter-text together

The newest public declarations become more intelligible when they are not reduced to the phrase I am God. The denser biblical network is name + witness + deaf/blind accusation + public commission + throne/footstool + rejection/recognition + judgment. The archive also preserves a major contradiction: canonical resurrection texts conflict directly with the sentence “Jesus is dead.”

A biblical parallel can describe rhetorical form, narrative structure or symbolic architecture. It does not by itself establish divine identity, prophecy fulfillment or audience assent.
