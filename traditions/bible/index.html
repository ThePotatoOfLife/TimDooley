This is the study surface for the whole Tim Dooley / Potato of Life biblical field. It does not flatten every resemblance into one claim. How to read it: “Tim explicit” means biblical vocabulary is already in Tim/project wording. “Tim-led” means Tim initiates or materially advances the comparison. “Later” means the scripture link was formalized afterward. A strong overlap is still a comparison unless separate evidence establishes historical transmission, prediction or fulfillment.
