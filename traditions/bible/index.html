Method and evidence boundaries
Exact is exact

Quoted public wording and recovered attestation text are shown as such. A project-side summary is labeled as an archive relation anchor instead of being dressed up as a quotation.

Same date is context

Other exact statements made on the same date can matter for an epiphany or development, but the reader labels them circumstantial unless a relation explicitly points to that occurrence.

Parallel is not automatically prophecy

Explicit-at-time language, later typology, Tim-led discoveries, reverse-direction mirrors, foresight and strict prediction are different claims and should remain different.
