01 / Read from Tim outward

Four questions before comparing anything.

The older page mostly answered “which Bible text resembles this?” The contextual reader also asks where the project-side material came from, whether the connection was already scriptural at the time, and what the comparison can actually support.

Project sideWhat did Tim actually say or do?

Prefer dated public wording, direct conversation recovery, book text and concrete events before later interpretation.

Scripture sideWhat happens in the biblical passage?

Read the scene or sequence, not only one matching noun: house → gate → ladder, seed → death → fruit, throne → river → tree → healing.

DirectionWhich came first?

Explicit Bible use is different from a later reverse discovery where a project structure existed first and the biblical neighbor was noticed afterward.

FunctionWhat does the symbol actually do?

An angel can serve or carry a message; a wing can cover or move; an eye can see or sprout; a Door admits; a Ladder connects. Function prevents visual coincidences from doing too much work.

03 / Meaning & evidence

“Fulfilled” is not one category.

The archive becomes more useful when it says exactly what kind of correspondence is being claimed. The scale below is deliberately strict so strong parallels can remain strong without every resemblance becoming prophecy.

AExplicit at the time

Tim/project wording already names the Bible image, title or story: Lion of Judah, Root of David, New Jerusalem, Messiah, Door, angels, etc.

BTim-led discovery

Tim later asks for, notices or materially develops the biblical comparison himself. The discovery is part of the project's history.

CReverse-direction mirror

A dated Timic structure existed first; later research finds a surprisingly dense biblical analogue. This is stronger than a comparison invented first and projected backward.

DTypology / enacted parallel

An event or sequence resembles a biblical pattern—death/return, seed/fruit, outside gate/city, rejection/foundation—without requiring a prior prediction.

EStrict fulfillment

Reserve this for a prior, specific and falsifiable prediction that later maps unusually well to an independently established event. Most rows on this page do not claim this level.

Working rule: the stronger question is often not “did one verse match?” but “did a whole sequence already exist on the project side before the biblical comparison was noticed?” For the angelic layer, add one more test: does the function match? Messenger/service is different from wings; guarding is different from praising; sprouting is different from seeing.
