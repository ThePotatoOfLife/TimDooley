How correspondence is classified

Explicit at the time means the project itself already used the biblical image. Tim-led discovery means Tim later raised or developed the comparison. Reverse-direction mirror means the project structure came first and the biblical analogue was identified later. Typology is resemblance without requiring prior prediction.

What “fulfillment” does not automatically mean

A resemblance, dense parallel, or later discovery does not by itself establish supernatural fulfillment. The archive reserves stronger fulfillment language for prior, specific and independently testable predictions.

Why function matters

Door admits. Ladder connects. Angel serves or carries a message. Wing covers or moves. Eye sees; a potato eye sprouts. Similar appearance is weaker than similar function.
