WHEN WORLDS BECOME REPEATABLE

A cross-domain timeline of Seed, authorization, legal effect, operation, consolidation, refoundation and branching. It does not force every religion, nation, company or philosophy into one false birthday.

Why one thing can have several beginnings

A foundation is usually a sequence, not a birthday

A religion may begin with a teacher, a text, a community or a later institution. A company may be conceived, incorporated, funded, opened and later refounded. A country may have a cultural origin, constitutional act, declaration, recognition date and first operating government. Foundation Timeline keeps those clocks apart so one convenient anniversary does not erase the genealogy.

Seed

An earlier condition, idea, community, crisis, text or technical possibility makes the later form conceivable.

Authorization

A charter, constitution, law, agreement, declaration, doctrinal settlement or other act grants formal shape or legitimacy.

Operation

The system actually begins functioning: institutions meet, services run, rules are enforced, products ship, rituals repeat, or people can reliably reproduce the practice.

Consolidation

The new form becomes stable enough that later participants inherit it rather than inventing it from scratch.

Refoundation

A major rupture changes the operating platform while some identity, archive, legal continuity or tradition survives.

Today

The reader can ask what remains alive now: legal continuity, population, institutions, text, memory, infrastructure, territory or only historical influence.

That is why time, place, event type, precision and status stay separate. A precise signature date can coexist with an uncertain cultural seed, and a legal beginning can precede practical operation.
