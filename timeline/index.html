Why the timeline matters

The timeline is not just where dates are stored. It is where the project protects development from hindsight. A later interpretation may illuminate an earlier event, but it must not silently travel backward in time and pretend it was already there.

The central historical question is often not “did this happen?” but what did it mean then, what source survives from then, and what meaning was added later?
