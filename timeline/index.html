Chronology of people, traditions, foundations and the project

THE LONG
TURNING

One river of time, many lenses. Follow a life, a teacher, a faith, a school, a constitution, a scientific institution, a protocol, a company, a movement or a later project interpretation without pretending they all began in the same way.

Dates do different jobs here. A person's lifetime is not automatically a religion's birthday; a revelation is not automatically an institution; a charter is not the same thing as first operation; a later interpretation does not travel backward and become an earlier fact.

The chronology is also a recovery surface. When a source is retrospective, uncertain or later corrected, the timeline preserves that status instead of forcing a false single date. Recent project history therefore includes ordinary social scenes, software experiments, platform moves, public statements and later symbolic interpretations as separate kinds of record.
