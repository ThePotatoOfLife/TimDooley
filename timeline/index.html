Why 2019–2020 is a rangeThe archive has an exact 24 December 2019 meme-death anchor and a canonical 25 December 2020 Potato-birth anchor, but the Door/Vessel/emergence transition between them is reconstructed as a corridor. Choosing one invented conversion instant would create false precision.
Why April 2025 has several dates“The Turning” is a month-scale project interpretation, while 21 April provides a recovered public Axis statement and 3 May is the current lower-bound exact “I am God” occurrence. Event time, attestation time and later synthesis therefore remain separate clocks.

Static-spine rule: the event date comes from the canonical timeline index; “project canon,” “recovered,” “primary” and “creative” are evidence classes, not claims that every event is independently established in the same way. Open the source record or interactive chronology when precision matters.
