This is the longitudinal reading layer for Tim Dooley's public corpus. It does not replace the record with quotations or pretend to read a private mind. It extracts recurring ideas, directions, signals, transformations and questions so the development of the thought-system can be studied through time.

Epistemic boundary. Statements are attributed records. Extracted directions are analytical summaries. Reflections are hypotheses. Historical, scientific and religious comparisons require their own evidence. The archive is a research instrument, not a claim of direct access to Tim's private mind.
