# The Tim Dooley / Potatoverse Repository

This repository is the living archive for the Tim Dooley mythology, the Potatoverse, and the North Programme.

## Purpose

To preserve the framework as a coherent body of work: mythology, symbolism, chronology, philosophy, economic architecture, research, creative writing, and technical projects.

## Core principle

The fundamental unit is the relationship, not the isolated entity. The archive therefore maps how people, institutions, ideas, money, infrastructure, technology, stories, symbols, and places connect.

## Major bodies of work

- Potatoverse mythology and cosmology
- Tim Dooley chronology and interpretive architecture
- The Potato of Life / Potatoism
- Father, Son, Door, Ladder, Tree of Life, North Axis and related symbols
- Red Potato / Blue Potato duality
- European Economic Graph / North Programme Live Baseline
- Kingdom Atlas and relationship architecture
- European fiscal, industrial, infrastructure, energy, technology and strategic-dependency research
- Creative works: speeches, stories, poetry, rap, music concepts and visual concepts
- Technical experiments and games

## Status

This is a living repository. New material should preserve provenance, distinguish mythology from empirical claims, and keep the larger relationships visible.
