RESEARCH LAYER · 2026

Extremism, hate groups & high-control movements

A structured public-source atlas of organizations and movements, designed to distinguish ideology, hate targets, membership evidence, affiliations, violence history, current status and uncertainty rather than flattening everything into the word “cult.”

Research boundary. This database does not publish private member identities, home addresses, weapons inventories or tactical instructions. “Active,” “armed,” “hate group” and “high-control” are separate fields.

METHOD

How to read the records

Status ≠ ideology

A group can be active without being violent, historical without being harmless, or fragmented without being gone. Status is time-dependent.

Membership ≠ followers

Followers, supporters, affiliates, chapter participants and formal members are different populations. The atlas keeps them separate where sources allow.

Armed ≠ hateful

Weapons or paramilitary activity are tracked independently from hate classification. A hate group is not automatically an armed group.

“Cult” is contested

High-control is treated as a research dimension involving authority, isolation, coercion, dependency and exit constraints—not a blanket diagnosis of religion.
