Read a legal claim as a chain, not a verdict

Legal material becomes clearer when the archive keeps the object, rule, institution, procedure and outcome separate. That prevents allegations, policy wishes and project interpretation from silently becoming legal findings.

ObjectPerson, act, contract, property, institution, decision or legal instrument actually at issue.
RuleStatute, regulation, constitution, treaty, case law or other authority that could apply.
InstitutionCourt, ministry, regulator, police, ombudsman or other body with jurisdiction or review power.
ProcedureNotice, hearing, evidence, appeal, review, limitation period and burden of proof.
StatusAlleged, investigated, charged, adjudicated, appealed, settled, dismissed or otherwise unresolved.

Return test: can a reader tell what happened, what rule is claimed to apply, who had authority to decide, and what remains disputed?
