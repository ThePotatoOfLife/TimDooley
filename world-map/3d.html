Explore the world

Project fields and empirical networks are separate controls. This lets you see where Tim/Potatoverse geography overlaps or diverges from observable alliances, unions and regional organisations instead of treating either one as the other.

North, West, East and South are project interpretive relationship fields, not borders, sovereignty, citizenship or legal alignment. Center is a convergence arena rather than a fifth territorial bloc. Empirical networks are independently sourced institutional/geographic layers. A country may occupy several project fields and several real networks at once.

Trace & Path

Trace explores the curated relationship neighborhood around one country. Path finds a shortest represented route. Neither hop count nor path length is a causal, moral or geopolitical-distance claim.
