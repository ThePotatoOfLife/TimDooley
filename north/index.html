01 / Architecture

What Tim has actually been building.

The pieces become coherent when read as a vertical stack: spiritual orientation above, world-model and economic mechanism in the middle, legitimate implementation below.

01

North of North — Source / orientation

In Potatoist canon, North of North is not a territory. It is the point beyond ordinary direction where Axis, Throne, Father, Sage-to-God transformation and responsibility converge. Tim's role here is symbolic and spiritual: the integrating viewpoint above the map.

02

Tikkun — the repair horizon

The higher the claimed viewpoint, the greater the obligation. Tikkun becomes the project's ethical question: if avoidable harm, fragility, waste or broken relationships can be understood, what would responsible repair require?

03

World relational atlas

People, nations, institutions, companies, religions, infrastructure, technology, communities and cultural systems are treated as nodes in a living graph. The fundamental unit is the relationship: who depends on whom, what flows, what binds, what transforms and what fails.

04

Banks, debt and obligations

The empirical middle layer maps bonds, loans, deposits, equity, guarantees, ownership, contracts, taxes, pensions, procurement, grants, trade, supply chains, energy, infrastructure and legal duties. This is where metaphor must give way to balance sheets, contracts, jurisdictions and sourced amounts.

05

North Programme

The map becomes policy: fiscal sustainability, productive investment, energy security, procurement, industrial capacity, research, labour, critical infrastructure, strategic resilience and voluntary European cooperation.

06

Legitimate implementation

The vision can orient, diagnose and propose. Real laws, budgets, treaties and deployments still belong to constitutional governments, public institutions, communities and authorized decision-makers. Spiritual sovereignty is not a shortcut around legal sovereignty.

03 / The world graph

Entanglement means relationships with mechanisms.

“Everything is connected” is too vague. The North project becomes useful when every connection is named, directional, sourced and measurable where possible.

Nodes

Countries · governments · EU institutions · central and commercial banks · companies · funds · households · NGOs · universities · infrastructure operators · defence institutions · religious bodies · communities.

Edges

Bonds · loans · deposits · equity · ownership/control · guarantees · pensions · taxes · contracts · procurement · subsidies · grants · trade · supply chains · energy · cables · research · security commitments · legal duties.

Required metadata

Direction · amount/value · date or period · geography/jurisdiction · source · evidence class · confidence · method · uncertainty.

Why it matters

A country's real capability often exists only through relationships: energy interconnectors, alliance commitments, shared infrastructure, trade, capital, knowledge, labour and reciprocal industrial capacity.

The working North geography

Canada→Greenland / Denmark / Faroes→Nordics + Iceland→Ireland / UK + continental Europe→Ukraine→Turkey

This is a versioned conceptual research network, not a fixed bloc or annexation map. Russia and Mesopotamian connections remain conditional/future questions in the archive; the United States, Israel, Australia and BRICS are tracked as external relationships.

05 / Three spiritual lenses

Norse, Christian and Jewish readings reveal different parts of the machine.

These traditions are not collapsed into one religion. They are used as distinct comparative lenses on Axis, kingship, responsibility, covenant, transformation and repair.

ᛟ

Norse — World Tree & ordeal

North, Yggdrasil, Odin's ordeal, knowledge, ravens/crows and Ragnarök-renewal supply an Axis grammar: a living structure joins worlds, knowledge is gained through ordeal, and destruction can become transformation rather than mere ending.

Closest project relation: North Axis as both vertical world-tree and branching relational network.

✝

Christian — Father, Son, Door & Ladder

Jacob's Ladder, John 1:51, the Door, narrow gate, Father's House, Tree of Life, New Jerusalem and enthronement make the project legible as Source → threshold → manifestation → return/renewal.

חי

Jewish — Tikkun, covenant & restitution

Tikkun/repair, covenant, justice, return, restitution and Jubilee/debt-release traditions provide a moral vocabulary for repairing broken relationships and institutions without pretending that Judaism contains Tim's 42T karmic account.

Closest project relation: observe → understand mechanism → repair → measure → revise.

07 / The job

What “spiritual King of Kings” means inside the mature project.

The coherent version is less “rule everything directly” and more “occupy the integrating viewpoint, understand the system, articulate repair, and hold the vision to a higher burden of responsibility.”

Orient

Ask where the whole system is going, not merely who is winning a local contest.

Witness

Preserve what happened, who was affected, what was said and what evidence supports each claim.

Integrate

Join economics, infrastructure, science, culture, religion and geopolitics without erasing their differences.

Judge by fruit

Evaluate structures by consequences: nourishment, resilience, dignity, capability, truth and future growth.

Design repair

Translate diagnosis into interventions, non-interventions and measurable outcomes.

Leave implementation legitimate

Leaders and institutions may apply ideas where they have authority; the spiritual layer does not itself create governmental power.
