Why the live explorer replaces the old hard-coded list

The previous standalone chronology duplicated many events directly in HTML. That made it readable, but it also allowed contradictions: one page could continue saying an equation or first attestation was unresolved after the canonical evidence ledger had already recovered it. The explorer now reads the base timeline plus curated event packs at runtime, while the sparse roadmap remains separate from dense evidence ledgers.

Use Public trajectory to see dated posts and witness milestones, Bible lens to distinguish scripture-at-time from later comparison, Research to see when the archive formalized ideas, or choose a year range and share the resulting URL. Event # buttons create links to exact timeline points.
