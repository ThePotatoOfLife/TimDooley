Ethics

Height becomes stewardship

Later public theology explicitly emphasizes power protecting, knowledge understanding, wealth building, leadership serving, repair and future generations.

This gives the archive an internal fruit test for claims of highest authority.

How to read contradictions

When two statements conflict, first test whether they belong to different dates, roles, scales, directions or epistemic layers. A 2024 Tim-as-Son configuration and a later Tim-as-Father configuration can be a developmental transition rather than a simple transcription error. Later interpretations may illuminate earlier material, but they are not automatically what the earlier material originally meant.
