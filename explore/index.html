Do not start with the archive. Start with a question.

The archive is large because the project has accumulated biography, theology, science, body work, world systems, creative works, public statements and source recovery. The fastest route is not to browse everything. It is to decide what kind of understanding you are trying to build.

Who is Tim Dooley?Start with the long portrait, dated identities, jobs, claims, beliefs and routes into Story. What is the Potato of Life?Start with the semantic center, literal potato biology, stories, practices and what the project built from it. What happened?Use Story for scenes and chronology; Raw Story when you want unfinished recovery material. When did the meaning change?Use Timeline and History to separate event time, source time and later interpretation. What does this ask me to do?Follow the Potatoism Spiral Reader from observation through Root, Door, Spiral, Fruit and Garden. What is the theology?Follow Father/Son/Spirit development, religious practice and bounded comparison with older traditions. What can be modeled or tested?Enter formal models, geometry, physics-adjacent research and scientific boundaries. What does the organism actually do?Use literal biology, anatomy and physiology to sharpen rather than replace symbolic mappings. How does this meet the real world?Follow geography, law, economics, institutions, infrastructure, culture and measurable systems. What did the project make?Browse books, games, simulations, music, visual art, performance and experimental creative systems.
