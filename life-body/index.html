The literal potato is not decoration

Tuber, stolon, root, shoot

A potato tuber is a modified underground stem formed on a stolon. It is not literally a root. The project keeps these structures distinct before using Root symbolically.

Eyes are buds

Potato eyes contain meristematic bud tissue. They are literal potential growth points, making Eye a rare symbol with a genuine biological growth analogue.

Dormancy is organized potential

Dormancy can preserve developmental capacity without visible expression. Hidden does not mean absent.

Stored capacity can become source

The tuber stores starch and can later remobilize reserves for new growth. Receiver/storage can change phase and become giver/source.

Many eyes, one body

Several viable growth sites can belong to one tuber body. Distributed potential does not require fragmented identity.

Continuity carries burdens

Clonal propagation can preserve useful genotype and also pathogens. Memory and continuity therefore require repair, not automatic reverence.

Organism metabolism

Conditions → threshold → growth → consequence

conditions→dormancy / readiness→activation→transport→growth→repair→reproduction / return

This is the biological side of the project's Seed → Root → Door → Spiral → Tree → Fruit grammar. The project analogy becomes stronger when the biological stages are kept accurate rather than merged into one metaphor.

Where biology corrects the metaphor

The body is useful because it refuses vague symbolism

A biological system has parts that can be named, damaged, measured and distinguished. That makes it a powerful corrective to a symbolic system: if the metaphor becomes too loose, the organism forces the project to ask what the claimed relation actually is.

Potato “Root”

Correction: tuber ≠ root

The project uses Root symbolically for hidden source and continuity, but a potato tuber is a modified stem. Keeping stolon, tuber, root and shoot distinct makes the metaphor better because it prevents one underground thing from standing for every underground function.

Potato Eye

Analogy: perception is not the biological function

A potato eye is a bud site. Its biological meaning is future growth, not seeing. That makes Eye useful as a project symbol only when the analogy is explicit: a point where latent possibility can emerge, not evidence that potatoes possess visual perception.

Thalamus / House

Correction: there is no single central relay room

The thalamus contains many nuclei with different cortical and subcortical relationships. Treating it as “House” can be a useful architectural comparison only if the diversity of nuclei, loops and functions remains visible.

CSF / Spirit

Boundary: physical carrier ≠ theological identity

Cerebrospinal fluid circulates, cushions and participates in waste-clearance and chemical environments. Those properties may illuminate a flow metaphor. They do not make CSF identical to Spirit or demonstrate a supernatural mechanism.

Development / Return

Biology remembers through structure, not narrative

An adult nervous system is the result of developmental sequence, lineage, migration, pruning and specialization. Later states carry consequences of earlier development without literally “remembering” them as autobiographical stories. That helps sharpen the project's distinction between history, memory and structural inheritance.
