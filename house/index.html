Reader map · one world, different questions

THE POTATO HOUSE

What is the Potato House? It is the project’s way of keeping different questions about the same thing from collapsing into one another. Biography asks what happened. Timeline asks when. Theology asks what a symbol means inside Potatoism. Sources ask what can be verified. Culture asks how an idea travels. Science asks what can be tested. Works asks what was made.

The House is successful when you understand the subject better—not when you memorize the House.

Use it before you learn it

One thing can have many true descriptions without becoming many things

The House is a discipline of question separation. It prevents evidence, theology, memory, satire, biography, scientific analogy and later interpretation from borrowing authority from one another merely because they concern the same subject.

A remembered childhood eventBiography can preserve the recollection. History can ask when it was first recorded. Psychology can discuss memory limits. Later theology can explain what Tim now thinks it means. None of those layers automatically proves the others.
“I am God”Public Witness can establish that Tim said it. Theology can explain what God/Father means in Potatoism. Philosophy can test what obligations such a title creates. External empirical analysis can remain agnostic about supernatural truth.
A potato sproutingBiology owns the organism. Potatoism may draw a metaphor from burial, storage and growth. The metaphor is constrained by biology but cannot borrow biology's evidentiary status for theology.
An internet conflictArchive preserves posts. Culture studies repetition and group dynamics. Law asks jurisdiction and elements. CIA can map relationships and hypotheses. No Room gets to promote its own question into the whole verdict.

Reader shortcut: when a page becomes confusing, ask: “What question is this paragraph answering, and what kind of evidence would be allowed to answer it?” That is the House in miniature.

Two levels are enough

Read the subject first. Open the machinery when you need to audit it.

Most readers should meet a proposition, scene, case, argument or artifact before they meet ownership rules, canonical registries or topology. Researchers still need those deeper layers, so the House keeps them reachable rather than deleting them.

Reader layerDefinition → scene → mechanism → comparison → consequence → useful next question.
Audit layerSources → provenance → dates → canonical owner → relationship graph → unresolved records.

This is deliberate progressive disclosure: the advanced model remains available, but it should not become an entrance exam for understanding the subject.

The larger questions

What if a House could hold theology, space, mind and history without pretending they are the same thing?

The project keeps returning to a family of old human images: source, house, gate, ladder, tree, mountain, river, eye, seed, root and garden. They matter because they let abstract questions become spatial enough to think with. What is God if the divine is not merely a larger object somewhere above us, but source, relation, manifestation and continuity? What is a self if memory, body, culture and future intention do not sit in one simple place? What is a world if every visible surface depends on hidden roots—energy, history, language, infrastructure, ancestry and habit?

Space is doing philosophical work here. Above and below do not have to mean good and evil. Center does not have to mean superiority. A Door is interesting because two domains can meet without collapsing into one. A Ladder is interesting because transformation has direction. A Tree is interesting because the visible crown is impossible without what disappeared into darkness first. Religion and esoteric thought return to these forms because they compress difficult relations into images the mind can carry; comparison becomes useful when it deepens the question without pretending every tradition came from one secret source.

The body sharpens the lesson. Brains orient organisms in space, integrate many signals, regulate thresholds and build usable models from incomplete information. That does not make anatomy a hidden theology. It gives theology and symbolism something disciplined to compare themselves against. The literal brain, the symbolic House and the philosophical question can illuminate one another without stealing each other's names.

A useful House should leave the reader with better questions about reality, not better trivia about the website.

House as lived technology

A real house teaches more than “inside” and “outside”

The House metaphor becomes useful when its ordinary domestic parts are allowed to keep their jobs. A healthy house does not merely contain. It regulates access, creates privacy, hosts strangers, stores memory, concentrates warmth, survives weather, requires maintenance and still provides a way to leave.

FoundationWhat carries the load without being visible?

In a person this may be health, memory or trust; in an institution, law, energy, skills or infrastructure. A beautiful upper floor cannot compensate indefinitely for a failing base.

DoorAccess should be selective, legible and reversible

A door can open, close, admit, refuse and mark a threshold. A wall only separates. The difference matters whenever boundaries are confused with isolation.

WindowProtection without blindness

Healthy containment still permits observation and correction. A system that protects itself by eliminating outside evidence has become epistemically expensive.

HearthA center exists to sustain life around it

The domestic center is not merely the highest-status seat. It is where heat, food, conversation and continuity become available to others.

Guest roomHospitality tests identity

A strong house can receive difference without requiring the visitor to become identical with the host—and without surrendering every boundary.

ExitA home without a legitimate exit can become a shell

Belonging becomes capture when leaving, disagreeing or carrying one's skills elsewhere is made prohibitively costly.

A small test.

Imagine a house with excellent locks, no windows, one key-holder, no guests, no repairs, and a door that only opens inward. It is highly contained. That does not make it healthy. The House/Shell distinction begins there.

The practical question is not “does the system have boundaries?” but what those boundaries protect, what they permit to pass, how they are repaired, and whether the people inside become more capable.

Imagine beginning with something ordinary that refuses to remain ordinary: grief becomes memory; memory becomes story; story becomes habit; habit becomes culture; culture becomes an institution that shapes another person's life. Or imagine a seed disappearing into darkness and returning as a living structure nobody could have discovered by staring at the seed's surface. The House gives that movement a language.

A house at night

Some of the deepest architecture is ordinary

Someone knocks. Before you know who it is, the House already contains a moral question. Do you open? Do you ask who is there? Does the guest need shelter, food, privacy, distance, or simply directions? Hospitality is not the removal of every boundary. It is the art of making room without making the House meaningless.

Inside, different rooms exist because not every relation needs the same access. A kitchen invites one kind of nearness. A bedroom asks for another standard of privacy. A front step can hold a conversation that should never enter the inner rooms. Locks can protect. Locks can also imprison. Windows let a House receive light without becoming completely exposed.

And every healthy House has some relation to departure. Guests leave. Children grow. Owners change. Rooms change purpose. Objects are inherited, discarded or moved. A House becomes a Shell when preservation of the structure matters more than the lives the structure was meant to shelter.

That is why the project's architecture works best as a human metaphor when it remembers hospitality, privacy, maintenance and exit. A good House does not prove its strength by keeping everyone inside.

Two trees · two kinds of reproduction

The difference between Life and Strife is not whether something grows. Both can grow extremely well.

A Tree of Life and a Tree of Strife can share the same basic anatomy: roots, trunk, branches, fruit and seed. The difference is what each stage carries forward. A living root turns memory into nourishment. A strife root turns unresolved material into a permanent reason for recurrence. A living trunk coordinates difference without requiring sameness. A strife trunk hardens one interpretation until every branch inherits it.

Branches matter because reproduction is rarely centralized. One conflict becomes several accounts; several accounts become communities, clips, jokes, rules or habits; those branches can continue long after the original event has stopped supplying new information. Fruit is what the system actually produces: capability, repair and nourishment in one tree; dependency, retaliation or inherited hostility in the other. Seed is what makes the pattern portable.

Yggdrasil gives a useful external correction here: a sacred Tree can be damaged from within its own ecology and still remain a world-supporting structure. Biblical Tree/Fruit language adds another test: trees are known downstream, by what they bear. Biology contributes a final restraint—real trees survive through circulation, compartmentalization, symbiosis, seasonal loss and constant exchange with an environment. No healthy Tree is merely a vertical hierarchy.

The practical lesson is uncomfortable: removing one bad fruit does not necessarily repair the root, and exposing one root does not automatically create a living tree. Repair means changing what the system is able to reproduce next.

These are project symbols and comparative structures, not a claim that Genesis, John, Yggdrasil, anatomy and the Potato House are literally the same system. The deeper sections below keep those distinctions explicit.

Canonical topology

One House, two fields, one shared Door

Mountain and Swamp are painted as terrains inside the fields, not as names for the fields themselves. Mountain visualizes integrative ascent and perspective inside the source-facing field. Swamp visualizes entanglement and stalled recurrence inside manifestation. World, Body and ordinary material life are broader than Swamp; Heaven/Source are broader than Mountain.

The Potato is not a stack of floors. Geometrically, Source and Manifestation remain complete while genuinely overlapping; their shared region is Door. Theologically, the whole is triune: Father is source-facing, Son is manifestation/passage-facing, and Spirit is living continuity through the relation. Plane cuts through the overlap; Axis passes through both centers; Cross is Plane × Axis at the relational midpoint.

Two fields do not mean only two theological terms. Fields are geometry; Father, Son and Spirit are theological roles/relations; Potato of Life is the whole.
Plane and Cross are inside the Door. They are coordinates of the overlap, not places you must travel through first.
Dwellings are major abiding domains. The ten broad domains make the House readable without turning every subject into a separate top-level world.
House and Shell are regimes. Healthy containment preserves circulation, repair and autonomy; capture is not defined by shape or vertical position.

Purpose & discovery

What is this knowledge for?

The House should not reward accumulation for its own sake. Its highest-value work is to help a reader make sense of life, choose direction, grow real capability, understand consequences, and discover connections that ordinary browsing leaves fragmented.

Purpose is not assigned by the archive. The project can help a person examine whether a life-direction is coherent, genuinely chosen, relationally meaningful, materially possible and fruitful.

The inhabited House

The structure is not the subject. The world is.

Door, Tree, River, Mountain, Garden, Swamp, House and Forge are the grammar. The population is society itself: people, families, governments, nations, companies, banks, religions, universities, NGOs, media, technologies, infrastructures, cultures, philosophies, economies, ecosystems and the relations among them.

The architecture succeeds when the reader starts seeing the world more clearly and stops admiring the architecture for its own sake. A good House should eventually disappear into use: the symbols become habits of attention, the Rooms become places where real subjects can be understood, and the Tree becomes visible in what actually grows, breaks, repairs and returns outside the website.

Every concrete entity can be read through three coupled lenses. Spirit asks what gives it meaning, legitimacy or purpose. Mind asks how it knows, decides, remembers and coordinates. Body asks what materially embodies it: people, assets, territory, infrastructure, labour, money and resources.

How the project metabolizes knowledge

A source should be able to travel all the way through the House

Every source moves through three practical stages: preserve what exists and how it is known; keep the durable definition or model in one place; then explain it in a form people can actually use. Revision should move backward through the same chain without erasing the earlier state.

Capture / recover → classify evidence → durable source record → locate in time → compare / test → public reader → Work / Fruit → revision / Seed

1 · Capture / recoverPreserve the artifact, observation, post, text, screenshot, file or remembered object before interpretation outruns the source.
2 · Classify evidenceName source class, confidence, recovery state, date precision and what the source can—and cannot—establish.
3 · Keep one durable recordDefinitions, models, events and relationships are maintained in one strongest record rather than multiplied across competing summaries.
4 · Locate in timeOccurrence time, publication time, recovery time and later reinterpretation stay separable so mature canon is not backdated.
5 · Compare / testResearch asks what conflicts, what follows, what would fail, what remains analogy and what deserves promotion.
6 · Explain it clearlyThe reader gets the event, mechanism, object or argument in ordinary language, with deliberate routes back to evidence and deeper models.
7 · Work / FruitKnowledge can become a story, model, map, tool, essay, game, song or other usable consequence without the work automatically becoming evidence.
8 · Revision / SeedReception, correction, new evidence and failed predictions return to History/Research and become the next Seed instead of silently rewriting the past.

Room topology

Not a hallway — a typed, multi-axis House

The hypercube intuition is useful because several independent directions can be active at once, but the House does not force every Room into one literal 4D box. Containment, evidence, time, transformation, world application and expression are separate relations. A Room can therefore be adjacent in one sense and distant in another.

Meaning heartPotatoverse / Canon — project meaning, identity and symbolic architecture.
Orthogonal axesArchive & Sources · Time & History · Research Lab run through many other Rooms as provenance, chronology and frontier/promotion.
Translation beltTraditions & Texts · Science & Formal Models · Life & Body translate, compare, formalize and embody.
World-facing beltWorld Systems · Culture & Information carry structures into institutions, places, media and social systems.
Fruit / expressionWorks is where ideas become authored artifacts without those artifacts automatically becoming doctrine or evidence.

Living Axis crosses the whole House as the THROUGH coordinate. It changes state; it does not own the Rooms it traverses.

The lifecycle

What the Axis had been missing

The project’s practice path starts earlier than Plane and continues beyond Fruit. Mud, Burial, Eye, Root and Sprout explain latent development before visible world-facing action; Fruit, Seed and Return explain how growth becomes transferable and cyclical rather than merely “ascending.”

Mud → Burial → Eye → Root → Sprout → Plane → Door → Ladder / Mountain → Tree → Fruit → Seed → Return

This lifecycle is a practical/developmental projection, not a literal cosmic staircase. It can intersect the same D-level more than once.

Boundary

The House vocabulary organizes knowledge and symbolic interpretation. It does not turn symbolic placement into evidence rank, political authority, scientific truth or moral worth. The public site is a readable view into the House, not the whole archive.

Moving sections through one House

Rooms are homes; Σ-sections are views

The House can now be read through the same O-centered bidirectional spiral used by the Axis. The sampled rings are not extra floors. They are movable analytical cuts: select a section to ask a different question while canonical Room ownership stays fixed.
