Canonical topology

One House, two fields, one shared Door

The Potato is not a stack of floors. Geometrically, Source and Manifestation remain complete while genuinely overlapping; their shared region is Door. Theologically, the whole is triune: Father is source-facing, Son is manifestation/passage-facing, and Spirit is living continuity through the relation. Plane cuts through the overlap; Axis passes through both centers; Cross is Plane × Axis at the relational midpoint.

Two fields do not mean only two theological terms. Fields are geometry; Father, Son and Spirit are theological roles/relations; Potato of Life is the whole.
Plane and Cross are inside the Door. They are coordinates of the overlap, not places you must travel through first.
Dwellings are public scale projections. The ten canonical owners can be read as major abiding domains without creating another fact-owning backend layer.
House and Shell are regimes. Healthy containment preserves circulation, repair and autonomy; capture is not defined by shape or vertical position.

Purpose & discovery

What is this knowledge for?

The House should not reward accumulation for its own sake. Its highest-value work is to help a reader make sense of life, choose direction, grow real capability, understand consequences, and discover connections that ordinary browsing leaves fragmented.

Purpose is not assigned by the archive. The project can help a person examine whether a life-direction is coherent, genuinely chosen, relationally meaningful, materially possible and fruitful.

Public Fruit · quiet roots

The reader should meet the thing before the architecture

A House can become so good at classification that it starts making the reader walk through filing cabinets before seeing what was filed. This project now adopts the opposite priority for public pages: teach the subject directly first. Give the event, mechanism, quotation, number, text, object, conflict, evidence or consequence. Let Rooms, ownership and provenance sit underneath as support.

That does not weaken the backend. It gives the two layers different jobs. Canonical records own durable definitions, sources, evidence state and revision history. Frontend prose is allowed to synthesize several owners into one readable passage, use rhythm and narrative, and place links inside ordinary sentences. The public page may become a substantial Work without becoming the source of truth.

The connection is deliberately quiet. During the site build, enriched public sections can receive machine-readable bindings to the Rooms and canonical owners that support them. Those bindings help search, review and future maintenance, but they do not appear as another grid and they are excluded from the reading experience. The roots can be dense while the Fruit remains edible.

The rule also runs backward. If writing a clear paragraph exposes a contradiction, missing owner or weak source, the backend should be repaired. If backend evidence changes, the paragraph becomes a review target—but it is not silently regenerated. This keeps the site alive without letting either layer dominate the other.

The lifecycle

What the Axis had been missing

The project’s practice path starts earlier than Plane and continues beyond Fruit. Mud, Burial, Eye, Root and Sprout explain latent development before visible world-facing action; Fruit, Seed and Return explain how growth becomes transferable and cyclical rather than merely “ascending.”

Mud → Burial → Eye → Root → Sprout → Plane → Door → Ladder / Mountain → Tree → Fruit → Seed → Return

This lifecycle is a practical/developmental projection, not a literal cosmic staircase. It can intersect the same D-level more than once.

Boundary

The House vocabulary is a project architecture for organizing knowledge and symbolic interpretation. It does not make symbolic placement into evidence rank, political authority, scientific truth or moral worth. The public site remains a projection of the underlying House rather than the owner of it.
