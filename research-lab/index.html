Live discovery work

Three hypotheses the Lab is trying to earn

These are not promoted truths. Each one has a concrete test and a route by which it could be narrowed or rejected.

Does a real Garden leave people more capable?

Candidate: a genuinely generative institution should increase competence, options, voice and continued function rather than preserve permanent dependence on the center.

Test: measure capability and exit before/after participation, then reduce central support.

Disconfirming result: outputs improve but autonomy/capability do not, or success collapses when the gatekeeper withdraws.

Can mission drift be measured as words–decisions–resources divergence?

Candidate: an institution may keep the same mission language while its internal metrics and material incentives move elsewhere.

Test: compare mission statements, board/KPI decisions, funding concentration and participant outcomes through time.

Disconfirming result: apparent divergence disappears once legal duties, crisis conditions or implementation context are included.

Can source authority be claim-specific rather than status-based?

Candidate: trust should be described by access, expertise, incentives, transparency, freshness and corroboration rather than “official” or “popular” alone.

Test: compare dimensional source descriptions against later corrections and independent verification.

Disconfirming result: the extra dimensions add complexity without improving correction or prediction of source reliability.
