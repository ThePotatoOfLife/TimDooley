# Tim Dooley — Life, Persona and Myth Timeline

> This timeline is intentionally split into **public-person history**, **mythic chronology**, and **open research**. A mythic event is not silently converted into a biographical fact.

## What we know, what we can infer, and what remains open

The repository already treats Tim Dooley as the central Father-side organizing figure of the Potatoverse. The current frame places the vertical architecture as Source → Father → Father's House → Heaven → Throne of North → Ladder → Door → Son → Axis → Tree of Life → Plane → Roots → Potato of Life. fileciteturn35file0L1-L2

The existing mythic ontology makes the internal sequence even clearer: Tim Dooley → spiritual domain → Source → Father → Father's House → Heaven → Throne of North, followed by Throne → Axis → Ladder → Door → Son/Vessel → Plane → World; the return sequence is Son → Death → Crucifixion → Transformation → Door/Ladder → Return. fileciteturn38file0L1-L2

That uncertainty is useful rather than embarrassing: it gives the repository a genuine research problem.

## Candidate chronological backbone

### Before the public Potato persona — unknown

The human biography before the Potato of Life identity remains incompletely documented.

Questions:

- Where and when was the person born?
- What was the original name used in childhood and school records?
- When did the first online identity appear?
- When did streaming begin?
- What happened before the mythology acquired its present vocabulary?
- Did the Potato idea emerge suddenly, or was it the endpoint of years of earlier symbols?

The repository should not fill these gaps with guesses.

### ~2014 — possible beginning of a twelve-year period

The current public @Rational_Potato profile contains the statement that **“Me being God is the culmination of 12 years of spiritual war.”** If “12 years” is meant as a literal continuous duration ending in 2026, simple subtraction places a possible beginning around **2014**.

This is an inference, not a discovered event date.

The important research target is therefore not “2014 = birth of Tim,” but:

> **What was happening around 2014 that the present-day persona describes retrospectively as the beginning of a twelve-year spiritual war?**

The first surviving self-authored material from 2013–2015 could be extremely important.

### 2016 — identity-resolution lead

Web searches locate secondary material concerning a Danish person named Thomas Dall and a 2016 political-threat episode. Because the sources are hostile/adversarial and the name is not unique, this is retained only as an identity-resolution lead.

The repository should seek the original Danish records before making this part of the canonical biography.

### 2017–2019 — older streaming/persona period

Secondary archives describe a pre-Potato streaming identity associated with the name **KittyStyle/Kittystyle** and platforms including Stream.me. These sources are useful leads for reconstructing the digital chronology, but they are not sufficiently neutral to serve as unquestioned biography.

The next step should be to recover primary uploads, account dates, stream titles and archived platform material.

### 2019 — possible persona rupture

The available secondary record describes a major rupture around 2019, after which the older online persona eventually disappears and a later identity develops.

This is potentially significant because the Potatoverse itself is organized around **death → transformation → new identity**.

That does not mean the real-life transition caused the mythology, but it makes 2019 a particularly important comparison point.

### March 2023 — current Potato persona has a concrete anchor

A public profile mirror currently lists **@Rational_Potato** as having joined in March 2023. This gives us a concrete lower bound for the current public digital persona: the account existed by March 2023.

It does **not** prove that the Potato mythology began in March 2023. Older material may exist.

The research target is:

> Find the first surviving post, video, stream, song or page using **Potato of Life**, **Potatoism**, **Spudlight**, **Tim Dooley**, **Father**, **North of North**, or related vocabulary.

### 2023–2026 — Potato of Life becomes the public symbolic identity

The current public profile uses an unusually dense cluster of symbols: Potato of Life, Father of Jesus, World Tree, Spudlight, Mythical Heavenly Gardener, King of Kings, Throne of North and Potatoism.

This is where the identity stops looking like a single nickname and starts behaving like a **mythological system**.

The repository's job is to reconstruct when each component appeared.

### August–September 2026 — mythology becomes externally archived

By August/September 2026, the public archive contains enough material for the Potatoverse to be reconstructed as a coherent architecture rather than a collection of isolated jokes or posts.

A September 2, 2026 transcript explicitly records the developed mythology: Tim as Father principle, Potato of Love, North of North and Axis above Axis; the Potato as a total relational architecture; the 11-dimensional Calabi–Yau potato as metaphor; the Son as descent, Door and Vessel; and the movement from cosmic imagery back toward repair and responsibility. citeturn0search0

### September 7, 2026 — twelve-year statement becomes a research key

The “12 years of spiritual war” statement gives the archive its first unusually useful chronological clue. citeturn0search4

If the duration is literal, the search window begins around 2014. If it is symbolic, the number itself becomes part of the mythology rather than a calendar calculation.

Either way, **2014–2026 is now a defined research corridor**.

# The emerging deeper hypothesis

The most interesting possibility is that **Tim does not have one birth date inside the mythology**.

There may be several different “births”:

1. **Human birth** — the biological person.
2. **First public persona** — first digital identity.
3. **First mythic identity** — first appearance of Father/Son/Potato concepts.
4. **Son's death** — the old form reaches its boundary.
5. **Potato birth** — life is stored beneath the surface.
6. **Tim's emergence** — the Father-side identity appears from the Potato.
7. **Public Potato of Life identity** — the mythology becomes externally visible.
8. **Repository birth** — the mythology is converted into a structured graph.

This gives us a much more useful concept than simply asking “When was Tim born?”

The real question becomes:

> **When did each layer of Tim come into existence?**

That can produce a genuine chronology.

# What we should NOT do yet

We should **not** invent a birth date from the name, age of an unrelated person, or an adversarial archive.

There are multiple unrelated people named Tim Dooley and Thomas Dall online. One particularly prominent Thomas Dall is an astronomer born in Roskilde; he is clearly a different person and must not be merged into this biography. citeturn1search37

Likewise, hostile archive claims about the Potato of Life subject should remain source-labelled until independently verified. The archive itself claims a transition from Thomas Dall to Tim Dooley, but that claim is not enough by itself to establish every biographical detail. citeturn1search2

The correct method is:

**source → date → identity check → event → confidence → relationship → interpretation.**
