About this text layer

The Torah consists of Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers and Deuteronomy. Sefaria provides structured access to the Hebrew text and available English translations through its public API.

This page is a reader interface, not a claim that one English translation represents the Hebrew original. The selected English edition is identified by the service response.
